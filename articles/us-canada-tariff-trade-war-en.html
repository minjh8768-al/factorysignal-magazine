Canada Fires Back 'It's War' Over Trump's 'Tariff Bomb' as US-Canada Conflict Escalates to Worst Point

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: KBS 세계는지금 · 트럼프 관세 폭탄에, 캐나다 “전쟁이다” (KBS_466회_2026.09.05.방송). Summarised from that video.

The trade conflict between the United States and Canada is escalating to its worst point as tariffs and retaliatory measures mount. After the US imposed high tariffs on Canadian products, Canada strongly pushed back, announcing its own retaliatory tariffs in response.

Lake Ontario Renaming Attempt and Sovereignty Infringement Controversy

US President Donald Trump signed an executive order changing the name of Lake Ontario, located on the border between Canada and the United States, to "Lake America." US Secretary of the Interior Doug Burgum stated that President Trump directly contacted companies to have the change reflected in mapping services.

In response, Premier Doug Ford of Ontario, Canada, strongly countered by installing large signs along the lakeshore. In the Ottawa City Council, calls were also raised to rename "Trump Street."

Breakdown of Tariff Talks and Canada's Retaliatory Measures

After tariff negotiations between the two countries officially broke down, the US imposed additional tariffs on Canadian imports. In response, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney strongly protested, stating that he could not accept the US proposal.

We cannot accept what they've offered, and we will not give what they've asked.

"We cannot accept what they've offered, and we will not give what they've asked."

Prime Minister Carney stated that Canada was under attack and expressed his position to respond decisively to the trade war.

Because we're attacked. You're at war when you get attacked.

"Because we're attacked. You're at war when you get attacked."

Canada levied retaliatory tariffs of equal scale, targeting tariff items aimed at US battleground states including Wisconsin, Maine, Michigan, and Kentucky. In response, Republican Senator Susan Collins assessed that imposing new tariffs on Canada was a mistake.

I still think imposing new tariffs on Canada is a mistake.

"I still think imposing new tariffs on Canada is a mistake."

Hardline Remarks by US Officials and Concerns Over Interference in Domestic Affairs

The words and actions of senior US officials also fueled the conflict. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent made remarks disparaging Canada's military scale, while US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth sparked controversy by posting about the Canadian military.

How are you going to be at war with Canada? What, are they going to take their two submarines from the Edmonton Mall and set them on us?

"How are you going to be at war with Canada? What, are they going to take their two submarines from the Edmonton Mall and set them on us?"

Professor Kim Jae-cheon of Sogang University analyzed that the US caused sovereignty infringement controversies by demanding control over Canada's third-country trade agreements and pressuring its French language protection regulations. Professor Kim explained that President Trump is clinging to tariff policies to rally his supporter base.

Expert Analysis and Economic Impact

Oh Keon-young, head of department at Shinhan Bank, pointed out that tariffs were cited as a cause of inflation in the US Federal Reserve's Beige Book. Oh projected that if trade suspension measures are implemented, inflation and supply chain disruptions could intensify.

Additionally, pollster Darrell Bricker diagnosed that Canadians facing economic difficulties might find it challenging to continuously support the government's hardline policy. Surveys within the United States also showed that 57% of respondents opposed imposing additional tariffs on Canada.
