Concerns Grow Over Delays to US Crypto Bill as Bitcoin Correction Likely to Continue

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 미국 크립토 정책 핵심인사 4명 줄줄이 사임...비트코인 4만불 밑으로 떨어지나. Summarised from that video.

As market attention focuses on virtual asset legislative procedures and regulatory moves in the United States, an analysis has suggested that caution is required regarding short-term policy uncertainty and the possibility of further Bitcoin corrections.

Legislative Delays and the Role of Regulators

Points have been raised that the speed of passing legislation could slow down following the recent departure of key figures who led virtual asset policy at major institutions including the US Department of the Treasury, the White House, and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). In particular, uncertainty has grown surrounding the likelihood of passing the CLARITY Act, which clearly distinguishes jurisdictional assets between the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC).

According to the analysis, the probability of the bill passing is estimated to be low due to factors such as requiring 60 votes in a full Senate vote, and regulatory uncertainty could impact the market in the short term as a result. However, even if legislation slows, forecasts were also presented that the crypto market itself will not regress to the past, as the SEC and CFTC have already been building a regulatory framework for virtual assets.

미국 의회가 암호화폐 법안 처리를 늦추더라도 이미 크립토 시장이 크게 성장하고 금융시장 안으로 들어왔기 때문에 이전으로 되돌아갈 수는 없다.

"Even if the US Congress delays processing crypto legislation, the crypto market has already grown significantly and entered the financial markets, so it cannot turn back to how it was before."

Mixed Technical Signals in the Altcoin Market

Amid policy uncertainty, chart trends for major virtual assets are showing mixed signals across different coins. Under Elliott Wave analysis, Bitcoin and Ethereum are assessed as still having a downward trend remaining, whereas some altcoins such as Solana and ADA exhibit relatively positive or differentiated trends in technical counts.

Bitcoin Chart Analysis and Correction Outlook

In the case of Bitcoin, the possibility of further declines was suggested based on Elliott Wave double combination (W-X-Y) pattern analysis. Both primary wave and alternate wave analyses indicated that additional correction processes are required before the downward wave completes.

Furthermore, considering a one-year cycle, analysis indicated that this corrective trend may not end in the short term and could extend beyond October.
