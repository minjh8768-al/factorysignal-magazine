"Downward Wave Remains for Bitcoin": Future Outlook via Elliott Wave Theory

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 비트코인, 마지막 상승 파동이 하나 더 남았습니다. 전고점 돌파 후 찾아올 하락 시그널. Summarised from that video.

As Bitcoin continues to trade around the $80,000 mark following a rebound, a market outlook based on Elliott Wave Theory has been presented.

Bitcoin's Current Position and the Elliott Wave Perspective

CEO Brian Kim maintained his existing view regarding Bitcoin's current wave position and explained the flow of the ABC corrective wave. Kim mentioned the possibility that Wave B is not yet complete or is forming a triangle pattern, noting that a final Wave C decline may still remain.

비트코인도 완전 추측이 아닙니다. 이것은 매수 매도의 사람들의 심리가 들어가서 생긴 것이고, 거기서 엘리어트 파동을 적용해 힌트를 얻는 것입니다.

"Bitcoin is not complete guesswork either. It is formed by the psychology of buyers and sellers, and we get hints by applying Elliott Wave theory to it."

Caution Against Golden Cross Signals

He also called for a cautious approach toward the moving average golden cross—a phenomenon where the 50-day moving average crosses above the 200-day moving average—which has drawn market attention. Kim pointed out that just because the 50-day line breaks above the 200-day line does not guarantee a 100% bull market, noting that there have been a considerable number of past failures.

He explained that rather than relying solely on a single indicator to make reckless investments, investors should make comprehensive judgments combining market participants' psychology and wave structure.

Applying Wave Theory: The Case of Venice Coin

Kim illustrated the accuracy of Elliott Wave guidelines by using the price movement of Venice Coin as an example. He presented a method for calculating target price ranges using the rules of a zigzag pattern, where the lengths of Wave A and Wave C form similarly.

Future Strategy

In conclusion, he advised that even if a short-term rebound or a time-buying triangle pattern develops, a cautious stance should be maintained until the downward wave is completely finished.
