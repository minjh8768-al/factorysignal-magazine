How to Invest in US Stocks Amid a Falling Exchange Rate: 'Distinguish What Changes from What Doesn't'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 광화문금융러 | 우상향하는 삶 · 원달러 환율 1300원대, 지금 환전해도 될까? 미국주식 투자자가 꼭 알아야 할 기준. Summarised from that video.

Recently, the won-dollar exchange rate underwent a rapid correction, falling to the 1,300-won level. This is the lowest level in 23 months, raising concerns among U.S. stock investors over whether to exchange currency now or wait for the exchange rate to fall further.

Two Major Reasons for the Recent Drop in the Exchange Rate

Interest rate movements in South Korea and the United States, along with dollar selling by domestic exporters, are cited as background factors behind the recent adjustment in the won-dollar exchange rate. While the Bank of Korea raised its benchmark interest rate repeatedly to boost the value of the Korean won, the U.S. maintained a stance of keeping rates on hold, inducing a trend of won appreciation.

In addition, domestic export performance was strong, led by semiconductor companies, resulting in a large influx of dollars into South Korea. In the process of companies converting their dollar holdings into won for tax payments, wage disbursements, and facility investments, a wave of selling volume emerged, accelerating the pace of the exchange rate drop.

Limits of Exchange Rate Forecasting and Principles That Do Not Change

With exchange rates, psychological factors play a strong role, making it feel as though the rate will rise more when it goes up and fall more when it goes down. However, because factors that could trigger a rebound in the exchange rate—such as the possibility of a U.S. benchmark rate hike or moves by companies to delay currency exchange—also remain, being confident in only one direction is risky.

한쪽 방향에 대한 확신이 커질수록 반대쪽의 가능성도 높아진다. 환율이 계속 떨어지는 시기에는 반대로 오를 수 있는 가능성을 열어둬야 한다.

"As conviction in one direction grows, the possibility of the opposite side also increases. During a period when the exchange rate continues to fall, one must leave open the possibility that it could rise instead."

A Company's Growth Potential Matters More Than Exchange Rates

Because exchange rates follow cycles of fluctuations, their impact on long-term investment performance is limited. On the other hand, because the stock prices of companies with outstanding competitiveness trend upward over the long term, a low price missed once may not return.

The core question investors should focus on is not what the exchange rate will be next month, but whether the stock price of a company of interest is undervalued and whether its prospects for earnings growth are clear. As U.S. AI technology stocks recently continue to demonstrate real earnings performance based on massive investment capital, it is desirable to maintain principle-based stock investing rather than being swayed by short-term exchange rate fluctuations.
