Repercussions of Stricter Property Tax: Mid-to-High-Priced Apartments Face Adjustments, Rental Market Instability Feared

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: MBN News · 다시 강력해진 종부세, 부동산 시장엔 어떤 파장이 [MBN 뉴스7]. Summarised from that video.

Apartments Priced at 2 Billion Won or More Broadly Affected by Comprehensive Real Estate Tax

As the burden of the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax is significantly strengthened under the government's tax reform plan, waves of impact are expected in the real estate market. Apartments with an officially assessed price exceeding 1.4 billion won, or a market price exceeding 2 billion won, are expected to fall within the main impact zone.

In Seoul, many standard-sized apartments in Mok-dong and Yeouido, as well as the so-called "Mayongseong" areas (Mapo, Yongsan, and Seongdong) and Dongjak, Gwangjin, and Gangdong Districts, are expected to be subject to the tax. In Gyeonggi Province, Bundang, Pangyo, and Gwanggyo, as well as parts of Dongtan, are included. In regional areas, Haeundae, Suyeong, and Nam Districts in Busan, as well as Suseong District in Daegu, are analyzed to be affected.

Comprehensive Real Estate Tax Could Rise Sharply Despite Tax Burden Cap

Despite the existence of a tax burden cap system that limits the increase in tax compared to the previous year, observers point out that the actual Comprehensive Real Estate Tax burden could jump significantly.

세부담 상한은 종부세 단독이 아니라 종부세와 재산세를 합친 보유세를 기준으로 적용된다. 이미 재산세로 상당 금액을 납부하고 있기 때문에 보유세 상한선 내에서도 종부세 자체는 내년부터 대폭 오를 수 있다.

"The tax burden cap applies based on holding taxes, which combine the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax and property tax, rather than the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax alone. Since a significant amount is already paid in property tax, the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax itself could rise sharply starting next year even within the holding tax cap."

Non-Resident Landlords Moving to Owner-Occupancy... Potential Supply-Demand Instability in Rental Market

As the tax burden increases on single-home owners who do not reside in their properties and multiple-home owners, moves to switch to owner-occupancy or to sell are expected to grow. If non-resident landlords move in to reside, there is a risk that rental listings will decrease.

While the government maintains that tenant demand will also drop, resulting in no impact on the rental market, popular areas such as Seoul are suffering from a chronic shortage of rental supply. With full-scale occupancy of the Third-Stage New Towns scheduled for after 2028, concerns are also being raised that rental supply-demand instability could intensify for the time being.

Limited Influx of Listings from Multiple-Home Owners and Price Outlook

The prevailing analysis is that the likelihood of a massive surge of listings from multiple-home owners flooding the market is limited. This is because the extent of tax relief has decreased compared to the period of temporary exclusion from heavy capital gains tax, and there is still time before the actual imposition of the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax.

Regarding the direction of housing prices, differentiation is expected depending on the price tier. Real estate experts predicted market price adjustments following the increase in the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax burden.

중고가 아파트는 종부세 부담으로 매수세가 위축되며 가격 조정 가능성이 있다. 반면 중저가 아파트는 세부담이 크지 않아 실수요자 위주의 매수세가 이어질 수 있다.

"Mid-to-high-priced apartments face potential price adjustments as buying interest shrinks due to the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax burden. On the other hand, mid-to-low-priced apartments do not carry a heavy tax burden, so buying demand centered on actual end-users could continue."

Other Details of the Tax Reform Plan

In addition to real estate, this tax reform plan includes various tax changes related to public livelihoods.

  • Education fee deduction for preschool children: To prevent private tutoring, the education fee deduction will apply only to arts and sports academy fees.
  • Income tax deduction for non-homeowner couples: Even if a non-homeowner couple lives apart, if each has taken out a jeonse loan, both can receive an income tax deduction.
  • Termination of liquor tax reduction for draft beer: The liquor tax reduction benefit for draft beer will expire this year, with draft beer prices expected to rise starting next year.
