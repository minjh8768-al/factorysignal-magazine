U.S. Crypto Regulation in Limbo as Clarity Act Faces Hurdles Ahead of Midterms

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: CNBC · Crypto Bet Big On U.S. Regulation, And Now Its Landmark Legislation Hangs In The Balance. Summarised from that video.

The U.S. cryptocurrency industry is making an all-out push to pass the Clarity Act, a bill aimed at bringing regulatory clarity to digital assets, but growing concerns over legislative delays are mounting due to hurdles in Congress and the upcoming midterm election schedule.

Political Hurdles and the Midterm Barrier

The Clarity Act contains comprehensive regulatory measures for the cryptocurrency industry as a whole, including defining the supervisory authority of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), registration requirements, and anti-money laundering provisions. However, ahead of a Senate vote, processing of the bill is being delayed due to conflicts between the ruling and opposition parties over issues such as the revenue-sharing model for stablecoin issuers, ethics regulations for public officials, and specifying executive branch authority.

John Darsie, CEO of investment firm SALT, pointed out that it is difficult for major legislation to be passed ahead of the midterm elections.

"개인적으로 클래러티 법안 통과에 대해 다소 회의적입니다. 역사를 보면 중간선거를 앞두고 이 정도 규모의 입법이 이뤄지는 경우는 드뭅니다."

"I personally am a bit pessimistic about the Clarity Act being passed. History would tell you that leading into the midterms, you don't often pass legislation of this magnitude."

Senator Cynthia Lummis stated that she has been negotiating with Democrats throughout the year to secure 60 votes to bring the bill to the Senate floor, but faced difficulties due to continuous demands for amendments.

Preemptive Moves by Regulators and Regulatory Frameworks

Amid delays in passing the bill, regulatory agencies and industry stakeholders are moving to establish their own regulations and standards. Sunayna Tuteja, former Chief Innovation Officer at the U.S. Federal Reserve, explained that the CFTC and SEC are conducting informal and public discussions and preparations from a rulemaking perspective even before the legislation is finalized.

Denelle Dixon, CEO of the Stellar Development Foundation, also stated that the SEC and CFTC should focus on establishing digital asset standards and frameworks, laying a sustainable regulatory foundation that persists into the next administration.

Concerns Over Weakening Global Competitiveness

Former New York Governor Andrew Cuomo pointed out that if the United States fails to pass clear legislation, it could fall behind in competition with overseas markets.

"클래러티 법안을 통과시키지 못하면 국제적 경쟁력에서 손해를 보게 됩니다. 유럽과 아시아가 우리보다 앞서 나가고 있습니다."

"If we don't pass the Clarity Act, it costs us internationally. Europe is ahead of us, Asia is ahead of us."

Former Governor Cuomo warned that if control of the House of Representatives changes depending on the outcome of the midterm elections, regulatory uncertainty could deepen further due to confrontation between the executive branch and Congress.

Long-Term Outlook and the Bill's Direction

Andrew McCormick, Head of Capital Markets and Asset Development at Chainlink Labs, emphasized that long-term legislation capable of withstanding changes in administration or the political environment must be enacted to secure capital inflows and market predictability.

Binance founder Changpeng Zhao offered the view that because the overall atmosphere in U.S. politics and regulatory agencies is leaning pro-crypto, the industry's long-term growth will continue regardless of whether any single bill passes. Meanwhile, according to data from prediction market Kalshi, the likelihood of major cryptocurrency legislation being finalized within the year is assessed as low.
