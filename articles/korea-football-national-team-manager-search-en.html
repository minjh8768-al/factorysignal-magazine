South Korea Face Rebuilding Task Amid Managerial Uncertainty and Links to Foreign Coaches

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: JTBC News · [단독] 벤투 "축구대표팀 임시 감독직 지원 안 한다"…정식 감독 의사는 유지 [이슈PLAY] / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

The South Korea national football team faces the task of reorganizing its leadership amid confusion over the managerial appointment process. As the Korea Football Association (KFA) moves to publicly recruit an interim manager and activate its National Teams Committee, foreign media and football experts are urging structural improvements and the establishment of a clear direction for South Korean football.

Foreign Managers Including Bento, Poyet, and Martínez Express Interest

Paulo Bento, former head coach of the national team, stated that he conveyed to the Korea Football Association that while he will not apply for the two-month interim manager position, he is interested in the permanent role. Gus Poyet, who previously managed Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors, also expressed his desire to lead the South Korean national team, showing an active attitude by posting the South Korean flag.

Reports also emerged that manager Roberto Martínez, who led the Portugal national team in the North and Central American World Cup, expressed interest in the South Korea manager position, as a number of foreign managers show interest in the leadership overhaul of the South Korean national football team.

Park Ji-sung and Park Moon-sung: "Need Modern Tactics That Capitalize on South Korean Football's Strengths"

Football experts also offered suggestions regarding the qualifications for the next manager. Commentator Park Ji-sung emphasized the need for a leader who can implement modern tactics based on South Korean football's mobility and physical strengths.

상대를 압박하면서 많이 뛰고 체력적인 부분으로 이끌어가며 투지 있게 경기할 수 있는 능력을 바탕으로 현대적인 색깔과 전술을 더할 수 있는 감독이 필요하다.

"We need a manager who can add modern style and tactics based on the ability to pressure opponents, run hard, leverage physical strength, and play with fighting spirit."

Commentator Park Moon-sung (박문성) also pointed out that the KFA must first set a clear direction for the national team and establish a system to appoint a leader who fits that vision.

Seol Ki-hyeon: "Lack of Tactical Detail Failed to Capitalize on Players' Strengths"

Appearing on a JTBC studio broadcast, former Gyeongnam FC manager Seol Ki-hyeon cited tactical limitations that failed to bring out the strengths of the squad as the cause of the team's poor performance. He pointed out that despite having highly skilled players such as Son Heung-min and Lee Kang-in, detailed preparation of formations was lacking.

전술 포메이션에 디테일이 있어야 하고 경기를 통해 보완해 나가야 하는데, 선수들의 개인 능력에 의존하면서 발전된 경기력이 나오지 못했다.

"There must be detail in tactical formations, which should be refined through matches, but relying on individual player abilities prevented improved performances."

In addition, foreign media including The Athletic and AP reported on the South Korean government's special audit of the KFA and criticism over transparency in the manager selection process, noting that calls for reform across South Korean football administration are mounting.
