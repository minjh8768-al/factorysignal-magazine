Yoo In-tae: "Democratic Party Leadership Race Was 'President vs. Ddanzi Ilbo'... Kim Min-seok's ABC Plan Was a Counterattack"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · “어떻게 저런 애들이 대표까지”..자칭 ‘정치 꼰대’ 유인태의 촌철 분석 | 💡지식의발견. Summarised from that video.

"President vs. Ddanzi Ilbo": Yoo In-tae's View on the Party Convention

Yoo In-tae, former secretary-general of the National Assembly, offered a diagnosis of the results of the recent Democratic Party of Korea convention. The former secretary-general assessed that this party leadership election went beyond a simple competition between individuals and revealed the dynamics of influence within the ruling bloc.

The former secretary-general defined the nature of this election by analyzing that external forces influenced former party leader Jung Chung-rae's voter support base.

이 싸움은 정청래의 힘이 아니고 이재명 대통령하고 소위 딴지일보 하고의 대결이라고 본다.

"I see this fight not as Jung Chung-rae's power, but as a confrontation between President Lee Jae-myung and the so-called Ddanzi Ilbo."

Kim Min-seok's 'ABC Plan' Is a Counterattack Against Rhyu Si-min

The former secretary-general also offered an interpretation of the 'ABC Plan' declared by newly elected party leader Kim Min-seok in his acceptance speech. Leader Kim emphasized livelihoods (Affordability), expanding political reach (Broad), and competence (Competence).

The former secretary-general noted that the contents of this speech could be a counterattack targeting writer Rhyu Si-min and others who had critically evaluated him in the past.

유시민이 보기에 김민석이 전형적인 B였을 거다. 취급당한 거에 대한 반격이라고 봐야 한다.

"In Rhyu Si-min's eyes, Kim Min-seok was probably a typical B. This should be seen as a counterattack against how he was treated."

Outlook on Supplementary Investigation Power Revisions and Party-Presidency Relations

The former secretary-general pointed out that discussions regarding Criminal Procedure Act amendments and supplementary investigation powers were among the main factors driving changes in the president's approval rating. He viewed that future state administration and internal party unity tasks depend on how the presidential office responds.

Noting the relatively high public opinion in favor of retaining supplementary investigation powers, he projected that discussions on re-amendments could be pursued in the future to rebound approval ratings and soothe public sentiment.
