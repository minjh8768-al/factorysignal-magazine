Over 50% Chance of Surprise Rate Hike at July FOMC, Says Economist Yoo Shin-ik

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 한국경제TV · FOMC, 매파적 동결 vs 인상? "충격 미리 대비하세요" | 유신익 KB WM스타자문단 이코노미스트 | 전략 인사이드. Summarised from that video.

As market tension rises ahead of the July U.S. Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting, an analysis suggested that the Federal Reserve could go beyond a hawkish pause and opt for a surprise interest rate hike.

Yoo Shin-ik (유신익), an economist at KB WM Star Advisory Group, appeared on a broadcast and delivered a detailed diagnosis of the Fed's rate decision outlook, along with oil price dynamics and Big Tech financial health risks.

Service Inflation Pressures and the Fed's Calculations

Economist Yoo Shin-ik viewed that while a hawkish pause is likely at this FOMC meeting, the possibility of a surprise rate hike cannot be ruled out. He explained that rising service prices are holding the Fed back.

깜짝 인상을 할 가능성도 50%가 넘는다고 봅니다. 서비스를 중심으로 한 물가 압력이 굉장히 강해지고 있기 때문입니다.

"I believe there is over a 50% chance of a surprise rate hike because inflation pressures centered on services are becoming extremely strong."

He pointed out that PCE inflation for major service items such as healthcare, transportation costs, and financial services exceeds the 3% level, making it difficult to expect overall inflation to stabilize based solely on falling oil prices.

Illusion of Oil Price Stability and the Refined Oil Dilemma

Although crude oil prices showed some stability due to easing geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, evaluations indicated that the actual burden of energy prices persists. Economist Yoo Shin-ik stressed that focus should be placed on refined petroleum product prices such as gasoline and diesel rather than WTI crude oil prices.

He noted that prices for diesel and marine fuels continue to soar as refining facilities become scarce due to reductions in global refining capacity and damage from bombardments. This leads to higher transportation costs, serving as a factor that limits downward pressure on inflation.

Big Tech Financing Limits and Stock Market Volatility

Excessive capital expenditure (CAPEX) and financing risks of Big Tech companies were also cited as new threat factors for the stock market. Economist Yoo Shin-ik pointed out that Big Tech firms are relying on external financing, such as bond issuances, to offset declining free cash flow (FCF).

올해 들어 투자 지출의 50% 이상이 외부 채권 발행을 통한 조달에 집중되고 있습니다. 수익성에 대한 의구심이 지속되는 상황에서 부채 리스크가 시장에 부담으로 작용하고 있습니다.

"Since the start of this year, more than 50% of investment expenditure has been concentrated on financing through external bond issuances. Amid persistent doubts about profitability, debt risk is acting as a burden on the market."

He added that pursuit by Chinese memory semiconductor companies and concerns over oversupply could increase downside volatility in domestic and international stock markets. Consequently, he urged a conservative stance for the time being to prepare for market shocks.
