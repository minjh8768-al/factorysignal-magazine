Tactile Data Is Key to Physical AI: OpenGraph Labs Partners with NVIDIA and ByteDance

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: EO Korea · “HW·SW 다 직접 만들어요” 창업 6개월 만에 엔비디아 뚫고 10억 매출 낸 20대들. Summarised from that video.

OpenGraph Labs Tackles 'Tactile Data' Bottleneck in Physical AI

As the Physical AI and humanoid robot markets grow rapidly, OpenGraph Labs, a startup building essential data infrastructure for robot reality perception and action processing, is drawing attention. Kim Ju-ho, CEO of OpenGraph Labs, pointed out that existing AI models face limits in fully understanding the laws of the physical world through visual and auditory data alone.

OpenGraph Labs utilizes its self-developed first-person cameras and tactile gloves to simultaneously collect behavioral and tactile data of humans handling objects, converting this into data formats that humanoids can learn from.

"I think our direction should be handing over today's work and finding what to do tomorrow, rather than replacement."

Successive Collaborations with Global Big Tech

OpenGraph Labs provides high-quality data collected and processed in-house to major global frontier labs and big tech companies, including NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and ByteDance.

In particular, its technological capabilities were recognized as it participated as an official partner in 'NVIDIA Isaac Teleop,' NVIDIA's humanoid robot platform ecosystem. OpenGraph Labs has built a vertically integrated in-house system spanning hardware manufacturing to software pipelines and field operations capabilities, maintaining data accuracy and sync quality.

Local Chinese Manufacturing Network and 7-Day Cycle

Based on feedback from researchers in San Francisco, U.S., the OpenGraph Labs team went directly to manufacturing sites in Shenzhen, China, and completed a 7-day prototype improvement cycle. Through an iteration system that rapidly replaces and improves hardware, mass production of approximately 500 data collection devices is currently underway.

In addition, to solve skilled worker retirements and labor shortages at domestic and foreign manufacturers, it is conducting proof-of-concept (PoC) projects to collect and preserve tacit technical data from actual factory sites, such as the Yeosu Industrial Complex.

A Future Where Humans and Robots Coexist

OpenGraph Labs is expanding its data collection infrastructure across various environments, including Korea, Mexico, and Europe. Co-founders including CTO Han Seung-ho and CPO Son Beom-su set a goal to establish global technical standards so that robots can naturally take over and assist human labor in next-generation physical AI environments.
