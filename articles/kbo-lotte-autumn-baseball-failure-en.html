'El-Lot-Gi' Fall Short of Postseason Together Again as Lotte's Slump Prevents Trio's Advance

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: KBS News · 프로야구 엘롯기 가을 야구는 언제? [뉴스in뉴스] / KBS 2026.09.07.. Summarised from that video.

As the late-season standings race in professional baseball grows fierce, the joint postseason advancement of the KBO League's representative popular teams, the so-called 'El-Lot-Gi' (LG, Lotte, and KIA), is expected to be thwarted again this year. While LG and KIA have virtually secured their postseason berths, Lotte has been unable to escape its slump and has fallen behind in the standings race.

Joint Advancement Foiled by Lotte's Slump

Currently, LG ranks third and KIA ranks fourth, placing them safely in line for autumn baseball. In contrast, Lotte remains in ninth place, with the gap between them and fifth-place Doosan widening to 10 games. With only 25 games remaining, reaching fifth place has become realistically difficult.

Han Sung-yoon (한성윤), a reporter from the Sports News Department, explained Lotte's situation as follows.

롯데가 지금 현재 순위가 9위입니다. 그리고 5위 두산과는 무려 10경기 차가 나거든요. 남은 경기가 25경기이기 때문에 10경기 차를 뒤집는 것은 현실적으로 불가능합니다.

"Lotte is currently in ninth place. And there is a gap of as many as 10 games with fifth-place Doosan. With 25 games left, it is realistically impossible to overturn a 10-game gap."

As Lotte's failure to reach the postseason becomes increasingly likely, observers point out that the team faces the worst-case scenario of failing to make autumn baseball for nine consecutive years.

The History of 'El-Lot-Gi' Originating from the 'Dark Ages'

The term 'El-Lot-Gi' originated not simply as a grouping of popular clubs, but from the dishonorable image of bottom-tier teams. Lotte finished in last place for four consecutive years from 2001 to 2004, and from 2005 to 2008, KIA and LG took turns finishing in last place.

As these three teams took turns finishing last for nine years, the phrase 'El-Lot-Gi Alliance' was born. Afterwards, although each team had periods of upward momentum—such as KIA's championship victory and Lotte's resurgence in 2009, and LG's leap into becoming a strong contender after 2019—there has not been a single time in professional baseball history that all three teams advanced to the postseason simultaneously.

Why There Was No Joint Advancement Even in Their Past Heydays

Even before the 2000s, when all three teams were considered powerhouses, joint postseason advancement did not happen. This was because during the early years of professional baseball, only the winners of the first-half and second-half leagues advanced to the Korean Series, or the number of teams advancing to the playoffs was limited to three or four. Additionally, the fact that the Samsung Lions set a record with 31 total postseason appearances also played a role.

In 1995, an opportunity for joint advancement arose when LG finished second, Lotte third, and Haitai (now KIA) fourth. However, under the regulations at the time, because the gap between third-place Lotte and fourth-place Haitai widened to 3.5 games or more, the Semi-Playoffs were not held, ruining Haitai's autumn baseball hopes.

The Need for Structural Improvement and Organization-Wide Change

There was a time when the three 'El-Lot-Gi' teams were strong enough to dominate the Korean Series titles, winning them all across 12 seasons from 1983 to 1994. However, Lotte remains the only team that has not won a Korean Series title in the 21st century since its last championship in 1992.

Reporter Han Sung-yoon emphasized that for Lotte to become a regular fixture in autumn baseball, the club needs an organizational overhaul across the board rather than relying on managerial changes. He suggested that the team must undergo changes with a mindset akin to refounding the club, taking cues from rival teams' success stories and data-driven baseball.
