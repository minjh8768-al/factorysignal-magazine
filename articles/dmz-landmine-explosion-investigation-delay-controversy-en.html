Controversy Grows Over Delayed Investigation into Landmine Explosion, Military Communication, and Defense Minister's Address

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 채널A News · [속보] 국민의힘 "다치니까 느그아들" … 민주당 "매국적 행태" [뉴스플레이리스트] / 채널A. Summarised from that video.

As soldiers were injured in a suspected landmine explosion near the Military Demarcation Line, controversy is growing over when the military will begin its on-site investigation and how it communicates with the public. With military authorities stating they will only conduct a joint site investigation with the UN Command after the Chuseok holiday, suspicions of downplaying and covering up the incident have been raised.

Delays in Site Investigation and Poor Public Communication Controversy

Following the accident, criticisms of inadequate response mounted as a Joint Chiefs of Staff official repeatedly told reporters during a military briefing that they would "check" rather than providing standard answers. Former Vice Minister of National Defense Shin Beom-chul (신범철) commented that while he understands the military's position that safety is the top priority and a cautious approach to dangerous areas is necessary, the military's inadequate public communications and response invited unnecessary misunderstandings and suspicions.

저희가 조사 결과가 안 나왔기 때문에 말씀드릴 수 없는 상황이고, 향후에 책임 있는 분이 내려와서 기자 여러분들과 국민 여러분들께 말씀드릴 수 있도록 하겠습니다.

"Because the investigation results are not out yet, we are not in a position to speak, and a responsible official will come down in the future to speak to reporters and the public."

Some pointed out that this contrasts with previous cases, noting that during the wooden-box landmine provocation in 2015, mine fragments were collected on the day of the accident and a joint investigation with the UN Command was conducted within two days, whereas this time the military has delayed the investigation without promptly responding to the UN Command's proposal for a site investigation.

Deployment of Remote Equipment and Political Clash

The Democratic Party criticized the opposition for using soldiers' lives and safety as a tool for political strife. On the other hand, the People Power Party countered that if it was too dangerous for personnel to enter directly, equipment such as drones and remotely controlled landmine detection and removal robots developed by the Agency for Defense Development should have been deployed immediately to inspect the site.

In response, former Vice Minister Shin noted that while remote equipment might not be operationally deployed to all frontline units, presenting operational limitations, the military should have mobilized available personnel and equipment and made efforts to secure trust by explaining the detailed situation to the public.

Controversy Over New Defense Minister's Inaugural Speech

Political tension also intensified over the wording in the inaugural address of the new Minister of National Defense Kang Shin-chul (강신철), which took place shortly after the accident. In his address, Minister Kang emphasized a strong military by quoting ancient Roman Julius Caesar's slogan.

왔노라, 보았노라, 이겼노라. 카이사르가 로마 군단을 이끌며 외쳤던 구호입니다. 압도적인 능력으로 적을 억제하여 경쟁에서 승리해야 합니다.

"I came, I saw, I conquered. This was the slogan Caesar shouted as he led the Roman legions. We must deter the enemy with overwhelming capability and win the competition."

Opposition figures and some political commentators strongly criticized the minister for using theatrical rhetoric immediately after soldiers suffered serious injuries from a landmine explosion. Professor Choi Jin-bong (최진봉) of Sungkonghoe University analyzed that while the inaugural address itself might contain principled content, given the timing, it is hard to avoid criticism for failing to consider the feelings of the injured soldiers and their families.

Need for Prompt Fact-Finding and Restoring Trust

Experts suggested that rather than unconditionally delaying announcements under the guise of military security, the military must transparently explain the situation to the public and press within allowable limits to quell unnecessary controversy. They also emphasized that clarifying North Korea's potential involvement through a prompt site investigation and fact-finding is the way to restore trust in the military.
