Lee Young-pyo, Seol Ki-hyeon, and Cho Won-hee Assess National Team's Four Match Series: "Interim Manager Should Be Given Chance Until Asian Cup"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 올스 · 대표팀에 무슨 일이? 이영표·설기현이 말하는 '원팀'의 조건? 모레노 감독, 아시안컵까지 갈까?[HOT다리영표] 시즌2 EP.16 KBS20261013. Summarised from that video.

Football commentators Lee Young-pyo and Cho Won-hee, along with former K League manager Seol Ki-hyeon, appeared on a broadcast to analyze from various angles the South Korean national football team's four A-matches played under interim manager Moreno. They exchanged views on the national team's record of 2 wins, 1 draw, and 1 loss, formation usage, players' defensive contributions, and whether the manager's contract should be renewed.

Evaluating Performance in Four A-Matches: Scores Above 80 for First Game

The panel generally gave high marks for the first match against Ecuador. Seol Ki-hyeon gave a positive evaluation by giving 80 out of 100 points for the first match, while Cho Won-hee gave 85 points, evaluating the compact spacing and attack-defense balance of the 4-4-2 formation positively. Lee Young-pyo also noted that the performance against Ecuador was a good debut worthy of 90 points or higher.

On the other hand, Seol rated the second match against Uruguay, which ended in a 1-4 defeat, at 60 points. Seol pointed out regrets revealed in the match against a strong team, saying that a large difference was felt in individual Uruguay players' strength and individual pressing.

Build-up from the Back and High Pressing: Discussion on Son Heung-min's Position

Cho evaluated that build-up from the back gradually improved as matches progressed. In response, Lee cited his experience with the 2002 World Cup national team, emphasizing the importance of strong forward pressure and aggressive football.

압박이 먹히니까 압박하면 체력이 하나도 안 힘들어. 왜냐하면 볼을 뺏어 갖고 공격하는 데 에너지를 쓰니까.

"When the pressure works, pressing doesn't drain your stamina at all. Because you spend your energy taking the ball back and attacking."

A discussion also followed regarding Son Heung-min's optimal position within the 4-4-2 formation. Considering mobility, Seol suggested deploying Son alongside a typical forward to utilize space, whereas Cho evaluated that Son's wide movements were still effective. Lee predicted that Son's position could be adjusted depending on the strength and situation of the opponent they face.

Kim Min-jae's Interview and Squad's Defensive Work Rate Habits

Comments by Kim Min-jae, who emphasized the whole team's defensive participation in a post-match interview, were also highlighted. Kim mentioned the burden on the defensive line and asked for unity from the whole team.

개인이 빛나기보다는 팀 전체가 하나가 되어야 좋은 결과를 가져올 수 있다고 생각한다.

"Rather than individuals shining, I think we can bring good results only when the entire team becomes one."

Seol viewed it as a statement that Kim, as a defensive leader, could naturally make, and recalled that dedicated defensive participation by forwards had been a traditional strength of South Korean football. Lee also pointed out that unless players build the habit of defending from the front during regular training and practice matches, it is difficult to face strong teams on big stages like the World Cup.

Advice on Squad Friction Rumors and Prospects of Continuing to the Asian Cup

Regarding recent rumors of internal conflict within the national team, Seol emphasized that complaints are common and that the manager's role in uniting players with diverse characteristics into one team is most important. Lee mentioned that processes can be interpreted differently depending on evaluation results, expressing hope that the current situation is a process of becoming 'one team.'

Finally, all panel members expressed positive views on whether the interim manager should accompany the team to the Asian Cup. Pointing out the practical difficulty of finding a new manager in a short period, Seol and Lee agreed that it makes sense to give him a chance to lead the team through the Asian Cup.
