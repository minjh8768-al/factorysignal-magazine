U.S. June PCE Inflation Stabilizes at 3.7%, Weakening Case for Further Rate Hikes

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · [속보] 인플레 급반전. 주가도 급반등 올것 [즉시분석]. Summarised from that video.

The U.S. Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index for June exactly matched market expectations, showing a slowdown in inflation. U.S. economic growth for the second quarter also came in lower than expected, confirming an economic slowdown resulting from high interest rates.

June PCE and Q2 GDP Release Results

The U.S. PCE inflation rate for June fell to 3.7% from 4.1% in the previous month. The core PCE inflation rate also dropped from 3.4% in the previous month to 3.3%, meeting market expectations.

The advance estimate of U.S. real Gross Domestic Product (GDP) growth for the second quarter, released alongside the PCE figures, stood at 1.5%. This was below the market expectation of 2.1%. However, a growth rate of 1.5% does not signal a recession, but is interpreted as a process of growth speed adjustment within a healthy economic trend.

Assessment of Further U.S. Interest Rate Hikes

With the stabilization of inflation indicators overlapping with slowing growth, analysts say the rationale for the Federal Reserve (Fed) to raise benchmark interest rates further has weakened. Kim Kwang-seok (김광석) evaluated the market impact of this indicator release as follows:

PCE 데이터가 시장에 미치는 영향은 거의 없다. 시장의 기대치에 정확히 부합하는 숫자가 발표됐다.

"The PCE data has almost no impact on the market. Figures that exactly match market expectations were released."

Kim pointed out that high interest rate levels have been maintained for a long time, causing U.S. economic growth to slow down significantly. He explained that from the perspective of real benchmark interest rates, current rates remain higher than the inflation rate, meaning that additional rate hikes are not required.

International Oil Price Fluctuations and Points to Note for Future Inflation Indicators

The fall in June inflation indicators was heavily influenced by the stabilization of international oil prices and a reduced contribution from energy prices. Both the Producer Price Index (PPI) and Consumer Price Index (CPI) recorded declines in June.

However, as international oil prices rebounded after July, warnings have emerged that upcoming inflation indicators could show a temporary rise again. Kim urged caution regarding the future indicators to be released.

8월에 발표될 CPI, PPI, PCE 데이터가 발표될 때마다 높아진 국제유가가 반영된 물가 데이터가 발표될 것이다. 이에 따라 조정 국면이 올 수 있으므로 유의할 필요가 있다.

"Every time the CPI, PPI, and PCE data scheduled for release in August are announced, inflation data reflecting higher international oil prices will be released. As a result, a correction phase may occur, so caution is required."

Future Monetary Policy and Market Outlook

Consequently, with inflation indicators managed within market expectation levels and signs of an economic slowdown confirmed, concerns over additional interest rate hikes have decreased significantly. Monetary authorities are also expected to focus on inflation stability while the labor market remains stable.
