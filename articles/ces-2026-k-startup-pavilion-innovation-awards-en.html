K-Startup Pavilion Opens at CES 2026 as 11 Companies Win Innovation Awards and 3 Claim Best of Innovation

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 티타임즈TV · AI 최고혁신상 쏟아졌다! CES2026 K-스타트업 활약상. Summarised from that video.

The K-Startup Pavilion, bringing together 81 promising Korean startups, opened at Eureka Park, the startup exhibition hall of CES 2026. Out of approximately 1,100 startups participating in Eureka Park, Korean companies numbered 411, accounting for 37% of the total and demonstrating an overwhelming presence.

11 Companies Win Innovation Awards, Best of Innovation Produced in AI Field

Marking its sixth year, the K-Startup Pavilion was established jointly by 19 public institutions and local governments, led by the Korea Institute of Startup & Entrepreneurship Development (KISED). Among the companies participating in the pavilion, 11 won CES 2026 Innovation Awards, three of which were awarded Best of Innovation. In particular, two of the Best of Innovation winners swept the artificial intelligence (AI) category.

Yoon Yeo-kyung (윤여경), head of the Global Cooperation Office at KISED, emphasized that the pavilion serves as a springboard for Korean startups to expand globally.

우선 K-스타트업 통합관은 단순 전시 참가가 아니라 한국 스타트업을 글로벌 무대로 등장시키는 공간입니다. 81개사가 전시 참가를 하고 있으며 이 중 11개 기업이 혁신상을 받은 기업입니다.

"First of all, the K-Startup Pavilion is not merely for exhibition participation, but a space that introduces Korean startups to the global stage. 81 companies are participating in the exhibition, and 11 of them are Innovation Award winners."

Support by Enterprise Lifecycle and Strategy for Elite Selection

The keyword for the K-Startup Pavilion this year is "streamlining into an elite force." Rather than focusing simply on the number of participating companies, substantial support was provided, including precision assistance tailored to the corporate lifecycle (pre-startup, early-stage, growth, TIPS, and super-gap), pre-exhibition mentoring, preliminary outreach to local investors, and IR matching.

Kang Ji-won (강지원), CEO of City Five, which won the Best of Innovation award in the AI category, shared reflections on achieving this outcome after pivoting.

사실 저희가 모바일 주변 기기 IoT 쪽으로 사업을 전개하고 있다가 AI로 피보팅하고 10개월 만에 받은 상이라서 정말 의미가 크고요. 어려운 상황에서도 끝까지 포기하지 않고 이 AI 분야를 개척해 보겠다는 생각을 가지고 했었습니다.

"In fact, we were conducting business in mobile peripheral IoT before pivoting to AI, so receiving this award just 10 months later is truly meaningful. Even in difficult circumstances, we worked with the mindset of never giving up until the end and pioneering this field of AI."

Building Global Networks and Promoting Inbound Policies

In addition to operating exhibition booths, the pavilion hosted the "Global Startup Summit" to foster exchanges with officials from major national pavilions such as Taiwan, Japan, and the Netherlands, as well as investment briefings and IR pitching events inviting local venture capitalists. On the day before the opening, the "K-Startup Night" networking event was held to connect overseas investors with domestic startups.

Furthermore, promotional booths for inbound policies aimed at attracting foreign entrepreneurs to Korea—including the "Startup Korea Special Visa," "K-Startup Grand Challenge," and Global Startup Center—were operated alongside a promotional booth for the global startup festival "COMEUP," highlighting the appeal of Korea's startup ecosystem.
