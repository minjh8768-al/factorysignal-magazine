From OpenAI's New Model Boost to US Rate, Oil, and Chip Pressure: Financial Market Trends

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · [머니버스] '반도체 과잉 투자' 걱정은 기우?..스스로 프로그램 설치하는 AI '아스트라' / SBS / 주영진의 뉴스브리핑. Summarised from that video.

As OpenAI unveiled a new artificial intelligence (AI) model, the domestic stock market showed an upward trend on expectations of expanded demand for semiconductors. On the other hand, external variables such as the US Federal Reserve's benchmark interest rate decision, international oil price instability, and US trade pressure emerged as major challenges for the market.

Semiconductor Stocks Surge on OpenAI's New Model Announcement

As OpenAI unveiled its new AI model 'Astra', expectations for the era of artificial general intelligence (AGI) rose. Accordingly, domestic semiconductor stocks showed strength as analyses suggested that demand for memory semiconductors would significantly increase.

Economic commentator Park Yeon-mi (박연미) explained, "The new model has evolved beyond the level of simply receiving and executing commands to judging and downloading necessary programs on its own to finish tasks," adding, "Expectations that such technological advancement will lead to increased demand for memory semiconductors drove up stock prices."

US FOMC Rate Direction... CPI Indicator Is the Variable

Ahead of the US central bank's benchmark interest rate decision, market forecasts regarding a rate hike versus a freeze are sharply divided. Former US President Donald Trump exerted pressure, urging the Fed to cut interest rates and mentioning a halt to trade.

Remarks hinting at a freeze also came from within the Fed. This was stated by Christopher Waller, a governor of the US Federal Reserve System.

향후 물가 지표에서 인플레이션 압력 완화가 확인된다면 기준금리 동결을 지지할 의향이 있다

"If future price indicators confirm an easing of inflation pressure, I am willing to support freezing the benchmark interest rate."

SBS editorial writer Park Jin-ho (박진호) analyzed, "John Williams, president of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, also mentioned that the recent surge in Treasury yields was a result of the economy's robustness," adding, "Whether the rate will be frozen will be decided depending on the results of the August Consumer Price Index (CPI) announcement."

International Oil Prices Soar Amid Middle East Risk

As military tensions in the Middle East escalated again, international oil prices showed a sharp rise. Both Brent crude and West Texas Intermediate (WTI) continued their upward trend, surpassing $90 per barrel.

Editorial writer Park Jin-ho noted, "In addition to the conflict between the US and Iran, clashes in the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait have significantly increased crude oil transportation costs," adding, "If oil price instability persists, it will inevitably have a negative impact on US consumer price indicators."

US Pressure Over 'Semiconductor Tariffs' and Troop Deployment

Analyses were also presented that the US government is raising the level of pressure on South Korea, demanding troop deployment to the Strait of Hormuz and the construction of new semiconductor plants in the US.

Commentator Park Yeon-mi emphasized, "Officials including the US Commerce Secretary are arguing that the proportion of semiconductor production used in the US must be raised, bringing up targeted tariffs," adding, "It is time to prepare to protect the domestic semiconductor industry and manage trade risks."
