SEC Approves 3x Leveraged Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs Amid Mass Liquidations in Volatile Market

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 닥터비트맨 · 비트코인 롱·숏이 모두 청산된 이유...?. Summarised from that video.

The US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has approved rule changes for trading 3x leveraged exchange-traded funds (ETFs) on major assets including Bitcoin and Ethereum. Amid expectations for the launch of new leveraged products, the cryptocurrency market experienced a sharp price surge followed by a pullback, recording massive forced liquidations.

3x Leveraged ETF Approval and Sharp Market Volatility

The SEC approved Cboe BZX Exchange's rule change application, allowing the listing and trading of 3x leveraged ETFs based on Bitcoin and Ethereum, as well as gold, silver, crude oil, and natural gas. As a result, options for high-leverage investment products related to cryptocurrencies have expanded further in the institutional market.

However, Bitcoin prices, which surged strongly during US market opening hours immediately following the approval news, quickly gave back their gains over the weekend morning. During the rally, short positions betting on a decline were liquidated, fueling the price surge, while long positions entered in anticipation of further gains were sequentially liquidated during the subsequent pullback.

$430 Million Liquidated in 24 Hours

According to data compiled by derivatives data analytics platform CoinGlass, approximately $430 million (around 600 billion won) worth of positions were forcibly liquidated across the overall cryptocurrency market over approximately 24 hours. Long liquidations accounted for the majority at approximately $320 million, while short liquidations stood at around $110 million.

Experts urged caution, stating that higher-leverage products carry greater risks of loss due to daily compounding effects and volatility. Unlike futures contracts, leveraged ETFs have their daily yield baselines reset every day, giving them a structural characteristic where losses from compounding effects can magnify when held long-term.

Trump Team Token Transfers to Exchanges and the Need for Market Buying Pressure

Meanwhile, on-chain data analysis of the Official Trump (TRUMP) token also attracted market attention. According to analysis by CoinNess and EmberCN, the Trump team moved approximately 81.87 million tokens (worth about $249 million) to centralized exchanges such as Binance and OKX over the past eight months.

The Trump team's initial allocation was around 800 million tokens, representing 80% of the total supply, and their remaining holding ratio is currently estimated at around 71.8%. Although it has not been confirmed whether the transfers to exchanges directly led to sales, the market is paying attention to the movement of large volumes itself as a potential supply pressure factor.

The Importance of Spot Buying Demand Amid Expanding Leverage

The diversification of derivatives and leveraged products can increase market trading volume and magnify short-term volatility. However, analysts point out that for long-term price appreciation and stability in the market, substantial spot buying demand and liquidity inflows to support derivatives demand must come first.
