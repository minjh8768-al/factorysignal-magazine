Ahjungdang CEO Kim Min-ki, Chosen by Won Bin: 'Secret to 600 Employees and 2x Annual Growth Lies in CS and Direct Management'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 딜스커버리 Dealscovery · 처음에는 사업할 생각 없었는데… 37세에 매출 2000억 만든 대표 | 아정당 김민기 대표 인터뷰. Summarised from that video.

Kim Min-ki, CEO of Ahjungdang, an internet and home appliance rental comparison platform, revealed the company's growth process and expansion strategy for new businesses. Ahjungdang has grown by roughly double each year, reaching a scale of around 600 employees.

Startup Originating from Helping His Father's Trucking Business… "Zero Revenue for 14 Months"

CEO Kim Min-ki began searching for a solution after witnessing his father face heavy brokerage fee burdens while working in the light cargo transportation business. Initially, he operated a fee-free transport matching Naver Cafe as a side business, gathering 43,000 members in 14 months.

In the early community stage, no revenue was generated as no brokerage fees were charged. However, as user reviews and traffic accumulated, inquiries for various advertisements such as internet services, water purifiers, and cleaning services followed.

돈을 안 내고 서로 연결되니까 대박 난 것까지는 맞는데 제가 돈을 번 거는 한 푼도 못 벌었어요. 중간에 수수료를 안 받기 때문에 너희들끼리 알아서 견적 내라는 식이었죠.

"It was true that connecting people without charging money made it a huge hit, but I didn't earn a single penny. Since we didn't take a middleman fee, it was a setup where users were left to figure out quotes among themselves."

Overcoming the Limits of the Brokerage Model… 70% of 600 Employees in CS

Judging that it was difficult to control customer service quality through third-party brokerage methods, Ahjungdang switched to a direct management system to provide services directly. Currently, about 70% of its roughly 600 employees registered for the four major public insurances are in CS and customer response roles.

To resolve internal communication issues arising from rapid organizational expansion, CEO Kim personally visits 8 to 9 centers nationwide every three months. Through company dinners and interviews, he collects 100 to 150 suggestions per visit and promptly reflects them in operational improvements.

내가 소통을 안 해서 그렇구나라는 것을 깨달은 때부터 거의 센터마다 한 세 달에 한 번씩 가요. 회식을 하면 건의사항이 100개에서 150개 정도 나와요.

"Ever since I realized that it was because I wasn't communicating, I've been visiting almost every center once every three months. When we have dinners, around 100 to 150 suggestions come up."

Expanding into New Businesses: Budget Phones, Real Estate, and Interior Design

Leveraging the customer base and CS organization secured through its existing internet and rental business, Ahjungdang is expanding into categories such as budget mobile phones (MVNOs), real estate, moving services, interior design, insurance, and funeral services. For its budget phone business, following the LG Uplus network, it is adding KT network services.

In the real estate business, licensed realtors with 7 to 15 years of experience have been recruited as store managers to operate a structure combining direct management and brokerage. The plan is to create customer synergy by bundling related services that arise during moving, such as interior design, real estate, moving, and internet installation.

Selecting Won Bin as Advertising Model and Preparing for Global Expansion

Ahjungdang's commercial video featuring actor Won Bin as its advertising model became a hot topic, surpassing 16 million views. CEO Kim explained that the rise in brand awareness positively impacted offline store expansion and customer trust building.

With goals of entering global markets and launching a K-cosmetics brand, CEO Kim recently enrolled in the Molecular Genetics program at the Seoul National University Graduate School of Dentistry. The intent is to personally gain specialized knowledge in anti-aging and biology to secure fundamental competitiveness for the cosmetics business.
