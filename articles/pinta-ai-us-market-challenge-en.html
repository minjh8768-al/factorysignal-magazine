Pinta AI Challenges US Market with AI Security Solution: "Finding Growth Drivers in a Larger Market"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: EO Korea · $1=1500원 시대, 상방이 무한한 미국에 부딪히는 98년생 창업가ㅣ아메리칸드림2.0. Summarised from that video.

Pioneering Global Markets from a San Francisco Base

Pinta AI, a developer of enterprise AI agent security solutions, has set out to pioneer the global market using San Francisco in the United States as its base. CEO Kim Do-hyun and Co-founder Kang Jun-seok are focusing on securing clients locally by providing services that identify and proactively block security issues that arise when introducing AI agents.

The United States is a market where the demand for AI security is rapidly increasing, with far more financial institutions and corporations than South Korea. CEO Kim Do-hyun stated that he decided to enter the local market in order to establish the company as a main player in a larger market.

저희가 미국에서 창업하기로 결심한 이유는 저희 경쟁사의 고객이 누구인지 봤기 때문입니다. 파이낸셜 섹터나 은행, 증권사가 제일 많았는데 한국에 은행 20개 있을 때 미국엔 4만 개가 있습니다. 여기서 의미 있는 결과를 내면 세상 어느 지역에서든 메인 플레이어라는 인식은 줄 수 있을 것 같습니다.

"The reason we decided to start our business in the United States is that we looked at who our competitors' clients were. Most were in the financial sector, banks, or securities firms; while South Korea has 20 banks, the US has 40,000. If we achieve meaningful results here, I think we can give the impression of being a main player in any region of the world."

Overcoming High Living Costs and Lack of Local Track Record

The biggest burdens in the process of starting a business in the US are high housing costs and living expenses. Local rent in San Francisco reaches millions of won per month for a single room, presenting a significant financial burden for early-stage startups.

Additionally, directly meeting decision-makers at local companies while lacking a local network and track record is another challenge. In response, the Pinta AI team is actively securing customer contact points through methods such as participating in pitching events and sending cold emails.

Strengthening Local Marketing Under Asan Voyage Support

Pinta AI was selected for support under the Asan Nanum Foundation's 'Asan Voyage' program, receiving support for living expenses, round-trip airfare, and occupancy at Maru SF. Through this, the company alleviated the burden of stay costs and established a springboard to focus on local sales and commercialization activities.

They are pitching at major startup events, such as the Plug and Play Silicon Valley Summit, and refining their value proposition method suitable for the US market through expert workshops.

Aiming to Become a Main Player in the Global AI Security Market

Pinta AI's top priority during its local activity period is to secure meaningful paying customers. The company is solidifying its position in the market by actively continuing discussions on proof of concept (PoC) with large corporations and collaboration with global companies.

CEO Kim Do-hyun expressed his determination for success in the global market, stating that facing bolder opportunities in a large market to increase the possibilities of raising capital and securing talent will serve as the driving force for continuous growth.
