The Ventures CEO Kim Cheol-woo on Why Founder Personal Charm and Conviction Are Key to Investment Decisions

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 머니비스트 ㅣ Money Beast · 투자 검토 20분, 관상을 보다ㅣ초기투자 VC 더벤처스 김철우 대표 #스타트업인터뷰 #대표인터뷰. Summarised from that video.

Kim Cheol-woo, CEO of early-stage startup investment firm The Ventures, revealed the company's investment review process and screening philosophy. The Ventures is an investor that backed early-stage companies such as HeyDealer, JobPlanet, and Gbike (Gcoo), and is helping startups in about 12 countries, including South Korea, grow.

20-Minute Q&A Meeting Without Pitching and Fast Feedback

The Ventures accepts investment review applications exclusively through a unified online channel. Initial feedback is provided two days after receipt, and in-person meetings are conducted with companies that receive positive feedback. Unlike typical investment pitches, the firm adopts an approach of studying the target company in advance and preparing a question list to conduct a 20-minute Q&A session only.

YES든 NO든 빠르게 답을 줘야 창업자가 본업에 집중할 수 있으니까..

"Whether it's a YES or a NO, providing a quick answer allows founders to focus on their core business..."

CEO Kim Cheol-woo noted that the density of the Q&A is more important than the length of the meeting. The explanation is that making decisions quickly helps founders devote themselves to their main business without unnecessary waiting time.

'Strong YES' Decision-Making, Not Unanimity

Another feature is that the firm does not adhere to majority rule or unanimous agreement when making investment decisions. This is based on the judgment that an idea agreed upon by all members is more likely to be ordinary. Even if four out of five judges oppose, if one person has strong conviction and advocates for the investment, the investment moves forward.

Introduction of AI Managers and Follow-up Investment Platform

Reviewing 2,000 to 3,000 deals annually, The Ventures introduced AI investment managers to increase efficiency in research and data collection tasks. By having AI assist in market research and extracting question lists, review speeds were reduced significantly.

Additionally, the firm built a platform system to avoid relying on individual managers' networks or personal condition when supporting follow-on investment attraction. Matching was automated so that follow-on VCs in the network can check portfolio company information and request meetings through the platform.

Founder's Personal Charm and 'Honorable Failure'

As key factors in investment decisions, CEO Kim cited a founder's grounded confidence and personal charm. He explained that he prefers founders who show conviction based on industry knowledge without crossing into arrogance, as well as talent with the charm to drive recruiting and sales.

Attitude Toward Failure

He demonstrated a positive attitude toward "honorable failure"—failing despite pouring in all one's efforts while acknowledging the high probability of startup failure.

성공하는 게 오히려 되게 특이한 일인 거죠. 그런데 실패했을 때 어떻게 대응하는지가 진짜 중요한 건데..

"Succeeding is actually the unusual thing. But how you respond when you fail is what really matters..."

CEO Kim stated that it is The Ventures' philosophy not to blame founders for failures where they did their best, but rather to support their next challenge.
