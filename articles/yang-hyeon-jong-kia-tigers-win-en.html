Yang Hyeon-jong Claims 8th Win with 6 Innings, 1 Run: "I Will Carry Responsibility Until the Day I Retire"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS Sports · "양현종 이름값은 유니폼 벗는 날까지 짊어져야 할 책임감" 대투수 광복절 쾌투 후 남긴 말 | 인터뷰 | 2026 KBO리그 (26.08.15). Summarised from that video.

KIA Tigers pitcher Yang Hyeon-jong delivered a strong performance with six innings allowing one run in a game against the Doosan Bears, leading his team to a 6-1 victory and recording his eighth win of the season.

Demonstrating Focus Amid Fierce Standings Battle

Yang Hyeon-jong emphasized focus on winning, stating that every game is important in the standings battle. He pitched aggressively to match the opposing batters' aggressive hitting, and shared that thanks to timely run support from the batters, he was able to pitch through the sixth inning without pressure.

Crisis Management and Breaking Ball Usage

Regarding the crisis situation in the third inning, he explained that although he faced a difficult game due to somewhat shaky command, he was able to overcome the crisis through reliable defense from the fielders and breaking balls thrown at key moments.

Behind the Scenes of Encouraging Kim Do-yeong

The content of a conversation with Kim Do-yeong, who hit a home run after a defensive error during the game, was also revealed. Yang Hyeon-jong stated that he offered warm words of encouragement to ease the mind of his junior, Kim Do-yeong, who expressed apology.

퉁치자고 이야기했습니다. 수비에도 집중을 많이 하는 친구인데 그게 타격까지 안 이어갔으면 하는 생각이 들었습니다.

"I told him let's call it even. He is a player who focuses a lot on defense, so I hoped it wouldn't carry over to his batting."

A Veteran's Sense of Responsibility and Promise to Fans

Regarding the pressure and responsibility he feels when standing on the mound, Yang Hyeon-jong mentioned that it is a fate he must carry until the day he finishes his playing career. He emphasized that he is always preparing to be a player who helps the team. He went on to ask fans cheering in the hot weather to take care of their health, and pledged to do his best so the team can finish the season in a higher position.
