US SEC Pushes to Allow Institutional Crypto Self-Custody as Citi Sets Bitcoin Price Target at $113,000

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · SEC가 코인 판을 뒤집었다…기관 ‘셀프커스터디’ 길 열리나?. Summarised from that video.

Along with regulatory reform movements to bring the virtual asset market into the institutional fold, various market issues were highlighted, including capital inflows, security risks, and the expansion of real-world payment infrastructure.

SEC Pushes to Allow Institutional Crypto Self-Custody as Hacking Damages Persist

The US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is pushing to revise cryptocurrency custody rules for institutions. The core of the plan is to allow investment advisers and funds to directly hold cryptocurrency through self-custody if they meet certain requirements. Regulations regarding custody entities have previously acted as a major obstacle when institutions hold cryptocurrency, but this rule revision is expected to ignite full-scale competition among asset managers, banks, and specialized custodians.

On the other hand, as the virtual asset market expands, the scale of hacking damage is also growing. According to data from security firm CertiK, 658 cryptocurrency security incidents occurred through September this year, with damages reaching approximately $2.68 billion. Among these, funds stolen by North Korea-linked hacking groups alone were estimated at over $1 billion. Yoon Kyung-chul (윤경철), head of Seoul Economic TV, evaluated the changing market environment as follows:

코인 시장의 다음 성장 단계에서는 수익률보다 자산을 얼마나 안전하게 지킬 수 있느냐가 핵심 경쟁력이 될 가능성이 큽니다.

"In the next growth stage of the crypto market, how safely assets can be protected is likely to become a core competitive edge rather than yields."

Continued Inflows into Bitcoin ETFs and Network Security Issues

Despite Bitcoin price volatility, institutional capital inflows into spot ETFs continue steadily. According to data from The Block, US Bitcoin spot ETFs recorded net inflows of approximately $2.65 billion during the month of September alone. This is interpreted as an indicator that buying pressure from institutions such as pension funds and asset managers remains ongoing.

Meanwhile, an incident occurred at NEAR Intents, a service related to NEAR Protocol, where approximately $3.8 million in funds was stolen due to a smart contract bug. NEAR stated that it had identified the hacker's identity through exchange tracking, demanded the return of the funds within 48 hours, and announced a policy to fully compensate the damaged amount. Additionally, in the aftermath of MetaMask's staking infrastructure issues, more than 523,000 ETH exit requests surged on the Ethereum network, causing a sharp increase in the queue.

Expansion of Blockchain Payment Networks and Citi's Price Forecast

The integration of virtual assets with real-world payment networks is also accelerating. The Ethereum Foundation and OpenAnonymous unveiled "zkAPI," which allows payments without exposing identity by utilizing zero-knowledge (ZK) proof technology. Payment company Stripe expanded support for the stablecoin OUSD across its payment infrastructure, while in Japan, Binance Pay integrated with local payment service PayPay, creating an environment where users can pay with cryptocurrency balances at convenience stores and other locations.

Global investment bank Citi presented a 12-month target price of $113,000 for Bitcoin and $3,000 for Ethereum. Citi analyzed that continuous capital inflows through spot ETFs are creating structural new demand in the virtual asset market.
