U.S. Rate Hikes and Rising Commodities Put Short-Term Pressure on Risk Assets Like Bitcoin

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 비트코인 전망 “단기적으로는 부담이다”…미국 금리 인상·국채 5%가 시장에 던진 경고. Summarised from that video.

As U.S. Treasury yields rise and the trend of benchmark interest rate hikes continues, an analysis has emerged on the impact of rate hikes on stock and crypto asset markets, including Bitcoin.

Correlation Between U.S. Rate Hikes and the Bitcoin Market

Brian Kim (브라이언 김), representative, explained that comparing past interest rate fluctuation cycles with Bitcoin price trends shows that the initial stage of rate hikes has acted as a burden on risk asset markets.

금리가 올라가는 초점에서는 아주 비트코인의 가격 흐름이 지금까지는 별로 안 좋았다. 금리가 내릴 때는 비트코인이 잘 간다.

"When the focus was on interest rates rising, Bitcoin's price performance so far was not very good. When interest rates fall, Bitcoin performs well."

He noted that while the Federal Reserve continues to raise benchmark interest rates, market trends may remain sluggish in the short term, but the market tended to rebound when the rate hike stance ended or shifted to rate cuts.

Rising Commodity Prices and Inflationary Pressure

Surging commodity and energy prices stemming from geopolitical tensions were also presented as a major factor stimulating inflation concerns. Kim pointed out that prices of commodities such as agricultural products and petroleum products have risen significantly following the Iran war.

He explained that rapid short-term increases in the prices of key commodities such as grains, sugar, gasoline, diesel, and natural gas created an environment where prices cannot easily stabilize. Accordingly, he assessed that the possibility remains open for the Fed to maintain high interest rates for the time being or implement additional hikes.

Risk Asset Correction and Growing Appeal of Treasury Bonds

The outlook continued that in a rising interest rate phase, higher yields on safe-haven U.S. Treasuries could trigger capital outflows from risk assets such as stocks and Bitcoin.

엘리엇 파동에서는 장기 차트를 아주 잘 잡아내야 된다. 장기 차트를 못 잡고 단기만 계속 연구해서 분석하면 아무 소용이 없다.

"In Elliott Wave analysis, you must capture the long-term chart very well. If you fail to capture the long-term chart and continuously study and analyze only the short term, it is of no use."

Kim analyzed that if the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield reaches the 5% level, a rotation phenomenon could occur where funds shift to Treasuries rather than taking on risks to invest in the stock market. Consequently, he viewed that risk assets overall, including stocks and Bitcoin, will face short-term downward pressure before undergoing market resetting and seeking direction once again.
