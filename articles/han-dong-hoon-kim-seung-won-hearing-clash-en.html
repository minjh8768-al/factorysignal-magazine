Han Dong-hoon Threatens Street Protests If Kim Seung-won Is Appointed, Clashing Head-On with Kim Min-seok

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 시사교양 라디오 - 시교라 · [뉴스직격] 한동훈, 단짠 아닌 짠짠전략? "과해" 국힘 지지율 상승은, 이 사람 덕분? /서용주 송영훈 배종찬. Summarised from that video.

Following the confirmation hearing for Minister of Justice nominee Kim Seung-won, political debate over his qualifications and explanations regarding allegations surrounding him is intensifying. Panelists offered various interpretations of the allegations raised during the hearing concerning the operation of a cooperative for people with disabilities, the nominee's tearful explanation, and independent Assemblyman Han Dong-hoon's strong backlash.

Controversy and Explanations at Nominee Kim Seung-won's Hearing

At the hearing, nominee Kim Seung-won broke into tears while addressing allegations regarding a developmental disability cooperative operated by his spouse, explaining that it was neither a family business nor run for profit.

저희 아내가 그렇게 아이들을 사랑으로 돌봤는데 이득을 위해서 돈벌이로 한 것은 절대 아니라는 점을 말씀드리고 싶습니다.

"I want to emphasize that my wife cared for those children with love, and it was absolutely not done for profit or to make money."

In response, debate panelists were sharply divided, with some arguing that the poor reality of care facilities for people with developmental disabilities and the background of parents establishing cooperatives must be taken into account, while others pointed out that scrutiny regarding procedural flaws or the appropriateness of family involvement cannot be avoided for a candidate for public office.

Han Dong-hoon Warns "To the Streets If Appointed Forced Through"... Warns of Unfinished Term

Independent Assemblyman Han Dong-hoon strongly criticized the possibility of forcing through Kim's appointment, hinting at potential street protests.

이렇게 오빠, 독약, 로비스트를 정의부의 수장으로 뻔뻔하게 임명하는 정권이 3년 더 갈 수 있겠습니까? 임기 못 마친다고 저는 단언합니다.

"Can an administration that brazenly appoints an older brother, poison, and a lobbyist as the head of the Ministry of Justice last another three years? I state with certainty that it will not finish its term."

Assemblyman Han warned that if the administration forces through the appointment while ignoring public opinion, its momentum for state affairs will rapidly deteriorate. Political analysts assessed that Han is deploying a strategy to strengthen his political standing and rally his supporters through hardline messaging.

Harsh War of Words with Kim Min-seok... Head-On Clash Between Ruling and Opposition Camps

Meanwhile, the debate with Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok grew even harsher. When Representative Kim attacked Assemblyman Han by citing similarities to former President Yoon Suk Yeol, Han countered head-on by bringing up past incidents.

누가 김민석 후배한테 칼 들고 협박했어요? 5·18 전야제에 새천년 NHK 룸살롱 가서 술 먹으라고 협박했습니까?

"Did anyone threaten junior colleague Kim Min-seok with a knife? Did anyone threaten him to go drink at the Millennium NHK room salon on the eve of the May 18 Memorial Service?"

As harsh exchanges between the ruling and opposition camps continue, the confrontation across the political sphere following nominee Kim's hearing is expected to persist for the time being.
