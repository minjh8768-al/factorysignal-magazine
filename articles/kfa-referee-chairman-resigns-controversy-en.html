Referees Committee Chairman Moon Jin-hee Resigns Following Refereeing Controversy and Hearing Clash

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: KBS News · [이슈] '극장골' 취소에 수원 팬 분노 폭발/"지면 심판 욕하니까" 남 탓하던 문진희 위원장은 사임/2026년 8월 3일(월) /KBS. Summarised from that video.

Resignation Submitted Following K League 2 Refereeing Controversy

Moon Jin-hee, chairman of the Korea Football Association's Referees Committee, has submitted his resignation. This comes as refereeing controversies in professional football's K League 2 collided with criticism over his behavior during a recent National Assembly hearing.

Controversy Over Misjudgments in Suwon–Chungbuk Cheongju Match

A series of questionable calls occurred during the K League 2 match between Suwon and Chungbuk Cheongju. Suwon's Reis collapsed after being struck in the face by an opponent's elbow, but play continued without a Video Assistant Referee (VAR) review, and no foul was called when Bruno Silva's shot hit an opposing defender's hand.

The biggest controversy arose during second-half stoppage time. Dubickas's goal was disallowed for allegedly pushing an opposing defender, but the match officials ended the game without checking VAR. A club official who protested the decision was also issued a red card. The Suwon club sent an official inquiry to the Korea Football Association.

National Assembly Hearing Conduct Controversy and Argument

Chairman Moon stated that he submitted his resignation for personal reasons after completing a Referees Evaluation Committee meeting. However, observers note that the controversy over his conduct at the National Assembly hearing served as the decisive catalyst.

During the hearing, Chairman Moon raised his voice and engaged in a heated argument during questioning by Representative Kim Jae-won.

거기는 끼지 마시고 좀... 의원님은 말씀하고 저는 말하지 말아야 합니까?

"Don't butt in there... Are you allowed to speak while I am not supposed to speak?"

In response, Lee Jae-jung, chairperson of the National Assembly's Culture, Sports and Tourism Committee, criticized the witness's attitude and issued a warning.

여기는 증인이 하고 싶은 이야기 하러 오는 곳이 아닙니다. 신문의 취지와 목적에 따른 질의 방식은 위원님들이 결정합니다.

"This is not a place for witnesses to come and say whatever they want. The method of questioning based on the intent and purpose of the examination is decided by the committee members."

KFA Begins Work on Referee Reform Plan

As distrust in referee decisions grew, the Korea Football Association held a public hearing on referee development and set out to devise improvement measures. The association stated that it is considering a random referee assignment system and establishing a communication framework to immediately explain controversial calls to the media and fans.
