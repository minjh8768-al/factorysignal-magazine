Current Account Surplus Hits Record $49.73 Billion in June; Interest Rates Cited as Key H2 Risk

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: YTN · [스타트 경제] 반도체 초호황에 올해 경상흑자 271조 원...작년 상반기 4배 / YTN. Summarised from that video.

The current account surplus reached $49.73 billion in June, breaking the all-time record. Strong exports in non-IT items such as automobiles and shipbuilding, alongside an explosive increase in semiconductor exports, drove the performance.

Semiconductor Export Boom and Record Current Account Surplus

Ju Won (주원), head of research at the Hyundai Research Institute, appeared on a broadcast and stated that the cumulative current account surplus for the first half of this year approached $200 billion. South Korea's ratio of current account surplus to GDP ranks second among OECD countries, trailing only Norway. Notably, the share of semiconductors in total exports has surged from around 20% in the past to more than 40%.

사상 최대 규모의 경상수지 흑자다. 반도체 호황이 이번 경상수지 흑자를 이끌었다.

"It is the largest current account surplus in history. The semiconductor boom led this current account surplus."

Upward Economic Growth Outlook and H2 Risks

Director Ju reported that the average economic growth forecast for South Korea this year presented by eight major investment banks (IBs) stood at 3.2%. He expected this year's annual growth rate to be in the low-to-mid 3% range. However, he pointed to interest rates as a key variable in the second half of the year, expressing concern that rising market interest rates could exacerbate weak domestic demand, including household consumption and construction investment.

Industry Minister's Remarks on the 52-Hour Workweek and Performance Bonuses

The Minister of Trade, Industry and Energy's recent remarks at the Kwanhun Club debate regarding the need for exceptions to the 52-hour workweek system in high-tech industries were also covered as a major issue. The Industry Minister argued that the will to work should not be forcibly obstructed, though a difference in perspective exists with the Minister of Employment and Labor. Additionally, he expressed opposition to performance bonuses set at a fixed percentage of operating profit, stating that shareholder and investor interests should take priority.

Real Estate Market and Concentration in Bogeumjari Loans

In the real estate market, Bogeumjari Loan supply reached 13.5 trillion won in the first half of the year, consuming 67.5% of the annual target. This resulted from actual end-users flocking to government-backed financial products with fixed interest rates as commercial bank lending regulations were tightened. Conversely, the number of housing guarantees dropped to its lowest level in seven years, and concerns were raised over a potential decrease in Jeonse listings in Seoul and a worsening lease shortage as non-resident single-home owners convert to owner-occupiers following tax code revisions.
