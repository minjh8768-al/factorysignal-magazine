National Assembly Passes Suite of Bills Supporting SMEs and Micro-Enterprises

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: JTV뉴스 · 국힘도 찬성, 이진숙 단독 반대......무슨 법안이길래?. Summarised from that video.

The National Assembly held a plenary session and passed legislative amendments to support small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and micro-enterprises, including the Act on the Promotion of Mutually Beneficial Cooperation Between Large Enterprises and Small and Medium Enterprises and the Act on Fostering One-Person Creative Enterprises.

Explanation of 13 Bills Under the Trade, Industry, Energy, Small and Medium Enterprises and Startups Committee

Ahead of the plenary vote, Representative Song Jae-bong (송재봉) of the Democratic Party of Korea, a member of the Trade, Industry, Energy, Small and Medium Enterprises and Startups Committee, presented explanations and review reports on the 13 bills on the agenda.

Rep. Song explained the purpose of the major bills on the agenda and the details of the revised drafts, asking the assembly to pass them as reviewed by the committee.

대·중소기업 상생협력 촉진에 관한 법률 일부개정법률안은 중소벤처기업부 장관의 시정권고 또는 시정명령의 구체적 내용을 명확히 규정하는 내용으로 수정의결하였습니다.

"The partial amendment bill to the Act on the Promotion of Mutually Beneficial Cooperation Between Large Enterprises and Small and Medium Enterprises was passed in a modified form to clearly define the specific details of corrective recommendations or orders issued by the Minister of SMEs and Startups."

Mutual Cooperation Act and One-Person Creative Enterprise Act Pass Plenary Session

In the subsequent voting, the partial amendment to the Act on the Promotion of Mutually Beneficial Cooperation Between Large Enterprises and Small and Medium Enterprises passed with 271 votes in favor out of 271 members present.

The partial amendment to the Act on Fostering One-Person Creative Enterprises, which laid the legal basis for preferential treatment of one-person creative enterprises in areas facing population decline, passed with 269 votes in favor and 1 abstention out of 270 members present.

Bills Including Certified Management Consultant Act and Kibo Act Passed

The partial amendment to the Act on Certified Management Consultants and Certified Technology Consultants, which clarified regulations regarding exemptions from the primary examination for consultant qualifications, passed the plenary session with 268 votes in favor out of 268 members present.

The partial amendment to the Korea Technology Finance Corporation Act, which focuses on adding virtual asset service providers to the list of entities subject to data requests by the Korea Technology Finance Corporation, was passed with 264 votes in favor and 1 abstention out of 265 members present.

Venture Investment Act and Micro-Enterprise Related Bills Passed

The partial amendment to the Act on the Promotion of Venture Investment, which strengthened regulations prohibiting unfair practices by major shareholders, passed with 268 votes in favor and 2 abstentions out of 270 members present.

The partial amendment to the Special Act on the Designation of Livelihood-type Business Types Suitable for Micro-enterprises passed with 248 votes in favor, 13 against, and 4 abstentions out of 265 members present.

In addition, other bills on the agenda, including amendments to the Act on Support for the Protection of Technology of Small and Medium Enterprises and the Act on Facilitating the Purchase of Small and Medium Enterprise-Manufactured Products and Support for Development of Their Markets, passed the plenary session with a majority in favor.
