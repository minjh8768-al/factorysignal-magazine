Rep. Lee Un-ju Says Real Estate Policy Should Focus on Rent Stability Over Taxes

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · 민주당 신임 지도부 출범, 최민희 직격은? / 이언주 의원 SBS 출연 / SBS 실시간 라이브. Summarised from that video.

Democratic Party Representative Lee Un-ju appeared in a broadcast interview to share her views on the recent Democratic Party national convention results, party-government relations, and pending real estate policy issues.

National Convention Results and Election of Party Leadership

Regarding the election of Kim Min-seok as the new party leader, Rep. Lee assessed that the overwhelming support from the Honam region best reflected the sentiment of the party members. Rep. Lee analyzed that party members' desire for stable state governance in the early stage of the Lee Jae-myung administration was reflected in these election results.

당은 이재명 대통령과 국민 주권 정부의 성공을 위해 뛸 것입니다. 그 성공이 국민의 성공이기 때문입니다.

"The party will work for the success of President Lee Jae-myung and the popular sovereignty government. That is because its success is the success of the people."

She then addressed the composition of the Supreme Council and internal party communication, emphasizing the importance of the leadership's role in maintaining smooth communication with the president and being able to offer direct advice.

Real Estate Policy and Emphasis on Rental Market Stabilization

In response to a question regarding the government's real estate policy, Rep. Lee expressed the view that taxes alone cannot stabilize the real estate market in principle. With housing shortages having accumulated over recent years, and given that visible results from housing supply take time, she argued that policy efforts should currently focus on resolving housing instability rather than entering controversies over tax reform.

너무 많이 건드려 벌집 만들어선 안 돼

"We must not touch it too much and turn it into a hornet's nest."

Pointing out that price instability in low- and mid-priced housing and rental market shortages felt mainly by working-class citizens are the most serious issues, Rep. Lee advised that focused measures should be established to stabilize the rental market for immediate housing stability.

Economic Security Roadmap and Legislative Tasks

Introducing her newly published book "The Free Order Is Over" (Gongjja Jilseoneun Kkeotnatda), Rep. Lee explained changes in the global economic security environment. Emphasizing that the era of free-riding free trade has passed and an era of survival ideology where technology and resources are weaponized has arrived, she urged that South Korea must expand its new economic territory based on talent and technology.

Furthermore, referring to her direct experience of harm regarding deepfake sexually explicit composite material, which has recently emerged as a social issue, she expressed her intention to actively take legislative measures and strengthen criminal penalties to eradicate digital sex crimes.
