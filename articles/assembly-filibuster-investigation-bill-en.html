Special Counsel Bill on Election Commission Passes as National Assembly Faces Filibuster Over Supplementary Investigation Power

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: MBN News · '선관위 특검법' 국회 통과 / 이진숙 배지달고 과방위 첫 출석 / 홍명보·정몽규·축구협회 난타 [프레스룸 뉴스라이브]. Summarised from that video.

As the special counsel bill targeting the Election Commission passed the National Assembly following an agreement between rival parties, opposition leadership held an outdoor rally to highlight their achievement, while strong standoffs continued in the plenary session and standing committees over bill processing and qualifications.

People Power Party Holds Outdoor Rally Following Passage of Election Commission Special Counsel Bill

Jang Dong-hyeok, leader of the People Power Party, took the microphone at an outdoor rally held at Olympic Park immediately after the passage of the special counsel bill targeting the Election Commission. Addressing the citizens in attendance, Representative Jang asserted that the passage of the special counsel bill was a victory for the people and appealed to them to continue gathering strength until the Liberation Day rally.

8월 15일 밤에는 다시 한번 이곳 올공에서 우리의 힘을 모아주십시오. 7월 17일 여러분께서 모아주신 함성이 특검법을 만들어냈듯이 8월 15일 올공의 함성이 특검 임명을 만들어낼 것입니다.

"On the night of August 15, please unite our strength here at Olympic Park once again. Just as the shouts you gathered on July 17 brought about the special counsel bill, the shouts at Olympic Park on August 15 will bring about the appointment of a special counsel."

Key party leadership and lawmakers attended the outdoor rally in large numbers on that day, adding weight to the opposition-led push for a special counsel.

Filibuster Standoff Over Criminal Procedure Act Amendment Abolishing 'Supplementary Investigation Power'

In the plenary session of the National Assembly, a filibuster led by the People Power Party took place over an amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act centered on abolishing prosecutors' direct investigation rights and supplementary investigation powers. After Democratic Party Representative Kim Seung-won completed the proposal explanation, People Power Party Representative Joo Ho-young took the floor as the first speaker to point out the injustice of the bill.

Prior to the plenary session, the People Power Party held a rally of condemnation and strongly criticized the ruling party's legislative push. Jeong Jeom-sik, floor leader of the People Power Party, voiced sharp criticism regarding the amendment.

보완수사권 폐지와 이재명 대통령 재판 취소를 맞거래한 추악한 이면계약은 반드시 이재명 정권의 몰락으로 돌아갈 것입니다.

"An ugly backroom deal trading the abolition of supplementary investigation rights for the cancellation of President Lee Jae-myung's trial will surely result in the downfall of the Lee Jae-myung administration."

Meanwhile, independent Representative Han Dong-hoon also requested to speak during the filibuster, but as his turn was pushed back and his speech inside the plenary chamber fell through, he met with reporters outside the chamber to state his position.

더불어민주당은 알량한 복수심을 만족시키기 위해서 이 보안수사를 받을 권리를 강탈했습니다. 이 악법으로서 범죄자가 이익을 보고 피해자가 피눈물을 흘리게 될 겁니다.

"The Democratic Party of Korea stole this right to receive supplementary investigation in order to satisfy a petty sense of revenge. Under this evil law, criminals will benefit and victims will weep tears of blood."

Representative Lee Jin-sook Attends First Science and Broadcasting Committee Session... Fierce Clash Between Parties

In the National Assembly's Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee, raised voices and heated arguments erupted between rival lawmakers as People Power Party Representative Lee Jin-sook attended for the first time as an active lawmaker. Ruling party lawmakers raised concerns about conflict of interest and her qualifications as a committee member, citing her past record of being reported to authorities while serving as chairperson of the Korea Communications Commission.

In response, Representative Lee Jin-sook and the People Power Party characterized the ruling party's assertions as collective bullying and countered by pointing out past criminal complaints and charges involving ruling party lawmakers. The meeting suffered disruptions from the start as emotional shouting matches were exchanged between the two sides.
