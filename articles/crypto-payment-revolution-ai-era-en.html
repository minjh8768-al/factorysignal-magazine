Next 5 Years Will Reshape Finance: Crypto and Real-Time Payment Revolution in the AI Era

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · "금융이 통째로 바뀝니다" AI 시대, 스테이블코인과 암호화폐가 필요한 진짜 이유 | 경읽남과 토론합시다 | 문창훈 작가 [3편]. Summarised from that video.

Outlooks suggest that alongside the spread of artificial intelligence (AI) technology, existing financial payment systems will transition into next-generation systems based on cryptocurrency and stablecoins. Author Moon Chang-hoon (문창훈) and Professor Kim Kwang-seok (김광석) analyzed that from the perspectives of technological change and cost efficiency, cryptocurrency is moving beyond being a speculative asset to establish itself as next-generation payment and settlement infrastructure.

Cost Efficiency and Means of Payment in the AI Agent Era

The discussion emphasized that just as unmanned kiosks were naturally adopted from the perspective of store owners to reduce labor and ancillary costs, payment methods will likewise be replaced by the most efficient options. Unlike disputes over payment processing fees between traditional credit card companies and payment gateways (PGs), blockchain and stablecoin networks offer significantly lower fees and real-time settlement capabilities.

In particular, an analysis was presented that in an era driven by AI agents, cryptocurrency networks—tamper-proof and highly secure through smart contracts—will inevitably be adopted as far more efficient payment and settlement methods than traditional currencies.

HTTP 402 and the X402 Standard: From the Movement of Information to Money

Author Moon Chang-hoon pointed out that the HTTP 402 code, which was left vacant as "Payment Required" when internet protocol standards were established in the 1990s, is recently becoming a reality through Coinbase's X402 standard and other developments. He explained that while the existing internet centered on the movement of information such as web pages and email, an era where money itself is settled and moved in real time is now opening.

The emergence of new stablecoins such as OpenUSD (OUSD), recently launched by a consortium of about 140 institutions including Wall Street, asset management firms, card systems, and major information technology (IT) companies, also supports this shift. Conservative traditional financial institutions directly stepping forward to build stablecoin ecosystems demonstrates that this trend has materialized.

앞으로 다가오는 향후 5년의 변화는 지금까지의 금융을 송두리째 바꿀 수 있는 새로운 금융 시스템, 차세대 실시간 결제 정산 시스템이 다가오고 있다.

"The changes coming in the next five years represent a new financial system and a next-generation real-time payment and settlement system that could completely alter finance as we know it."

The Second Monetary Revolution and the Four Major Crypto Sectors

Diagnosis followed that the changes occurring over the next five years will be far greater than the pace of change in the Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication (SWIFT) network, which began in 1973 and has been maintained for over 50 years. Following the first monetary revolution, when goldsmith receipts (goldsmith notes) evolved into paper money in the past, the current phase is evaluated as the unfolding of a second monetary revolution based on blockchain.

In his book "Token to the Moon," Moon presented four main sectors for understanding the market: smart contracts, backend finance (bridge currencies), interoperability and oracles, and the meme sector. Grasping the tokenomics and institutional integration status of representative coins in each sector was cited as key to portfolio design.

Market Outlook and Investor Stance

Regarding the future outlook for the cryptocurrency market, it was predicted that market capacity will expand with the passage of institutional legislation and the securing of regulatory clarity, such as the Clarity Act. Price movements are also expected to move past a bottom-building phase and gradually gain upward momentum.

Experts advised approaching cryptocurrency not merely as a short-term speculative market, but as an industrial sector and structural paradigm shift that combines with AI to reshape the landscape of finance.
