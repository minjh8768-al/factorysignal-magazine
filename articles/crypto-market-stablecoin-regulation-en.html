Analyst Kim Se-hee: Crypto Stagnation Driven by Shift to AI and Chips; Distribution Platforms Key for KRW Stablecoins

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 한국경제TV · '클래리티법' 통과는 시간문제...디지털자산 판이 바뀐다ㅣ김세희 유진투자증권 연구원. Summarised from that video.

As the virtual asset market continues to trade sideways around the $63,000 mark, shifts in global capital priorities have been identified as the main cause of the market slowdown. Eugene Investment & Securities analyst Kim Se-hee (김세희) analyzed the current state of the virtual asset market, key legislation, and strategies by company.

Institutional Capital Shifts to AI, Semiconductors, and Large IPOs

Analyst Kim Se-hee diagnosed the recent quiet trend in the virtual asset market, including Bitcoin, as largely influenced by the movement of global capital. She explained that as expanded AI investment became a given, institutional funds concentrated in semiconductor-related stocks and the large IPO market.

Kim emphasized the need for institutional capital inflows into the virtual asset market.

올해 여러 모멘텀이 소진되며 기관 자금이 AI, 반도체, 대형 IPO로 이동했다. 비트코인이 추세적으로 상향하기 위해서는 기관 자금 유입이 필요하다.

"As various momentum factors were exhausted this year, institutional funds moved to AI, semiconductors, and large IPOs. For Bitcoin to trend upward, inflows of institutional capital are necessary."

KRW Stablecoins and Discussions on the Basic Act on Digital Assets

With discussions on the Basic Act on Digital Assets resuming in the National Assembly, expectations for South Korean won-denominated stablecoins are also rising. However, Kim pointed out the need to separate and evaluate the Basic Act on Digital Assets and KRW stablecoin legislation.

She projected that it would be difficult to accelerate legislation in the short term, as consensus on KRW stablecoins has not been reached even within the ruling party. On the other hand, she noted that the government and the Bank of Korea are pushing for the commercialization of payment systems centered on CBDCs and deposit tokens.

Differences in Naver and Kakao's Virtual Asset Market Strategies

Kim assessed that a clear difference exists in the virtual asset market approach of major domestic IT platform companies Naver and Kakao. While Naver approaches the market from a financial platform expansion perspective through exchange linkages and other means, Kakao is focusing on building a distribution ecosystem utilizing global digital dollars, according to her analysis.

Kim diagnosed the decisive battleground for the KRW stablecoin market as follows:

원화 스테이블코인이 발행된다면 발행 주체는 은행 중심이 될 가능성이 높다. 따라서 실질적인 승부처는 유통을 담당하는 플랫폼이나 거래소가 될 것이다.

"If KRW stablecoins are issued, the issuers are highly likely to be bank-centric. Therefore, the actual battleground will be the platforms or exchanges responsible for distribution."

Likelihood of US Clarity Act Passing and Investment Strategy

Regarding the delayed processing of the US Clarity Act, she assessed that it is merely a short-term postponement stemming from political calculations between both parties, and that the likelihood of eventual passage remains high. She expressed expectation that if the bill passes, market uncertainty will be resolved, allowing institutional capital to flow back in.

From an investment strategy standpoint, she suggested that a mid-to-long-term perspective is needed as the attractiveness of the virtual asset market has declined in the short term. She added that an approach focused on major coins such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, or related stocks such as US-based Coinbase and Robinhood, remains valid.
