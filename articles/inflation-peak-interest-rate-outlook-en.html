Kim Young-ik: "BOK Rate Hike Cycle Finished at 3.0%... Rate Cut Possible in Second Half of Next Year"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 김영익의 경제스쿨 · 8월 물가 급등 .. 그러나 오래가지 않을 것 같습니다 | 김영익 교수. Summarised from that video.

Following the release of the August Consumer Price Index (CPI), an outlook was presented suggesting that inflation peaked in August and will gradually slow down, making a base rate cut possible in the second half of next year.

Causes of August Inflation and Base Effects

August consumer price inflation reached a high level, recording 3.1% year-on-year, while core inflation reached 3.4%. However, Professor Kim Young-ik (김영익) analyzed that this price increase was heavily influenced by base effects resulting from telecommunications fee cuts in August of last year.

He explained that the contribution of telecommunications fees to inflation reached 0.6 percentage points, meaning that excluding this factor, actual consumer price inflation was around 2.5%. Consequently, he expected inflation to gradually slow starting in September after peaking in August.

Economic Slowdown and Potential Rate Cut

Professor Kim pointed out that future economic growth could contract and the GDP gap could narrow or turn negative. He also forecasted that as import prices stabilize due to a falling exchange rate, inflation will approach the Bank of Korea's target of 2% in the second half of next year.

Accordingly, he stated that the Bank of Korea's base rate hike cycle appears to have ended at 3.0%, and that after maintaining a wait-and-see stance for the time being, the central bank could turn to rate cuts in the second half of next year.

한국은행 금리 인상 사이클은 3.0%에서 마무리됐다고 보고 있고요. 3.0%를 지켜보다가 내년 하반기에는 금리를 인하할 수 있다 이렇게 보고 있습니다.

"I believe the Bank of Korea's rate hike cycle has ended at 3.0%. My view is that after observing the 3.0% level, it could cut interest rates in the second half of next year."

Impact of Rising Oil Prices and U.S. Interest Rates

Regarding recent concerns over rising oil prices, he explained that while they could push up prices in the short term, with a time lag they will depress economic growth even further, ultimately acting as a factor that lowers interest rates. He also expected that the rise in U.S. Treasury yields would contract consumption and economic growth, leading to a future decline in U.S. interest rates.

Asset Allocation Strategy Amid Falling Leading Indicators

Professor Kim explained that during phases when the cyclical component of the leading economic index declines, the KOSPI and market interest rates tend to fall together. Therefore, he emphasized that risk management for equity assets is necessary at this time.

선행지수 순환변동치가 하락할 때는 코스피도 떨어지고 시장 금리도 하락하니까 주식 비중을 20% 혹은 그 이하로 유지하고 만기가 짧은 단기 채권을 보유하셨다가 주가가 충분히 조정을 받으면 그때 주식 비중을 더 늘리셔도 될 것 같습니다.

"When the cyclical component of the leading index falls, both the KOSPI and market rates drop, so maintaining your equity allocation at 20% or lower and holding short-term bonds with brief maturities, then increasing your stock allocation once equity prices have adjusted sufficiently, seems like a good approach."

In conclusion, during periods when both inflation rates and the leading economic index are falling, the advice is that a strategy of reducing equity holdings and responding through short-term bonds, followed by expanding equity holdings after stock prices have sufficiently adjusted, is effective.
