Moreno Appointed Caretaker Manager of National Team, Addresses 'AI Use' Controversy

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: JTBC News · "한국 괜찮겠어?" 모레노 선임에 스페인 '들썩'..소환된 과거 #뉴스다 / JTBC NEws. Summarised from that video.

The Korea Football Association has appointed Spanish manager Robert Moreno as the caretaker head coach of the national football team. Manager Moreno will lead six international matches scheduled through November, and depending on evaluation results, the possibility remains open for him to lead the team through the Asian Cup held next January.

Aiming for Data-Driven Tailored Tactics

The Korea Football Association cited his ability to utilize data and design tactics based on opponent analysis as the reasons for appointing Moreno. Moreno uses a 4-4-2 formation as a base, but is known to pursue flexible football that adapts to player composition and match situations.

In an interview, Moreno directly explained his preferred playing style and tactical direction.

Siempre intento estructurar las situaciones dependiendo de qué quiero hacer yo a la hora de jugar, que es tener el balón y llevarlo de manera controlada por el suelo con pases de 10 o 15 metros.

"I always try to structure situations depending on what I want to do when playing, which is to have the ball and move it in a controlled manner along the ground using 10 or 15-meter passes."

Strong Clarification Regarding Past 'AI Coaching' Controversy

Following the appointment announcement, controversy resurfaced over Moreno's past reliance on artificial intelligence (AI) during his time managing Russian club Sochi to determine training schedules and player signings. At the time, a former Sochi employee claimed that Moreno relied on instructions from ChatGPT to set irregular schedules or sign underperforming players.

In response, Moreno's side clarified that AI was used merely as an auxiliary tool for data analysis and language translation, and that all match and tactical decisions were made directly by the coaching staff.

Five-Member Coaching Staff Formed... Debut Against Ecuador

Moreno has finalized his five-member coaching staff and is embarking on reorganizing the national team in earnest. The South Korean national team is scheduled to play its first match under caretaker manager Moreno in a friendly against Ecuador on the 24th.
