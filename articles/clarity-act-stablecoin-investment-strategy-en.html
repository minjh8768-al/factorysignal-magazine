Kim Deok-ho Advises Taking Profits on Clarity Act Ahead of Sept. 15 Before Re-entering

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 스테이블코인의 실체, 미 단기채 담보화… ‘클래리티 법안’ 파장. Summarised from that video.

As expectations for the passage of the "Clarity Act"—which clarifies real-world asset collateral standards for stablecoins—are reflected in the U.S. market, analysis suggests that a differentiated investment strategy tailored to political variables and schedules is necessary.

Essence of the Clarity Act and the U.S. Treasury's Bond Strategy

Kim Deok-ho, CEO of Stock Insight, pointed out during a broadcast appearance that interpreting the Clarity Act merely as a crypto theme or short-term speculative play is a narrow perspective. He explained that the bill is an attempt to clearly define the value of cryptocurrencies, which lacked physical substance, by linking them to real-world assets such as U.S. short-term Treasury bills.

Furthermore, CEO Kim emphasized that the U.S. Department of the Treasury's measures to issue short-term bonds and buy back long-term Treasuries should not be confused with quantitative easing (QE) by the Federal Reserve (Fed). He analyzed that this is a Treasury-level fund management policy aimed at securing liquidity in financial markets and preventing long-term interest rates from rising by redeeming long-term bonds stuck in long-term holdings and increasing short-term bond issuance.

스테이블코인의 존재 가치를 명확히 하기 위해 미국 단기 국채를 담보물로 설정하는 것이 클래리티 법안의 핵심이다.

"Setting U.S. short-term Treasury bills as collateral to clarify the value of stablecoins' existence is the core of the Clarity Act."

Political Conflicts and Background of Delayed Bill Passage

Political conflict was cited as the reason why the Clarity Act has not easily passed despite long discussions. Pointed out was a complex interplay between conflict-of-interest concerns raised by former President Donald Trump directly operating crypto-related businesses, and a power struggle by Democrats unwilling to hand over a political achievement to the Republican Party and former President Trump.

CEO Kim also analyzed that former President Trump pressing Sept. 15 as the decision point for the bill, linked to the Republican political schedule, is a move aimed at political showmanship and rallying his support base.

민주당 입장에서는 통과를 안 시키자니 미국 정부 부채가 걱정되고, 통과시키자니 트럼프에게 공을 뺏길까 우려하는 파워 게임 양상이다.

"From the Democrats' perspective, it is a power game dynamic where they worry about U.S. government debt if it does not pass, but fear giving credit to Trump if it does pass."

Recommendation to Take Profits Before Sept. 15

CEO Kim Deok-ho advised investors trading stablecoins and related assets to take profits before Sept. 15. The view is that under unresolved political uncertainty, it is advantageous to first lock in profits to reduce volatility risk.

CEO Kim added that it is desirable to judge whether to re-enter based on market conditions after confirming whether the Clarity Act passes or is rejected around Sept. 15.
