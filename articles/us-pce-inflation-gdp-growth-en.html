US August PCE Inflation Eases Further as Q2 GDP Growth Is Revised Up to 2.2%

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · [속보] *PCE 물가지수 측정 방식 개편* '인플레 피크아웃' PCE 물가 예상 부합. 금리인하 시그널 될까? [즉시분석]. Summarised from that video.

US August personal consumption expenditures (PCE) price data and final second-quarter gross domestic product (GDP) figures have been released. While inflation showed signs of stabilizing by coming in below market expectations, GDP growth was revised upward, confirming the strong trend of the US economy.

August PCE Price Index Confirms Disinflationary Trend

The headline PCE price index rose 3.4% year-on-year, below the market forecast of 3.7%. The month-on-month increase also came in at 0.3%, lower than the expected 0.4%.

The core PCE price index, which excludes food and energy, also rose 3.0% year-on-year and 0.2% month-on-month, coming in lower than market forecasts of 3.4% and 0.3%, respectively. As all four major PCE indicators came in below market expectations, it proved that the disinflation trend, in which inflation is easing, is continuing.

8월 PCE 물가지수 지표는 네 가지 모든 관점에서 시장의 기대에 부합하거나 이를 밑도는 수치를 보여주었다.

"The August PCE price index indicators showed figures that met or fell below market expectations from all four perspectives."

Q2 GDP Final Figure Recorded at 2.2%

Released alongside the inflation indicators, the final US second-quarter GDP growth rate was recorded at 2.2%. This is a 0.7 percentage point upward revision from the previous preliminary figure of 1.5%.

As it was confirmed that the US economy is maintaining stronger growth than expected, expectations for an economic soft landing have heightened.

Interest Rate Policy Direction and Financial Market Reaction

With economic growth remaining solid and inflation stabilizing, market assessments suggest that the Federal Reserve's rationale for additional interest rate hikes has weakened. Following the release of the PCE data, financial markets are leaning toward predictions that rates will be frozen at the October Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting.
