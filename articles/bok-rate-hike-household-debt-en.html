Bank of Korea Raises Key Rate Back to 3% Range as Household Debt Surpasses 2,000 Trillion Won

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 연합뉴스TV · 한은 '백투백' 금리인상 강수, 게임은 이제 부터?…금통위원 6개월 금리전망 추가 인상 우세｜'빚투 이자 폭탄' 시작되나 [이슈ZIP] / 연합뉴스TV(YonhapnewsTV). Summarised from that video.

The Monetary Policy Board of the Bank of Korea raised the base rate by 0.25 percentage points to 3.00% per annum, marking its second consecutive monthly rate hike. The move aims to curb stronger-than-expected economic growth, persistent core inflation, and household debt that has ballooned to an all-time high.

Two Consecutive Rate Hikes... Annual Growth Forecast Raised to 3.3%

It is the first time in about four years that the Bank of Korea has implemented back-to-back rate hikes. It has also been about one year and six months since the base rate returned to the 3% range.

Behind the BOK's consecutive rate hikes is economic growth that exceeded market expectations. Driven by strong semiconductor exports and a surprise growth streak, including a 0.6% growth rate in the second quarter, the BOK sharply revised up its annual growth forecast for this year by 0.7 percentage points from 2.6% to 3.3%.

Meanwhile, inflation anxieties persist. Although consumer price growth fell to the 2% range last month, core inflation excluding food and energy remained high at 2.6%, providing justification for the rate hike.

Household Debt Surpasses 2,000 Trillion Won for First Time... Mortgage and Credit Loans Surge

South Korea's household debt surpassed 2,000 trillion won for the first time in history. According to the Bank of Korea, household credit balance at the end of the second quarter stood at 2,019.8 trillion won, up 25.9 trillion won from the previous quarter. This marks the largest increase in four years and nine months.

Among household loans, mortgage loans increased by 12.2 trillion won, while other loans, including credit loans, expanded by 12.8 trillion won. The Bank of Korea analyzed that this was the combined result of loan demand driven by increased real estate transactions and a stock market boom.

한국은행 관계자는 "물가 상승 압력과 가계부채 증가세를 감안할 때 기준금리 인상이 불가피했다"고 밝혔다.

"Considering inflationary pressures and the rising trend in household debt, a base rate hike was inevitable," a Bank of Korea official said.
