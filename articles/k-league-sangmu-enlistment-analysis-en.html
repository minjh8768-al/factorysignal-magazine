"Multi-Player Penalty?" Examining Gimcheon Sangmu's Selection System and Visiting Foreign Clubs

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 스포츠N플러스 · 월드컵 국가대표 이기혁 김천상무 탈락 이유?. Summarised from that video.

Achievements and Operational Hiccups of Overseas Club Visits and Jeju Friendly Match

Football officials shared various evaluations regarding recent friendly matches and events in Korea featuring prestigious overseas clubs. In the case of the event held in Seogwipo, Jeju, over 20,000 spectators gathered, proving the local passion for football, but it left much to be desired due to un-smooth operations in media coverage routes and player interview procedures.

In particular, a lack of communication among journalists on site, event organizers, and club officials was pointed out during the operation of the mixed zone and the adjustment of official press conference schedules. Regarding this, a reporter conveyed the atmosphere at the scene as follows:

해외 빅클럽의 엄격한 원칙과 기준에 비해 현장 안내나 소통이 원활하지 못해 취재진과 구단 간 시각 차이가 불거진 해프닝 성격이 강하다.

"Compared to the strict principles and standards of big overseas clubs, on-site guidance and communication were not smooth, strongly giving it the character of an incident where differences in perspective between the press and the club came to light."

Gimcheon Sangmu Enlistment Evaluation... Controversy Over Disadvantages for Multi-Position Players

Next, a discussion took place regarding institutional blind spots surrounding the player selection process for the Korea Armed Forces Athletic Corps (Gimcheon Sangmu) football team. Controversy arose as cases occurred where key players, who had displayed outstanding skills in the K League and were even members of the national team, failed the document and practical evaluations for Sangmu enlistment.

The calculation method for appearance record scores by position was cited as the cause of rejection. Critics pointed out a structural problem where players who played multiple positions—such as center-back, full-back, and midfielder—in line with recent football trends ended up receiving disadvantaged evaluations compared to full-time single-position players, as their appearance scores in specific positions were dispersed.

올해 센터백으로 주로 뛰었더라도 이전 시즌 여러 포지션을 오간 기록 때문에 포지션 기준점수에서 감점을 받는 등 멀티 플레이어가 불이익을 받는 제도의 한계가 드러났다.

"Even if a player mainly played as a center-back this year, because of records of moving between multiple positions in previous seasons, points were deducted from the position benchmark score, revealing limitations of a system where multi-position players face disadvantages."

Communication with Fans and Summary of K League Club Issues

The broadcast also continued with an analysis of the situations of K League clubs that experienced conflict with fans due to poor performance, as well as the scenes of face-to-face encounters with fans. Although rumors differing from the facts spread in the process of managers and players standing before fans after matches to explain and apologize for their performance, efforts to resolve misunderstandings through sincere dialogue between clubs and supporters were emphasized.

The panelists agreed that more refined rule revisions and systematic communication by the K League and related organizations are urgent for players' military service issues, club operations, and hosting major events to blend smoothly together.
