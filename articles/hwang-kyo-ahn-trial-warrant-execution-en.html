Special Counsel Details Supporter Resistance and Non-Compliance During Warrant Execution at Hwang Kyo-ahn's Home

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 안동MBC NEWS · “비켜주세요” 외쳤지만… 황교안 영장집행 당시 아수라장 된 현장, 법정서 영상 공개. Summarised from that video.

An investigator from the special counsel team appeared as a witness in a trial on charges including incitement of rebellion, testifying about the process and situation during the execution of warrants at the defendant's residence. Prosecution presented evidence, including on-site arrest videos and social media posts, confirming specific circumstances at the time of the warrant execution.

First and Second Search Warrant Execution Attempts and Failure

The special counsel investigation team attempted to enter the defendant's residence to execute a search warrant, but faced setbacks due to resistance from supporters. An investigator who was dispatched to the scene explained that clashes occurred with supporters occupying the stairs, forcing them to withdraw over concerns of safety accidents and casualties.

자신들에게 영장을 제시해야 한다는 이해할 수 없는 소리를 하면서 특검팀의 진로를 막았고 계단 상단 쪽에서 가로막혀 옴짝달싹 못 하던 상황이었습니다.

"They blocked the special counsel team's path while making incomprehensible demands that the warrant be presented to them, and we were trapped and unable to move at the top of the stairs."

The investigation team subsequently attempted a second search warrant execution, but the defendant did not open the door or respond. Supporters were waiting while occupying the apartment entrance and the front door, and it was confirmed that they broadcast the situation live via YouTube and other platforms.

Failure to Comply with Summonses and Incitement via Social Media

After the warrant execution failed, the investigation team sent multiple summonses to the defendant and notified him via text messages and registered mail. However, citing the need for preparation time such as completing attorney appointment procedures and filing requests for information disclosure of complaints and warrants, the defendant submitted written explanations for non-appearance and applications to postpone the interrogation dates.

The defendant also failed to appear on the subsequent second and third requested appearance dates and did not respond to contact from the investigation team. Meanwhile, through his social media accounts, the defendant continuously uploaded posts referring to the warrant execution as the "Namsan Struggle" and encouraging supporters to obstruct and block the execution.

Third Warrant Execution Attempt and Issuance of Arrest Warrant

The investigation team obtained a reissued arrest warrant and search warrant for the defendant, and attempted a third execution accompanied by a locksmith for forced entry. To prevent supporters from occupying the stairs in advance, they entered the apartment complex quickly in the early morning hours.

The investigator stated that although supporters spotted the investigation team and attempted to resist, countermeasures prepared in advance allowed them to enter the inside of the residence and proceed with warrant execution procedures.
