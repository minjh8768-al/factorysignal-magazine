US Treasury Buybacks and Won's Strength Are Short-Term, Says Advisor Shin Hwan-jong

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 한국경제TV · 美 바이백에도 금리 다시 뛴다?...1300원대 환율, 수급 개선 청신호 | #국채금리 #장기채 #베센트 #환율. Summarised from that video.

Hawkish Tone in Fed Minutes and Assessment of Treasury Yields

The release of the July Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) minutes confirmed a hawkish stance within the Federal Reserve, indicating that further rate hikes may be necessary if inflation is not stabilized. Amid continued vigilance against entrenched inflation, the U.S. Department of the Treasury announced plans to at least double the size of its buybacks to stabilize long-term Treasury yields.

In response, Shin Hwan-jong, advisor at Korea Investment & Securities, analyzed that buyback measures are unlikely to lead to a long-term decline in interest rates given the expanding U.S. fiscal deficit and ongoing inflationary pressures.

단기적으로 금리 하락 요인은 되겠지만, 구조적으로 계속 올라가고 있는 상황이기 때문에 정책 의지를 보여줘서 단기에는 내려가겠지만 다시 상승하지 않을까 생각하고 있습니다.

"While it could serve as a factor pushing yields down in the short term, rates are structurally continuing to rise. Showing policy determination may lower them short-term, but I expect they will rise again."

Causes Behind Exchange Rate Entering the 1,300-Won Range and Future Outlook

The dollar-won exchange rate entering the 1,300-won range recently, signaling a strengthening Korean won, was attributed to a weaker U.S. dollar, a stronger Japanese yen, and a sustained current account surplus. In addition, easing net foreign stock selling, dollar selling by exporters, and interim corporate tax prepayments were interpreted as combined contributing factors.

However, Mr. Shin projected that any further drop in the exchange rate would be limited, and pressures toward a weaker won could build again late this year or next year. He explained that an exchange rate between 1,350 won and 1,400 won is an attractive level to induce overseas investment by domestic retail investors in foreign stocks and domestic institutions such as the National Pension Service, meaning outflows of overseas portfolio capital could resume.

Market Strategy Amid Heightened Stock Market Volatility

Amid recent sharp volatility in the domestic stock market, Mr. Shin expected the range-bound market trend to continue for the time being, citing macroeconomic uncertainty and supply-demand imbalances.

당분간은 박스권을 예상하고 있습니다. 매크로 상황이 상당히 불확실하고, 수급이 여전히 기관이 무력한 상태에서 개인이 칠성 매물대에 깔려 있고 외국인에게 달려 있기 때문에 박스권 대응을 하는 것이 좋다고 생각합니다.

"For the time being, I expect a range-bound market. The macroeconomic environment is quite uncertain, and regarding supply and demand, local institutions remain helpless while retail investors are bogged down under heavy selling pressure, leaving everything dependent on foreign investors, so I think it is best to prepare for a range-bound market."

Mr. Shin noted that while corporate valuations themselves have not deteriorated, supply-demand dominance rests heavily with foreign investors, advising investors to avoid excessive optimism or pessimism and adopt a cautious strategy with the upper and lower bounds of the trading range in mind.
