"Targeting Areas Big Tech Can't Touch": Korean Founders in Their 20s Raise $1.3 Million in Silicon Valley

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: EO Korea · 30일만에 0원에서 18억, 언더독이 미국에서 이기는 방법ㅣ아메리칸드림2.0. Summarised from that video.

Sume Labs, a startup led by Korean founders in their 20s, is drawing the attention of local investors as it achieves results on the Silicon Valley stage in the United States. CEO Lim Do-hyun (임도현), a graduate of Korea University's Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, and CEO Heo Chae-won (허채원), who launched the business after taking a leave of absence from POSTECH, successfully secured pre-seed investment after being accepted into a local U.S. accelerator program.

Heading to the U.S. with Entire Savings... Accepted into Accelerator 'Founders, Inc.'

Co-founders Lim Do-hyun and Heo Chae-won had aimed to enter the U.S. market from the early stages of their venture. After hearing about the recruitment for the "Artifact" program run by Silicon Valley-based accelerator Founders, Inc., the two received acceptance notifications through documents and a demo video submitted right before the deadline.

Upon the sudden news of their acceptance, the two gathered their available funds and immediately boarded a flight to San Francisco. At the time, the remaining runway they could sustain with their business capital was only about 1.5 months.

저희 전 재산의 절반을 털고 무작정 샌프란시스코에 날아왔습니다.

"We spent half of all our savings and blindly flew to San Francisco."

AI Companion 'Clawra' Embodying 'Samantha from the Movie Her'

Sume Labs' flagship product is "Clawra," an AI companion service designed after Samantha, the AI character in the movie Her. Operating on Openclaw, the service was developed with the goal of integrating beyond simple conversation to include video calls, image sharing, and real-life assistance functions such as e-commerce ordering.

클라라는 오픈클로에서 작동하는 AI 여자친구 입니다. 각각의 채널에서 클라라와 채팅을 하거나 영상통화, 사진을 볼 수 있고 당신을 위해 무언가를 주문해줄 수 있습니다.

"Clawra is an AI girlfriend running on Openclaw. On each channel, you can chat with Clawra, make video calls, view photos, and she can even order something for you."

Reaching 1 Million Views on X (Twitter) and Securing $1.3 Million Investment

Immediately after its release, the Clawra service went viral on social media platform X (formerly Twitter), creating a major buzz. It recorded 1 million views on X in just one day and secured 1,000 GitHub stars and tens of thousands of users. Based on this traction, interest followed from prominent local podcasts and venture capital (VC) firms.

After successfully completing Founders, Inc.'s demo day, Sume Labs succeeded in raising $1.3 million (approximately 1.7 billion KRW) in pre-seed funding over the course of a month.

"Competing with Speed and Virality That Surpass Big Tech"

The Sume Labs team emphasizes that their strength lies in the agility unique to startups—trying and executing quickly even when competing with major technology companies. Their strategy is to expand market share through rapid execution, nimble pivoting, and a viral sense tailored to the B2C market.

빅테크들이 가진 자원을 버리고 건드리지 못하는 영역들부터 시작해서 시장 파이를 먹으려고 하는 거고, 두 번째로 빅테크는 일단 너무 느리기 때문에 저희가 제일 빨리 움직이면 이길 수 있지 않을까 하는 생각들이 듭니다.

"First, we are trying to capture a share of the market by starting from areas that Big Tech cannot touch because they have to abandon their existing resources. Second, Big Tech is just too slow, so we believe we can win if we move the fastest."

Having wrapped up demo day, the Sume Labs team has set up base in Redwood City, California, and is accelerating the development of subsequent versions and service enhancements.
