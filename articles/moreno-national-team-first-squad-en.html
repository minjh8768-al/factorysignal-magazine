Manager Moreno Set to Announce First Squad: 'Examining Both K League and European Players'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SPOMAN · 모레노 감독 첫 선택이 심상치 않다… K리그까지 뒤졌다. Summarised from that video.

Player Selection Gateways and Realistic Constraints

The national football team under manager Roberto Moreno is facing the announcement of its first player roster. The national team's first match will be played against Ecuador, and there is limited time remaining until the squad convenes. In the early stage of a managerial change, it is common to gradually add talent that fits the tactics while maintaining the existing core force rather than completely overhauling the squad.

While maintaining the backbone around already proven existing key assets in the national team such as Son Heung-min, Lee Kang-in, and Kim Min-jae, players who align with the manager's tactical preferences are expected to be newly listed.

Thorough Analysis of K League and Overseas Players

It is reported that manager Moreno and the coaching staff have been broadly analyzing the player composition and matches of each K League team even before their official inauguration. They are evaluated as using current performance, work rate, pressing ability, and tactical suitability as key criteria rather than name value or past careers.

In this process of analysis, players active in the K League and overseas are being mentioned as candidates for national team selection.

  • Lee Seung-woo: Possesses consistent scoring ability in the league, a unique game-breaking capability, and creative dribbling power
  • Jeong Seung-won: Recorded 8 goals and 5 assists in the league, outstanding work rate and tactical execution capability
  • Kim Dae-won: Ranked near the top in league assists, proving sharp attacking chance-making ability
  • Kim Min-soo: Belongs to Rangers in Scotland, understanding of Spanish football and passing link-up ability

First Squad to Be Revealed on the 14th... Will It Be a Tactical Signal Flare?

While waiting for administrative procedures such as visa issuance, manager Moreno is also pursuing a plan to directly check and observe the condition of Korean players playing locally in Europe. Although a complete change within a short period is not easy, as he has closely examined K League clubs overall including Gimcheon Sangmu, the possibility of surprising unexpected selections cannot be ruled out.

With attention from fans and the football community focused on the first squad list to be announced on the 14th, interest is centering on how boldly new faces will be selected while maintaining the existing core framework.
