Crypto Market Sentiment Recovers on Institutional Inflows and Expanding XRP Utility

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 비트코인, 판 뒤집을 ‘초대형 자금’ 들어왔다…“11만3000달러” 기습 전망 파장. Summarised from that video.

Amid interest rate and regulatory burdens, spot ETF investments and direct corporate purchases are increasing, showing a recovery in crypto market expectations. As capital inflows through spot ETFs and expanding corporate holdings occur simultaneously, interest in the fourth-quarter market is also rising.

Expanding Institutional Capital Inflows and Buying Pressure from Major Companies

In the Bitcoin spot ETF market, a net inflow of approximately $2.39 billion was recorded over a single week, marking a high weekly inflow rate for this year. Capital inflows were not limited to Bitcoin, extending to Ethereum spot ETFs and XRP-related products.

Along with expanding investment demand through equity markets, corporate asset expansion is also drawing attention. Major companies such as MicroStrategy and Bitmine are increasing their purchases of Bitcoin and Ethereum, accumulating crypto assets as key treasury assets. Citigroup projected an increase in institutional allocation and set a 12-month target price for Bitcoin at $113,000.

Ripple and Evernorth's Blueprints to Expand the XRP Ecosystem

Ripple unveiled a blueprint connecting payment and credit businesses around the XRP Ledger. The strategy aims to expand the real-world utility of XRP by linking lending functions so payment customers can temporarily raise needed funds, while broadening decentralized exchange functionality and tokenized asset utilization.

Evernorth, an XRP asset manager, also presented an asset management strategy ahead of its Nasdaq listing entry. It outlined a vision to generate revenue by utilizing held assets in blockchain financial services, reinvesting the proceeds into additional XRP purchases to increase holdings per share.

Approval of 3x Leveraged Products and Altcoin Trends

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approved rule changes for listing leveraged products that track 3x the daily returns of major assets, including Bitcoin and Ethereum. As the product structure involves daily rebalancing, investors should be mindful of potential volatility-driven losses during long-term holding.

Meanwhile, an analysis suggested that an increased proportion of short-term treasury bills in the U.S. Treasury issuance structure could serve as a positive variable for market liquidity. In the altcoin market, Quant (QNT) surged after being selected as a technology partner for a deposit token settlement network project, while Solana showed a breather as ETF inflow momentum slowed despite strong network activity.
