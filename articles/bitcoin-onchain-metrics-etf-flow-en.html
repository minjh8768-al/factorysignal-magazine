Crypto Market Eyes Turnaround Ahead of Bitcoin's $80K Threshold Amid On-Chain Indicators and Institutional Inflows

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 박작가의 크립토연구소 · 비트코인 강세장 신호 급등했다! 온체인 활성 가격 뚫으면 날아갈까? 이번 주 재료에 방향성 달렸다 이더리움 리플 솔라나 하이퍼리퀴드. Summarised from that video.

After showing a strong rally recently, Bitcoin prices have slowed down slightly over the weekend. Investor attention is focused on major macroeconomic events moving the market this week and changes in on-chain indicators.

Macroeconomic Events Overlap This Week… Focus on PCE Data and Jackson Hole Meeting

Multiple macroeconomic variables are scheduled for the crypto market this week. Notable events include the release of the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index closely watched by the U.S. Federal Reserve, Nvidia's earnings report, and the Jackson Hole meeting held over the weekend.

Oil price volatility resulting from economic sanctions between the U.S. and Iran is also cited as a market anxiety factor. The continuing rebound trend in U.S. Treasury yields is another factor limiting the market's upward momentum.

Institutional Inflows Continue… Spot ETF Net Inflows and Open Interest Decline

In terms of indicators, significant signs of improvement were captured. Last week, a total of $1.92 billion in net funds flowed into the Bitcoin spot ETF market. Analysts point out that inflows must continue to confirm a short-term trend reversal.

Changes in open interest in the futures market are also positive. While the price of Bitcoin rose by about 20%, open interest actually decreased by 12%. This shows that it was a healthy rise driven by position liquidation and spot demand rather than a speculative rally resulting from expanding leverage.

On-Chain Overheating Signals… Breaking the 80K Sell Wall Remains Key Variable

Caution over short-term overheating also exists. A sell signal occurred in the Stablecoin Supply Ratio (SSR) indicator. This means that stablecoin liquidity supply is failing to keep pace with the growth rate of Bitcoin's market capitalization.

According to technical analysis and Binance order book (CVD) data, a strong sell wall is formed near 80K. Whether prices break through the resistance in the 79.5K and 80K range and recover to the active realized price level of 83K is expected to be a major inflection point for further gains. Conversely, in the event of a correction, whether support holds at the 76K level is analyzed to serve as the benchmark for short-term response.
