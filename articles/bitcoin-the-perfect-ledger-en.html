Professor Oh Tae-min: Bitcoin is 'The Most Perfect Ledger,' Shares 2026 Crypto Outlook

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · min read

Source: 지식인사이드 · 지식인초대석 EP.90 (오태민 교수 1부). Summarised from that video.

Professor Oh Tae-min of Hanyang University's Bitcoin Monetary Philosophy department is considered a first-generation Bitcoin investor in Korea. In an interview on the 'Knowledge Inside Invitation' segment of the Knowledge Inside channel with host Han Seok-jun, he defined Bitcoin as "the most perfect ledger ever created by humanity."

The "Ledger" Perspective

Professor Oh's logic is as follows: whether it's an accounting ledger, a conscription warrant, or a court judgment – our society is run by countless "ledgers," and these ledgers have always been managed by someone (a central institution, an accountant, a server administrator). The problem is that these administrators can modify the ledgers at will, and various public official corruption cases reported in the news can ultimately be explained as "the abuse of power by someone in a position to alter the ledger." His main point is that Bitcoin is the first case to implement a ledger that cannot be arbitrarily altered without such a central administrator.

He explains that Bitcoin's roots trace back to the cypherpunk group, which sought to avoid state intervention. While the exact identity of its creator (Satoshi Nakamoto) remains unknown, he named Hal Finney, who passed away from ALS in 2014, as a strong candidate.

"우리가 잘 생각해 본 그 장부는 누가 관리하지라는 퀘스천을 할 수밖에 없는데요, 비트코인 이전에는 그거를 중앙 기관이 한 거죠."

"When we deeply consider the ledger, we inevitably ask who manages it. Before Bitcoin, central institutions did."

Why It Cannot Be Hacked

He explains that Bitcoin's security principle is that "over 20,000 servers (nodes) worldwide hold identical copies of the ledger." Even if one or two nodes are brought down, it does not affect the overall network. To manipulate the ledger, one would need to expend more computing energy than all 20,000 nodes combined to successfully execute a "51% attack," a cost he argues is practically insurmountable. To illustrate this, he presented an extreme hypothetical scenario: "The U.S. government shuts down all power plants worldwide and runs only its own nuclear power plants to manipulate the ledger," emphasizing that it would be realistically impossible without such a national total effort.

The Economics of Mining

He explains that mining is not about pickaxes, but "a competition for the right to operate a node and record new entries on the ledger." There are two types of rewards: newly issued Bitcoin and transaction fees. The total supply is fixed at 21 million (approximately 19.97 million currently mined, expected to exceed 20 million in March this year), with new issuance virtually ending around 2140. As new issuance decreases, the focus of miners' revenue will shift towards transaction fees. He presented a calculation: "If people are willing to pay about 100,000 KRW per transaction, the current level of mining power can be maintained."

His Price Outlook — Optimism and Caution Simultaneously

Professor Oh's long-term outlook is clearly optimistic. Based on "market capitalization comparison with gold," he believes Bitcoin could eventually reach the $1 million (approximately 1.5 billion KRW) level – arguing that the super-rich will choose Bitcoin as a "weightless, border-crossing asset" instead of physical gold.

However, he is cautious about 2026 itself. He pointed out that Bitcoin follows a 4-year cycle, and 2026 is "the worst year in the cycle." While mentioning the possibility of further decline to the $60,000-$70,000 range, he simultaneously emphasized that "the probability of Bitcoin eventually surpassing $1 million is higher than such a decline scenario." This is a passage where an optimistic long-term outlook and a pessimistic short-term cycle forecast coexist in one sentence.

Why He Recommends Including Ethereum

When asked what he would do if a novice investor put 10 million KRW into crypto, he suggested a 50/50 allocation: "5 million KRW for Ethereum, the rest for Bitcoin." His rationale is that Ethereum is engaged in an "asset tokenization platform war" with companies like Samsung Electronics, Google, and Meta, and a significant portion of dollar stablecoins are already issued on Ethereum. Conversely, he drew a clear line, stating that other coins besides Bitcoin (over 20,000 listed coins share about half of the total market capitalization) are "quite unstable."

Stablecoins: A Trigger Point

A particularly notable part of the interview was the discussion about stablecoins. He pointed out that dollar stablecoins could effectively render national central banks "closed for business" and threaten existing payment networks like commercial banks and Visa/Mastercard, a risk also warned by the IMF. An interesting point is his skeptical view on KRW stablecoins – he stated that "foreign workers would prefer to receive money earned in Korea in dollars, not KRW stablecoins," suggesting that attempts to promote KRW stablecoins based on monetary sovereignty arguments might not be highly effective.

2026 Checklist

He presented five key factors Bitcoin investors should watch this year.

  • U.S. midterm elections (correlation between Trump's approval ratings and Bitcoin price)
  • Interest rates and liquidity supply
  • Occurrence of major exchange hacking incidents
  • Buying and selling trends of "whales" (early large holders)
  • Progress of legislation for financial asset tokenization

What He Learned Personally

The most practical part was his personal investment experience. He stated that over the past 11 years, he experienced losing more than half of his assets almost instantly three or four times – two 80% drops and five 50% drops – and advised that constantly watching charts is detrimental to mental health. His advice is simple: use money you won't need for at least 3 years, don't buy all at once but purchase systematically over time, and hold for a minimum of 3 years after buying.

"제 얘기 오늘 듣고 비트코인 사시면 이제 행복 끝, 불행 시작이라고 말씀드리죠. 그런데 이런 영상을 보고도 안 사시면, 2014년에 제 강의를 들었던 한양대생들하고 똑같아집니다."

"If you buy Bitcoin after hearing me today, I'd say happiness ends and misfortune begins. But if you don't buy after watching a video like this, you'll end up like the Hanyang University students who listened to my lecture in 2014."
