Confirmation Report Hearing Disrupts Legislation and Judiciary Committee as Ruling and Opposition Parties Clash Over 'Surprise Motion' and 'Legal Deadline'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 채널A News · 법사위, 김승원 인사청문보고서 여당 주도 채택 [현장영상] / 채널A. Summarised from that video.

Sharp Clash Between Ruling and Opposition Parties Over Agenda Addition

A fierce debate broke out between the ruling and opposition parties in the National Assembly's Legislation and Judiciary Committee over adding an agenda item to adopt a confirmation hearing report for the Minister of Justice candidate. The People Power Party criticized the ruling party for unilaterally introducing the agenda without prior consultation, while the Democratic Party of Korea and the committee chair countered that it was a procedure to comply with the legal deadline specified in the Personnel Hearing Act.

People Power Party: "Unilateral Agenda Tabling Without Prior Consultation"

People Power Party Representative Yoon Sang-hyun expressed regret during a point of order, stating that the motion to adopt the confirmation hearing report was introduced unilaterally without consultation between the floor leaders of both parties. Representative Yoon pointed out that various allegations regarding the candidate were not resolved during the hearing process and that the candidate did not meet public expectations.

Representative Park Hyung-soo of the People Power Party, who spoke next, also pointed out that not a single witness or reference requested by the opposition was adopted, strongly protesting that adding the agenda item late the previous night was an action that reduced the hearing itself to a formality.

Democratic Party and Committee Chair: "Adoption Within 3 Days Under Personnel Hearing Act Is a Legal Obligation"

In response, Legislation and Judiciary Committee Chair Seo Young-kyo emphasized that under Article 9, Paragraph 2 of the Personnel Hearing Act, submitting a confirmation report to the Speaker within 3 days from the date of concluding the confirmation hearing is a legal obligation. Committee Chair Seo stated that it was a schedule for report drafting and submission procedures, refuting the opposition's claim of unilateral agenda processing.

인사청문회법 9조 2항에는 인사청문회가 있은 날로부터 3일 내에 청문 보고서를 채택해야 한다라고 법으로 되어 있습니다.

"Under Article 9, Paragraph 2 of the Personnel Hearing Act, the law states that a hearing report must be adopted within 3 days from the day the confirmation hearing took place."

Representative Kim Eui-kyeom of the Democratic Party of Korea also revealed that attempts at prior consultation between party floor leaders had occurred, explaining that it was simply a situation where agreement could not be reached due to large differences of opinion between the two parties, rather than tabling without consultation.

Bill Introduction Motion Approved Amid Disruption

Amid discord marked by shouting back and forth between lawmakers of the ruling and opposition parties, Chair Seo Young-kyo tabled the agenda item for bill examination and declared it passed. As the standoff between the ruling and opposition parties over the adoption of the confirmation report for the Minister of Justice candidate continues, friction in the National Assembly's Legislation and Judiciary Committee is expected to persist.
