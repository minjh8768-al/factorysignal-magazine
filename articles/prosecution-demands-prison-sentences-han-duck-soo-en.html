Prosecutors Demand 5 Years for Former PM Han Duck-soo, 4 Years for Ex-Chief of Staff Chung Jin-suk

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 안동MBC NEWS · 정진석 “보고만 받았다”는데… 검찰이 짚은 핵심 정황과 징역 4년 구형. Summarised from that video.

Prosecutors have requested prison sentences for key defendants, including former Prime Minister Han Duck-soo, former Presidential Chief of Staff Chung Jin-suk, former Senior Presidential Secretary for Civil Affairs Kim Joo-hyun, and former Secretary for Public Office Discipline Lee Won-mo, who were indicted on charges including abuse of power and obstruction of the exercise of rights.

Pointing Out Disruptions in Constitutional Court Justice Nominations and Vetting

Prosecutors stated that after the Dec. 3 emergency martial law, the defendants interfered with the National Assembly's right to form the Constitutional Court and caused the normal personnel vetting process to derail. They pointed out that the defendants completely excluded the personnel secretary, who manages the personnel procedures of the statutory Personnel Committee, and approved or directed flawed vetting.

Prosecutors emphasized that although the defendants, as legal experts and high-ranking public officials, well understood the need for the proper vetting procedures required by law, they reduced the national personnel vetting system to a tool for justifying the decisions of those in power.

Calling for Severe Punishment for "Abandoning Duty as Public Officials During Crisis"

Prosecutors criticized the defendants for failing to refuse unjust orders, pandering to power, and attempting to shift responsibility onto subordinate employees. They also pointed out that the defendants abandoned the professional ethics and constitutional duties that public officials must uphold in times of national crisis.

견리사이 견위수명, 이익을 보거든 정의를 생각하고 위태로움을 보거든 목숨을 바치라.

"When you see profit, think of righteousness; when you see danger, offer your life."

Citing the words of patriot An Jung-geun, prosecutors stated that while public officials are not required to become heroes or give up their lives, refraining from using their authority to protect their positions and power when the Republic of Korea is in crisis is the absolute minimum duty of a public official.

Demanded Sentences by Defendant

Taking into account the status, roles, and degree of participation in the crimes of the defendants, prosecutors requested that the court hand down severe sentences.

  • Defendant Han Duck-soo: 5 years in prison
  • Defendant Chung Jin-suk: 4 years in prison
  • Defendant Kim Joo-hyun: 4 years in prison
  • Defendant Lee Won-mo: 3 years in prison

Prosecutors added that this ruling should serve as a wake-up call to numerous public officials who may face national crises in the future, and clearly demonstrate that they must be held accountable when they misuse the authority entrusted to them by the people.
