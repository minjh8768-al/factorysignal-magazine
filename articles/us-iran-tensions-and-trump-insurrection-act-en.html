Trump Issues Fresh Threats Against Iran, Mentions Possible Invocation of Insurrection Act

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 연합뉴스TV · [월드앤이슈] 트럼프, 연일 이란 위협…내란법 발동 가능성도 / 연합뉴스TV (YonhapnewsTV). Summarised from that video.

As tensions between the United States and Iran continue, the US is reinforcing its military deployments against Iran and introducing a series of economic sanctions. According to foreign media reports, US aircraft carrier forces, including the USS Theodore Roosevelt, are additionally moving to the Middle East, with the deployed troop strength expected to reach up to 10,000.

Tightening Sanctions Against Iran and Deadlocked Negotiations

The US Department of the Treasury stepped up economic pressure by adding key Iranian automotive and motorcycle manufacturers, as well as foreign parts suppliers, to its sanctions list. Meanwhile, bilateral negotiations stalled after the US Department of State demanded the immediate departure of an Iranian delegation visiting New York for the UN General Assembly.

Trump Says Invoking the Insurrection Act "Not Ruled Out"

In an interview with news magazine Time, former US President Donald Trump made remarks leaving open the possibility when asked about declaring a state of emergency or invoking the Insurrection Act if violence breaks out ahead of the midterm elections.

"어떤 가능성도 배제하지 않아. 두고 보자"

"I don't rule out anything. We'll see."

Former President Trump also stated his position regarding the application of the Insurrection Act, which allows the president to mobilize the military using presidential authority.

"내란법을 한번도 사용한 적 없지만 그걸 사용할 수도 있어"

"I've never used the Insurrection Act, but I could use it."

In-Flight Disturbance Incident and US Military Push for 'Autonomous Warfare Command'

An incident occurred aboard a passenger plane traveling from Dubai to Israel in which a co-pilot attacked the pilot and attempted to crash the aircraft, but the plane made an emergency landing after crew members and passengers subdued him. Some authorities are investigating a potential connection to Iran. The US Department of Defense has begun preparing for future warfare by pursuing the establishment of a new 'Autonomous Warfare Command' to oversee AI, drone, and robotics technologies.

UK MI5 Warning and Chinese Airline Power Trip Controversy

British intelligence agency MI5 warned that Chinese research institutes attempted to collect advanced technology and knowledge by approaching British scholars under the guise of academic support, but the Chinese Embassy in the UK hit back, calling it groundless fabrication. Meanwhile, a controversy arose at a Chinese airline after a flight attendant was subjected to loud protests and knelt to apologize simply for brushing a passenger's elbow with a beverage cart.
