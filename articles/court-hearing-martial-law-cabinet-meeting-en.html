Defense Counsel Argues Prime Minister Han Duck-soo's Claim Lacks Credibility, Citing Cabinet Summoning Timeline

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 안동MBC NEWS · “국무위원 전원이 반대합니다” 윤석열 “가 있어”. Summarised from that video.

During an appellate hearing for the insurrection leadership case held at the Seoul High Court, defense counsel for the defendant argued that Prime Minister Han Duck-soo's claims regarding the circumstances of convening the Cabinet meeting at the time of the martial law declaration do not align with statements from key officials and CCTV footage.

Circumstances of Convening the Cabinet Meeting as Seen Through Statements and CCTV

Counsel reconstructed the situation at the time by compiling statements from Minister of the Interior and Safety Lee Sang-min, Minister of Justice Park Sung-jae, Minister of Foreign Affairs Cho Tae-yul, and Director of the National Intelligence Service Cho Tae-yong. According to counsel's explanation, after leaving the presidential office, Cabinet members waited in the main reception room and discussed the emergency martial law and the necessity of Cabinet deliberation.

Counsel explained that during this process, communications to summon additional Cabinet members were already underway, which took place before Prime Minister Han re-entered the presidential office.

Rebuttal to Prime Minister Han Duck-soo's Claims

Counsel for the defendant pointed out that Prime Minister Han's claim—that he suggested convening the Cabinet meeting during the initial discussion in the presidential office and that the meeting was convened accordingly—was merely his own independent assertion.

Counsel emphasized as follows:

국무회의 개최와 국무위원 소집은 한덕수와 무관함을 확실히 하는 증거가 됩니다.

"This serves as evidence confirming that the holding of the Cabinet meeting and the summoning of Cabinet members were unrelated to Han Duck-soo."

Citing relevant court rulings and statements from officials, counsel stated that prior to Prime Minister Han's recommendation, the defendant had already recognized the necessity of Cabinet deliberation and was proceeding with the summoning process.

Time-by-Time Analysis of Movement between Main Reception Room and Office

Counsel presented CCTV footage records from inside the presidential office, showing the movements of Cabinet members and officials by time. Counsel explained that they identified the movement details of Minister of National Defense Kim Yong-hyun, Chief Private Secretary Kim Jeong-hwan, Presidential Security Service Chief Park Jong-jun, and Chief National Policy Officer Sung Tae-yoon between around 21:13 and 21:35.

Counsel added that, noting in particular that Minister Kim Yong-hyun stopped by the main reception room and indicated with his fingers the number of attendees required to meet a quorum, actions to check the quorum and summon additional members were already taking place before Prime Minister Han's second entry into the presidential office.

Defendant's Future Plans for Response

Counsel stated that they plan to continue additional verification regarding related testimony and trial rulings.

Counsel stated that they will secure relevant evidentiary materials through requests for document transmittal orders and clearly establish the facts surrounding the convening of the Cabinet meeting.
