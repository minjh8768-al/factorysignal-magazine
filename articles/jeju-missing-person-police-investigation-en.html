Controversy Over False Closure of Jeju Missing Person Case Prompts Family to Request Renaming

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: JTBC News · "골절 없다" 브리핑 뒤 유족에 "설골 골절" 설명..논란 커지자 #뉴스다 / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

Controversy is growing over police inadequacy and false closure regarding a case in which a missing person was found dead in Jeju 104 days after disappearing. The bereaved family has demanded a strict investigation into the detective in charge and requested that the case be renamed.

Naming of the Senior Officer Bu Case and Bereaved Family's Call for Investigation

To restore the victim's honor and correct the focus of the investigation, the bereaved family requested that the case be called the 'Senior Officer Bu Case,' using the rank of the police officer who was in charge of the case. While holding a quiet funeral, they urged the police to focus their investigation on Senior Officer Bu, who falsely closed the missing person report.

Controversy Over Cause of Death and Review of Possible Homicide

Discrepancies between the police's initial announcement and what was conveyed to the bereaved family have sparked controversy over the cause of death. The police shared autopsy findings showing a fracture in the hyoid bone in the neck area, but reported an appraisal result stating that the possibility of this occurring during post-mortem decomposition could not be ruled out. No unusual fractures were found in other areas such as the head or torso.

Stating that it is difficult to determine the exact cause of death due to severe decomposition and skeletalization of the body, the police are conducting an investigation without completely ruling out the possibility of homicide.

Investigation into Crime Motive and Progress of Response

The arrested Senior Officer Bu denies the charges, claiming to have actually spoken on the phone with the missing person, but an investigation into phone records revealed this to be untrue. Police have deployed profilers to focus on determining Senior Officer Bu's exact motive for the crime.

Koh Pyung-ki, Chief of the Jeju Provincial Police Agency, visited the funeral home and promised the bereaved family a thorough investigation.

한 점의 억울한 점이 없도록 철저히 수사해서 유족들에게 통보해드릴 겁니다.

"We will thoroughly investigate so that there is not a single point of injustice and inform the bereaved family."

It was also confirmed that after falsely closing the case, Senior Officer Bu received multiple awards and commendations in recent years, including a commendation for contributions to searching for another missing person.
