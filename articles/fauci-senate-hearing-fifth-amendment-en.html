Former NIAID Director Fauci Invokes Fifth Amendment 111 Times at Senate Hearing, Escalating Political Clash

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: KBS 세계는지금 · 청문회 소환된 파우치, 111차례 묵비권 행사 (KBS_462회_2026.08.08.방송). Summarised from that video.

Former National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID) Director Anthony Fauci appeared at a U.S. Senate Committee on Homeland Security hearing and invoked his right to remain silent more than 100 times in response to questions from Republican lawmakers, causing widespread controversy.

Appearing at Senate Hearing, Invoking Fifth Amendment 111 Times

Former Director Fauci, who oversaw the COVID-19 response, was summoned to the Senate hearing to examine the origins of the virus and the appropriateness of quarantine policies. Amid intense questioning from Republican senators, former Director Fauci refused to answer, citing his rights under the Fifth Amendment to the U.S. Constitution.

On the advice of counsel, I respectfully decline to answer based upon my rights under the Fifth Amendment to the Constitution.

"On the advice of counsel, I respectfully decline to answer based upon my rights under the Fifth Amendment to the Constitution."

When Senator Josh Hawley asked questions such as the day of the week or the color of his tie, former Director Fauci repeated the same answer. As a result, some local observers mocked former Director Fauci's response.

Conflict with Trump Revealed Through Disclosure of Private Diary

Ahead of this hearing, Homeland Security Committee Chairman Rand Paul sparked controversy by releasing the contents of a diary written by former Director Fauci during the pandemic. The disclosed records were shown to contain clashes with former President Donald Trump and critical personal recollections.

Department of Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. reported that the diary was written on a work computer and was discovered to have been sent via email before his resignation.

Political Battle and Impact on US-China Relations

During the hearing, Republicans continued their offensive by raising allegations of a cover-up regarding a leak from the Wuhan laboratory. In response, experts noted that former Director Fauci had maintained a consistent position that while he viewed a natural origin as highly likely, all possibilities should be investigated.

Furthermore, experts analyze that political motives to rally the support base by stoking anti-Chinese sentiment and conspiracy theories were at play, and are paying close attention to the impact on the future political situation and U.S.-China relations.
