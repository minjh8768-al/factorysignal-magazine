Parties Clash Ahead of Hearing for Justice Minister Nominee Kim Seung-won

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · "김승원 강행하면 지지율 20%" 국민의힘 압박.민주당도 "공소취소, 연임 개헌 깔끔하게 정리하고 가야" / SBS 논스톱 라이브. Summarised from that video.

Confirmation Hearing Without Witnesses… Clashes Between Ruling and Opposition Parties Intensify

Ahead of the National Assembly confirmation hearing for Minister of Justice nominee Kim Seung-won, political clashes between ruling and opposition parties are intensifying. Although there are numerous issues to verify, including allegations of soliciting clinical trial approval from the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, false registration of residence, and preferential hiring of judges, the hearing will proceed without a single witness adopted.

The People Power Party continued its political offensive, strongly demanding the withdrawal of Kim's nomination. Jang Dong-hyeok, leader of the People Power Party, urged the withdrawal of Kim's nomination at a party leadership meeting.

추석 앞둔 국민에게 '범죄종합세트'라도 선물하겠다는 것입니까? 이제라도 김승원 지명을 즉각 철회해야 합니다.

"Are you trying to gift the public a 'comprehensive set of crimes' ahead of Chuseok? Even now, Kim Seung-won's nomination must be immediately withdrawn."

On the other hand, the Democratic Party of Korea defended Kim, defining the opposition's claims as false agitation. Han Byung-do, floor leader of the Democratic Party, criticized the opposition's allegations at a meeting.

'아니면 말고' 식의 의혹을 100번 되풀이해도 거짓 선동이 검증이 될 수는 없습니다.

"Repeating 'if not, so what' style allegations a hundred times cannot turn false political agitation into verification."

Allegations of Solicitation and Administration of Genencell's New Drug Clinical Trial Approval

A major issue in the verification process is the allegation of solicitation regarding approval for clinical trials of Genencell's COVID-19 treatment. Controversy centers on whether nominee Kim requested swift processing regarding the approval of Genencell's new drug clinical trial plan from the head of the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety in 2021.

In addition, it was confirmed that Genencell's new drug was actually administered to 93 patients following approval by the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety. Controversy surrounding the safety of the clinical trial continues, along with questions over whether Genencell omitted non-clinical trial side effect data during the approval application process.

Outlook for the Political Situation

As ruling and opposition parties continue their hardline confrontation over the witness-less hearing, the outcome of the confirmation hearing is expected to serve as a major watershed for the future political situation.
