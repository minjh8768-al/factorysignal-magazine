'Never Seen Food Like This at Any Games'… Cost-Cutting Measures Spark Food and Lodging Controversy at Nagoya Asian Games

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · "국대 식사를 이렇게 준다고요?" 불만 터진 선수들 만났습니다..'돈 아끼려다 대망신' 아시안게임 숙소 가보니 / SBS / 나고야체크인 EP.2. Summarised from that video.

The Aichi-Nagoya Organizing Committee preparing for the 2026 Nagoya Asian Games released a plan to use prefabricated container homes, a cruise ship, and general hotels as accommodations instead of building a large-scale athletes' village, but complaints over poor meals and cramped quarters are erupting among delegation members on site.

Prefabricated Container Village and Delivery Food Allowed

About 2,000 athletes and officials are staying in the container accommodations set up in Nagoya. However, privacy is difficult due to the narrow space, and the lack of large beds even for tall athletes such as those in basketball and volleyball is adding to the inconvenience.

In addition, unlike typical international multi-sport events that strictly control outside food due to concerns over doping and food poisoning, a delivery pick-up location was set up at the entrance of the container accommodation village. On site, delivery meals from ordinary lunchbox vendors, such as karaage and hamburger steak, are currently being provided to athletes.

Registration Delays and Operational Confusion on Cruise Ship Accommodation

The Costa Serena, a large cruise ship prepared to accommodate about 4,000 people, also revealed operational issues. The athlete delegation list was not properly processed, leading to instances where athletes had to wait under the blazing sun for over an hour, or took more than 16 hours including travel time, only entering their rooms late at night.

Varun Sanal, a member of India's national mixed martial arts team, strongly criticized the local operational situation in an interview.

If this happened in India, oh my God, the media would be going crazy. Not just in India, like globally. And you would have been the butt of the joke.

"If this happened in India, oh my God, the media would be going crazy. Not just in India, like globally. And you would have been the butt of the joke."

Poor Meals and Cramped Spaces for Athletes Staying in General Hotels

The situation is no different for the national teqball team staying in general hotels. When match schedules end late, they are unable to use the athletes' village or venue cafeteria and are forced to get dinner at ordinary restaurants nearby, such as rice bowl shops.

Kim Eun-jun, a national teqball player, expressed disappointment over the nutritional value and quantity of the meals provided at the venue.

어느 대회 가도 이렇게 밥을 주는 건 처음 봤습니다. 점심은 너무 터무니없이 영양소나 배를 채우는 데 부족함이 있습니다.

"I have never seen food served like this at any competition. Lunch is absurdly lacking in nutrients and far from enough to fill us up."

Lee Yong-seop, head coach of the national teqball team, stated that coaching staff had to rush to a nearby supermarket during the athletes' warm-up time to personally buy bananas and drinks, and that requests made through the Korean Sport & Olympic Committee to adjust the side dishes served identically every day were not reflected.

It was also confirmed that hotel rooms were so cramped there was hardly enough space to properly unpack suitcases, making the environment inadequate for athletes to warm up or rest.

Organizing Committee: "Aim Was Cost Reduction... Acknowledge Budget Overrun"

Regarding these difficulties, Hideaki Omura, president of the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games Organizing Committee (Governor of Aichi Prefecture), positively evaluated hosting the games through new accommodation formats, but on-site complaints persist.

Ichiro Hirosawa, vice president of the organizing committee (Mayor of Nagoya), explained the reason for introducing containers and cruise ships instead of building the originally planned athletes' village as follows.

Vice President Hirosawa stated that while they originally planned to build an athletes' village, they decided on the current format to cut expenses for a sustainable tournament due to the significant cost. However, he admitted it is true that costs exceeded the original plan considerably.
