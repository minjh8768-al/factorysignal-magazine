'Blue Whale Project' Declared Economically Unviable After Groundless Five-Drilling Announcement Plan

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: JTBC News · "곧 대통령 긴급 브리핑" 8분 전 기습..'계엄 얘기 아냐' 충격적 #뉴스다 / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

As the "Blue Whale Project," a deep-sea gas field development in the East Sea, came to an end with the conclusion that it lacks economic viability, it was revealed that premature announcements, a lack of scientific basis, and attempts to withhold information occurred during the project's execution.

Premature Presidential Briefing and Demands to Shorten Drilling Schedule

Following an audit of the Blue Whale Project's implementation process, the Board of Audit and Inspection judged that there had been premature presidential intervention and pushing of the project. The Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy had suggested in a report to the Office of the President that public promotion should be refrained from due to uncertain exploration success chances, but a policy for a public announcement was nevertheless decided upon.

It was also revealed that the originally planned drilling completion timeline was adjusted following demands to shorten it within the presidential term. The audit results further showed that when it was pointed out that political judgment was necessary, rebukes followed stating that political judgment would be made directly.

Groundless 'Five-Drilling' Decision and Success Rate Controversy

Criticism was raised that there were no official research or analysis reports regarding the five-drilling plan requiring a project cost of 500 billion won. Even though each drilling requires more than 100 billion won, a logic was applied that simply calculated a 20% exploration success rate to assume one success out of five drillings.

In response, criticism emerged from academia and the National Assembly regarding the lack of scientific basis. Choi Kyung-sik (최경식), a professor in the Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences at Seoul National University, pointed out as follows:

과학적으로 근거가 부족한 확률들을 가지고 와서 다섯 번 하면 한 번은 걸리지 않겠느냐, 그러니까 우리가 다섯 번을 해야 한다, 이런 논리는 전혀 과학적인 근거도 아니고...

"Bringing probabilities that lack scientific basis and saying if we do it five times, won't we hit it once, so we must do it five times—this logic is not a scientific basis at all..."

National Assembly Member Park Ji-hye (박지혜) also noted that the estimated actual success rate of the Blue Whale promising structure was 19.1%, and the average success rate across all seven promising structures was only around 15%.

사실 20%라는 탐사 성공률에 대해서도 근거가 없는 상황입니다. 대왕고래가 19.1%고요. 7개의 유망 구조에 대해서 탐사 성공률 평균을 내면 15%정도밖에 안 나오거든요.

"In fact, there is no basis even for the 20% exploration success rate. The Blue Whale structure is 19.1%. If you average the exploration success rate for the seven promising structures, it only comes out to about 15%."

First Drilling Results Conclude "Lacks Economic Viability"

Korea National Oil Corporation concluded after detailed analysis of samples from the first test drilling that recovering gas at an economically viable level is impossible. A primary reason identified was that gas saturation reached only about 6%, far below the initially expected 50–70%. Consequently, the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy suspended drilling operations.

Criticism continued in the National Assembly that a massive budget was wasted due to the unreasonable pushing of the project. Representative Song Jae-bong (송재봉) of the Democratic Party of Korea pointed out that public trust in resource development was damaged.

결국은 1200억원이라는 엄청난 예산을 낭비한 그런 결과가 초래한 것이고 무리하게 정권의 홍보수단이라든가 그런 것으로 활용하려다가 자원 개발과 관련해서도 국민적인 신뢰를 잃어버린...

"In the end, it brought about the result of wasting a huge budget of 120 billion won, and by unreasonably trying to use it as a promotional tool for the administration, public trust regarding resource development was lost..."

Controversy Over Legal Advice to Refuse Disclosure of Verification Data

It was also revealed that the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy requested legal advice from a major law firm to refuse the National Assembly's request to submit verification data. Criticism was raised that it spent over 15 million won in consultation fees trying to secure grounds for non-disclosure.

The National Assembly strongly criticized the act of spending state funds to hide public data instead of transparently disclosing information.
