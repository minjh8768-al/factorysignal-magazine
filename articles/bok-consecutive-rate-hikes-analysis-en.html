Bank of Korea Delivers Second Consecutive Rate Hike to Tame Inflation and Housing Market

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · [속보] 한국은행 2연속 기준금리 인상. "가래 아닌 호미로 물가 잡을 것" [즉시분석]. Summarised from that video.

The Bank of Korea carried out its second consecutive key interest rate hike, continuing its monetary tightening stance. With this increase, the interest rate gap between South Korea and the United States narrowed to 0.75 percentage points, ushering the South Korean economy into a full-fledged high-interest rate environment.

Response to Continue Until Inflation Stability Is Assured

The Governor of the Bank of Korea expressed a strong determination to maintain the tightening stance until the inflation rate converges to the target level.

통화정책을 수행하는 데 있어 물가상승률이 저희 목표 수준(2.0%)까지 안정적으로 수렴할 것이라는 확신이 들 때까지 대응하겠다

"In conducting monetary policy, we will continue to respond until we are confident that the inflation rate will stably converge to our target level (2.0%)."

He further emphasized that taking preemptive action before price increases spread is the way to prevent a greater economic burden in the future.

물가 오름세 확산 전, 가래 대신 호미로 막았다

"Before the rise in prices spread, we stopped it early with a hoe instead of a spade."

Although consumer price inflation eased somewhat, falling from 3.2% to 2.8% and core inflation recording around 2.5%, import price inflation remained at a high level, reflecting the judgment that it is still too early to be confident about price stability.

Consideration of Rising Seoul Housing Prices and Financial Stability

In assessing financial stability, domestic and international conditions were comprehensively reviewed. Although the Korean won to U.S. dollar exchange rate entered a phase of rapid stabilization, concerns were raised that a steep decline in the exchange rate could weaken the price competitiveness and profitability of domestic exporting companies.

Domestically, the Seoul apartment sales price index continued a strong upward trend without adjustment, increasing the need for monetary policy coordination to stabilize the real estate market and household debt. The intention is to promote the effect of stabilizing housing prices by conducting consecutive interest rate hikes in line with the government's measures to stabilize the real estate market.

Confidence in Offsetting Economic Slowdown via Upward Growth Revision

The Bank of Korea raised its economic growth forecast, easing concerns about an economic contraction resulting from interest rate hikes. The Bank of Korea raised its growth forecast for 2026 from the previous 2.6% to 3.3%, and for 2027 from 2.1% to 2.9%.

This is based on the judgment that even if raising the key interest rate dampens demand from economic agents, the economy can maintain a sufficient recovery. Based on the economic recovery trend, the Bank of Korea is expected to determine the future direction of monetary policy with price stability and real estate market stability as top priorities.
