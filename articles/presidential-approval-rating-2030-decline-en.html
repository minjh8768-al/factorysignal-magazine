As Approval Ratings Among 20s and 30s Plunge, Seong Han-yong Says "President and Democratic Party Must Learn Lessons"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · [성민종합정치] 국정 지지율 40% 초반까지 하락..성한용 "민주 전대 후도 하향세면 정권 위기" / SBS / 주영진의 뉴스브리핑. Summarised from that video.

According to recently released public opinion poll results, positive evaluations of the president's performance showed a downward trend, while negative evaluations rose. In particular, negative evaluations far exceeded positive evaluations among voters in their 20s and 30s, raising the need for a response at the administration level.

Plunge in Approval Ratings Among 20s and 30s and Public Opinion Trends

According to the Realmeter survey results, negative evaluations of the president's performance recorded 54.6% and positive evaluations recorded 42.0%, continuing the downward trend in positive approval. By age group, negative evaluations among men in their 20s reached 72.0% and men in their 30s reached 70.0%, while negative evaluations among women in their 20s and 30s were recorded at 62.7% and 55.7%, respectively.

In the party support survey released together, the Democratic Party recorded 44.4% and the People Power Party recorded 39.0%. Panelists analyzed that current issues such as real estate and stock market volatility and protection for victims of violent crime likely affected the departure of young people.

Seong Han-yong: "If Approval Ratings Drop Further, It's a Crisis for the Administration... Overall Review Needed"

Seong Han-yong, a senior reporter at The Hankyoreh, appeared on a broadcast to point out the negative shift in sentiment among voters in their 20s and 30s and called for measures from the government and the ruling party.

대통령과 민주당, 지지율 하락에 교훈 얻어야 한다. 여기서 지지율 더 떨어지면 정권 위기다.

"The president and the Democratic Party must learn a lesson from the falling approval ratings. If approval ratings drop further from here, it will be a crisis for the administration."

Senior reporter Seong Han-yong added that despite the sharp drop in approval ratings among the 2030 generation, the opposition People Power Party is failing to fully act as a clear alternative force to absorb young voters.

Heo Min: "A Signal That Future Voter Base Is Changing... Must Guard Against Consolidation"

Heo Min, a former reporter at Munhwa Ilbo, also warned of the danger of young voters departing and urged a meticulous review of state affairs.

2030세대 지지율 변화, 미래의 표밭 바뀌는 것이다. 이것이 집권 중반 이후 구조화되고 고착화되면 정권 전체에 큰 위기가 될 수 있다.

"The change in approval ratings among the 2030 generation signals a shift in the future voter base. If this becomes structural and entrenched after the middle of the term, it could pose a major crisis for the entire administration."

Experts agreed that the public opinion poll results serve as a warning sign for overall governance, stressing that livelihood and economic policies that young people can feel, along with sophisticated state administration reform, are urgent.
