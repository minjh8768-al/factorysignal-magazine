'Ultra-Conservative Hardliner' Michelle Steel Named US Ambassador to South Korea: "Could Become a Variable in Diplomacy"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 스픽스 · [뉴스분석] 한국계라 반겼는데… 트럼프 '미셸 박 스틸' 카드가 한미관계에 몰고 올 치명적 파장. Summarised from that video.

Attention is focusing on the potential impact on South Korea-US diplomatic relations following the nomination of former Federal Representative Michelle Park Steel as the first US ambassador to South Korea under the second Trump administration. As a hardline conservative politician was nominated for the post of US ambassador to South Korea, which has mostly been held by career diplomats, observations suggest political repercussions could arise during the coordination of major security and diplomatic agendas between the two nations.

Hardline Conservative Politician Championing 'America First'

Former Hankook Ilbo editor-in-chief Ko Jae-hak (고재학) assessed nominee Michelle Park Steel as a figure with very strong conservative leanings even within the Republican Party. He explained that she is categorized as a politician who has maintained a hardline stance, particularly on issues regarding China and North Korea.

Ko analyzed that, unlike in the past when career diplomats served as US ambassadors to South Korea, appointing an ultra-conservative figure who puts Trump-style America First front and center is an extremely unusual appointment.

주한 미국대사라고 하면 한미 관계에서 가장 중요한 민감한 사안을 직접 다루어야 하는데, 한국 외교 안보의 변수가 될 수 있다는 이야기도 나오고 있다.

"The US ambassador to South Korea must directly handle the most important and sensitive matters in South Korea-US relations, and there are growing comments that she could become a variable in South Korea's foreign policy and security."

Concerns Over Difficulties in Coordinating Security Issues Like Defense Spending Increases and OPCON Transfer

Possibilities have been raised that, following the launch of the Trump administration, the US may strongly demand an increase in defense spending from South Korea or the securing of strategic flexibility for US Forces Korea. Concerns are also emerging that Ambassador Michelle Park Steel will serve as a key conduit for delivering such pressure from the US.

Points were made that verification criteria and conditions could become far more stringent even on sensitive issues requiring discussion between South Korean and US leaders, such as the transfer of wartime operational control (OPCON) or the acquisition of nuclear-powered submarines. Ko stated that the ambassador's hardline disposition could work in a direction that increases South Korea's security burden.

Caution Against Domestic Political Exploitation and Need for Thorough Working-Level Response

Concerns were also raised that far-right conservative factions in South Korea might use Ambassador Michelle Park Steel's political leaning to fuel partisan conflict. However, analyses were also presented that because matters pass through coordination by career diplomat staff within the US Embassy in Seoul, there will be certain limits to unconventional moves by the ambassador individually.

Ko urged that person evaluation and policy response should be approached separately. He emphasized that while maintaining a cautious stance publicly at the government level, official channels should make prompt contact at the working level to convince the ambassador of the South Korean government's foreign policy and security principles and manage the relationship closely through diplomatic channels.
