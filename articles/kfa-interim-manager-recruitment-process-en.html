KFA Closes Applications for Interim National Team Manager, Begins Review and Interviews

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 새벽의 축구 전문가 · “외국인 감독이 대부분이다” 포옛, 토렌드.. 국대 임시감독 서류접수 마감! 어떤 감독이 지원했을까?. Summarised from that video.

The Korea Football Association (KFA) has closed applications for the open recruitment of an interim national team manager and entered the full-scale screening and interview process.

Formation of Evaluation Committee and Commencement of Interview Procedures

The KFA will launch a candidate evaluation committee within this week, composed of select members of the National Team Committee and outside experts. The evaluation committee plans to review the adequacy of the submitted documents and then conduct online interviews with the candidates.

이후엔 평가위원회의 평가 결과에 따라 우선 협상 순위를 확정하고 협상에 들어갑니다.

"Afterward, based on the evaluation committee's assessment results, the priority negotiation order will be finalized, and negotiations will begin."

Negotiations will begin with the top-priority candidate based on the interview results, and if terms cannot be agreed upon, negotiations will proceed with the next candidate in line. The list of applicants will not be disclosed, considering the potential impact on the screening and negotiation process.

Large Influx of Foreign Manager Applications and Document Review Status

It was confirmed that a large number of foreign coaches whose names had been mentioned in foreign media and news reports applied for this position. In the case of domestic coaches, individuals with extensive managerial experience in the K League who currently do not have a team submitted their documents.

In this recruitment drive, the KFA required specific detailed documentation, including a coaching staff setup of 5 to 7 members, coaching licenses, coaching philosophy, and a national team operational plan. Because the requirements are strict, the association is requesting supplementary materials if submitted documents are incomplete, and translation work on application materials submitted by foreign coaches is also underway.

Background Behind Stricter Procedures and Need for Swift Appointment

Establishing complex document screening and evaluation criteria even for an interim manager selection process is a measure taken to avoid repeating controversies over procedural fairness that arose during previous manager appointments. In addition, structural limitations played a role, as the KFA is currently operating under an acting leadership system due to the absence of a president, making it difficult to sign long-term contracts.

The national team faces an upcoming schedule of four consecutive matches against Ecuador, Uruguay, Venezuela, and Uzbekistan during the upcoming international match window. Swiftly appointing a competent manager to reshape the team and prepare for the Asian Cup is considered a key task for future national team operations.
