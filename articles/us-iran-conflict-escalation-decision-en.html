Trump Hints Decision on Iran Is Imminent as Middle East Teeters on the Brink of All-Out War

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: KBS News · [이슈] 트럼프 "이란전 결단 임박" 중동 전운 다시 고조…미 수뇌부, 예정 없던 '극비 회의'/2026년 10월 4일(일)/KBS. Summarised from that video.

U.S. President Donald Trump has hinted that a major decision on the Iran issue is imminent. As top U.S. officials hold classified discussions on response measures, military clashes are intensifying across the Middle East, pushing tensions to a peak.

Trump's Ultimatum to Iran: "The Easy Way or the Hard Way"

U.S. President Trump delivered a strong message of pressure toward Iran, stating that he would make a final decision soon. He emphasized that the options include a diplomatic settlement through an agreement with Iran or the resumption of military operations against the country.

We'll have it the easy way or the hard way.

"We'll have it the easy way or the hard way."

Top-Secret Camp David Meeting and Rejection of '7-Day Plan'

The U.S. administration held a top-level foreign affairs and national security meeting at Camp David, chaired by Vice President Vance without prior announcement. Observers believe major decisions were discussed to alleviate the burden of a prolonged war with Iran ahead of the midterm elections.

President Trump explicitly rejected the "7-Day Plan" previously proposed by Iran—which offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz on the condition that the naval blockade be lifted and frozen assets unfrozen—stating that its contents were insufficient. The U.S. Treasury Secretary also hinted at the possibility of additional sanctions against Iran, citing alternative oil pipeline routes.

Houthis Seize Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, Threatening Red Sea Shipping Routes

Pro-Iranian Yemen Houthi rebels launched a surprise takeover of strategic strongholds near the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, the gateway to the Red Sea, including Mokha, Dhubab, and Perim Island. By securing control of the strait, which accounts for a major portion of global trade, even Saudi Arabia's crude oil export routes now face the threat of blockade.

As the Houthi rebels struck Saudi oil refining facilities, Saudi Arabia launched retaliatory airstrikes on the Yemeni capital of Sanaa and other areas, causing clashes to spread rapidly across the Middle East in a chain reaction.

U.S. Airstrikes and Iranian Retaliation Bring Region to Brink of All-Out War

U.S. Central Command resumed airstrikes against Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) targets in Iran, with explosions confirmed in southern Iranian locations such as Bandar Abbas and Qeshm Island. Iran also launched retaliatory operations, firing missiles and drones at U.S. military bases in the Middle East.

As strikes and counterstrikes continue between the two sides, concerns are growing that the entire Middle East could be drawn into the vortex of an all-out war, regardless of moves to resume dialogue.
