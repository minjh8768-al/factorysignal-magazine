Lee Hwa-young Sentenced to 7 Years, 8 Months for North Korea Remittances; Receives Extra Prison Time for Perjury

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 연합뉴스TV · 이화영, 청와대에 '석방 청구서' 날렸다 "이 대통령에 매우 화난 상태…나만 방치 더는 못 기다려" [이슈ZIP] / 연합뉴스TV(YonhapnewsTV). Summarised from that video.

Former Gyeonggi Province Vice Governor for Peace Lee Hwa-young, who was brought to trial on charges of bribery and involvement in Ssangbangwool Group's illegal remittance case to North Korea, has had his sentence of seven years and eight months in prison finalized by the Supreme Court. Meanwhile, controversy continues over an additional prison sentence for perjury before the National Assembly and requests for his parole.

7-Year, 8-Month Sentence Confirmed for North Korea Remittances and Bribery

The Supreme Court upheld the lower court's ruling sentencing former Vice Governor Lee to seven years and eight months in prison on charges including receiving hundreds of millions of won in bribes from Ssangbangwool Group and conspiring in illegal remittances to North Korea. The Supreme Court dismissed all appeals from both prosecutors and Lee, stating that there were no legal errors in the lower court's judgment that affected the ruling.

Lee had been on trial on charges including having Ssangbangwool pay a total of $8 million on behalf of Gyeonggi Province—$5 million for a smart farm project promised to North Korea and $3 million for the governor's visit to North Korea—as well as receiving illegal political funds.

Four-Month Sentence Handed Down for Perjury Regarding 'Salmon and Sake Party'

Lee claimed he was bribed and coaxed into making statements during the prosecution's investigation, raising allegations of a so-called "salmon and sake party," but the court ruled this to be a false claim. In a trial by jury for violating the Act on Testimony and Appraisal Before the National Assembly (perjury), the court sentenced Lee to an additional four months in prison.

Lee, who appeared as a witness in a National Assembly audit, had testified that he drank alcohol inside the prosecutors' office building.

박상용 검사실 1313호 영상녹화실에서 먹었습니다. 술을 페트병 같은 것에 해서 종이컵에 저하고 박상용 검사...

"I drank in video recording room No. 1313 of Prosecutor Park Sang-yong's office. The alcohol was in something like a plastic bottle, in paper cups with me and Prosecutor Park Sang-yong..."

The jury and the panel of judges accepted the prosecution's argument that Lee's testimony lacked credibility and found him guilty of perjury. However, they found him not guilty of violating the Political Funds Act regarding split donations, and dismissed the indictment on abuse of authority charges, citing the prosecution's abuse of prosecutorial power.

Controversy Surrounds Parole Request Amid Prolonged Imprisonment

With his prison sentence confirmed and his incarceration prolonged, Lee's defense counsel voiced their stance regarding a parole request and his condition in prison via social media. The defense lawyer expressed dissatisfaction, stating that Lee requested parole from the Presidential Office, but received no response.

Lee's lawyer, Kim Kwang-min (김광민), argued regarding the trial process as follows:

이렇게 검찰권을 동원해 가지고 다 조작 수사를 해서 억울하게 재판받는 사람은 3년 동안 갇힌 상태에서 철저하게 방어권이 제한된 상태에서 재판을 받았습니다.

"Someone who is being unjustly tried after a completely fabricated investigation using prosecutorial power was tried while locked up for three years, with their right to defense thoroughly restricted."

Lee's legal team expressed deep regret over the perjury conviction and announced their intention to appeal, continuing the legal battle.
