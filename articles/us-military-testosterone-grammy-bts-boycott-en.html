From Testosterone Testing for a 'Strong US Military' to BTS's Grammy 'Asian Pop' Boycott

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: KBS 세계는지금 · ‘에겐남’ 금지 / 그래미 (KBS_462회_2026.08.08.방송). Summarised from that video.

Repercussions surrounding notions of gender, race, and cultural divisions continue across the globe. As the U.S. Department of Defense announced plans to mandate male hormone level testing for service members in the name of a strong military, controversy is spreading in the popular music industry after BTS abruptly refused to submit their music in protest of a newly created category at the Grammy Awards.

U.S. Department of Defense Pursues Hormone Level Testing for Service Members Aged 30 and Older

The U.S. Department of Defense is pursuing a policy to screen military personnel aged 30 and older for testosterone deficiency during annual routine health checkups. The goal is to artificially manage male hormone levels that decline with age to maintain service members' combat performance at its peak.

U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth personally disclosed the plan to manage service members' hormone levels in a video message.

I'm authorizing a new screening program for testosterone deficiency... ensuring you have the right testosterone levels to operate at your absolute best.

"I'm authorizing a new screening program for testosterone deficiency... ensuring you have the right testosterone levels to operate at your absolute best."

Emphasis on ‘Strong Masculinity’ and Intention to Rally Support Base

Secretary Hegseth has long made strong demands regarding grooming and weight loss. In fact, he emphasized soldierly appearance and physical standards during a gathering of generals.

Frankly, it's tiring to look out at combat formations, or really any formation, and see fat troops. No more beards.

"Frankly, it's tiring to look out at combat formations, or really any formation, and see fat troops. No more beards."

Professor Kim Jae-cheon (김재천) analyzed that these measures align with the 'MAGA' sentiment centered on the white male working class in the U.S. and the trend toward restoring traditional masculinity. He explained that there is a political intention to rally the support base ahead of elections by highlighting traditional family values and a macho image in backlash against the trend of addressing social discrimination—such as racial and gender equality—emphasized under the Biden administration ('wokeism').

BTS Refuses Grammy Submissions... Controversy Over 'K-Pop Glass Ceiling'

In the popular music industry, the controversy over 'K-pop discrimination' reignited as BTS declared it would not submit music to the Grammy Awards. When the Grammys established a new 'Best Asian Pop Music Performance' category, criticism arose that isolating music by language or region could act as a 'glass ceiling' preventing artists from winning main awards.

BTS clearly stated its position via social media.

저희는 올해 그래미에 출품하지 않기로 했습니다. 음악이 지역이나 언어로 구분되기보다 음악 그 자체로 들리고 사랑받을 수 있기를 바랍니다.

"We have decided not to submit to the Grammys this year. We hope that music can be heard and loved for what it is, rather than being divided by region or language."

As the fallout grew, Grammy CEO Harvey Mason Jr. posted an explanation, countering that the purpose was merely to highlight the diversity of Asian music, not to exclude artists from main awards. However, as non-white artists have repeatedly been sidelined from main awards in the past behind specific genre categories, criticism continues that segregating K-pop—which has established itself in the global mainstream—into a separate category is itself reverse discrimination.
