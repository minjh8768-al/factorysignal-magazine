Controversy Surrounds Justice Minister Nominee Kim Seung-won Over 'Drug Request and Party Dissolution' As Rival Parties Clash

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: JTBC News · 국회, 오늘 김승원 인사청문회…'신약 청탁' 등 쟁점 여야 격돌 [이슈PLAY] /JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

Political strife is escalating over allegations that Justice Minister nominee Kim Seung-won solicited approval for a new drug's clinical trial and his remarks regarding reviewing the dissolution of the opposition party. As independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon pressed an offensive by releasing a series of audio transcripts and text messages related to the drug solicitation, the opposition party urged the nominee's resignation and withdrawal of his nomination, while the ruling party counteracted, calling it a targeted investigation by the prosecution.

Opposition Strongly Reacts to 'Party Dissolution Review' Remarks

Nominee Kim Seung-won drew strong backlash from the opposition party after stating his position that he would review the requirements for petitioning the dissolution of the People Power Party upon taking office as minister. Nominee Kim stated in a written response that he would carefully examine the intent of Constitutional Court rulings and related cases to review whether they warrant party dissolution.

In response, People Power Party lawmaker Na Kyung-won criticized it as an attempt to erase the very existence of the opposition party.

대통령 죄를 지우는 것만 하는 줄 알았더니 위헌정당 해산 요건 검토해서 야당의 존재 자체를 지우려고…

"I thought they were only erasing the president's crimes, but now they are reviewing unconstitutional party dissolution requirements to erase the very existence of the opposition party..."

Clash Over Audio Transcripts and Text Messages Regarding 'New Drug Clinical Request'

Independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon released related text messages and audio transcripts, raising allegations that nominee Kim had previously requested the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety to quickly process clinical trial approval for a specific pharmaceutical company's COVID-19 treatment. The disclosed materials included content showing nominee Kim forwarding a civil complaint to the head of the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety and checking on its progress.

Lawmaker Han questioned nominee Kim's qualifications, arguing that it was an improper solicitation rather than lawful handling of a civil complaint. On the other hand, nominee Kim explained that he had never made an improper solicitation, that it was a legitimate forwarding of a civil complaint, and that the matter had resulted in a decision not to prosecute during a prosecution investigation.

Ruling and Opposition Leadership Fully Mobilized... 'Targeted Investigation' vs. 'Withdrawal of Nomination'

The Democratic Party defended nominee Kim, stating that the raising of these allegations stemmed from an unreasonable targeted investigation by the prosecution. Han Byung-do, floor leader of the Democratic Party, emphasized that it was a matter the prosecution had already determined difficult to conclude as an illegal solicitation.

On the other hand, the People Power Party demanded the withdrawal of the nomination, raising additional allegations such as preferential treatment regarding a family-related cooperative, in addition to nominee Kim's drug request suspicion. As the ruling and opposition parties continue their sharp confrontation ahead of the hearing, a fierce battle is expected during the upcoming personnel hearing process.
