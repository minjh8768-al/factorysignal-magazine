"Need Alternatives, Not Burning Bridges": Practical Business Tips from Serial Entrepreneur in His 20s

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 머니비스트 ㅣ Money Beast · 엑싯한 20대 창업가의 찐 사업 꿀팁 (현실) #스타트업인터뷰 #대표인터뷰. Summarised from that video.

An entrepreneur in his 20s, who experienced starting and closing businesses before selling an application with a cumulative 150,000 users to a global company, shared practical advice gained during his business journey.

Importance of Preparing Alternatives and Managing Physical Fitness

The entrepreneur emphasized that when pursuing a business, preparing alternatives such as a fallback option or a side income stream reduces risks rather than blindly burning bridges.

사업이 망하면 대안이 있어야 합니다. 배수의 진 느낌으로 하나에 몰입하는 것은 위험할 확률이 매우 높습니다.

"If the business fails, there must be an alternative. Immersing oneself in a single option with a burning-bridges mindset carries a very high probability of risk."

He also recommended exercising consistently every day, even for just 10 minutes, to improve a sense of accomplishment and clear decision-making. He explained that exercise goes beyond simple health management and helps maintain mental calmness.

Proactive Networking and Continuous Follow-up

Regarding networking and business collaboration, he urged quickly approaching people whom one wants to work with during business without hesitation. The point is that it is important to make immediate contact to create opportunities rather than postponing attempts.

He added that one should avoid contacting people only when needed, emphasizing that a follow-up process of consistently sharing one's achievements, concerns, and areas of focus on a regular basis is essential.

Refraining from Giving Unsolicited Advice

He pointed out that unless one is willing to take responsibility for the other person's growth until the end, one should not freely give hasty advice or admonitions. He explained that when delivering sincere advice, an attitude of offering mental support while treating the person to a meal or drink before carefully sharing an opinion is required.

Key Tips for a Successful Business

  • Securing alternatives and side income streams in case of business failure
  • Consistent daily exercise for clear decision-making
  • Making immediate contact when meeting someone you want to collaborate with
  • Regularly sharing achievements and concerns beyond simple greetings
  • A cautious approach to giving advice by opening one's wallet and conveying sincerity
