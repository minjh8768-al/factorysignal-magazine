Controversy Sparks Over Military Exemption After Athletes' "I'm Not Going to the Army" Remarks... Defense Ministry and Lawmakers Consider Overhaul

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: JTBC News · "그 어려운 여야 통합 해냈다"..병역특례 폐지 '급물살' 타자 #뉴스다 / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

As the conduct and remarks of some players on the national men's football team that won gold at the Asian Games sparked controversy, demands for reforming the military service exemption system for the arts and sports have resurfaced.

Public Criticism Spreads Over Players' Inappropriate Remarks

The controversy began after interviews and social media posts by players were made public immediately after their victory. Captain Lee Ki-hyuk (이기혁) made remarks in an interview saying he would not be going to the army, while Yang Min-hyuk (양민혁) made comments to the effect that he played solely looking forward to the reward of military exemption. Park Seung-soo (박승수) also faced public criticism for posting a photo of himself wearing a military discharge cap in the locker room.

Ministry of National Defense "Expresses Regret... Plans to Reduce or Abolish Military Exemptions"

As controversy mounted, the Ministry of National Defense expressed regret over the players' conduct and remarks, stating it would include plans to reduce or abolish the military exemption system for arts and sports in the Basic Defense Reform Plan.

Ministry of National Defense spokesperson Jeong Bit-na (정빛나) emphasized the sacredness and fairness of military duty during a briefing.

일부 스포츠계에서 나온 언행에 대해서 국방부 입장에서 굉장히 유감스럽게 생각을 합니다. 병역 의무는 우리 사회 모든 구성원이 함께 존중하고, 숭고하게 생각을 해야 될 신성한 의무입니다.

"From the perspective of the Ministry of National Defense, we feel very regretful about the conduct and remarks made by some in the sports sector. Military duty is a sacred obligation that all members of our society must respect together and hold as noble."

The Ministry of National Defense explained that it is reviewing the reduction or abolition of supplementary service, comprehensively considering fairness and equity among those subject to military duty, the decline in military manpower resources, and public consensus.

Ruling and Opposition Politicians Unanimously Demand Overhaul of System

A consensus is also forming among ruling and opposition lawmakers on the National Defense Committee of the National Assembly regarding the abolition of exemptions for arts and sports.

People Power Party Representative Yu Yong-won (유용원) cited the case of BTS in a broadcast interview, arguing that the issue of abolishing special exemptions should be actively reviewed.

형평성 면에서도 그렇고, 이번 일을 계기로 해서 예체능 특례 폐지 문제에 대해서 저는 적극적으로 검토할 필요가 있다고 생각을 하고...

"Both in terms of fairness, and taking this incident as an opportunity, I believe there is a need to actively review the issue of abolishing special exemptions for arts and sports..."

People Power Party senior spokesperson Park Sung-hoon (박성훈) also pointed out in a commentary that representing the national team is not a means to avoid military service.

종목별 경쟁 수준과 국제적 위상 등을 종합해 기준을 재설계하고, 제도를 유지한다면 그에 상응하는 복무와 공적 책임도 강화해야 합니다.

"We must redesign the standards by comprehensively considering the level of competition and international standing by discipline, and if the system is maintained, corresponding service and public responsibility must also be strengthened."

Opposition lawmakers also stated that the criteria and fairness are ambiguous and that the time has come to abolish or significantly reduce the system, drawing attention to whether this will lead to actual institutional reform.
