KBO League Faces Unprecedented Heatwave Suspension, Discussions Underway for Schedule Adjustments and Tiebreakers

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: KBS News · [이슈] 역대급 폭염에 사상 초유 프로야구 중단…내년부터 확 바뀐다/개막일 앞당기고 한여름에 2주 휴식…한국시리즈는 11월에?/2026년 8월 10일(월)/KBS. Summarised from that video.

Due to a record-breaking heatwave, KBO Pro Baseball decided on an unprecedented full league suspension. As the risk of heat-related illnesses among spectators and players grew, the Korea Baseball Organization (KBO) held an emergency meeting to devise measures for spectator and team safety, as well as plans to adjust future league schedules.

Heatwave with Turf Temperatures Exceeding 50 Degrees... Spectators Collapse and Games Canceled

Incidents occurred across nationwide ballparks, including Incheon SSG Landers Field, where spectators collapsed from cardiac arrest and heat-related illnesses. Sweltering heat persisted as turf temperatures at stadiums surpassed 51 degrees, seating area temperatures at Daejeon Hanwha Life Eagles Park exceeded 75.7 degrees, and ground temperatures rose above 60 degrees. Consequently, KBO made the decision to cancel all weekend games for the first time in history.

KBO Secretary General Park Keun-chan explained the background behind the decision to suspend the league as follows:

최우선으로 고려해야 할 것이 향후 일정보다는 안전을 최우선으로 고려해야 한다는 그런 의견으로 결정하게 되었습니다.

"The decision was made based on the consensus that safety must be considered as the top priority over the future schedule."

Delaying Game Start Times and Considering Midsummer Break

Following the resumption of play, KBO decided to delay game start times by 30 minutes compared to before and significantly expand break times during games. In addition, to prepare for heatwaves that intensify each year, the league is strongly considering introducing a midsummer break of about two weeks starting in late July next year. To accommodate this, options such as shifting the All-Star break or moving Opening Day forward by about a week are being discussed.

Limitations in Discussions on Tiebreakers and Reducing Game Numbers

Some point out that extra-inning tiebreakers should be introduced like in international tournaments or Major League Baseball to shorten game times and ease players' physical burdens. However, its implementation fell through due to opposition from some KBO League managers, driven by concerns over criticism of managerial tactics in the event of a team loss.

Meanwhile, the prevailing analysis is that proposals to reduce the number of season games lack realism. If the number of games decreases, club revenues from TV broadcasting rights, gate receipts, and advertising revenue would drop significantly, involving complex interests such as linking player salaries accordingly.

Ripple Effects of Heatwave Rest on Teams

Clubs facing the forced break are taking it as an opportunity to reset physically. Manager Lee Kang-chul of the KT Wiz, which had been on an upward trend, stated his position as follows:

저희 선발투수들도 많이 좀 힘들었고, 저희는 또 고참들이 많이 끌어가는 팀이기 때문에 긍정적으로 생각하고 있습니다. 좋은 휴식이라고.

"Our starting pitchers were also having a really tough time, and since our team is largely driven by veteran players, we view this positively as a good rest."

Other clubs with high reliance on key starters also anticipate that the heatwave break will serve as an opportunity to restore players' physical strength ahead of the second-half pennant race.
