Federal Court Bars Warrantless ICE Arrests as Key US Issues Unfold

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: KBS America · "미국 불법 체류자 단속 강화!" ...한국 국적자 429명 추방 / 2026.09.18. Summarised from that video.

A federal district court in Los Angeles has put a check on the Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) practice of arresting undocumented immigrants without a warrant in Southern California. Civil rights groups welcomed the court's preliminary injunction and urged an end to warrantless crackdowns on undocumented immigrants.

Federal Court Issues Injunction Banning Warrantless ICE Arrests

The LA federal court issued a preliminary injunction ordering ICE to immediately halt its practice of arresting undocumented immigrants without a warrant. The court noted that conducting arrest operations without a warrant could violate the law and that it cannot be assumed that targets would flee before a warrant is issued.

The injunction takes effect immediately across seven counties within the federal court jurisdiction of Southern California, including Los Angeles, Orange, and Riverside. Local civil rights organizations stated that they strongly support the court's ruling targeting warrantless enforcement operations conducted without due process.

Tightened Crackdowns on Undocumented Immigrants... 429 South Koreans Deported

According to data released by Stop AAPI Hate, a non-profit organization responding to anti-Asian hate crimes, a total of 528 South Korean undocumented immigrants have been detained since the launch of the second Trump administration. Among them, 488 were placed in detention facilities, and 429 were confirmed to have undergone actual deportation proceedings.

By nationality, India (5,235), China (4,681), and Vietnam (2,019) topped the detention numbers, with South Korea ranking seventh. Stop AAPI Hate analyzed that the intensity of crackdowns on Asian undocumented immigrants surged more than fivefold compared to the Biden administration.

Early Voting Begins for 2026 Midterm Elections; Non-Citizens Indicted for Voting

Ahead of the midterm elections that will determine control of the U.S. Congress, early voting has begun in several regions, including Virginia. The election will select all seats in the U.S. House of Representatives and a portion of the Senate, and the results are expected to have a decisive impact on the future direction of state affairs.

Meanwhile, the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Northern District of Texas formally indicted seven individuals who participated in voting as non-citizens, such as permanent residents, or falsely claimed citizenship. Prosecutors stated that evidence of illegal voting was confirmed during an intensive investigation conducted in preparation for the midterm elections.

U.S.-South Korea Foreign Ministers' Meeting and Economic and Livelihood Trends

Foreign Minister Cho Hyun, visiting the United States, met with U.S. Secretary of State Rubio to discuss pending issues in the South Korea-U.S. alliance and the situation on the Korean Peninsula. Minister Cho conveyed the South Korean government's willingness to contribute to restoring navigation in the Strait of Hormuz and expressed active support for resuming U.S.-North Korea dialogue.

In the economic and livelihood sectors, the LA County sales tax will increase by 0.5 percentage points starting on the 1st of next month, adjusting the sales tax rate in the City of Los Angeles to 10.25%. The average gas price in LA County reached $6.165 per gallon, continuing an upward trend for 31 consecutive days.
