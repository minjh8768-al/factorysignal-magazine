National Assembly Passes Special Counsel Bill on Local Election Ballot Shortage, Extends Parliamentary Investigation by 30 Days

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 전주MBC News · [속보] ‘선관위 특검법’ 국회 본회의 통과, 반대 2인 누군지 살펴봤더니.... Summarised from that video.

The National Assembly has passed both a special counsel bill to uncover the truth behind the shortage of ballot papers in the 9th nationwide local elections and a motion to extend the activity period of the special committee for parliamentary investigation.

Special Counsel Bill on Local Election Ballot Shortage Passed

The National Assembly held a plenary session and passed an alternative proposal for the bill on the appointment of a special counsel to investigate allegations of suffrage infringement, such as the shortage of ballot papers in the 9th nationwide local elections, with 226 votes in favor and 2 against out of 228 members present.

Representative Kim Nam-hee (김남희), who presented the proposal on behalf of the Legislation and Judiciary Committee, explained the purpose, scope of investigation, and scale of the special counsel bill.

선거관리 부실 사태와 관련한 직무유기, 직권남용, 은닉·음폐 의혹 등 선거관리 전반의 위법 행위 전모를 신속하고 철저하게 수사함으로서 훼손된 국민의 참정권을 회복하고 선거관리 시스템의 근본적 혁신을 도모하고자 하는 것입니다.

"This is intended to quickly and thoroughly investigate the full extent of illegal acts across election management, including allegations of dereliction of duty, abuse of authority, and concealment or cover-up related to poor election management, thereby restoring the damaged suffrage of the public and pursuing fundamental innovation of the election management system."

According to the bill, the special counsel team will consist of one special counsel, five assistant special counsels, up to 20 dispatched prosecutors, up to 70 special investigators, and up to 70 dispatched public officials. The investigation period can last up to 170 days, including a 20-day preparation period, a 90-day basic investigation period, a 30-day extension, and a second 30-day extension.

30-Day Extension of Parliamentary Investigation Special Committee Activity Period

The National Assembly subsequently passed the motion to extend the activity period of the special committee for parliamentary investigation with unanimous approval from all 225 members present.

Yoon Sang-hyun, chairman of the special committee for parliamentary investigation, pointed out that extending the activity period was necessary to complete in-depth investigations, including on-site verifications and additional questioning.

현장 검증, 관련자 추가 조사 등을 통한 진상 규명 및 실효적인 선관위 개혁 방안 마련을 위하여 우리 위원회의 활동 기간을 2026년 8월 31일까지 30일간 연장하려는 것입니다.

"We intend to extend our committee's activity period by 30 days until August 31, 2026, in order to uncover the truth through on-site verifications and additional investigations of relevant parties, and to establish effective reform measures for the Election Commission."

Following the passage of this motion, the activity period of the special committee for parliamentary investigation was extended by 30 days until August 31, 2026, allowing the investigation to continue.
