President Lee Says 'Time to Act on Preamble Inclusion of Buma and May 18... Government Must Focus Capabilities on Livelihoods and Safety'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 전주MBC News · “이거 아닌데??” 노트북 보더니 이 대통령 멈칫. Summarised from that video.

The 49th Senior Secretariat Meeting was held at Yeomin-gwan in the Blue House. Ahead of the meeting, newly appointed Chief of Staff Lim Kwang-hyun delivered opening remarks, expressing his intention to assist the president to improve the lives of the public.

47th Anniversary of Buma Democratic Uprising and Constitutional Preamble Inclusion

In his opening remarks, President Lee Jae-myung commended the courage of the citizens of Busan and Masan who defended democracy, human rights, and freedom of the press on the occasion of the 47th anniversary of the Buma Democratic Uprising. President Lee emphasized that the Buma Democratic Uprising and the May 18 Democratization Movement are the history supporting the Democratic Republic of Korea.

부마민주항쟁과 5·18 민주화운동을 헌법 전문에 새기자는 점에 대해서는 여와 야를 가릴 것 없이 모두가 일치된 의견을 가지고 있습니다. 이제는 말이 아니라 행동으로 실천할 때입니다.

"Both the ruling and opposition parties hold a unanimous opinion on etching the Buma Democratic Uprising and May 18 Democratization Movement into the preamble of the Constitution. Now is the time to act, not just speak."

State Administration Marking 500 Days in Office and Three Key Livelihood Policies

Ahead of the government's 500th day in office, President Lee urged officials to attend to the will of the people with humility at the crossroads of a great transformation. He assessed that while economic indicators project per capita national income to surpass $40,000—making South Korea the sixth country in the world with a population of over 50 million to do so—the perceived recovery of public livelihoods remains slow.

Accordingly, he stated that state capabilities would be focused on three key livelihood tasks: housing, jobs, and finance. He instructed the acceleration of life-cycle housing supply, private and public job creation projects, as well as policy fund supplies and tailored debt restructuring in response to interest rate hikes.

Response to Relational and Indiscriminate Crimes and Establishment of Joint Government Body

President Lee called for a severe sense of crisis regarding the series of recent relational crimes and indiscriminate violent crimes such as knife attacks. Pointing out that a significant portion of the victims are vulnerable individuals including women, he instructed the formulation of extraordinary measures with the mindset of declaring war.

전담할 범정부 합동대응기구 같은 걸 만들어서 기존 대책이 혹시 어떤 빈틈이 없었는지부터 면밀하게 점검해야 되겠습니다. 피해자 보호장비가 제대로 작동하는지 또 전자발찌 착용자 등 범죄 유발 고위험군에 대한 관리가 현장에서 제대로 이루어지고 있는지도 봐야겠습니다.

"We must create something like a dedicated joint government response body and closely inspect whether there were any loopholes in existing measures. We need to check whether victim protection equipment works properly and whether management of high-risk groups that incite crime, such as electronic ankle monitor wearers, is being conducted properly in the field."

Call for All-Out Response to Secure Public Safety

Based on a thorough assessment of the situation, he requested the preparation of tailored preventive measures according to risk levels and effective victim protection plans. In addition, he instructed a major reinforcement of field response personnel, organization, and equipment, including the police, to prepare for emergency situations.

President Lee concluded his remarks by reiterating that not even a minor flaw can be permitted when it comes to protecting the lives and safety of the public, calling for the full mobilization of all government means to establish a thorough crime prevention and rapid field response system.
