"Democracy Cannot Be Held Hostage": Ukraine's 'Drone Hero' Clashes With Zelenskyy

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: KBS 세계는지금 · 우크라이나 집안 싸움, 젤렌스키 맞선 드론 영웅 (KBS_465회_2026.08.29.방송). Summarised from that video.

At Ukraine's Independence Day event, a large array of advanced unmanned assets was unveiled, including combat robots, unmanned surface drones, and large drone formations. At the center of the unmanned weapon systems that have become a key force for the Ukrainian military amid the ongoing Russia-Ukraine war was former Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov, known as the "drone hero."

Dismissal of the '30-Something Minister' Who Led Drone Weaponization

Fedorov, who was appointed defense minister at the age of 35 after serving as minister of digital transformation, led the structural transition toward a high-tech unmanned military, increasing Ukraine's drone production more than 100-fold compared to the previous year. The number of drone manufacturers rose from just seven before the war to more than 500, and the world's first unit dedicated to unmanned aerial vehicles was established. The former minister emphasized the need for military system reform as follows:

Проте сьогодні не можна воювати новими технологіями зі старою організаційною структурою. Необхідні комплексні зміни.

"We cannot fight with new technologies under an old organizational structure today. Comprehensive changes are necessary."

However, following conflicts with military leadership, he was dismissed from his post as defense minister just six months after taking office. Angered by this, thousands of citizens gathered in the streets of the capital, Kyiv, demonstrating to demand Fedorov's reinstatement. Rally participant Bohdan Hurak criticized the move, saying, "Fedorov is someone who demonstrated results on the battlefield, and forcing him out after just six months makes no sense."

Calling Out Wartime Corruption and Demanding Wartime Presidential Elections

After his dismissal, former Minister Fedorov took a stand against the administration by publicly calling out allegations of illegal money laundering and corruption involving President Zelenskyy's closest confidants, including the former chief of staff to the president's office. Former Minister Fedorov mentioned the dangers of wartime corruption:

Ми повинні говорити і про корупцію. Під час війни вкрадені гроші можуть означати дрон, який не купили, боєприпас, який не доїхав, систему РЕБ, якої не було. Тому корупція під час війни - це не просто фінансова проблема, це питання нашої здатності вижити і перемогти.

"We must also speak about corruption. During war, stolen money can mean a drone not purchased, ammunition that did not arrive, or an EW system that was missing. Therefore, corruption during wartime is not merely a financial problem; it is a matter of our ability to survive and win."

In addition, former Minister Fedorov pointed out that President Zelenskyy, whose term ended in May 2024, was not holding elections citing martial law during wartime, and demanded wartime presidential elections.

Тому ми повинні знайти законний, безпечний і реалістичний механізм, який дозволить Україні відновити повноцінний демократичний процес навіть за умов тривалої війни. Демократія не може бути заручницею Росії.

"Therefore, we must find a legal, safe, and realistic mechanism that allows Ukraine to restore a full democratic process even under conditions of a prolonged war. Democracy cannot be held hostage to Russia."

On the other hand, President Zelenskyy rejected holding wartime elections, stating at a press conference that "holding elections right now would be a tsunami that divides Ukraine." However, in a trust survey of political figures, former Minister Fedorov ranked second at 26.8%, while President Zelenskyy stood in sixth place at 18.0%, raising analysis that political checking was at play.

Western Military Support and Escalation Warnings

Amid political turmoil inside Ukraine, Western military support continues. Britain's new Prime Minister Burnham visited Ukraine as his first overseas trip destination and decided to share design information for British-made key components of the Storm Shadow cruise missile. Experts assessed that this decision paved the way for Ukraine to build its own long-range missile production capacity.

A sense of security crisis is heightening in Eastern Europe and the Baltic region. As the Russian military increased large-scale military exercises around the Baltic Sea and Russian drones and missiles were frequently detected in Polish and Romanian airspace, vigilance among NATO member states soared to its highest level.

Secret U.S. Visit and Russia's Backlash

U.S. Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) Director John Ratcliffe made a top-secret visit to Moscow and met with leadership of Russian intelligence agencies. Foreign media reported that Director Ratcliffe warned the Russian side not to escalate tensions against the Baltic states.

Inside Russia, rumors of a large-scale additional conscription spread, causing crowds of people attempting to flee to the Georgian border, while President Putin and hardliners even mentioned the possibility of using tactical nuclear weapons. In response, Western countries mobilized their entire satellite surveillance network to monitor Russia's movements while issuing strong warnings that any use of nuclear weapons would be met with a catastrophic military response.
