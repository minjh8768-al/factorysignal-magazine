Bank of Korea Rate Hike Nears End of Cycle; Analysts Recommend Increasing Short-Term Bonds

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 김영익의 경제스쿨 · [속보] 기준금리 3.0% 인상, 시장은 이미 움직였습니다 | 김영익 교수. Summarised from that video.

As the Bank of Korea raised its benchmark interest rate from 2.75% to 3.00%, an analysis suggested that the rate-hike cycle has effectively entered its final phase. Observers noted that market interest rates are proactively declining, reflecting the potential for a future economic slowdown.

Rate-Hike Cycle Nears End, Market Rates Expected to Fall Ahead of Time

Professor Kim Young-ik assessed the Bank of Korea's latest benchmark rate hike as a measure reflecting inflationary pressures and higher-than-expected economic growth. However, he analyzed that with this hike, the rate-increase stance is drawing to a close, and market interest rates are highly likely to drop preemptively.

제가 생각하기에는 기준금리 인상 사이클이 거의 마무리되지 않았느냐, 미리서 시장금리는 하락할 것이다, 이렇게 보고 있습니다.

"In my view, the benchmark rate-hike cycle is nearly finished, and market interest rates will decline in advance."

According to the future rate projections of the Bank of Korea's Monetary Policy Board members, a majority held the view that rates could be raised one more time within six months. However, Professor Kim determined that considering economic trends and appropriate interest rate levels, there is little room for an actual further increase.

Concerns Over Downside Risks Despite Upward Growth Revision

The Bank of Korea revised its annual economic growth forecast upward and projected a significant expansion in the current account surplus. Analysis indicated that strong exports in major items, such as semiconductors, are driving growth.

However, Professor Kim pointed out that downside economic risks, such as concerns over overinvestment in artificial intelligence and increased financial market volatility, are more significant. He emphasized that the economic growth rate could peak in the first half of the year and gradually decline, warning that preparations must be made for the possibility of slowing growth.

Market Rate Trends and Asset Allocation Strategy

Key market interest rates, such as the 3-year Treasury bond yield, tend to move ahead of the benchmark interest rate. Professor Kim explained that market rates have already priced in the possibility of rate hikes by rising first and are now showing a slight downward trend.

In addition, he cited the possibility of a stock market adjustment as an impending economic slowdown becomes visible, advising investors to adjust their asset allocation.

자산배분 측면에서는 아직도 주식 비중이 아주 높다면 이러한 반동 국면에서 좀 줄이시고 단기 채권을 늘려가시는 게 좋은 자산배분이 아닐까 이렇게 생각하고 있습니다.

"In terms of asset allocation, if your stock allocation is still very high, I think a good allocation strategy would be to reduce it somewhat during this rebound phase and gradually increase short-term bonds."

Professor Kim advised that while a stock rebound driven by falling interest rates may continue for the time being, a strategy of reducing stock holdings and increasing stable assets like short-term bonds will be advantageous when economic slowdown pressures build up.
