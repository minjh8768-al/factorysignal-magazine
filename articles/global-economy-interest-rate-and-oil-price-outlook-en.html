Kim Young-ik Warns of Q4 Stock Correction and AI Bubble Risk Amid Rising Oil Prices and High Interest Rates

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 김영익의 경제스쿨 · 다음주 시장이 열리기 전 꼭 확인하세요 | 김영익 교수. Summarised from that video.

As financial market volatility expands with recent international oil prices surpassing $100 per barrel and the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield surging, an analysis and outlook on major economic indicators, interest rates, foreign exchange, and stock markets have been presented.

Declining OECD Leading Indicators and Forecast of Slowing Export Growth

With the OECD and G20 leading indicators peaking and showing a downward trend, forecasts suggest that future export growth could slow down. Analysis indicates that South Korea's leading indicator is also highly likely to fall after forming a peak, creating the possibility of a correction for the KOSPI index as well.

In terms of export performance, semiconductors account for a very high proportion of total exports and are driving growth, but compared to the daily average export value, the KOSPI is evaluated as still being in overvalued territory.

Shock from U.S. Inflation Data and FOMC Rate Outlook

The U.S. Producer Price Index (PPI) for August shocked financial markets as it was announced at 5.4%, exceeding the market expectation of 5.3%. Consequently, strong upward pressure acted on interest rates, with the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield surging to 4.97%.

Regarding the upcoming Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting, whether to raise the benchmark interest rate has emerged as a key issue, but the majority of major investment banks view a rate freeze as highly likely.

FOMC에서 금리를 동결할 것인가 인상할 것인가 가장 큰 이슈입니다.

"Whether the FOMC will freeze or raise interest rates is the biggest issue."

Impact of Rising Oil Prices and Stock and Foreign Exchange Outlook

Rising international oil prices induce inflation in the short term, but with a time lag, they bring about the effect of lowering real GDP growth rates. This slowdown in economic growth was analyzed as a factor that will drive interest rates down in the future.

Regarding the KRW/USD exchange rate, a short-term rebound is mentioned after dropping sharply from the 1,550 won level to around 1,340 won, but over the medium to long term, the trend of a weaker dollar and stronger won is expected to persist.

우선 물가 상승 효과가 미리 반영되고요, 조만간 실질 GDP 성장률 하락 효과가 금리에 점차 작용하리라고 보고 있습니다.

"First, the inflation effect is reflected in advance, and I expect the effect of declining real GDP growth to gradually act on interest rates soon."

Prolonged High Interest Rates and Concerns Over AI Bubble Burst

If the high interest rate stance continues, household consumption will inevitably shrink and the burden of investment on companies will grow. In particular, if U.S. Treasury yields remain high, financing costs for risk assets and some AI companies could surge, raising the possibility that investments will decrease significantly.

Accordingly, he suggested that stock prices could undergo a correction as early as the fourth quarter, and as there is a risk that the AI bubble in the stock market could partially burst in the first half of next year, close attention must continuously be paid to U.S. 10-year Treasury yields and corporate financing trends.
