"Wall Street Dominance Shifts from Commercial Banks to Asset Managers": Inside the U.S. Sovereign Wealth Fund and Tokenisation Strategy

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 30조 달러 거물들의 역대급 공모: 코인 시장 판도를 통째로 바꿀 법안의 비밀 [김창익 대표]. Summarised from that video.

A battle for dominance in crypto between giants of traditional finance—commercial banks and massive asset managers—is shaping new capital flows on Wall Street. Appearing on Seoul Economic TV's "Coin One Shot," author Kim Chang-ik (김창익) highlighted differences in crypto strategies between traditional commercial bank JPMorgan and BlackRock, which is seeking to expand into real-world asset (RWA) tokenisation and DeFi, analysing structural changes in U.S. capital markets.

Divergent Positions of Commercial Banks and Asset Managers on Crypto Legislation

In recent U.S. legislative discussions, a clash of opinions between the commercial bank and asset manager camps has surfaced. One of the key issues is third-party interest payments and activity rewards. Commercial banks worry that if interest payments on crypto or stablecoin custody are permitted, deposit funds will rapidly drain into the DeFi market.

On the other hand, giant asset managers such as BlackRock, Fidelity, and Vanguard, alongside investment banks like Goldman Sachs, have taken an opposing stance by issuing statements urging the passage of the legislation. For asset managers, regulatory clarity must be secured before they can roll out their RWA businesses in earnest and expand fee revenue models.

제3자 이자 지급이 금지되면 수익률을 좇는 자금이 자산운용사의 RWA 상품으로 흘러들어갈 가능성이 커지기 때문에 자산운용사에게는 호재가 된다.

"If third-party interest payments are banned, funds chasing yield are more likely to flow into asset managers' RWA products, making it a positive development for asset managers."

Limits of the Loan-to-Deposit Margin Model and Wall Street's Shift in Hegemony

Author Kim Chang-ik pointed out that behind this conflict lies a fundamental difference in revenue models between commercial banks and asset managers. Commercial banks rely on net interest margin—the difference between deposit and lending rates—as their primary income, whereas asset managers earn fee income by managing client assets under custody in funds or derivatives.

As the assets under management by major asset managers like BlackRock, Fidelity, and Vanguard exceed 30 trillion dollars, Wall Street's leadership is rapidly shifting from traditional major commercial banks to the asset manager camp. Consequently, it has become difficult for political circles to accommodate only the voices of traditional commercial banks.

The U.S. Administration's Debt Problem and Asset Tokenisation Strategy

As U.S. government debt approaches nearly 40 trillion dollars, the conventional method of issuing massive amounts of Treasury bonds and selling them to commercial banks has reached its limits. As a result, the U.S. administration is also shifting away from simple credit-based financing toward a strategy of managing and multiplying its owned assets.

Key elements of the sovereign wealth fund and tokenisation strategy envisioned by the U.S. government are as follows:

  • Diversification of Collateral Assets: Incorporating key assets into the Treasury basket, such as government-owned real estate, gold, Bitcoin reserves, AI computing power, and semiconductor equity.
  • Liquification via Financial Engineering: Tokenising assets and selling them to global investors without fully transferring ownership.
  • Issuance of Collateral-Backed Bonds: Building a virtuous fiscal cycle by issuing Treasuries and bonds backed by expanded assets.

Kim explained that the U.S. government's asset expansion scheme aligns intricately with BlackRock's RWA business plans.

기존에는 달러를 찍어 국채를 사주는 구조로 재정을 충당했지만, 이제는 소유권을 유지한 채 금융공학으로 유동화된 토큰을 팔아 자산을 불려야 하는 시점이다.

"In the past, fiscal needs were met through a structure of printing dollars to buy Treasuries, but now is the time to expand assets by selling tokens liquidity-enabled through financial engineering while retaining ownership."

Future Outlook and Implications

Ultimately, the passage of relevant regulatory legislation is essential for the RWA market to mature and for the U.S. administration's sovereign wealth fund vision to take off. Kim predicted that because political hegemony and capital flow controls have already shifted toward asset managers, positions within political circles will change, accelerating financial restructuring centred on RWA.
