White House Press Secretary Ousted After Four Days as Security Threats and Political Tensions Rise Ahead of US Midterms

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: KBS America · 잠긴 문 부수고 난사...학교·주택가 연쇄 총격 '충격' / 2026.10.16. Summarised from that video.

As political conflicts and security threats surge ahead of the US midterm elections in November, political instability is heightening amidst the firing of the White House press secretary and threatening letters sent to election-related agencies.

New White House Press Secretary Dismissed After Four Days Amid 'Falsified Resume' Controversy

The new White House press secretary was abruptly dismissed just four days after taking office. The decision followed allegations raised immediately after her nomination that her claimed background as a lawyer was false, alongside controversy over past interview remarks derogatory toward China that drew an official rebuke from the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

US President Donald Trump reportedly moved quickly to dismiss her due to concerns over potential negative impacts on the midterm elections. Speaking to reporters, President Trump said:

I said to her, honestly, if I were you, I'd reconsider. You think about it, but I would reconsider. She's got a beautiful husband and family.

"I told her honestly, 'If I were you, I'd reconsider. Think about it, but I would reconsider.' She has a wonderful husband and family."

MAGA Faction Spreads Election Fraud Claims and Demands Martial Law

Claims that the midterm elections will be rigged are spreading among hardline MAGA supporters, a key Republican constituency, and far-right activists. Citing security vulnerabilities in electronic voting systems and the potential for widespread election fraud, some election deniers have called for declaring martial law to halt machine counting and deploy the military to conduct manual hand counts.

Experts analyzed that because the constitution assigns authority over election administration to state governments, it is legally impossible for the president to alter voting methods through martial law or deploy federal troops, with military intervention in elections also strictly prohibited. Consequently, critics point out that the MAGA faction's demands are nothing more than unfeasible political demagoguery.

'White Powder' Letters Delivered to Election Commissions... FBI Launches Full Investigation

Security threats targeting election facilities are also materializing. Around 100 suspicious letters containing unidentified white powder have been delivered or intercepted at election commissions and lawmakers' offices across the country, including in Oklahoma and Louisiana.

Immediately following the incidents, the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the US Postal Inspection Service launched a full-scale investigation to trace distribution routes and identify those behind the letters.

Internal Conflicts Deepen in Both Parties... Calls for Generational Change and Distancing from Trump

As the midterms approach, both the Democratic and Republican parties face internal disputes and confusion over campaign strategy. Within the Democratic Party, calls for leadership generational change are growing stronger, while disciplinary conflicts have erupted between leadership and hardline progressives over cooperation with the Trump administration during budget negotiations.

Meanwhile, in the Republican Party, candidates in key battleground races are noticeably distancing themselves from President Trump. To win over moderate voters, battleground Republican candidates are attempting policy differentiation from President Trump, and some candidates have even been confirmed to have removed President Trump's photos and endorsement statements from their campaign websites.
