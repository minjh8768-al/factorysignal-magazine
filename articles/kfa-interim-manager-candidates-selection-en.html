KFA Narrows Down Temporary National Team Manager Candidates: Poyet Out, Torrent, Casas, and Moreno Advance to Interviews

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 풋볼 포레스트 - Forrest Football · [속보] 한국 감독 서류합격 면접 대상자 3명 공개됐다. 서류 탈락한 포옛 제친 감독들은 누구? 전 스페인 국대 감독, 과르디올라 수석코치, 이라크 국대 감독 등장. Summarised from that video.

As the Korea Football Association progresses with an open recruitment process to appoint a temporary national team manager, the shortlist of candidates who passed document screening and face upcoming interviews has emerged. Former Jeonbuk Hyundai manager Gus Poyet, who had initially shown high interest in managing the Korean national team and was considered a strong candidate, was eliminated during the document review process.

Five Candidates Advance to Interviews Amid Poyet's Elimination... Three Spanish Coaches Emerge

The Korea Football Association issued an open recruitment notice for a temporary manager and coaching staff to lead the national team during the A-match windows from September to November and accepted applications. A total of five candidates passed document screening to advance to the interview stage, with three individuals clearly identified through media reports: Domènec Torrent, Jesús Casas, and Robert Moreno.

Coincidentally, all three identified strong candidates hold Spanish nationality and share the common background of having gained coaching experience at prestigious club FC Barcelona. However, distinct differences exist in the coaching careers, strengths, and weaknesses of each candidate.

Domènec Torrent, Former Member of Guardiola's Staff

Domènec Torrent is known as a tactician who assisted manager Pep Guardiola for a long period. Starting at FC Barcelona and continuing through Bayern Munich and Manchester City, he served as Guardiola's assistant coach for over 10 years, experiencing build-up and possession-oriented football on the front lines.

However, question marks remain over his achievements as an independent head coach. Although he managed New York City, Flamengo, Galatasaray, and Monterrey, he is evaluated as not having achieved firm success as a manager, with his most recent team, Monterrey, falling to 13th place in the league.

Jesús Casas Highlights National Team Management Experience

Jesús Casas is the candidate whose national team management experience stands out the most. After assisting manager Luis Enrique with the Spanish national team, he took charge of the Iraq national team, leading them to victories in the Arabian Gulf Cup and the King's Cup. In particular, he drew attention by securing a victory against the Japanese national team during the last Asian Cup tournament.

However, leading the Iraq national team, he was eliminated after losing to Jordan in the round of 16 of the Asian Cup and was dismissed after failing to deliver results against relatively weaker opponents such as Kuwait and Palestine in the North and Central America World Cup qualifiers. He currently manages Singapore's Lion City Sailors, presenting a situation where buyout issues must be resolved for his appointment.

Robert Moreno Embarks on Second National Team Bid

Robert Moreno is a figure who also applied for the Korean national team manager position during the process of selecting Paulo Bento's successor following the 2022 Qatar World Cup. After serving as a Barcelona coach and assistant coach for the Spanish national team, he led Spain to qualification for Euro 2020 as caretaker and head manager in 2019.

However, his career following his departure from the Spanish national team managerial post was marked by persistent slumps. At AS Monaco, Granada, and PFC Sochi, he was dismissed after short tenures or suffered poor performance, failing to establish his standing as a head manager.

Expectations for Advanced Football Philosophy Amid the Challenge of Verifying Tangible Results

The three Spanish managers who made the interview shortlist possess the advantage of being able to implant an advanced football system and philosophy into the national team. However, evaluations coexist that their independent achievements and practical capabilities as head managers leave something to be desired compared to their illustrious credentials as assistant coaches.

Football fans are focusing their attention on which leader the Korea Football Association will ultimately appoint as temporary manager following the interview process.
