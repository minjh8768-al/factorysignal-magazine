Examining the Possibility of KOSPI Hitting 8,000: 'Reachable by Year-End, Barbell Strategy Effective'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 한국경제TV · 지금은 반도체 반, 고금리주 반 "이렇게 버티세요"ㅣ김영환 NH투자증권 수석연구원. Summarised from that video.

Stock Market Volatility Persists Amid High Oil Price and High Interest Rate Concerns

Volatility in domestic and international stock markets continues due to rising international oil prices and interest rate anxiety driven by the escalating Middle East war. In the KOSPI market, while institutional investors have recently increased buying, retail investors' selling streak continues, capping the upper limit of the index.

Kim Young-hwan, senior research analyst at NH Investment & Securities, forecast that while oil prices are temporarily rebounding after peaking, they are unlikely to lead to a prolonged surge. He assessed that if oil prices remain around $100, pressure on the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates will persist, acting as downward pressure on the stock market.

Semiconductor Stocks Expected to Re-emerge Amid AI Theme Sector Rotation

Within the stock market, sector rotation across themes such as the AI value chain, optical communications, robotics, and secondary batteries is actively taking place. In particular, remarks by the U.S. Treasury Secretary supporting AI investment and news of infrastructure investments by global big tech companies drove up related stock prices.

Senior Research Analyst Kim Young-hwan analyzed that leading stocks within the AI industry continue to rotate from big tech to software, and back to semiconductors.

우리는 AI 개발을 멈출 수 없습니다. 중국은 멈추지 않을 것이기 때문입니다.

"We cannot stop AI development. Because China will not stop."

Analyst Kim projected that if the earnings sustainability of semiconductor companies is confirmed through the third-quarter earnings season, buying of semiconductor stocks could strengthen again.

Possibility of KOSPI Reaching 8,000 Level by Year-End and Market Response Strategy

Analysis suggests that although KOSPI is hitting a supply wall in the short term, upside potential remains through the end of the year. While the mid-7,000 level, where retail buying was concentrated last June and July, is acting as a resistance level, the view is that an upward breakthrough is possible if earnings momentum recovers alongside the easing of geopolitical risks.

Senior Research Analyst Kim Young-hwan recommended that holding both growth stocks and interest rate beneficiaries is an effective response at this point.

반도체주와 고유가·고금리 수혜주를 반반씩 가져가는 바벨 전략이 적당한 시기라고 생각합니다.

"I think this is an appropriate time for a barbell strategy of holding half semiconductor stocks and half high oil price and high interest rate beneficiaries."

Experts advised that if geopolitical uncertainty resolves after the U.S. midterm elections and a joint strengthening trend of the won and yen settles in, foreign capital inflows could expand, strengthening the market rebound momentum.
