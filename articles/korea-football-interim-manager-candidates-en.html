Five Candidates Shortlisted for Interim Manager of Korean National Football Team as Gus Poyet Misses Cut

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 서형욱의 뽈리TV · [명단취재] '포옛 탈락' 대표팀 임시감독, 최종 면접 대상자는? 명장들의 '오른팔들' 눈길!. Summarised from that video.

Background on Gus Poyet’s Elimination in Document Screening

Gus Poyet, who was considered a strong candidate in the open recruitment process for the interim manager of the South Korea national football team, was eliminated during the document screening stage and excluded from the final interview list. This was the result of a relative evaluation process that assessed not only the individual manager's career, but also the composition and experience of the accompanying coaching staff division.

According to the Korea Football Association announcement, the selection process required applications to be submitted as a division team of at least five and up to seven members, including one head coach and coaching staff. During this process, factors such as insufficient proof of individual coaching experience or qualification requirements relative to evaluation criteria affected the overall score.

Three Spanish Managers Selected as Final Interview Candidates

A total of five managers passed the document screening to advance to the final interview stage, all of whom are foreign coaches. Among them, three Spanish managers were included.

  • Roberto Moreno: Served as caretaker manager of the Spain national team in 2019, leading them to the Euro finals, and reportedly received the highest praise in the document screening evaluation.
  • Jesús Casas: A former Barcelona analyst and Spain national team coach who previously managed the Iraq national team and currently serves as the head coach of Lion City Sailors in Singapore.
  • Domènec Torrent: Worked as assistant manager to Pep Guardiola for 11 years and managed teams including New York City FC, Flamengo, and Galatasaray.

Profiles of Final Candidates Including Koeman and Stojković

In addition to the Spanish managers, coaches from the Netherlands and Serbia joined the pool of final interview candidates.

Dutch manager Erwin Koeman, the older brother of Ronald Koeman, has served as assistant manager of the Netherlands national team and head coach of Feyenoord and the Hungary national team. Serbian football legend and former football association president Dragan Stojković possesses rich Asian football experience, having managed Nagoya Grampus, Guangzhou R&F, and the Serbia national team.

Ensuring Procedural Legitimacy and Future Schedule

To adhere to procedural legitimacy, the Korea Football Association is conducting the selection process in accordance with sports organization guidelines, using an open recruitment method and involving external evaluation committee members.

The association plans to finalize the candidate through a final remote interview process and complete the appointment of the interim manager as soon as possible.
