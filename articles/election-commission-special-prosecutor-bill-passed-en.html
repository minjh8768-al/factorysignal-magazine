National Assembly Passes NEC Special Counsel Bill via Bipartisan Agreement Amid Debate over Dispatched Prosecutors' Powers

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 뉴스TVCHOSUN · [신통방통] '선관위 특검법' 통과에 힘 받는 장동혁?···다시 올공 찾았다. Summarised from that video.

The special counsel bill to investigate various corruption and fraud allegations surrounding the National Election Commission (NEC) has passed the plenary session of the National Assembly through a bipartisan agreement. During the plenary session processing, last-minute negotiations continued over the targets and scope of the investigation, drawing attention to ruling and opposition party interpretations and future ramifications.

Agreement on Investigation Scope and Controversy Over Dispatched Prosecutors' Investigative Authority

Cheon Jun-ho (천준호), senior deputy floor leader of the Democratic Party of Korea, stated that time was delayed as consultations proceeded in the process of dealing with the investigation's targets and scope. With the passage of this special counsel bill, investigations into major targets such as the computer systems and servers of the NEC, an independent agency, have become possible.

However, following the reduction of direct prosecutorial investigative powers, controversy arose regarding the legality surrounding the investigative authority of prosecutors dispatched to the special counsel. Some within the Ministry of Justice and legal academia pointed out the possibility that suspects might raise objections based on unconstitutionality or lack of investigative authority if prosecutors with restricted investigative powers were dispatched to the special counsel to conduct investigations. Accordingly, it is reported that supplementary provisions of this special counsel bill included a clause exceptionally recognizing the investigative authority of dispatched prosecutors.

Ruling Party Leadership and Lawmakers' On-Site Actions

Following the passage of the special counsel bill, party leadership and key figures, including People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyeok and lawmakers Park Dae-chul, Kang Myeong-gu (강명구), Kim Jang-kyom (김장겸), Jeong Hee-yong (정희용), Choi Soo-jin (최수진), Cho Kwang-han (조광한), and Joo Jin-woo, visited Olympic Park to share their thoughts with citizens. Lawmaker Park Dae-chul, who attended the site, stated that they would make efforts so that it becomes a special counsel that the public desires.

Evaluations and Future Tasks by Political Experts

Regarding the passage of this special counsel bill, various evaluations emerged among political circles and broadcast panelists. Lawmaker Park Chung-kwon (박충권) evaluated it as the first case of overcoming seat count limitations under opposition leadership to pass a special counsel bill, saying that the voices of citizens were a great source of strength.

Commentator Seo Yang-ho (서양호) viewed that clarifying vote-counting results and internal NEC issues through investigation rather than outdoor protests served as the momentum for the special counsel agreement. In addition, he analyzed that whether leadership moves toward NEC reform in the future will determine support from moderates. Commentator Kim Ji-ho (김지호) mentioned that attention should be paid to the shift in political patterns that connected the demands of hardline supporters to achievements within the institutional system.
