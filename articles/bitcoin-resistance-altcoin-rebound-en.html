Bitcoin Faces Upper Resistance as Altcoins Hold Rebound Expectations Through Weekend

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 코인왕자 · 오늘 변곡점. Summarised from that video.

As Bitcoin struggles to break above key resistance levels, fostering a short-term wait-and-see stance, analysis suggests the altcoin market is instead strengthening its upward structure.

Continued Attempts to Break Bitcoin's Upper Resistance

Chart analyst Coin Wangja (코인왕자) pointed out that although Bitcoin has repeatedly tested its upper resistance line, it has failed to break above it on a closing basis. On minute charts as well, prices repeatedly pull back upon reaching resistance, amidst a lack of significant volume inflows.

However, he analyzed that because the distance to a resistance breakout is small, a breakout during trading hours this week could lead the entire market.

비트코인이 뚫리면서 이더리움과 알트코인들이 다 따라오게 될 것이다. 단기적으로는 단타 매매자들의 주의가 필요하지만, 중장기 관점에서는 비트코인과 이더리움 모두 올라가는 구조로 되어 있다.

"As Bitcoin breaks through, Ethereum and altcoins will all follow. In the short term, day traders need to be cautious, but from a medium- to long-term perspective, both Bitcoin and Ethereum are structured to go up."

Potential Ethereum Leadership and Altcoin Rebound

Structural differences between Bitcoin and Ethereum were also highlighted. Ethereum has upside potential open, whereas Bitcoin is in a structure that continuously tests upper resistance. If Bitcoin resolves its resistance, Ethereum is expected to become the primary leading asset and strongly drive the market.

The altcoin market was also evaluated as gaining additional momentum from the upward structure formed late last week, maintaining valid expectations for a rebound through the weekend.

Assessment of Volatile Blast Coin

Regarding Blast coin, which plunged around 50% over the past two days, it was noted that despite damage to the short-term chart, selling volume was not large.

주봉과 월봉상 주요 지지 자리가 붕괴되지 않고 지켜졌다는 점에서 5 대 5의 가능성을 보고 있다. 추가 붕괴만 나오지 않는다면 홀딩이나 재공략을 고려해볼 수 있다.

"Given that key support levels on the weekly and monthly charts held without collapsing, I see a 50-50 chance. Unless further collapse occurs, holding or re-entering could be considered."

Overall Assessment and Market Outlook

Although a short-term correction is possible as Bitcoin failed to break resistance on a closing basis, the altcoin market and medium- to long-term positions are still evaluated positively. Accordingly, short-term traders are advised to exercise caution, while medium- to long-term investors are encouraged to respond while gauging market conditions.
