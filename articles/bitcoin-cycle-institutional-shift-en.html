Bitcoin's Changing Cycle: Institutional Capital Reshapes Market Post-Spot ETF Approval

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 박작가의 크립토연구소 · 비트코인 4년 사이클 붕괴? 이번 폭락장이 다른 이유ㅣ박작가의 크립토 연구소. Summarised from that video.

A Changed Bitcoin Cycle… Milder Declines

An analysis shows that the Bitcoin market has entered a new phase, exhibiting relatively milder volatility compared to past bear markets. In previous cycles, the market experienced a steep decline of 70% to 80% from its peak one year after reaching the high, but in the current cycle, the drop from the peak remains around 32%, maintaining a more stable trend than before.

Author Park Jong-han (박종한) identified the approval of spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds (ETFs) as the most decisive turning point in changing the market structure. He explained that since the ETF approval, the proportion of speculative funds centered on retail investors and leverage has decreased, while institutional capital has established itself as the main market player, improving the market's fundamental structure.

Market Outlook from Short-, Medium-, and Long-Term Perspectives

Park suggested that the current cryptocurrency market should be viewed across three timeframes: short-term, medium-term, and long-term.

  • Short-term perspective: There is a possibility of correction driven by macroeconomic factors and liquidity impacts, such as rising long-term US Treasury yields, geopolitical tensions, and oil price volatility.
  • Medium-term perspective: The resolution of regulatory uncertainty, such as the passage of bills in the US Congress, along with sustained ETF net inflows, is expected to serve as a catalyst for expanding institutional funds.
  • Long-term perspective: As the weakening of US dollar purchasing power continues due to US fiscal deficits and debt burdens, Bitcoin may emerge as a major hedge asset for debasement trades.
과거에는 고점 이후 1년 시점에서 최고점 대비 -70~80% 정도를 기록했으나, 이번 사이클은 최고점에서 -32% 수준으로 훨씬 완만한 흐름을 보이고 있다. 투기 자금은 줄어들고 ETF 중심의 기관 자본이 들어오면서 자산 배분 시스템 내에 안착하고 있다.

"In the past, the market recorded drops of about -70% to -80% from the peak at the one-year mark after the high, but this cycle is showing a much milder trend at around -32% from the peak. Speculative capital has decreased, and ETF-centered institutional capital is entering, settling into asset allocation systems."

Response Over Prediction… "Step-by-Step Strategy Needed"

Park emphasized that in a highly volatile market, rather than betting early based on hasty price predictions, investors should respond while confirming whether support holds at key levels. In particular, he noted that it is crucial to verify points where major overhead resistance levels are absorbed and turn into support, as well as checking institutional inflow trends.

He added that investors who already have high returns may consider taking profits if key levels are breached, but for investors with small holdings, correction phases could present an opportunity to gradually accumulate.
