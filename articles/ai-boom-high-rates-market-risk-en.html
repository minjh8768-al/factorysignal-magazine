AI Boom Prolongs High Interest Rates, Raising Risks of Broad Asset Market Decline

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 과거와는 다른 비트코인 바닥, 경기와 증시의 딜레마: AI 호황의 역설. Summarised from that video.

Amid the artificial intelligence (AI) boom being cited as a key driving force supporting recent asset markets, an analysis has emerged suggesting that AI investment is actually causing the Federal Reserve (Fed) to maintain its high interest rate stance for a prolonged period.

A broadcast commentator pointed out that massive investments and growth among large AI-related tech stocks are bolstering U.S. economic growth, thereby reducing the Fed's justification to cut interest rates early out of concern for a recession. The commentator explained that a structure has formed where AI investment keeps the economy solid and maintains inflationary pressure, prolonging high interest rates.

Projections of $3 Trillion in AI Corporate Debt and Liquidity Risk

Projections were also presented indicating that if current trends continue, the debt of AI companies could rise to approximately $3 trillion. This figure significantly exceeds the $300 billion in debt during the dot-com bubble and $1.3 trillion during the 2008 global financial crisis.

The commentator warned that if large-cap AI stocks, which have served as a mainstay for the market, begin to stumble, the impact could spread across asset markets as a whole.

가장 큰 위험은 AI 주식이 무너지는 순간이다. 시장을 떠받치는 AI 대형주가 흔들리는 동시에 금리와 에너지 물가가 높은 상태라면 유동성 축소와 강제 매도로 이어져 주식, 금속, 비트코인까지 동반 하락할 수 있다.

"The biggest risk is the moment AI stocks collapse. If the large-cap AI stocks supporting the market stumble while interest rates and energy prices remain high, it could lead to liquidity contraction and forced selling, causing equities, metals, and even Bitcoin to fall together."

Global Central Banks Shift Away from U.S. Treasuries to Increase Gold Reserves

Major countries selling U.S. Treasuries was cited as a reason U.S. Treasury yields remain high amid the high interest rate stance. China, which previously held the largest amount of U.S. Treasuries, has continuously reduced its share of U.S. Treasuries since 2011 while significantly increasing its gold holdings.

This move is expanding across global central banks. A phenomenon is emerging where the value of gold assets in foreign exchange reserves of global central banks is overtaking their holdings of U.S. Treasuries.

The commentator stressed that Bitcoin prices have also shown a complex relationship with past interest rate hike and cut cycles, emphasizing the need to be cautious about asset market volatility intertwined with liquidity contraction trends.
