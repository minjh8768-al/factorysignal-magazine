President Lee Jae-myung Delivers UN Keynote Address, Agrees to Deepen Nuclear Submarine Fuel and Shipbuilding Cooperation with US

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: MBC강원영동NEWS · 유엔 무대서 빛난 대한민국의 저력! 트럼프·호주 총리와 연속 회담, 글로벌 외교의 새 지평을 열다. Summarised from that video.

As President Lee Jae-myung is visiting New York to attend the high-level meeting of the 81st UN General Assembly, the outcomes of major bilateral summits and his keynote address were announced.

Keynote Address at UN General Assembly and Emphasis on Global Solidarity

President Lee expressed South Korea's intent to contribute to finding solutions for shared global challenges, including AI, climate change, and energy security, during his keynote speech at the UN General Assembly general debate. In addition, during a meeting with UN Secretary-General António Guterres, he sought the international community's understanding and support for the policy of peaceful coexistence on the Korean Peninsula.

The Director of National Security explained the main significance of attending this UN General Assembly as follows.

첫째로는 글로벌 책임 강국으로서 대한민국의 위상을 공고히 했습니다. 세계 최대의 다자 외교무대인 UN총회에서 2년 연속 정상이 참석하여 우리 정부의 정책과 비전을 알린다는 것 자체가 의미가 크다 하겠습니다.

"First, it solidified South Korea's standing as a globally responsible power. The fact that the president attended the UN General Assembly, the world's largest multilateral diplomatic stage, for two consecutive years to promote our government's policies and vision carries great significance in itself."

South Korea-US Summit and Major Security and Economic Cooperation

President Lee held a summit meeting with US President Donald Trump for about 30 minutes. The two leaders welcomed the progress in discussions on strategic investment projects between the two countries and agreed to further deepen cooperation in areas including nuclear submarine fuel, enrichment and reprocessing, shipbuilding cooperation, and the transfer of wartime operational control.

President Trump reaffirmed his willingness to engage in dialogue with North Korea, and the leaders of South Korea and the US agreed to communicate closely to pave the way for dialogue and establish sustainable peace on the Korean Peninsula. President Lee then wished for the successful hosting of the upcoming US-China summit.

Energy Supply Chain and Defense Industry Cooperation with Australia

President Lee held his third summit with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese to discuss major agenda items, including energy supply chains and defense industry cooperation. The leaders of both nations reaffirmed that they are key mutual partners amidst the global energy crisis.

The Director of National Security remarked on the discussions between the two leaders as follows.

양 정상은 글로벌 에너지 위기에 함께 대응해 나가는 과정에서 양국이 서로에게 LNG, 석유 제품 등 주요 에너지 자원 공급을 의존하고 있는 핵심 파트너임을 다시금 확인하고 핵심 광물 청정에너지 등 분야에서도 협력을 심화해 나가자고 하였습니다.

"The two leaders reaffirmed that both countries are key partners relying on each other for the supply of major energy resources such as LNG and petroleum products in the process of jointly responding to the global energy crisis, and agreed to deepen cooperation in fields such as critical minerals and clean energy."

Hosting of the Korea Investment Summit 2026

President Lee is scheduled to attend the 'Korea Investment Summit 2026', attended by major figures from the US financial and economic sectors. He plans to explain the changes and growth strategies of the South Korean economy to global investors and explore new growth opportunities together.
