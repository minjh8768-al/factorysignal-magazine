September FOMC Hikes Rates by 0.25%p with Hawkish Outlook… Prof. Kim Young-ik Warns of Possible US Recession in First Half of Next Year

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 김영익의 경제스쿨 · [속보] FOMC 금리 만장일치 인상.. 앞으로의 투자 전략은 ‘이렇게’ 가져가세요 | 김영익 교수. Summarised from that video.

Financial markets are showing volatility as the U.S. Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) raised its benchmark interest rate by 0.25 percentage points and signaled the possibility of further rate hikes ahead. Professor Kim Young-ik (김영익) analyzed the FOMC results and warned of the possibility of a recession in the U.S. economy alongside the Federal Reserve's hawkish interest rate stance.

FOMC Results and Financial Market Reaction

In this FOMC meeting, the benchmark interest rate was raised unanimously by 0.25 percentage points. According to the dot plot, another rate hike could take place within this year. The Fed chair repeatedly emphasized price stability, maintaining a hawkish stance.

Due to this rate hike stance, the 10-year U.S. Treasury yield and the Dollar Index rose slightly, while the stock market showed a downward trend. In particular, the market reacted sensitively, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average falling by about 1.2%.

Fed's Revised Economic Outlook and Dot Plot Analysis

The Fed revised up its economic growth and inflation forecasts compared to previous projections, while lowering its unemployment rate forecast. This reflects that the U.S. economy is stronger than expected and inflation pressures persist.

The dot plot was also revised upward, reflecting the possibility of an additional rate hike for the remainder of this year. Professor Kim explained that while the majority of members projected maintaining a high interest rate level, a tiny minority predicted a sharp rate cut next year, indicating that pessimistic views on the U.S. economy also exist.

Signs of U.S. Economic Recession and Concerns Over Shrinking Consumption

Professor Kim diagnosed that the U.S. economy is at risk of falling into a recession, citing shrinking consumer sentiment, the inversion of short- and long-term yield spreads, and an upward trend in unemployment.

미국 가계의 저축률이 떨어지고 실질 소득이 감소하는 가운데 이자 부담이 가중되고 있습니다. 올 4분기부터 소비가 줄어들며 내년 상반기에는 미국 경제 성장률이 떨어지거나 경기 침체에 빠질 수 있습니다.

"As the savings rate of U.S. households falls and real income decreases, the interest burden is worsening. Consumption will shrink starting in the fourth quarter of this year, and in the first half of next year, U.S. economic growth could drop or fall into a recession."

While the Consumer Sentiment Index has dropped to historically low levels, inflation expectations remain high, placing a heavy economic burden on households. If consumption, which accounts for a large portion of U.S. GDP, contracts, downside pressure on the economy is expected to increase.

Investment Strategy and Asset Allocation Recommendations

Synthesizing stock indicators such as the PER, Tobin's Q ratio, and long-term regression analysis, Professor Kim analyzed that the current U.S. stock market is in a historically overvalued zone.

He suggested that as the rate hike cycle is highly likely to end this year, an asset allocation strategy prepared for an economic slowdown and potential rate cuts is necessary.

내년을 대비해 채권 투자는 차츰 늘려가는 것이 유리하며, 과대평가 영역에 있는 주식 시장은 신중하게 접근하는 것이 바람직합니다.

"In preparation for next year, it is advantageous to gradually increase bond investments, and it is desirable to approach the stock market, which is in overvalued territory, with caution."
