US July PCE Inflation Hits 3.7%... Analysts Say Medium-Term Disinflation Trend Persists

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · [속보] '인플레 불안 재고조' PCE 물가 기대치 상회. 피크아웃 확인인가? 금리인상 시그널인가? [즉시분석]. Summarised from that video.

With the release of the U.S. Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index for July and the revised second-quarter gross domestic product (GDP) growth rate, analysis suggests that despite short-term downward market pressure, inflation has passed its peak and entered a stabilization phase.

PCE Exceeds Market Expectations... Short-Term Downward Pressure Applied

The headline PCE inflation rate for July came in at 3.7%, slightly above the market expectation of 3.6%. Core PCE inflation recorded 3.3%, matching market forecasts.

An economic expert explained that immediately following the release of figures exceeding market expectations, short-term volatility emerged, with stock prices, cryptocurrency, and gold prices coming under downward pressure while Treasury yields faced upward pressure.

지금 단기적으로 이 순간에는 주가가 하방 압력을 받는, 역시 코인 가격도 하방 압력을 받는, 그리고 국채 금리는 상승 압력을 받는 그런 데이터가 발표됐다라고 표현해 볼 수 있겠습니다.

"We can describe it as data being released where, in this short term and at this moment, stock prices face downward pressure, crypto prices also face downward pressure, and Treasury yields face upward pressure."

Disinflation Journey from a Medium- to Long-Term Perspective

Unlike the short-term market reaction, disinflation (a slowing of inflation) is evaluated to be maintaining its medium- to long-term trend. Despite reflecting the period when international oil prices reached $100 per barrel due to Middle East instability and tensions in the Strait of Hormuz, the July PCE figure remained at 3.7%, lower than the peak of 4.1% seen two months prior.

Major inflation indicators, including the Consumer Price Index (CPI), Producer Price Index (PPI), and expected inflation rate, have all continuously declined after peaking, supporting expectations of inflation stabilization. In particular, core PPI, which acts as a leading indicator, has also shown a downward stabilizing trend since peaking at 4.9% in April, projected to ease future inflation pressures.

Q2 GDP Revised at 1.5%... Price Stabilization Effect of U.S.-China Summit

The revised U.S. second-quarter GDP growth rate, released alongside the PCE data, was recorded at 1.5%, unchanged from the advance estimate. This indicates that the economy is continuing moderate growth, neither in crisis or recession nor overheating.

Additionally, analysis suggested that increased imports from China following tariff deferrals on certain IT and consumer goods after the U.S.-China summit are exerting downward pressure on prices within the U.S. The explanation is that large volumes of imported daily necessities and parts from China are helping cool inflation.

Monetary Policy Outlook and Interest Rate Trajectory

Immediately after the indicator release, market caution heightened on the CME FedWatch tool, where the probability of keeping the benchmark interest rate unchanged dropped from 64% to 59.6%, while the probability of a rate hike rose from 36% to 40.4%.

However, from the perspective of monetary policy mechanisms, the stance of keeping rates on hold is likely to persist for the time being, and expectations for rate cuts could surge again depending on whether upcoming inflation indicators such as the CPI and PPI stabilize.

지금 통화정책의 메커니즘상 금리를 계속 동결해야 되는 입장이라고 생각합니다. 정치적으로는 금리 인하할 수도 있지만 통화정책 메커니즘상으로는 금리 인하하기 어렵다. 계속 동결이 맞다라고 생각을 전합니다.

"I believe that under the current monetary policy mechanism, the stance should be to keep interest rates frozen. Politically, they might cut rates, but under monetary policy mechanisms, it is difficult to cut rates. I convey my view that maintaining the freeze is correct."
