After 11 Failures and 50 Billion Won in Revenue, Kmong CEO Park Hyun-ho Says Execution and Long-Term Vision Are Key to Business Success

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 장사건물주 강호동 · "대박나기 직전 신호" 신용불량자에서 500억씩 버는 회사를 만든 방법 (부자들의 은밀한 술토크 ㅣ 크몽 대표 박현호). Summarised from that video.

Park Hyun-ho ( 박현호), CEO of the freelance platform Kmong, who overcame the severe trial of 11 business failures and bad credit status to build a company with 50 billion won in net revenue, revealed the essential mindset and startup insights needed for business success.

The Principles of Wealth Realised After 11 Failures

CEO Park entered the startup world at the young age of 20 and experienced 11 business failures over a period of about 10 years, including restaurant reviews, local communities, shopping malls, and game item trading platforms. After numerous failures, he ended up with bad credit, seized assets, and an inability to open bank accounts, but he realized the principles of wealth while analyzing the causes of his failures.

CEO Park cited "execution ability" and a "long-term perspective" rather than the excellence of the business idea as the most important factors determining business success or failure. He explained that impatience to achieve big results in a short period leads to unreasonable investments and failure.

무조건 장기적으로 봐야 한다. 6개월 안에 대박을 내겠다는 조급한 마음을 가지면 실수를 너무 많이 하게 된다. 결국 사업 성패는 실행력과 장기적인 관점에 달려 있다.

"You must look at it from a long-term perspective no matter what. If you are impatient to hit the jackpot within six months, you will make too many mistakes. In the end, business success or failure depends on execution and a long-term perspective."

Three Motivations Leading to Success

CEO Park presented enjoyment, a sense of mission, and self-development as the most powerful motivating factors that make a business sustainable. According to research findings, the greatest driving force behind working consistently over a long period and producing creative results comes from enjoying the work.

He also emphasized that the probability of success in work can be significantly increased only when backed by a sense of mission to help others and a desire for personal growth. He added that in reality, the most important criterion that MZ generation workers consider when deciding to change jobs or resign is their own "growth."

성공에 이르는 가장 큰 동기부여는 일 자체가 주는 즐거움이다. 내가 하는 일이 재밌고 적성에 잘 맞아야 오랜 기간 사업을 이끌어갈 수 있다.

"The greatest motivation leading to success is the enjoyment provided by the work itself. What you do must be fun and suit your aptitude so that you can lead the business for a long time."

The 200,000 Won Miracle Born from Seclusion on Jirisan

After suffering severe financial hardship due to business failures, including having electricity and water cut off, CEO Park moved to his mother's house in Sancheong, Jirisan, to resolve food and shelter, remaining in seclusion for a year and a half. In an environment where even mobile phones barely worked, he connected local cable to get internet access and turned his attention to overseas success stories.

Coming across the model of Israel's freelance marketplace 'Fiverr', he planned a Korean version of a 5,000-won talent market platform. Purchasing a basic website source code for 200,000 won, he completed and launched the Kmong service in just two weeks. In the early stages of launch, 50 transactions occurred per day, recording a daily net profit of 50,000 won, which served as the first step for Kmong to grow into South Korea's representative freelance platform.

내 아이디어를 고집하기보다 해외에서 이미 잘되는 모델을 보고 한국 시장에 맞게 벤치마킹하는 것이 실패 확률을 줄이는 방법이다.

"Rather than insisting on my own idea, looking at a model that is already working well overseas and benchmarking it to fit the Korean market is the way to reduce the probability of failure."

The Fatal Mistake 99% of Entrepreneurs Make

CEO Park pointed out the attitude of trying to receive investment right from the start without even validating the service as the biggest mistake prospective entrepreneurs commit. He noted that businesses launched relying on business plans and investment funds induce impatience and hinder long-term growth.

He suggested that constantly testing one's business model on a small scale in the market and attracting investment only after validating hypotheses while observing customer reactions is the safe sequence. In addition, he mentioned that thanks to recent AI technology and various digital tools, the best environment in history has opened up for starting a solo business, encouraging execution to generate asset income beyond earned income.
