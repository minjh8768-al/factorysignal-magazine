Son Heung-min Shows His Class in MLS, Overcoming Tight Coverage to Lead Team to Victory

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 발빠른 뉴스 · "5명이 손흥민 한명을 못막네요.." 손흥민 3골 만들고 MVP 넋나간 미국 반응. Summarised from that video.

Son Heung-min overcame tight opposition marking to deliver an outstanding performance and lead his team to victory.

MLS Round 17 Played Amid Criticism and Doubt

Despite scoring his first league goal in Round 16, some local media expressed skepticism, noting that his first goal came only mid-season. Some evaluations went as far as saying his prime had passed or that a single goal changed nothing.

However, in the following Round 17 home match against Salt Lake, Son proved his worth just 10 minutes into the game.

A Shot with an xG of 0.05 Breaking Through Tight Marking

Under manager Pablo Mastroeni, who had previously conceded a goal to Son, Salt Lake deployed a strategy specifically focused on marking Son tightly. Every time Son held the ball, three to as many as four defenders surrounded him simultaneously to cut off space.

Yet, just 10 minutes into the match, Son snatched a deflected ball, drove forward down the center, and struck a left-footed shot through a narrow gap in the defensive wall to find the net. The expected goals (xG) value for this goal was merely 0.05, representing a difficult chance with only a 5% probability of scoring out of 100 shots.

Superb Tactical Movement Opening Up Space for Teammates

Son's performance went beyond simply scoring. He turned the opposition defense's focus on him to his advantage, creating crucial space for his teammates.

Dropping down from the center-forward position near the halfway line, he pulled the defense toward him by delivering immediate return passes, opening a clear path for teammates to penetrate the space behind. He also maintained threatening explosiveness, recording a top speed of 35.43 km/h during sprints.

Forcing an Own Goal to Secure a 3-1 Victory

In a subsequent counterattack, Son penetrated behind the opponent's defense with precise timing. A ball deflected off the foot of Yedlin, who attempted a desperate tackle to stop Son, went into the net for an own goal.

Driven by Son's stellar performance, his club LA finished the match with a 3-1 victory. Dispelling local media doubts in a single match, Son proved that he remains a decisive player capable of changing the flow of the game.
