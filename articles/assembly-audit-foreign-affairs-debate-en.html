Clash Between Rep. Han Dong-hoon and Foreign Minister Cho Tae-yul Highlights Parliamentary Audit

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 시사교양 라디오 - 시교라 · [뉴스직격]“한동훈, 정치 과호흡 왔다”에 ‘공달’ 윤희석 대답은 심플했다!. Summarised from that video.

A sharp clash between Representative Han Dong-hoon and Minister of Foreign Affairs Cho Tae-yul during the National Assembly Foreign Affairs and Unification Committee's parliamentary audit was covered as a major issue on current affairs programs. Panelists debated the unprecedented situation of an audited agency head declaring a refusal to answer, as well as the appropriateness of the language used during questioning.

"I Will Refuse to Answer": Head-on Collision at Foreign Affairs and Unification Committee Audit

At the audit, Rep. Han Dong-hoon went on the offensive against Foreign Minister Cho Tae-yul, comparing the South Korean government's level of response to North Korea with its response to issues involving Ukraine. Rep. Han pointed out the disparity between the government's handling of statements by Kim Yo-jong, vice department director of the Workers' Party of Korea, and its response to announcements by the Ukrainian government.

북한이 발표하는 김여정이 떠드는 거에 대해서는 왜 찌꺼기소리도 못하면서 우크라이나만 쥐잡듯 하십니까? 북한이 주적입니까 우크라이나가 주적입니까?

"Why can't you say even a tiny word about what North Korea releases and what Kim Yo-jong blabbers about, while hounding only Ukraine like catching a rat? Is North Korea the main enemy, or is Ukraine the main enemy?"

In response, Minister Cho strongly pushed back, taking issue with some of the expressions used in the questioning. Minister Cho expressed his position that he could not proceed with answering the questions unless the provocative vocabulary was corrected.

그런 표현이 있는 한 제가 답변을 거부하겠습니다.

"As long as such expressions remain, I will refuse to answer."

Analysis of Unprecedented Refusal to Answer and Foreign Minister's Response

Commentators appearing on broadcast programs noted that it is uncommon for a career-bureaucrat Foreign Minister to directly declare a refusal to answer at a parliamentary audit venue. Some evaluated that an executive branch minister who has spent decades in public service should have flexibly and skillfully handled rough questioning by a lawmaker, but the political handling was clumsy.

One commentator expressed regret over the diplomat-turned-minister taking an emotional, confrontational stance during a Q&A session with the National Assembly, a representative body, saying he should have calmly explained the policy justification instead.

Rep. Han Dong-hoon's Political Moves and the Task of Seizing the Agenda

Regarding Rep. Han Dong-hoon's aggressive style of questioning, views were expressed that it is excellent for leading political issues and impressing a strong presence. Analysis suggested that he is clearly showcasing his strengths in the early stages of his parliamentary activities.

On the other hand, concerns were also raised that frequently using provocative vocabulary or confrontational expressions typical of online and offline settings could cause unnecessary emotional loss regardless of winning the debate. Refining future messaging and pacing political rhetoric were projected as key tasks for expanding appeal among moderates and improving favorability.
