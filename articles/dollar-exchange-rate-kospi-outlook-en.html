Dollar Rate Adjustment Nearing End: Caution Urged Over Kospi Volatility

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 달러 폭락의 진짜 이유 공개! 환율 왜곡 끝나는 순간 국내 증시 ‘이 폭풍’ 터집니다. Summarised from that video.

Regarding the recent decline in the won-dollar exchange rate and stock market volatility, a market expert analyzed that complex factors, such as currency exchange demand from a specific company and government guidelines, are affecting the foreign exchange market. Consequently, a cautious approach is required regarding the timing of dollar purchases and the direction of the domestic stock market.

Dollar Exchange Rate Fluctuations and Foreign Exchange Market Outlook

The expert explained that dollar futures prices and the exchange rate showed a downward trend due to a specific company's large-scale dollar currency exchange demand and the influence of government policy. The expert pointed out that temporary distortions occurred on exchange rate charts as guidelines limiting daily currency exchange above a certain scale came into effect.

The expert viewed that once artificial adjustments in the foreign exchange market are completed, the dollar will regain its original direction, noting that the current moment could be an opportune buying time for those needing dollars.

만약에 해외 송금하셔야 되는 분, 해외 여행 가셔야 되는 분, 달러가 필요하신 분은 지금이 천천히 달러를 싸게 매수할 수 있는 시기다.

"For those who need to send money abroad, travel overseas, or otherwise need dollars, now is a good time to gradually buy dollars cheaply."

The expert added that if the dollar rebounds after the end of the rate decline phase, concerns over foreign exchange losses among domestic export companies, along with pressure for re-adjustment across the overall stock market, could be exerted.

National Pension Stock Allocation and the Direction of the Kospi Index

Regarding the direction of the domestic stock market, the adjustment of the National Pension Service's stock holdings allocation was presented as an important variable. It was explained that as rising stock prices caused the National Pension Service's domestic stock proportion to significantly exceed its target, profit-taking sell-offs could weigh on the market.

The expert assessed that while the index would inevitably face downward pressure if large-scale stock sales occur, the timing of these sales is being adjusted in conjunction with political situations such as elections.

Market Strategy and Buy-Sell Timing

The expert advised retirees who need to secure cash or short-term asset managers to refrain from aggressive stock purchases during periods of high index volatility. The expert also emphasized short-term strategies utilizing inverse products or buying in installments after confirming a bottom.

하이니크스 효과로 달러가 떨어진 이 구간은 시장에 대해서 왜곡 현상이 벌어지는 겁니다. 이건 개입이 된 환율입니다.

"This period where the dollar has dropped due to the Hynix effect represents a market distortion. This is an intervened exchange rate."

Finally, the expert suggested that conservative asset management is necessary to prepare for the possibility of further index adjustments while closely monitoring the dollar's direction after foreign exchange market distortions clear up.
