US Stocks Close Higher as Applied Materials Reports Strong Q3 Earnings and Bright Outlook

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: Bloomberg Television · S&P 500 Hits Record as Inflation Cools | Closing Bell. Summarised from that video.

Major US stock indexes closed higher, driven by easing inflation metrics and corporate earnings. As price indicators such as the Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Producer Price Index (PPI) registered moderate levels, investor concerns were somewhat alleviated, sustaining a positive market trend ahead of the Federal Reserve's interest rate decision.

Applied Materials Reports Q3 Earnings Beating Market Estimates

Semiconductor equipment manufacturer Applied Materials released its fiscal third-quarter results, stating that performance exceeded market expectations. The company's third-quarter net sales reached $9.12 billion, topping the market forecast of $9.02 billion, while adjusted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $3.50, surpassing the expected $3.42.

Broadcast host Tim Stenovec shared a positive outlook on the company's future revenue growth, citing statements from Applied Materials Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Gary Dickerson.

We are further raising our semiconductor systems revenue expectations for calendar 2026. They're confident that they'll grow faster than the market this year based on the increased demand visibility.

"We are further raising our semiconductor systems revenue expectations for calendar 2026. We are confident that we will grow faster than the market average this year based on increased demand visibility confirmed by customers."

The company subsequently offered a fourth-quarter net sales guidance range between $9.75 billion and $10.75 billion, surpassing the market average forecast of $9.62 billion.

Mixed Stock Performance: SanDisk and Workday Gain While Cisco and Hertz Decline

Tech and memory-related shares showed strength during the session. SanDisk saw its stock price surge after unveiling a long-term financial model at its Investor Day event, while Western Digital, Micron, and Seagate Technology also advanced. Workday rose sharply following reports of buyout talks with private equity firm Silver Lake, and Birkenstock also gained on an upgraded full-year outlook.

Conversely, several companies dropped on negative news. Cisco Systems fell about 8.4% as its AI data center revenue forecast failed to meet investor expectations. Hertz Global Holdings plummeted over 16% following news that Pershing Square sold its stake, citing dissatisfaction with financing plans. StubHub also dropped around 10% due to missing second-quarter earnings estimates and customer dissatisfaction regarding World Cup ticketing.

Bond Yields Fall as Manhattan Rents Hit All-Time High

In the bond market, Treasury yields generally declined, supported by stabilizing inflation figures. Yields on both 2-year and 10-year Treasuries edged down slightly, displaying a stable movement.

Meanwhile, real economic indicators in New York showed persistent housing cost pressures. Manhattan's median rent reached a record high of $5,000, while active listings plummeted 39% year-over-year, pointing to a severe supply-demand imbalance.
