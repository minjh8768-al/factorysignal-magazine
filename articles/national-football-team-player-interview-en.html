National Team Call-Ups and Returning Players Interviewed: "We Will Give Our Best Through Competition and Adaptation"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · "득남 축하해" '모레노 1기' 훈훈한 분위기..첫 경기 앞둔 이한범·송민규 선수 만나 보니 / SBS 실시간 라이브. Summarised from that video.

First Call-Up Under New Manager and Resolve to Adapt

Players newly joining or returning to the national team after a long absence shared their thoughts on being called up and their future resolve through interviews. As this was the first call-up following the managerial change, the players said their top priority was to quickly adapt to the team and training.

A defender currently playing in the Belgian league shared his feelings on being selected for the national team and vowed to adapt quickly.

감독님이 바뀌시고 나서 첫 소집인데 이렇게 처음부터 뽑히게 돼서 정말 영광인 것 같고요. 아직 훈련을 선수들과 해보지는 않았지만 최대한 빨리 적응하도록 노력하겠습니다.

"It is a great honor to be selected from the very beginning in this first call-up since the manager changed. Although I haven't trained with the players yet, I will try to adapt as quickly as possible."

Competition for Starting Spots and Adapting to Formations

The players also shared their candid thoughts on competing for starting positions within the national team and adjusting to formation changes. Regarding teamwork and competition with existing squad members such as Kim Min-jae, they expressed a determination to prepare thoroughly on their own to seize opportunities.

They added that because they play in a back-four system for their club teams, the burden of formation changes is not significant.

스스로 잘 준비해서 이번 경기부터 바로 뛸 수 있게 잘 준비하도록 하겠습니다.

"I will prepare well on my own so that I am ready to play right away starting from this upcoming match."

Move to Europe and Thoughts on Returning to National Team

Another player who returned to the national team after a long absence also expressed gratitude for being selected again following his move to Europe. He conveyed his commitment to faithfully performing the role required by the manager on the pitch.

Additionally, he shared an anecdote about watching Lee Kang-in's match while traveling and stated that he would focus on managing his condition to deliver good performances on the pitch.

This article summarises the content of a YouTube video and reflects the personal views of the people appearing in it. Factory Magazine has not verified or endorsed these statements.
