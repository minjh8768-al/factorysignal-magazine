US Treasury Yields Surpass 5%... Author Kim Chang-ik Says Stablecoins Could Be Solution for Short-Term Debt

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 천하태평한 트럼프? 물밑에서 은밀하게 준비 중인 '스테이블코인' 큰 그림 [김창익 작가]. Summarised from that video.

As the 30-year US Treasury yield surpassed 5%, the US government's fiscal burden and the supply-demand imbalance in Treasury bonds resurfaced. Author Kim Chang-ik assessed that the rise in US Treasury yields is an indicator reflecting structural changes across US finances and the overall bond market, beyond mere financial market volatility.

When long-term Treasuries issued at low interest rates in the 1% range during past pandemics and financial crises are refinanced at maturity with yields soaring to the 5% range, the annual interest expense burden rises sharply. Kim pointed out that this growing interest burden poses a significant challenge for the US administration and fiscal authorities.

Exodus of Major Treasury Market Players and Shift in Supply-Demand Structure

The purchasing capacity of key entities that previously supported the US Treasury market has weakened significantly. Due to capital regulations tightened after the financial crisis, such as Basel III and the Dodd-Frank Act, alongside the burden of unrealized losses on held Treasuries, Wall Street commercial banks and primary dealers face difficulties in absorbing additional long-term Treasuries.

Furthermore, foreign central banks, including China's, continue to reduce their US dollar holdings and increase reserves of alternative assets such as gold, leading to a trend of selling long-term Treasuries. Consequently, a supply-demand imbalance has been created as large-scale buyers capable of absorbing long-term US Treasuries disappear from the market.

Short-Term Debt-Focused Response and the Role of Stablecoins

Under these circumstances, US authorities continue to respond by increasing the proportion of short-term Treasury issuances to avoid the burden of issuing long-term bonds. However, with the Federal Reserve's reverse repo balance, which acted as a short-term liquidity reservoir, running low, there is a dire need for new liquidity sources to consistently purchase short-term debt.

Kim presented the stablecoin market and big tech participation as a solution to this Treasury supply-demand dilemma. The analysis suggests that as stablecoin issuers purchase large amounts of short-term US Treasuries as reserves, stablecoins could establish themselves as a key source of demand for short-term US debt.
