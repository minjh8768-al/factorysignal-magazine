Trump Sparks Controversy with Self-Aggrandizing Moves and '$5,000 Per Adult' Midterm Cash Pledge

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: KBS 세계는지금 · 오직 트럼프, 내 이름을 위대하게 (KBS_467회_2026.09.12.방송). Summarised from that video.

U.S. President Donald Trump is causing controversy with extreme self-aggrandizing actions, such as attaching his name to airports and cultural facilities and unveiling coins and passports featuring his portrait. Struggling in approval ratings ahead of the midterm elections, he has even proposed a massive campaign pledge to distribute cash to all adult voters if his party wins the election.

Renaming Public Facilities and Deification Controversies

President Trump changed the name of Palm Beach International Airport in Florida to "President Donald J. Trump International Airport." Naming an airport after a sitting president is unprecedented. He then drew widespread backlash by adding his name to the exterior wall of the Kennedy Center building in Washington, D.C. without congressional approval, ultimately receiving a court order to remove it. Local residents strongly criticized the move.

이건 이 건물에 대한 모독입니다. 워싱턴 D.C.의 민주주의가 죽은 날이 있다면 바로 오늘입니다.

"This is a desecration of this building. It is a day democracy died in Washington D.C."

In addition, he engraved his face on $1 coins commemorating the 250th anniversary of U.S. independence, as well as on passports and National Park memberships. Republicans even introduced a special bill to issue a $250 bill featuring Trump's portrait, but controversy over its illegality under current law continues. Criticism was also raised that he blatantly reflected his personal tastes during the White House renovation project and the construction of a new banquet hall.

Self-Promotion on Social Media and Internal Discipline Concerns

President Trump posted a large volume of messages on his own social media platform referring to himself as "the greatest of all time" and labeling key political opponents as "losers." He also actively used AI-generated synthetic images and fake news targeting figures who have criticized him, such as actor Robert De Niro. This storm of uploads was reportedly supported by a dedicated special assistant who printed and delivered articles praising Trump 24 hours a day.

On the other hand, opposition lawmakers pointed out President Trump nodding off during official meetings, questioning his ability to perform his duties as commander-in-chief. Criticism continued that even in key cabinet meetings and policy discussions, he focused on his own renovation projects or personal honor rather than national issues.

Failed Convention Box Office and Competitive Race in Texas

The Republican National Convention held in Dallas, Texas, for the midterm election campaign was evaluated as a failure in attracting crowds, with empty seats throughout the arena. During his speech, President Trump even pleaded with attendees not to go to the bathroom so the seats would not look empty. This was because many Republican moderates and lawmakers from competitive districts refrained from attending due to the burden of being closely aligned with Trump.

In Texas, which had been a Republican stronghold for decades, a razor-thin race unfolded with the Democratic candidate due to Senate candidate Ken Paxton, who was impeached by the House on corruption charges but was elected with Trump's endorsement. In response, a Trump-linked super PAC deployed $10 million in funds to launch an all-out effort.

'$5,000 Per Adult' Pledge and Fiscal Concerns

President Trump announced a massive cash distribution pledge conditional on a midterm election victory during his convention speech.

이번 중간선거에서 공화당이 상원, 하원 모두 이기면 성인 1인당 5천 달러씩 지급하겠습니다.

"If the Republicans win the House of Representatives and the United States Senate, I will issue a dividend to every adult citizen in the United States of America for 5,000 dollars."

Calculated based on the U.S. adult population of approximately 270 million, the required budget reaches about $1.3 trillion (approx. 1,800 trillion won). With the U.S. national debt already surpassing $40 trillion, concerns over massive fiscal spending are growing. Expert organizations such as the Tax Foundation expressed doubt about the feasibility of claims that it would be funded through tariff revenues, noting that it far exceeds total tariff revenue. Furthermore, as economic burdens on farmers and voters increase due to Yemeni Houthi rebels' control of the Red Sea and surging oil prices, attention is focused on the direction of voter sentiment.
