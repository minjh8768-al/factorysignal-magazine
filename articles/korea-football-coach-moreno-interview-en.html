New South Korea Manager Loreno: "Great Honor to Lead National Team... Will Focus on Given Tasks"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 연합뉴스TV · "몇 년 전부터 한국 대표팀 원했다"…모레노 감독 입국 첫 일성 "국민이 자부심 갖도록" [현장쏙] / 연합뉴스TV(YonhapnewsTV). Summarised from that video.

Manager Loreno, who has taken charge of the South Korea national football team, held a press conference immediately upon arriving in South Korea, sharing his thoughts on leading the national team and his future management approach.

Thoughts on Becoming National Team Manager and Korean Football

Asked about his feelings on arriving in South Korea to take the national team manager post, Manager Loreno expressed joy and responsibility. Revealing that he had been interested in Korean football and the national team for several years, he said taking charge of the team was a great honor.

Es un orgullo y un honor el poder dirigir a esta selección.

"It is a pride and an honor to be able to lead this national team."

Preparation Process and Analysis

When asked how he prepared while awaiting his appointment, Manager Loreno replied that he continuously analyzed match videos daily and studied Korean culture.

He added that he made continuous efforts to understand not only the team's existing playing style and operating history, but also overall Korean culture.

Development Goals and Determination for the Fans

Regarding the national team's current situation and the goals he wishes to achieve over the next six matches, Manager Loreno emphasized that rather than dwelling on past disappointments, he will look ahead and focus on uniting the team.

Trabajar al máximo para hacer que los aficionados se sientan orgullosos de su selección.

"I will do my utmost so that the fans can feel proud of their national team."

Perspective on Official Manager Appointment

Regarding questions about a formal permanent manager appointment or long-term commitment, he stated that prioritizing the tasks currently assigned to him and preparing for upcoming matches comes first.

Manager Loreno concluded the press conference by stating that he will do his best for the immediate matches and pour all his capabilities into tasks currently within his control.
