Roberto Moreno Appointed Manager of South Korea National Football Team: 3 Key Reasons Revealed

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SPOMAN · 한국 축구를 이렇게까지 분석했다고? 모레노 선임 이유가 나왔다. Summarised from that video.

The Korea Football Association (KFA) has introduced an open recruitment process for the first time to appoint Spanish manager Roberto Moreno to lead the national football team. Behind the selection of Moreno among numerous candidates are three key reasons outlined by Hyun Young-min, head of the National Teams Committee.

Deep Understanding of Korean Football and the Squad

The first reason for his appointment was his deep understanding of Korean football. During an interview conducted in Barcelona, Spain, where Committee Chairman Hyun Young-min visited in person, Manager Moreno demonstrated that he had already thoroughly analyzed the Korean national team.

He impressed the evaluation panel by specifically understanding not only famous overseas players, but also the recent strengths and recurring problems of the national team, the operating system of the K League and Gimcheon Sangmu, and the characteristics of young players active in Europe.

Data-Driven, Specific, and Flexible Tactical Alternatives

The second reason was his presentation of concrete tactical solutions. Manager Moreno did not stop at identifying problems, but proposed tactical solutions for various match situations based on data-driven analysis.

Rather than sticking to a single tactic, he explained flexible responses that adjust build-up methods and the number of attacking players according to the opponent's pressing level and defensive setup, earning high marks for his tactical planning.

Extraordinary Passion for Leading the National Team

The third reason was his extraordinary passion as a coach. Manager Moreno did not approach the managerial role simply as a career opportunity, but expressed a strong determination to shift the atmosphere of the struggling national team and bring new change to Korean football.

The KFA attached significance to this appointment in that it moved away from name-based hiring and comprehensively verified preparation levels and tactical plans. However, whether the thorough preparation and analysis demonstrated during the interview process will translate into results in upcoming friendlies and actual matches remains a key focal point.
