Tax Reform Without Supply Measures Will Fail, Real Estate Experts Warn of Jeonse Crisis

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · [3편] “이대로 통과되면 전세대란 옵니다” 집값 잡으려다 세입자가 직격탄 맞는다 | 경읽남과 토론합시다 | 3자토론 김인만x한문도x김광석. Summarised from that video.

Cautionary voices are rising among experts regarding the government's real estate tax reform plan, with concerns over instability in the jeonse market and policy effectiveness. Real estate experts pointed out that tax reform unaccompanied by supply measures could instead trigger a jeonse crisis and pass the burden onto innocent tenants.

"Differentiating Between Resident and Non-Resident Owners Will End in Tenant Harm"

Kim In-man, director of the Kim In-man Real Estate Economic Institute, emphasized the side effects of applying differential taxation to single-home owners based on resident and non-resident status. Director Kim analyzed that in light of fair taxation principles—such as social consensus, benefits, ability-to-pay taxation, and predictability—the current reform proposal does not align with the intent of fair taxation.

Director Kim explained that if tax cuts are granted to owner-occupiers while non-resident owners are penalized, the tax burden will ultimately be passed on to tenants in the jeonse market.

자기 집에 거주하는데 왜 혜택을 주지? 피해자는 이제 쫓겨나가는 세입자는 집이 없으니까 올려줘야 되는 불이익을 당합니다.

"Why give tax benefits just for living in one's own home? The victims will be the displaced tenants, who, having no home, suffer the disadvantage of having to accept higher prices."

Furthermore, Director Kim suggested that practical alternatives are needed, such as avoiding retroactive application in capital gains tax surcharges and special deductions for long-term holdings, and providing reasonable deduction benefits for holdings of 20 years or more.

"Tax Reform Lacking Supply Measures Cannot Stabilize Housing Prices"

Professor Han Moon-do assessed that, contrary to the justification of tax rationalization, political factors heavily intervened, diminishing policy credibility. Professor Han viewed that tax reform alone would be insufficient to stabilize housing prices and that market supply measures must be pursued in tandem.

공급 대책 없는 세제 개편은 다 실패한다. 집값 안정에 도움이 안 된다라고 다 결론이 났어요.

"Tax reforms without supply measures all fail. It has already been concluded that they do not help stabilize housing prices."

Professor Han warned that reducing benefits for registered landlords or pressuring the sale of multi-family and multi-household housing could lead to a decrease in jeonse supply. Citing examples such as higher acquisition tax rates for officetels—frequently used by young adults—compared to regular apartments, he urged for delicate tax design aimed at practical housing stability, alongside the introduction of advanced systems like transaction tax cuts and capital gains tax deferrals.

"Growing Instability in the Jeonse Market… Innocent Victims Must Be Protected"

Kim Kwang-seok, CEO, who moderated the discussion, expressed concern over the potential impact of the current reform proposal on the jeonse market, despite some positive intentions regarding tax fairness. He maintained that the top priority of housing stability policy must be stabilizing the jeonse and monthly lease market.

이대로 만약에 통과가 된다면 전세 대란 옵니다.

"If this passes as it is, a jeonse crisis is coming."

CEO Kim emphasized that policies risking burning down the house to catch a flea should be avoided, stressing the need for institutional adjustments during the National Assembly's legislative process by gathering diverse expert opinions to prevent harm to vulnerable residents and innocent victims.
