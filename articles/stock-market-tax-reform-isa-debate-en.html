Controversy Grows Over Government Tax Reform Plan, Sparking Calls to Reconsider ISA and Stock Suppression Measures

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · 곧 발표될 미 물가…증시 호재?/제동 걸린 ISA 개편·'맹탕' 주가 누르기 방지법/'부동산 세제개편' 입법의견 수천 건, 추가 대책 움직임은?/머니버스/SBS/주영진의 뉴스브리핑. Summarised from that video.

US Inflation Data and Corporate Earnings Take Center Stage Amid Stock Market Volatility

As the domestic stock market shifted into a flat range after initial gains, market participants focused their attention on the release of US inflation data and key corporate earnings reports.

Economic commentator Park Yeon-mi (박연미) reported that while the Kospi index showed an upward trend early in the session, it trimmed its gains as major semiconductor stocks such as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix turned downward. Park analyzed that the upcoming US Consumer Price Index announcement will be a key variable determining the future direction of interest rates.

Park Jin-ho (박진호), an editorial writer at SBS, predicted that international oil prices and US inflation would have a decisive impact on the US Federal Reserve's interest rate decisions. Park projected that Nvidia's earnings announcement and its CEO's remarks could serve as an opportunity to settle the ongoing debate over an AI bubble.

Backlash Against Stock Price Suppression Law and ISA Overhaul

Controversy is also intensifying over the effectiveness of the proposed stock price suppression law and the overhaul of Individual Savings Accounts (ISA) within the government's tax reform package.

The stock price suppression law was pushed for introduction to prevent major shareholders from artificially depressing stock prices to reduce inheritance or gift taxes. Park noted criticisms that the requirements for presumption are overly strict while loopholes remain large, explaining that figures in political circles, including Representative Lee So-young (이소영) of the Democratic Party of Korea, are raising doubts about the government plan's effectiveness.

Regarding the ISA reform, provisions eliminating the rollover of annual contribution limits and capping contract periods at a maximum of five years drew backlash from young investors. Park reported that as resistance mounted against measures reducing tax-exempt benefits and tax-deferral effects of existing ISA accounts while attempting to steer investment toward domestic stocks, Lee Jae-myung, leader of the Democratic Party, ordered a full review.

ISA 개편안은 이월 폐지와 계약기간 축소로 장기투자 절세 기능이 약화된다는 비판을 받고 있다.

"The ISA reform plan faces criticism that its long-term investment tax-saving features are weakened due to the elimination of rollovers and the reduction of contract periods."

Fallout Over Taxation of Non-Resident Single-Home Owners

Regarding the real estate tax reform, fallout continues as thousands of legislative comments were submitted over the tightening of taxation on non-resident owners of a single home.

Park conveyed market complaints that strengthening taxation on ultra-high-priced homes might actually restrict residential mobility for working-class and middle-class households or place a burden on actual buyers. Observers noted that an increased tax burden on ultra-high-priced homes could limit trading of such properties to within the wealthy class and cause demand to concentrate in mid-tier locations.

Park Jin-ho explained that although the government provided non-residence exception grounds such as job relocation, medical treatment, and overseas stays, there are strong calls to expand eligibility criteria for non-residence to include childcare and family caretaking.

Outlook for Future Policy Adjustments and Revisions

Amid strong pushback from the market and political circles, the government is expected to devise additional measures and complementary solutions based on collected legislative opinions and feedback from the field.

Experts urged that precise revisions should follow for provisions that dampen investment sentiment or disadvantage actual demand buyers contrary to the policy's original intent.
