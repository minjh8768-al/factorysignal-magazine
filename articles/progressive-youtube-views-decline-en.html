Kim Ou-joon and Choi Wook's YouTube Views Plunge: Cracks Form in the Heyday of Progressive Channels

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 한입정치 · 김어준 뉴스공장 100만 무너졌다… “민심 이동 시작됐다” #정치 #김어준. Summarised from that video.

An analysis shows that view counts and viewer engagement metrics of major progressive YouTube channels have recently been showing a sharp decline.

Major Channels See Views Fall Below 1 Million

Viewership metrics across major progressive YouTube channels are declining across the board. In the case of News Factory hosted by Kim Ou-joon, view counts that previously reached millions per episode have recently been observed falling below 1 million.

Other progressive channels such as Maebul Show have also seen significant drops in weekly views and likes compared to the past, leading to continued points that signals of loyal viewers departing are emerging.

Criticism of Hosts Failing to Identify the Cause

Criticism has been raised that despite this drop in metrics, show hosts are failing to properly diagnose the cause. A scene where Maebul Show host Choi Wook expressed confusion on air over the decrease in likes and stated he could not find an answer drew focused criticism.

기운 내서 내가 해내겠다. 방법을 찾겠다. 생산적으로 가야죠. 아직 좋아하고 아끼는 분들 많습니다.

"I will cheer up and get it done. I will find a way. We must go productively. There are still many people who like and care for us."

Internal Conflicts in the Progressive Camp and Departure of Supporters

Conflicts brought about by figures within the progressive camp were cited as the fundamental cause of the sharp drop in viewership metrics. Analyses suggest that remarks regarding a party merger by Representative Jung Chung-rae and author Rhyu Si-min's mention of Cho Kuk's presidential candidacy ignited internal friction among progressive supporters.

Evaluations indicate that despite such political words and actions triggering conflict within the progressive camp, attitudes that dismissed them as merely objective phenomena or attempted to defend them deepened viewers' disappointment.

Arrogance Disregarding Public Sentiment is the Cause

Points have been made that arrogance, which views audience members and the public as a passive group that unconditionally agrees with their assertions, is accelerating the downfall of these channels.

As the voluntary departure and exit of supporters begins in earnest, forecasts suggest that the shrinking influence and pace of collapse of progressive YouTube channels could proceed even faster and more steeply in the future.
