Stock Strategies Amid Exchange Rate Volatility: 'Depositors Exploited in High Inflation Era'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 김작가 TV · 환율 1,300원대, 미장만 들고 있으면 환차손 봅니다 (박세익 전무, 김학균 센터장). Summarised from that video.

As high interest rates and exchange rate volatility persist, financial market experts discussed stock investment strategies based on recent trends in the won-dollar exchange rate and changes in the interest rate environment.

Exchange Rate Volatility and the Outlook for U.S. and Korean Stock Markets

Experts analyzed that a rising won-dollar exchange rate could help improve margins for exporting companies. They noted that even if stock prices fall when the exchange rate rises, expectations of improved performance by exporters can reflect in prices, presenting investment opportunities.

With the interest rate gap between the United States and South Korea recently showing a narrowing trend, forecasts suggested that the won-dollar exchange rate could stabilize downward if geopolitical risks in the Middle East ease and the U.S. Federal Reserve's stance on freezing interest rates takes hold.

Assessing the Likelihood of a Recurrence of the Yen Carry Trade Unwinding

The prevailing assessment was that the likelihood of a recurrence of the yen carry trade unwinding, which shocked the market last August, is low. This is because market interest rates in both the U.S. and Japan have already risen preemptively and been reflected in borrowing rates.

An analysis indicated that since market interest rates reflected rate hikes before central banks adjusted key interest rates, any future additional policy rate hikes would have a limited frictional impact on capital markets.

High Inflation, Real Interest Rate Environment, and the Limitations of Bank Deposits

It was pointed out that despite nominal deposit interest rates remaining high, bank deposits are unlikely to serve as a useful means of wealth accumulation from the perspective of real interest rates taking inflation into account. Under ongoing inflation, conditions work to the advantage of debtors holding debt, inevitably diluting the real asset value of depositors.

지금은 예금자들이 착취당하는 세상이다. 정부 부채가 많은 상황에서 인플레이션은 부채를 희석시키지만 예금자의 부를 앗아가는 흐름으로 작용한다.

"We live in a world where depositors are being exploited. When government debt is high, inflation dilutes debt while stripping away the wealth of depositors."

Expanding Shareholder Returns and Investment Strategies in Dividend Growth Stocks

Positive assessments were also expressed regarding structural improvements in the domestic stock market and the expansion of shareholder return policies. Analysts noted that instances of large companies actively pursuing shareholder returns, such as treasury stock buybacks and cancellations, could mark the starting point for modernizing the domestic stock market.

A strategy of long-term investment was recommended for sustainable dividend growth stocks that have consistently increased dividends for over 10 years or high-quality companies that maintain a return on equity (ROE) above a certain level, rather than companies that simply pay one-time high dividends.
