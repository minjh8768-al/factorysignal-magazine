Crypto Policy Beneficiaries and Cosmetics ODMs Highlighted for Selective Investment

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 스테이블코인이 미 국채 금리 잡는다? 클래리티 법안 통과 사활. Summarised from that video.

A stock market expert has presented selective investment strategies for the cryptocurrency and cosmetics sectors. The analysis suggests focusing on stocks with high potential to benefit from policy changes rather than those directly tied to coin prices, and considering both the value chain and stock price positioning in the cosmetics market.

Crypto: Avoid Stocks Tied directly to Coin Prices, Focus on Policy Beneficiaries

Regarding investment in the cryptocurrency sector, experts pointed out that priority should be given to stocks benefiting from policy changes, such as the passage of the U.S. Clarity Act, rather than stocks simply linked to Bitcoin market prices.

Lee Min, an expert at SCTV, stated that investors should be cautious about investing in stocks that track coin prices directly, citing the difficulty of accurately predicting the causes of price fluctuations in cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin. Instead, he recommended paying attention to policy-themed stocks related to discussions on U.S. stablecoin legislation.

코인 시세 관련된 종목들은 안 하셨으면 좋겠습니다. 비트코인 시세가 올라가면 같이 연동돼서 올라가는 종목들보다는, 미국의 클래리티 법안 관련해서 스테이블코인이 정착될 때 수혜를 받는 종목들로 보시는 게 훨씬 더 좋습니다.

"I hope you avoid stocks related to coin market prices. Rather than stocks that rise together as Bitcoin prices go up, it is much better to look at stocks that benefit when stablecoins take root in relation to the U.S. Clarity Act."

The expert explained that the U.S. Department of the Treasury is highly likely to actively pursue the legalization of stablecoins as a means to stabilize long-term Treasury yields. The analysis indicates that if funds from stablecoin issuance are used to purchase short-term Treasury bills, it creates an effect that lowers long-term Treasury yields.

Cosmetics: ODMs Stable… Short-Term Opportunities in Brand Stocks on Dips

In the cosmetics sector, original design manufacturing (ODM) companies that can reduce individual brand risks were assessed as structurally stable. Cosmax, Kolmar Korea, and Cosmecca Korea were mentioned as key ODM companies.

Lee explained that while Kolmar Korea is larger in terms of total sales volume, Cosmax has an edge when considering only the proportion of cosmetics ODM supplied to global online and offline channels (such as Amazon, TikTok, and Sephora). However, he added that because stock prices of leading ODM companies have already broken through previous highs or risen significantly, chasing buys at this point carries high risk.

단일 브랜드로 보실 거면 에이피알과 달바글로벌만 보시는 게 좋고, ODM을 보신다면 코스맥스, 코스메카코리아, 한국콜마까지 보시는 게 좋습니다. 조금 더 안정적으로 보시려면 ODM 쪽이 좋지만, 지금 주가들이 많이 올라와 있습니다.

"If you are looking at single brands, it is best to look only at APR and d'Alba Global, and if you are looking at ODMs, it is good to look at Cosmax, Cosmecca Korea, and Kolmar Korea. If you want a more stable approach, the ODM side is better, but their stock prices have already risen a lot right now."

Among single-brand companies, APR and d'Alba Global were positively evaluated. In particular, d'Alba Global's stock price is positioned at the lower end of its box range, providing a relatively favorable entry opportunity from a short-term technical chart perspective.
