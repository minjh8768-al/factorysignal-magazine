Enhance Founder Lee Seung-hyun: 'AI Agents Are Collaborative Colleagues, Ushering in an Era of Execution Beyond Simple Chatbots'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 8 min read

Source: EO Korea · AI 에이전트로 매출 100억 만든 스타트업 | 인핸스 이승현. Summarised from that video.

Enhance founder Lee Seung-hyun emphasized that the era has arrived where artificial intelligence (AI) agents are utilized in all business tasks, and he presented key elements for agents to stably perform tasks and contribute to business. He stated that Enhance supplies industry-specific AI agents, with recent sales approaching 10 billion won and agents active in over 50 countries.

Inspiration and the Seeds of Entrepreneurship

From a young age, Lee was deeply interested in computers, enjoying programming and hacking. The most impactful moment for him was falling in love with the charm of artificial intelligence while watching the movie 'Her' during his university days. He recalled that the process of AI conversing with people, doing work for them, and even becoming friends felt beautiful and wonderful to him.

"사람과 이렇게 대화를 하면서 일도 대신 해주고 친구도 대주고 하는 그런 과정들이 제 눈에는 좀 아름답고 좀 멋있게 보였던 것 같습니다."

"The process of AI conversing with people, doing work for them, and even becoming friends, seemed beautiful and wonderful to me."

His interest in entrepreneurship was sparked when he attended a meeting with a medical professor, accompanying one of his professors. The medical professor wanted to develop a program to recognize carotid arteries, but his accompanying professor said it would be difficult. However, Lee replied that he thought he could do it, and he actually created a prototype with three friends, which received a good response. As this project generated profit, and he shared it with his friends, he decided to start a business, believing that 'creating something meaningful and distributing the profits' was fulfilling.

Learning and New Challenges at Samsung Electronics

Lee, who understood development but felt a lack of diverse knowledge in areas like product sales, marketing, human resources, and finance necessary for running a business, joined Samsung Electronics, which he considered unrivaled in these fields. He explained that starting as an engineer and experiencing various roles, including HR team, AI projects, and UX/design team, allowed him to accumulate knowledge in diverse areas, which later became a significant foundation for his entrepreneurship.

While working at Samsung Electronics, he saw his roommate achieve great success selling shoe boxes on Amazon Shopping. However, he observed that all processes, from sourcing goods from China to processing orders and responding to inquiries, were done manually, leading to a surge in workload. He questioned why so many repetitive tasks were performed manually and, with the idea of automating them, he founded Enhance in 2021 with 'automation' as its theme.

Agents Confront the Wall of Reality

Enhance's first project was to build a system where products could be searched from a Chinese factory and, with a single click, go through all logistics processes to arrive at a Korean warehouse. However, they encountered several problems during the actual implementation. First, there was a significant difference in quality between products seen in photos and the actual items, and numerous real-world details and human judgment factors, difficult to grasp with numerical data, were crucial. Second, contrary to expectations, small-volume purchases predominated rather than large-volume purchases, and large-volume buyers did not use their existing service.

When he visited Namdaemun Market directly to understand the reality of the wholesale distribution market, a merchant taught him an important lesson. This merchant sourced goods through a "sourcing uncle" (사입 삼촌) in China. This uncle provided trust and service beyond simple pricing, such as finding and delivering goods even from Korea if an item was out of stock, or procuring similar products if the desired one was unavailable. The merchant explained that if he used Lee's service, the uncle's help would cease, posing a significant risk to his business.

"가끔은 너희 물건이 쌀 때도 있을 텐데, 나는 중국에 '사입 삼촌'이라는 사람이 있어서 그 사람이 내 걸 다 사 주는데… 내가 너네 물건을 산다는 걸 알면 그 사입 삼촌이 더 이상 나를 그렇게 많이 안 도와줄 거야. 그러면 내 사업에 리스크가 너무 커."
<

"Sometimes your goods might be cheaper, but I have a 'sourcing uncle' in China who buys everything for me... If he knows I'm buying your goods, that sourcing uncle won't help me as much anymore. Then the risk to my business becomes too great."

Through this experience, he realized the importance of understanding and reflecting the entire customer workflow and the tacit knowledge hidden within it, rather than just theoretical price competitiveness. In a subsequent AI development project for ad optimization, he also learned that advertising methods differed slightly for each company and felt the pressing need for a system that could quickly create customized features for each enterprise.

Finding a Breakthrough with 'Ontology'

To solve this problem, Lee recalled the concept of 'Ontology' which he had dealt with during his time at the Samsung Electronics research lab. Ontology is a concept that developed alongside Tim Berners-Lee's creation of the World Wide Web (WWW) in the late 1980s, representing a knowledge system that systematically structures data and relationships for computers to understand. He explained the importance of ontology using the example of going to a car wash.

"세차장 가는데 차를 안 가져가면 안 되잖아요. 그러니까 AI는 세차장과 차, 그리고 나와의 관계가 어떻게 돌아가는지를 사실은 모르는 거죠. 근데 온톨로지가 있으면은 나는 세차장 갈 때 차를 씻기러 가는 것이기 때문에 차를 타고 가야 돼. 요런 개념을 구성해 놓는 거거든요."

"You can't go to a car wash without a car, can you? So, AI doesn't actually know how the relationship between a car wash, a car, and me works. But with ontology, I can configure the concept that 'when I go to the car wash, I go to wash my car, so I need to take a car.'"

Ultimately, the most crucial element in building a good ontology is to incorporate the knowledge of domain experts, especially tacit knowledge. Lee revealed that they have accumulated know-how in quickly structuring human knowledge into ontologies using AI. After the ontology was built, agents finally began to perform tasks stably and started providing real value to customers.

Customers inquired if this technology could be applied to other industries such as manufacturing, finance, and defense. Based on the judgment that it was technically scalable, Enhance is expanding its domain knowledge beyond commerce retail to various industrial sectors.

The Era of Human-AI 'Collaboration'

Lee emphasized that we are moving beyond an era where AI is simply considered a conversational chatbot, entering an 'agent era' where AI agents grasp customer intentions and carry out actual execution. He stated that on March 9, 2026, the 10th anniversary of the AlphaGo vs. Lee Se-dol match, he wanted to hold an event with Lee Se-dol to convey the message that the relationship between AI and humans has shifted from a competitive dynamic to a 'collaborative dynamic.'

For agents to perform tasks stably, he added, it is necessary to clearly explain our knowledge to them, define the problems they need to solve, and critically assess whether sufficient data is available for problem-solving, and if not, how to accumulate data and connect existing information.

"에이전트가 안정적으로 업무를 수행하기 위해서는 우리가 알고 있는 지식을 에이전트에게 잘 설명해 주는 것이 필요하고 어떤 문제를 해결하고 싶은가 에이전트로. 그리고 거기에 대한 우리가 데이터는 충분히 있는가… 데이터를 어떻게 쌓을 수 있는가, 있는 정보를 연결할 수 있는가. 이거를 기본적으로 점검해 보시는 것을 추천드립니다."

"For agents to perform tasks stably, it is necessary to clearly explain the knowledge we possess to the agent, define what problem we want the agent to solve, and assess whether we have sufficient data for that... how we can accumulate data, and how we can connect existing information. I recommend fundamentally checking these points."

Lee stated that Enhance aims to become an operating system (OS) company for the AI era, like Microsoft or Apple. He conveyed his ambition that Korean technology will leave an impactful footprint globally, and that a Korean company will achieve the No. 1 market share in the AI agent field.
