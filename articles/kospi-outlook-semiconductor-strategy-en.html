NH Investment's Baek Chan-gyu: Focus on Earnings-Driven Rotation, Chips, and AI Infrastructure in H2 Stock Market

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 한국경제TV · 7천선 찍고 다시 밀렸다... 코스피 상승세 꺾이나? | 백찬규 NH투자증권 자산관리컨설팅센터장ㅣ김치형 앵커. Summarised from that video.

Amid expanding stock market volatility, interest in the direction of the equity market and investment strategies for the second half of the year is growing. Baek Chan-gyu, head of the Wealth Management Consulting Center at NH Investment & Securities, diagnosed changes in liquidity and earnings momentum appearing in the stock market and outlined his outlook for future market trends.

Second-Half Market: Earnings-Driven Market Amid Easing Volatility

Baek predicted that the sharp market volatility experienced in July would ease somewhat through August and September. He analyzed that with policy and earnings momentum having paused, investors' attention is shifting back to corporate performance.

7월 급격한 변동성을 겪었고 8월과 9월은 모멘텀이 약간 부재한 상황이다. 실적 중심으로 투자자들이 다시 바라보면서 실적 시즌까지는 변동성이 줄어들고 평탄화될 것으로 기대한다.

"We experienced sharp volatility in July, and August and September lack momentum somewhat. As investors look back toward corporate performance, we expect volatility to decrease and flatten out until earnings season."

However, he pointed out that variables such as interest rates, oil prices, and inflation continue to act as factors capping the market's downside and limiting upside gains.

Shareholder Return Policies and Earnings Improvement Trends

Market expectations regarding shareholder return policies of major semiconductor companies, such as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, are also growing. Baek noted that while announcements of shareholder return policies could trigger corporate valuation re-ratings, the sequence of capital execution is just as important as the scale.

He explained that with various pending issues such as semiconductor cluster construction costs, continuous research and development (R&D) investments, and incentive payments, companies' capital execution priorities will determine the direction and strength of future stock prices. He added that even if stock prices undergo a short-term correction because the scale of shareholder returns falls short of heightened market expectations, stock prices will ultimately move based on earnings improvements, including revenue and profit margins.

Easing Semiconductor Concentration… Sector Rotation Strategy

As the concentration in the semiconductor sector seen in the first half comes to a pause, sector rotation is continuing across industries. Baek presented two criteria, 'current value' and 'future value,' when constructing portfolios to match these conditions.

현재 가치 측면에서는 반도체와 AI 인프라, 미래 가치 측면에서는 로봇, 우주항공, 2차전지, 원전까지 다양하게 보면서 순환매 때 비중을 조절할 필요가 있다.

"In terms of current value, we need to look at semiconductors and AI infrastructure, and in terms of future value, at a range of sectors including robotics, aerospace, secondary batteries, and nuclear power, adjusting weightings during sector rotations."

He recommended that if it is difficult to respond directly with individual stocks during rapid sector rotations, a strategy of using sector ETFs to sell in installments during rebounds and re-enter during corrections could be useful.

US Macro Variables and Second-Half Asset Allocation

Regarding US economic indicators and interest rate trends, he analyzed that weakening employment indicators and stabilizing inflation could be positive for the market in the short term, but if oil prices rise again, pressure for interest rate hikes could increase. Consequently, he predicted that the political and policy will displayed during the US midterm and presidential election environment this coming November will be a key point to watch.

Baek added that as economic growth slows and upward pressure on interest rates persists heading into the second half, risk management efforts are necessary, such as reducing the proportion of risk assets compared to the first half and adjusting the proportion of safe assets.
