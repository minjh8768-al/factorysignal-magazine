SK Chairman Chey Tae-won Vows to Build Honam Semiconductor Plant at 'Fastest Speed'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: KBS News · [풀영상] 뉴스9 : 한밤중 '먹통' 된 쿠팡·이츠…주문·배송 차질에 "캐시·쿠폰 보상" – 2026년 10월 9일(금) / KBS. Summarised from that video.

SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won visited the site of the Gwangju Military Airport and expressed his commitment to constructing semiconductor production facilities in the Honam region at the fastest possible speed. His personal visit to the site comes about three months after the government announced its Honam region mega-project.

Responding to Surging Semiconductor Demand... "Timing Is Key"

During his site inspection, Chairman Chey emphasized that boosting supply speed to meet surging semiconductor demand is an urgent matter.

하도 빠른 속도로 수요가 늘기 때문에 그걸 쫓아가는 공급 속도를 최대한 빨리 당겨야 하는 게 현재 상황입니다. 따라서 여기서 지을 수 있는 상황이 된다고 생각하면 가장 빠른 속도로 최대한 짓도록 하겠습니다.

"Demand is growing at such a rapid pace that the current priority is to accelerate supply as quickly as possible to keep up. Therefore, if conditions allow us to build here, we will construct as much as possible at the fastest possible speed."

Chairman Chey signaled a strong determination to execute the plan, stating that he would not miss the timing for breaking ground even if infrastructure construction is somewhat delayed.

설사 용수와 전력이 늦는다 하더라도 저희가 타이밍을 빨리 맞출 수만 있으면 먼저 들어가 버리겠다라는 생각을 합니다.

"Even if water and power supplies are delayed, if we can secure the timing quickly, my idea is to move in first."

Two-Thirds the Size of Yongin Complex... "Potential for Further Expansion"

Chairman Chey also outlined specific plans regarding the scale of the production facility at the Gwangju site and potential future expansion.

현재까지는 용인의 3분의 2 정도 사이즈의 계획을 갖고 있는데요. 땅이 더 있고 수요가 더 있다면 저희는 이쪽이 더 커질 것도 충분히 예상하고 있습니다.

"For now, we have a plan for about two-thirds the size of Yongin. If more land and demand are available, we fully expect this site to become even larger."

According to government announcements, the site spans 8.26 million square meters, with plans to invest 800 trillion won to construct four semiconductor fabs for SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics. However, the relocation of the military airport and the provision of infrastructure support such as water and power from local governments and the national government are cited as key variables that will determine the pace moving forward.
