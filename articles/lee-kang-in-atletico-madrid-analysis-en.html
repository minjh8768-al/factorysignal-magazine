Spanish Media: "Lee Kang-in Has the Talent to Transform Atletico's Attack... Evolution of the Team, Not a Griezmann Replacement"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: JTBC News · "이 분석 그대로 골 넣음ㄷㄷ"…'국뽕' 차오른다는 이강인 상황 #뉴스다 / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

Lee Kang-in scored on his Atletico Madrid debut and was named Man of the Match (MOM). Local Spanish media and football data analytics outlets focused on the tactical changes his addition will bring to the team's attack.

Local Media Analysis and Optimal Position

Spanish sports outlet Marca and football data analytics firm Driblab assessed that Lee possesses the talent to transform Atletico Madrid's attack. Data analysis indicated his optimal position is as a right winger who freely cuts inside, or as an attacking midfielder playing behind a central striker to organize the game. They explained that rather than a typical winger who beats opponents down the flank, he is closer to a linking midfielder who smoothly facilitates attacking build-up prior to shooting.

Link-Up Play Under Pressure and Unlocking Defensive Blocks

Lee's greatest strength was identified as his ability to stably manage the game even in tight spaces under pressure. Analyses highlighted his outstanding capacity to protect the ball between opposition defensive lines using his low center of gravity and to deliver precise passes that dismantle defensive blocks. This could prove to be a useful weapon in addressing Atletico Madrid's weakness of sometimes struggling against deep defensive blocks.

Comparison with Griezmann and Manager Simeone's High Praise

Local media pointed out that Lee should not be viewed as a simple direct replacement for club legend Antoine Griezmann. While Lee can contribute to attacking build-up, tempo control, and taking set pieces, it is impossible for him alone to replicate the scoring ability, leadership, and defensive impact Griezmann demonstrated. Therefore, they reported that Lee's addition should be seen as an opportunity for the entire team's attacking approach to evolve, rather than a simple one-for-one player replacement.

Atletico Madrid manager Diego Simeone highly praised Lee's dedicated attitude in a post-match interview.

Es un futbolista comprometido con el equipo, lo hemos visto en su selección, lo hemos visto en los equipos donde ha jugado anteriormente.

"He is a footballer committed to the team; we have seen it with his national team, and we have seen it in the teams he has played for previously."

Player Thoughts and Future Outlook

Having successfully completed his debut, Lee also expressed his gratitude to the team and his determination.

Estoy muy feliz. Desde el primer momento me quedé super, no sé cómo explicarlo la verdad. Muy contento y con muchas ganas de seguir trabajando fuerte para devolver la confianza que nos está dando.

"I am very happy. From the first moment, I felt amazing, to be honest I don't know how to explain it. I am very happy and eager to keep working hard to repay the trust being shown in us."

With an accurate analysis from Spanish media and a strong start, expectations are building over whether Lee can establish himself as a new core of Atletico Madrid's attack.
