Pensive Spearheads AI Evaluation Innovation in Silicon Valley: 'We Will Become a Credential Agency Beyond Universities'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: EO Korea · 2년간 샌프란시스코에서 살아남은 00년생 창업가의 새로운 도전ㅣ아메리칸드림2.0. Summarised from that video.

Yang Yoon-seok (양윤석), CEO of Pensive, an AI-based talent evaluation startup operating in San Francisco, revealed his story of launching a business in Silicon Valley and expanding his operations. Pensive started as an AI grading tool for universities and is expanding its scope into an AI-native evaluation and recruitment-linking platform for major companies.

Silicon Valley Challenge and the Success of University AI Grading Tools

CEO Yang stated that he decided to start a company in Silicon Valley to take on the biggest challenge in an environment where top talents gather. An alumnus of UC Berkeley, CEO Yang developed an AI grading system based on his interest in education and learning.

축구 선수면 누구나 프리미어 리그에 가고 싶고 야구 선수면 누구나 MLB에 가고 싶잖아요. 당연히 정말 뛰어난 창업가는 누구나 저는 실리콘밸리 창업을 하고 싶다라고 생각을 하고 있고

"Every soccer player wants to go to the Premier League, and every baseball player wants to go to MLB. Naturally, I believe every truly outstanding entrepreneur wants to start a business in Silicon Valley."

Pensive's first product is a grading tool for universities that drastically reduces grading time compared to before by having AI automatically recognize questions when a test paper PDF is uploaded. This service has been adopted by more than 100 universities in the U.S., including Harvard and UC Berkeley, and has been used to grade over 3 million exam questions.

Expansion into the Talent Evaluation Platform 'Builders'

Pensive went beyond university grading tools to introduce its second service, 'Builders,' which connects students with top companies based on evaluation data. Builders is structured to verify outstanding talent through AI-native evaluations and recommend them to major U.S. companies.

CEO Yang explained that while he initially faced difficulties in the enterprise sales process due to his tech-focused background, he mastered a sales strategy of listening to customer feedback as he gained meeting experience.

Overcoming Crises and the Vision as a Credential Agency

In the early stages of the business, the company experienced friction with university officials due to pushback against introducing AI into education and misunderstandings regarding data usage. Although they received acquisition offers worth tens of billions of won, CEO Yang and his team decided not to sell the company and to continue their challenge.

우리가 만든 AI 튜터가 정말 좋은 AI 튜터라고 피치를 하는 것보다 우리는 전 세계 최고의 기업들이 원하는 인재상과 그들을 평가하는 방법과 그걸 통과했을 때 너희를 연결시켜 줄 수 있는 크레덴셜링 파워를 가지고 있어

"Rather than pitching that the AI tutor we made is a really good AI tutor, we have the ideal talent profile that the world's top companies want, the methods to evaluate them, and the credentialing power to connect you once you pass that."

Pensive presented a vision to grow into a 'credential agency' that advances traditional university degree and resume verification systems by providing evaluation and certification standards trusted by global companies.
