KRW/USD Exchange Rate Plummets 150 Won in One Month: Causes and Investment Strategies

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: JTBC News · '쑥' 올랐다가 '뚝' 떨어진 환율…전문가가 짚은 '핵심 요소' #이주엔머니 / JTBC 장르가 머니. Summarised from that video.

Recently, the KRW/USD exchange rate stopped its steep rise and plummeted nearly 150 won in a single month, exhibiting extreme volatility. As the average daily fluctuation range exceeded that of the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic and approached levels seen during the 2009 global financial crisis, attention is focused on the trajectory of the exchange rate and its impact on the real economy and stock market.

Volatility at Global Financial Crisis Levels: Background to the Plunge

Experts analyzed that a complex mix of domestic and international factors was behind the recent sharp drop in the KRW/USD exchange rate. The Bank of Korea's hints at further interest rate hikes, foreign exchange authorities' active use of foreign reserves and dollar-selling policy, and verbal intervention by the U.S. Department of the Treasury stimulated selling pressure in the market.

It was also pointed out that cooperation with the U.S. to defend the Japanese yen, specifically dollar supply measures utilizing the U.S. Federal Reserve's FIMA repo facility, contributed to stabilizing Asian currency values.

미국 국채를 맡기면 달러를 빌려주겠다고 해서 국채가 시장에 풀리지 않고 일본은 달러를 조달할 수 있게 되었다.

"By offering to lend dollars if U.S. Treasuries were deposited, Treasuries were not released onto the market, and Japan was able to procure dollars."

Impact of Strong Exports and Shift to 24-Hour Foreign Exchange Trading

Domestic factors include strong exports centered on semiconductors and large inflows of dollars. In particular, SK Hynix raised approximately 26.5 billion dollars (about 40 trillion won) through a U.S. Nasdaq listing, bringing a massive dollar supply effect to the local foreign exchange market.

In addition, as the domestic foreign exchange market transitioned to a 24/7 trading system, the structure where speculative forces drove exchange rates in the overnight offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) market improved, and currency liquidity was secured, contributing to exchange rate stabilization.

Widening Exchange Rate Volatility: How Individual Investors Should Respond

Experts warned that the phenomenon of increasing volatility itself, rather than a simple rise or fall in the exchange rate, heightens economic uncertainty. This is because it makes it difficult for companies to formulate investment and import plans, and can weigh heavily on households' perceived inflation burden.

Regarding strategies for retail investors, experts advised distinguishing between dollar assets and direct investments in the dollar itself. Caution is needed as investing in dollar assets exposes one to the dual risks of asset price fluctuations and exchange rate volatility.

단순히 환율만 보고 달러 자산에 들어가는 섣부른 판단을 하기보다는 환율 움직임을 확인하며 보수적으로 접근해야 한다.

"Rather than making a hasty decision to enter dollar assets simply by looking at the exchange rate, one should approach conservatively while checking exchange rate movements."

However, they mentioned that simple dollar purchases during exchange rate downturns are necessary for portfolio diversification as part of risk management.
