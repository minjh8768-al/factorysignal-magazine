Property Value-Centered Tax Reform Plan Announced: Strengthening Protection for Owner-Occupiers and Taxes on Ultra-Luxury Homes

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · [속보] 세제개편안, 투기·다주택자 과세 강화: 부동산 시장에 미칠 영향 [즉시분석]. Summarised from that video.

A tax reform plan centered on easing the tax burden for single-home owner-occupiers while strengthening taxation on multi-homeowners and owners of ultra-high-value homes has been presented. Aimed at alleviating polarization in the real estate market and enhancing tax equity, the proposal encompasses an overall overhaul of holding taxes and capital gains tax.

Protecting Owner-Occupiers and Tightening Taxation on Ultra-Luxury Homes and Multi-Homeowners

According to the announced tax reform plan, the comprehensive real estate holding tax base deduction for single-home owner-occupiers will be raised from the current 1.2 billion won to 1.4 billion won. In addition, the special deduction limit on capital gains tax for long-term holding will be significantly expanded for single-home owners who have resided in their property for 10 years or more. Conversely, for non-resident homes or multi-homeowners holding properties for investment purposes, deduction benefits will be reduced and taxation will be tightened.

The criteria for levying the comprehensive real estate holding tax will also shift from the number of homes owned to the combined total home value. As a result, applicable tax rates will increase as the total value of owned properties rises, regardless of mere multiple-home ownership, which is expected to increase the tax burden in areas with a concentration of ultra-high-value homes.

Raising Fair Market Value Ratio for Holding Tax and Overhauling Special Deductions for Long-Term Holding

The fair market value ratio used to determine the tax base for the comprehensive real estate tax will also be raised in phases. Plans call for raising the ratio from the current level of 60% to 70%, and up to 80% for owners of 3 or more homes, bringing the tax base closer to officially declared prices.

The special capital gains tax deduction system for long-term holding will likewise be restructured to favor residency duration over holding period. The holding deduction rate will be gradually reduced or abolished, shifting focus toward the residency deduction rate, thereby reducing tax benefits for investment properties that are not owner-occupied.

Real Estate Market Outlook Intertwined with Financial and Monetary Regulations

Analysts suggest that changes in real estate market transactions are inevitable as financial policies tightening loan limits and interest rate conditions operate simultaneously alongside this tax reform proposal. With dampening buyer sentiment and property lock-in phenomena combining, market price trends may experience temporary distortion and fragmentation.

However, observers note that such adjustments may be temporary phenomena arising during the implementation of tax reforms and loan regulations, and whether long-term market trends change will require continuous monitoring of the National Assembly legislative process and additional economic conditions.
