Yong Hye-in Remains Silent Amid Backlash Over Holding Dual Roles as Lawmaker and Minister

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · "용혜인, 하나를 택한다면 의원직 지켜야" / SBS / 성민종합정치 / 주영진 뉴스브리핑. Summarised from that video.

Criticism is pouring in from political circles and the media as Yong Hye-in, nominee for Minister of Gender Equality and Family, expressed her intention to join the cabinet while retaining her seat as a proportional representation member of the National Assembly.

Media Editorials and Political Circles Focus Criticism

Major newspapers strongly criticized nominee Yong, pointing out that after securing proportional representation seats twice through satellite party systems, she is now seeking to concurrently serve as a minister. Critics point out that she is refusing to resign from her legislative post out of concern that her political party, the Basic Income Party, would be relegated to an extra-parliamentary party and see its party subsidies cut significantly if she steps down.

Avoiding Position Statement in Response to Press Questions

Nominee Yong was questioned by reporters at the National Assembly regarding the controversy over proportional representation succession and critical sentiments within the ruling camp, but she moved on without giving a clear answer. She also did not directly respond to questions regarding qualifications over her lack of legislative sponsorship performance.

Past Precedents and Dual-Role Customs

In the past, when proportional representation National Assembly members were appointed as cabinet ministers, they customarily resigned from their legislative seats. Appearing on broadcast programs, former senior reporter Heo Min and senior reporter Seong Han-yong pointed out that simultaneously serving as a minister and a proportional representation lawmaker could face criticism for prioritizing party circumstances over public responsibility, and could trigger fairness controversies among the 2030 generation.

Ruling and Opposition Reactions and Outlook

People Power Party Representative Na Kyung-won continued her criticism via social media, questioning the intent behind the nomination. Studio panelists noted that as the controversy could intensify during the confirmation hearing process, the issue can only be settled if resolution comes from either the nominee herself or the ruling camp.
