"Earning 2 Billion Won with Just a Phone": Creator Shares Instagram AI Monetization Secrets

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 온도스토리 · "그냥 자유를 원하는 것 같아요" 누적 소득 20억을 말하는 플로우의 직업인터뷰 [온도스토리|부업의온도]. Summarised from that video.

A 25-year-old creator who started a side hustle with just a smartphone and reached cumulative earnings of 2 billion won has disclosed in detail their Instagram monetization structure and AI tool-based content creation methods.

Instagram View Bonus and Monetization Requirements

The creator verified a lifetime total income of approximately 1.722 billion won as of last year through a tax-linked income verification app, stating that total earnings up to this year reach around 2 billion won. They explained that unlike YouTube or TikTok, Instagram's bonus payout cycle based on view counts occurs twice a month, every 15 days.

The Instagram view monetization program is currently operational for users in only three countries worldwide: South Korea, the United States, and Japan. The basic requirements for monetization are being a South Korean national over 18 years of age and achieving 1 million cumulative views over the past three months. Upon meeting these conditions, a bonus income of around 500 won per 1,000 views is calculated.

인스타그램은 이미지 한 장만으로도 수익을 낼 수 있어 영상 편집 부담이 큰 타 플랫폼에 비해 진입 장벽이 훨씬 낮다.

"Instagram allows you to generate income with just a single image, making the barrier to entry much lower compared to other platforms where video editing is a heavy burden."

1-Minute Creation Method Using AI and Web Design Tools

The content creation method is also simplified. By using web design platforms like MiriCanvas to create templates on news or trending topics and combining them with images of public figures and topical issues gathered from Google, a card-news style post is completed in just one minute.

The caption text accompanying the post is automatically generated using customized ChatGPT (GPTs). Entering just the title and link of the original news article generates Instagram-optimized body text and hashtags in a matter of seconds, meaning the work is finished simply by copying and posting it to Instagram.

Generative AI Characters and Expanding Short-Form Video

In addition to image-based content, 3D character video creation techniques using generative AI were also introduced. High-resolution images are obtained by entering desired character traits and compositions as text into ChatGPT, and uploading them along with dialogue into a video-generation AI tool easily creates animated, conversational short-form videos.

An example was shared where a single video produced through such AI video content recorded over 5 million views and secured 40,000 followers. As the account grows, creators can even land major corporate ad sponsorships, earning additional revenue ranging from 800,000 won to 1.6 million won per post.

Emphasis on Execution and Market Scalability

Based on experience built from attempting various online side hustles since middle school, the creator mentioned the importance of having a sense to flexibly adapt to platform changes. Stating that because the monetization market is vast, others copying the methods will not affect personal earnings, they urged people not to hesitate to enter the market.

무엇이든 완벽하게 준비하려 하기보다 사진 한 장이라도 직접 만들어 올리는 작은 시도와 실행이 가장 중요하다.

"Rather than trying to prepare everything perfectly, the small attempt and execution of creating and posting even a single photo yourself is what matters most."
