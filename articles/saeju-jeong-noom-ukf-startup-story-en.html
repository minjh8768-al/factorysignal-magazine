"You Must Think 20 Times Harder Than Others to Succeed": Noom Co-Founder Saeju Jeong Shares Advice on Global Entrepreneurship

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: EO Korea · 한국인만 모르는 미국살이의 진짜 모습 | UKF, Noom 정세주. Summarised from that video.

Saeju Jeong, who founded healthcare company Noom in New York and built it into a global enterprise, shared the lessons he learned while starting a business abroad and the importance of community activities to build a Korean founder ecosystem.

Why He Left Korea's Success Formula for New York

Questioning the typical formula for success and tech tree set by Korean society, Jeong left for New York at the age of 25 in poverty. His goal was to break free from Korea's rigid evaluation criteria and test his potential in a broader market.

한국에서 전반적으로 통용되는 성공 방정식, 테크트리 같은 거, 저는 거기에 끼기에는 반항심이 많았던 것 같아요.

"I think I was too rebellious to fit into the overall success formula or tech tree that is commonly accepted in Korea."

During his early days in New York, Jeong endured tough conditions, living in a single basement room and making a living through part-time jobs. To resolve visa issues, he enrolled in a community college, pursuing his studies and his business simultaneously.

Solid Confidence Built on Deep Consideration

Jeong emphasized that founders must think through things much more deeply than others to overcome the uncertainty and loss of self-esteem experienced in the early stages of a startup. Even while providing CardioTrainer, the predecessor to Noom, and growing it into the world's No. 1 application, he constantly took time to ask himself questions and refine his ideas.

고민을 남들보다 10배 20배 이상 해야 돼요. 그렇게 해야지 우리 제품을 사가는 클라이언트나 우리 회사를 보고 투자하시는 분들께서도 마음이 놓이고 우리를 신뢰하겠죠.

"You have to think 10 to 20 times harder than others. Only then will clients buying our product or investors looking at our company feel at ease and trust us."

He explained that true confidence and trust are formed only when the depth of reflection on the business model and vision allows one to perfectly answer sharp questions from investors and customers.

UKF: A Korean Founder Network Reducing Trial and Error

Jeong co-founded United Korean Founders (UKF) with Lee Ki-ha (이기하) to foster a Korean entrepreneur ecosystem and create a community of mutual growth. The goal is to help Korean entrepreneurs taking on the global market share experience and know-how, ensuring they do not fight a lonely battle alone and reducing unnecessary trial and error.

At UKF events, senior entrepreneurs, angel investors, and experts in various fields participate as voluntary helpers, offering active networking and advice between seniors and juniors. Jeong expressed hope that more Korean entrepreneurs will build solidarity with one another on the global stage and expand their positive influence.
