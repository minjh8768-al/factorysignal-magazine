KIA Tigers Reclaim 4th Place as Oller Leads Wins and Lee Eui-lee Shines in Bullpen

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: KBS NEWS 광주전남 · [스포츠7] ‘위닝시리즈’ KIA, ‘1경기 차’ 4위…3위 맹추격 / KBS 2026.08.18.. Summarised from that video.

KIA Tigers Reclaim 4th Place with Series Wins Over Samsung and Doosan

The KIA Tigers returned to fourth place in the league after taking back-to-back series wins against the Samsung Lions and Doosan Bears in games that resumed following the heatwave break. They built positive momentum by recording four wins and two losses during a six-game home stand.

Ace Oller made two starts, winning both to take the sole league lead with 11 wins on the season. Appearing out of the bullpen, Lee Eui-lee delivered an impressive performance by pitching a fastball that topped out at 158 km/h alongside stable control to record his first career save.

Gwangju FC Players Praised for Rescuing Driver Following Tough Loss to Pohang

In professional football, Gwangju FC dominated the match against Pohang Steelers with 11 shots on target out of 15 total attempts, but fell 1-2 after being thwarted by the opposing goalkeeper's saves.

Despite the defeat, a good deed by players on their way back from the away match came to light. Shin Chang-moo and Fridjonsson spotted a burning vehicle and stepped in to rescue the driver. Shin Chang-moo stated that he simply did what any citizen should do, adding that he hopes to bring joy to fans next time with news of a victory.

Women's Pro Volleyball Team SOOP Supers Prepares for Season Under Manager Kim Se-jin

New women's professional volleyball team SOOP Supers is building its roster under the guidance of manager Kim Se-jin. Following the recruitment of Go Eui-jeong and Seo Ji-hye, the team continues to strengthen its squad through ongoing trades.

SOOP Supers will begin their league schedule with an away match against Heungkuk Life, followed by their home opener against Korea Expressway Corporation.
