Approval Rating Drops to Record Low of 32%: Trump Goes All Out Ahead of US Midterms with Dividend Promise and Insurrection Act Card

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: KBS News · [이슈] 지지율 ‘폭락’ 트럼프… ‘탄핵 초비상‘/한달 뒤 중간선거 ‘폭망각“ 판세 분석 /2026년 10월 5일(월)/KBS. Summarised from that video.

With the US midterm elections just one month away, US President Donald Trump's job approval rating has fallen to 32%, the lowest level since he took office. As public sentiment worsens due to surging energy prices from a prolonged war in Iran and high inflation, President Trump is making all-out efforts to rally his base by embarking on a nationwide campaign tour focused on Republican strongholds.

Wavering Republican Strongholds and Record-Low Approval Rating

The US midterm elections, to be held on November 3, will contest 35 of the 100 Senate seats and all 435 House seats. Currently, Republicans hold a slim majority with 53 seats to the Democrats' 47 in the Senate, and 218 seats to 214 in the House.

However, recent opinion polls show close races with Democrats in six Republican-held Senate states—Alaska, Texas, Iowa, Ohio, North Carolina, and Maine—with some areas like North Carolina shifting in favor of the Democrats. As 47% of US voters cited the cost of living as their top voting priority, the Consumer Price Index in August rose 3.4% compared to the same period last year.

Controversy Over $5,000 Per Person Dividend Promise and $500 Refund Plan

To overcome his falling approval ratings, President Trump has unveiled a series of unconventional cash assistance promises. After promising to pay a dividend of $5,000 per adult if the Republican Party retains control of both the Senate and the House, he announced a plan to refund fees totaling $500 million to 1 million Obamacare enrollees at $500 per person.

President Trump claimed he was returning fees that were overcharged during the Biden administration.

They were forced to pay excessive fees totaling at least 500 million dollars. The last administration knew about this and they studied it, but they kept the money.

"They were forced to pay excessive fees totaling at least 500 million dollars. The previous administration knew this and investigated it, but did not return the money."

However, Democratic Representative Robert Garcia strongly criticized President Trump's promise for lacking a funding plan.

This is the biggest liar to ever serve in government period... The dividend is not happening. He promised a dividend on tariffs, that didn't happen.

"President Trump is the biggest liar to ever serve in government. He said he would return tariff revenue to the public as a dividend, but that never actually happened."

Hints of Invoking the Insurrection Act and Full-Scale Campaigning

President Trump toured traditionally strong Republican regions such as Tennessee and Ohio, mentioning even the possibility of invoking the Insurrection Act, which allows the deployment of the military under the pretext of maintaining law and order and ensuring fair elections. In an interview with the US news magazine Time, when asked whether he would rule out declaring a state of emergency, he answered, "I'm not ruling anything out or confirming anything."

In addition, he appealed for support for Republican candidates, warning that if Democrats win a majority in the House, they will launch hearings, intense investigations, and impeachment proceedings against him.

Clashes with the Press and White House Press Access Bans

Meanwhile, as the White House banned entry to critical news outlets such as CNN, MSNow, and Politico, the affected media organizations filed constitutional lawsuits claiming their freedom of the press was violated. In response, not only ABC, CBS, and NBC, but also the pro-Trump Fox News suspended participation in the White House press pool, leading to a situation where White House video feeds to major broadcasters were cut off.

President Trump criticized these outlets as "third-rate clowns" and responded by broadcasting state-controlled programs 24 hours a day through the White House's own YouTube channel, "TRUMP TV."

I think I have an obligation not to let fake news... They're very bad for the country, very bad for national security. So I have that obligation.

"I believe it is my duty not to let fake news into the White House. Because they are very harmful to the country, and very harmful to national security."

Midterm Election Outlook and Prospects

Experts predict that these midterm elections will be the biggest battleground, determining not only the balance of power in Congress but also the momentum for the remainder of President Trump's term and his political fate. The White House Counsel's Office is reportedly preparing measures to respond to various hearings and investigations in case the Democrats retake the House.
