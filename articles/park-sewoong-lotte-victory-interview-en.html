Park Se-woong Earns First Win in 83 Days: "Pitched More Boldly After Manager's Advice"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS Sports · 83일 만에 웃은 박세웅, 김태형-이순철이 한 조언은? | 인터뷰 | 2026 KBO리그 (26.09.08). Summarised from that video.

Lotte Giants pitcher Park Se-woong shared his thoughts on becoming the winning pitcher for the first time in 83 days and explained what prompted him to shift his pitching strategy following advice from manager Kim Tae-hyoung during the game.

First Win in 83 Days... "I Wish I Had Pitched Better"

Park led his team to a 6-3 victory over the NC Dinos, throwing 5 innings with 4 strikeouts and 3 runs allowed. This marked his first win in 9 games and 83 days.

In a post-game interview, Park reflected on earning the long-awaited win.

승리 투수가 돼서 기쁘기는 한데 조금 더 좋은 투구를 하고 승리 투수가 됐으면 좀 더 좋았을 것 같다는 생각이 많이 든다.

"I am happy to become the winning pitcher, but I strongly feel it would have been better if I had achieved it with a better pitching performance."

Manager Kim Tae-hyoung's Advice and the Shift to Aggressive Pitching

Park pitched well in the first and second innings, but faced difficulties in the third when a combination of walks and hit batters led to runs allowed and an increased pitch count. After the third inning, manager Kim Tae-hyoung called Park over and instructed him to pitch more boldly.

Park relayed his conversation with the manager at that moment.

감독님께서 맞더라도 들어갔어야 했는데 왜 자꾸 볼을 던져서 피해 가는 것처럼 피칭을 하느냐며, 좀 더 과감하게 들어갔으면 좋겠다고 말씀을 해주셨다.

"The manager told me that even if I get hit, I should have attacked the zone, asking why I kept throwing balls as if trying to avoid contact, and said he wished I would pitch more boldly."

Following the manager's feedback, Park faced batters with a more aggressive approach starting from the fourth inning, producing effective results by utilizing the area around the strike zone.

Resolution for the Remainder of the Season... "Will Pitch Simply and Straightforwardly"

When asked what kind of mindset he would show in his remaining regular season appearances, Park emphasized a simple mindset.

He pledged to maintain an aggressive approach in his remaining starts, stating his belief that good results will follow when he keeps his thinking easy and simple while pitching boldly.
