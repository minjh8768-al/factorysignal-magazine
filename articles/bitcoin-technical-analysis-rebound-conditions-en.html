5 Conditions for Bitcoin's 'Real Rebound'... "Recovery of 64.5K and Trading Volume Are Key"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 차트보는 두더지 · 비트코인, 꼭 보세요.. Summarised from that video.

As Bitcoin seeks a rebound, an analysis suggested that multiple technical conditions must be met for further gains to be valid. Chart analyst Dudeoji (두더지) presented five key conditions during a briefing that will determine the possibility of Bitcoin's rise.

Breakout of 66K at Upper Boundary of Long-Term Triangle and Support Zones

The first condition cited was the need to break above the 66K level, the upper boundary of the long-term triangle pattern. It was analyzed that for a direct rise following the end of Wave 4 in Elliott Wave theory without a Wave 5 decline, trading volume must surge to strongly break through the upper boundary, but current volume is insufficient, making a short-term decline highly likely.

The second and third conditions involve defending support zones. Beneath the 67–70K FVG (Fair Value Gap) and 70.9–71.7K order block resistance, the current short-term order block support zone of 63.7–62.2K must not be broken under any circumstances. Based on Fibonacci retracements, the short-term 0.5 level at 63.8K and the medium-term 0.5 level at 62.2K serve as key support lines.

Recovery of Daily Moving Averages and Accompanied Trading Volume

The fourth condition is that prices must be pushed back up to 64.5K in the short term. This is because with candles closing below the daily moving averages (5-day, 10-day, and 20-day lines), failure to climb back above the 5-day line could form a dead cross, continuing the downward trend.

오늘밤 64K 사수와 64.5K까지 말아 올리느냐 이 두 가지가 중기적인 추세의 방향을 결정 저을 것이라고 예상을 하고 있습니다.

"We expect that these two factors—defending 64K tonight and pulling the price back up to 64.5K—will determine the direction of the medium-term trend."

The fifth and final condition is mandatory accompanying trading volume. Since a divergence occurred where prices rose while trading volume declined, the analysis noted that if volume does not surge, short-term support lines could easily break again.

Downside Open to 56K If Conditions Are Not Met

The analyst pointed out that if the 63.8K and 62.2K support lines are breached, the probability of a decline toward the upper boundary of the 60K order block and the lower boundary of the triangle at 56K increases significantly, in line with the originally anticipated downward movement. Consequently, whether a recovery to 64.5K accompanied by volume occurs will determine the medium-term trend.
