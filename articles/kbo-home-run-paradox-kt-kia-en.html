KT's Race to Top Spot Despite Lowest Home Runs and Kim Do-young's Bid for Youngest 40-Home Run Mark: KBO's 'Home Run Paradox'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: KBS News · 홈런 꼴찌가 사상 첫 1위? 홈런에 얽힌 사연 [뉴스in뉴스] / KBS 2026.08.24.. Summarised from that video.

As the professional baseball regular season nears its end, competition among top teams is fierce. From an unprecedented situation where the team with the fewest home runs leads the league, to a star player challenging the record for the youngest to hit 40 home runs, various home run-related stories are heating up both the mound and the batter's box.

Will Last-Place Home Run Team KT Write a New Milestone After 44 Years?

KT Wiz, currently leading the regular season standings, remains at the very bottom in team home runs. In the 44-year history of the KBO League, there has never been an instance of a team with the fewest home runs finishing the regular season in first place.

Sports reporter Han Seong-yoon (한성윤) noted the possibility of a last-place home run team finishing in first place during a broadcast:

홈런 꼴찌 팀이 정규리그 1위를 차지하는 것은 지금까지 단 한 번도 없었습니다. 만약 KT가 홈런 최하위이면서 정규시즌 1위까지 차지하게 된다면 이것은 1992년 롯데를 넘어서는 더 대단한 기록입니다.

"There has never been a case where the team with the fewest home runs took first place in the regular season. If KT finishes in first place while having the lowest home run total, it would be an even greater record than Lotte's in 1992."

Despite a sharp drop in home runs due to injuries in their core lineup, KT ranks fourth in run production. They maintain the top spot based on a balance of offense and defense without any standout offensive statistics. While the 1992 Lotte Giants (68 team home runs) won the Korean Series despite having the fewest team home runs, achieving first place in the regular season remains unprecedented.

KIA's Kim Do-young Seeks Youngest 40-Home Run Record Ahead of Lee Seung-yuop

KIA Tigers superstar Kim Do-young is on the verge of setting the record for the youngest player to reach 40 home runs. Kim hit his 38th home run of the season, leaving him just two short of the 40-home run mark.

Kim has about two weeks left to surpass Lee Seung-yuop's record for the youngest player to hit 40 home runs (22 years, 11 months, 5 days), set in 1999. Powered by Kim's explosive home run pace, KIA is also aiming to return to first place in team home runs for the first time in 22 years.

Samsung Holds Second Place Despite Drop in Team Home Runs

The Samsung Lions, who boasted the top spot in team home runs by a wide margin over the past two years, face the exact opposite situation this year. With Diaz's home run count plummeting to 19 after hitting 50 last year, Samsung's team home run ranking dropped to fourth place.

However, despite the drop in home runs, Samsung is running second in the regular season and battling KT for the lead. Instead of relying on home runs, they have driven up results by balancing pitching and hitting. The battle for first place between home run-starved KT and slugging club Samsung is expected to heat up further during the upcoming three-game weekend series.
