“AI Makes the Decisions, Humans Stay on the Edge”: Jack Dorsey’s 40% Layoffs and Organizational Redesign Experiment

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 티타임즈TV · “AI가 조직의 중심, 사람은 가장자리에서 일한다”는 잭 도시. Summarised from that video.

Block Cuts 4,000 Jobs Despite Strong Performance

Block, an American fintech company led by Twitter co-founder Jack Dorsey, has executed a restructuring plan cutting 4,000 employees, representing 40% of its total workforce. This layoff is not due to deteriorating performance. As of 2025, Block's gross profit increased by 17% year-on-year, and gross profit in its personal money transfer service, Cash App, rose 33%, demonstrating smooth sailing.

Jack Dorsey explained that the real reason for this headcount reduction is a fundamental redesign of the organizational structure following the full-scale introduction of internal AI agents. Block has launched an experiment to test the hypothesis that utilizing AI agents can quadruple its gross profit per employee target from the current level of $500,000 to over $2 million.

“From Hierarchy to Intelligence”… AI Replaces Management's Coordination Role

Right after the layoffs, Jack Dorsey published an organizational theory paper titled “From Hierarchy to Intelligence” alongside Roelof Botha, former head of Sequoia Capital. The paper attributes the necessity of hierarchical structures in organizations over the past 2,000 years to human cognitive limits, where the maximum number of people one person can manage is only about eight.

The paper argues that if AI, as digital intelligence, can replace the tasks of distributing information vertically and horizontally—such as passing information, coordinating schedules, and delegating instructions, which middle managers have handled until now—the hierarchical structure itself becomes unnecessary.

"AI and systems make the decisions, while humans work on the edge."

The 4 Layers Composing an AI-Native Organization

The new organizational structure proposed by Block consists of four layers from the bottom up: Capabilities, World Model, Intelligence Layer, and Interfaces.

  • Capabilities: Foundational financial function pieces of fintech services such as payments, loans, card issuance, and banking.
  • World Model: Composed of a “Company World Model,” where decision-making and team progress are accumulated in text, and a “Customer World Model,” which learns customer transaction data to predict financial status.
  • Intelligence Layer: The AI decision-making domain that proactively suggests necessary loans or customized savings plans to customers on its own based on the World Model.
  • Interfaces: Channels where customers meet services, such as the Square app or Cash App.

Jack Dorsey emphasized that among these, a “Company World Model” that grasps real-time status by learning internal meeting minutes and decision records must be in place to enable hierarchical innovation without middle managers.

3 Human Roles in an AI-Centric Organization

As AI stands at the center of organizational operations and coordination, the roles that humans take on are also redefined into three categories.

  • Individual Contributor: Experts who perform development and work by making autonomous judgments based on the context provided by the World Model without waiting for instructions from above.
  • Directly Responsible Individual (DRI): A temporary lead empowered to pull resources from other teams without consultation to solve a specific challenge. Once the task is resolved, the position disappears.
  • Player-Coach: A role that performs hands-on tasks such as writing code and designing models directly instead of checking meetings or reporting tasks, while simultaneously helping colleagues grow.

Rebuttals and Challenges Regarding Technical Maturity and Cost

The market showed an enthusiastic response to Jack Dorsey's unconventional attempt, with stock prices surging over 20% immediately after the announcement, but cautious views are also raised. While the Customer World Model and proprietary AI agents are operational, the “Company World Model,” which is the prerequisite for eliminating hierarchy, is still in the building phase.

Additionally, research indicates that while AI adoption reduces labor costs, overall cost savings may be minimal due to new operational costs for AI and maintenance costs for engineers. The key to this experiment is whether AI technology can overcome past precedents where managerless organizations attempted at Zappos, GitHub, Medium, and others failed due to information silos and coordination breakdowns.
