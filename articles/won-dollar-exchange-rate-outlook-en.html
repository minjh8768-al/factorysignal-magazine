Kim Young-ik: KRW/USD Exchange Rate to Rebound Short-Term Before Falling; Increase Weight in Korean Stocks Over US Stocks

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 김영익의 경제스쿨 · 지금 같은 달러 약세, 주식시장에는 이런 변화가 나타나고 있습니다 | 김영익 교수. Summarised from that video.

Sharp Drop in KRW/USD Exchange Rate and Medium- to Long-Term Decline Outlook

Amid the recent sharp drop in the KRW/USD exchange rate from the 1,550-won level to the 1,380-won level, an analysis suggested that the undervaluation of the Korean won is being resolved. Professor Kim Young-ik (김영익) projected that a decline in the US dollar index and net purchases by foreign investors drove the exchange rate down, and that the value of the won will continue its upward trend over the medium to long term.

Professor Kim raised the possibility of a long-term decline in the dollar index, noting that the US dollar index accounts for more than half of the fluctuations in Korea's exchange rate.

중장기적으로는 계속 환율이 하락할 가능성이 높은데요. 단기적으로는 9월, 10월 미국 금융 시장이 불안해지면서 일시적으로 상승할 가능성이 높습니다.

"In the medium to long term, there is a high possibility that the exchange rate will continue to fall. In the short term, however, it is highly likely to rise temporarily as US financial markets become unstable in September and October."

US Economic Structure and Foreign Exchange Environment Driving Dollar Weakness

Pressure on the value of the dollar to fall is growing as the net external debt of the US reaches approximately 28 trillion dollars and fiscal deficits accumulate. Major economic experts, including Ray Dalio, have previously warned that persistent US fiscal deficits and increased issuance of government bonds could trigger currency devaluation and an economic crisis.

The outlook for a turn toward a stronger Japanese yen and South Korea's current account surplus are also cited as factors driving won strength. If the yen turns strong due to the narrowing interest rate differential between the US and Japan and interventions by foreign exchange authorities, the value of the won will rise alongside it. The fact that South Korea's real interest rate has become higher than that of the US, as well as a large current account surplus driven by the semiconductor boom, are also elements supporting a decline in the KRW/USD exchange rate.

Possibility of a Short-Term Rebound in September–October and Impact on KOSPI

However, the assessment is that the exchange rate could temporarily rebound in the short term. As a result of applying the HP filter, a statistical trend analysis, the recent exchange rate has fallen excessively relative to its trend. Around September to October, when shrinking US consumption and political uncertainty overlap, if the dollar index rises due to preference for safe-haven assets, there is a possibility that the KRW/USD exchange rate will exceed 1,400 won.

During a period of medium- to long-term exchange rate decline, the KOSPI market is expected to perform relatively well. When the KRW/USD exchange rate falls by 1%, the KOSPI tends to rise by an average of 0.7%.

그다음에 환율이 하락할 때는 수출주보다는 내수주들이 상대적으로 더 올랐었습니다.

"Furthermore, when the exchange rate fell, domestic demand stocks rose relatively more than export stocks."

Asset Allocation Strategy: Focus on Domestic Stocks and Domestic Demand Stocks

By sector, stocks related to domestic demand—such as finance, construction, steel, metals, and transportation and warehousing—showed higher elasticity during periods of exchange rate decline than export stocks such as electrical and electronics. Historically, during periods when the dollar index fell, returns on South Korea's KOSPI were higher than those of the US S&P 500 index.

Professor Kim suggested that as the trend of dollar weakness is highly likely to continue over the next five years, a strategy of relatively increasing the proportion of Korean stocks over US stocks in asset allocation would be advantageous.
