Trump Demands Allies Reimburse Iran War Costs, Heightening Pressure

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: JTBC News · '선거 이겨야 돼' 조급한 트럼프.."한국이 이상하네" 독촉 #뉴스다 / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

U.S. President Donald Trump has reiterated his stance that the massive war costs incurred during the armed conflict with Iran should be passed on to allies and other relevant nations. With a Congressional Budget Office study showing war costs have already exceeded 50 trillion won, the Trump administration, ahead of the midterm elections, is demanding defense costs for the Strait of Hormuz and military contributions, deepening the concerns of allies, including South Korea.

Iran War Costs Surpass 52 Trillion Won, Requiring Over 4 Trillion Won More Each Month

The U.S. Congressional Budget Office analyzed that the U.S. government's war costs since the armed conflict with Iran have reached an average of about 330 billion won per day, already topping a total of 52 trillion won. If the war continues, it is estimated that an additional budget of over 4 trillion won will be required each month.

President Trump demanded war cost reimbursement via social media, targeting countries that used the Strait of Hormuz without helping the United States.

The Countries of the World, which have been no help to us whatsoever, should, and will, reimburse the United States of America when this SCAM Conflagration is all over.

"The countries of the world, which have been no help to us whatsoever, should, and will, reimburse the United States of America when this SCAM conflagration is all over."

Beyond Troop Deployment Demands to 'War Bills': Pressure on Allies Intensifies

Experts analyzed that President Trump, ahead of the midterm elections, is putting pressure on allies to demonstrate to American voters that the country is not suffering losses. Park Hyun-do, a professor at Sogang University's Euro-MENA Research Institute, explained that it is an attempt to show results to voters while the United States is in a difficult situation.

In previous remarks, President Trump claimed that South Korea had not assisted in military operations in Iran. The South Korean government, through the National Security Council, has ruled out direct troop deployment for now, but is considering response measures such as non-military contributions or financial burden-sharing.

Maritime Blockade Crisis in Hormuz and Red Sea: Global Oil Prices Soar

Tensions are escalating in the Strait of Hormuz, with a Panama-flagged vessel catching fire after a drone strike, while Iran released a sanctions list of 77 vessels, including South Korean ships, threatening navigation. On top of this, the Middle East situation continues to deteriorate as Yemen's Houthi rebels seize key positions in the Red Sea and attack a Saudi Arabian airbase.

Consequently, global oil prices surged, with Brent crude futures surpassing $107 per barrel and West Texas Intermediate (WTI) topping $102. U.S. diesel prices also exceeded $6 per gallon, raising alarm bells over potential energy paralysis.

Trump Blames Zelenskyy for Rising Oil Prices

As the economic shock from the oil price surge materialized, President Trump blamed the rise in oil prices on Ukraine's attacks on Russian oil refineries.

Zelenskyy has to do one thing. He has to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia. Don't hit diesel fuel, because that's hurting the world.

"Zelenskyy has to do one thing. He has to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia. Don't hit diesel fuel, because that's hurting the world."

President Trump is attempting to overcome the political situation by reiterating claims that oil prices will soon fall once the war ends.
