President’s Approval Rating Drops to 40.2%, Hitting New Low for 6th Straight Week

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 뉴스TVCHOSUN · [핫라인] 李 지지율, 40%선 '위태'···리얼미터 "부동산·조희대·안보" 분석. Summarised from that video.

The President's approval rating for state affairs has fallen for the sixth consecutive week, setting a new low since taking office. Support for the ruling Democratic Party of Korea also showed a downward trend, narrowing the gap between the two major parties to within the margin of error.

Positive Evaluation of State Affairs at 40.2%… Decline for 6th Consecutive Week

According to public opinion poll results by Realmeter, positive evaluations of the President's performance in state affairs were calculated at 40.2%, down 2.8 percentage points from last week. This is the lowest figure since taking office, continuing a decline for six straight weeks. On the other hand, negative evaluations rose by 2.8 percentage points from the previous survey to record 56.9%, surpassing positive evaluations outside the margin of error.

Realmeter cited distrust in real estate policies, such as controversies over housing supply in Yongsan Park, along with controversies surrounding Supreme Court Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae, and security anxieties due to the scaling back of South Korea-U.S. joint military exercises, as major causes for the decline in approval ratings.

Close Race in Party Approval Ratings… Democratic Party Lacks Convention Effect

In the party approval rating survey, the Democratic Party of Korea recorded 41.9% and the People Power Party recorded 37.6%. While support for the Democratic Party fell 6.0 percentage points from the previous week, the People Power Party rose 4.5 percentage points, bringing the gap between the two parties into a close race within the margin of error.

It was analyzed that the Democratic Party failed to enjoy the so-called national convention effect, as its support fell despite having elected new leadership through its national convention not long ago.

Ruling Bloc Recognizes Situation and Deliberates on Countermeasures

Regarding the declining approval ratings, Park Sung-jun, senior spokesperson for the Democratic Party of Korea, acknowledged the gravity of the poll results and stated that internal situational awareness was being shared.

어떻게 보면 좀 심각한 수준이 된 거죠. 당대표 취임 이후에 우리가 앞으로 해야 될 일과 방향에 대한 것들에 대한 상황 인식에 대한 공유는 충분히 하고 있다, 이 정도 말씀드리겠습니다.

"In a way, it has reached a somewhat serious level. I will say this much: since the party leader took office, we are sufficiently sharing situational awareness regarding what we need to do and our future direction."

Within the ruling camp, positions were expressed to seek momentum for a rebound in approval ratings by establishing policies for public livelihood and strengthening consultations among the party, government, and presidential office through upcoming political schedules, such as the regular session of the National Assembly and lawmakers' workshops.
