Lee Seung-woo Swallows Disappointment After National Team Omission: "I'll Earn My Chance at My Club"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · '명단 탈락' 다음날 풀타임 활약.."늘 아쉬워" '솔직' 고백 모레노 반응? / SBS / 모아보는 뉴스. Summarised from that video.

Lee Seung-woo, who was left off the national football team roster, shared his honest feelings and expressed his determination to seize another opportunity through his performances for his club.

National Team Exclusion and Lee's Reaction

Speaking with reporters, Lee did not hide his honest emotions about being omitted from the national team roster. While expressing his disappointment, he emphasized that playing well for his club takes priority.

당연히 마음은 늘 똑같고, 그냥 아쉽다. 소속팀에서 더 잘해야 또 기회가 찾아오는 것이기 때문에 계속 좋은 모습을 보여드리고 싶다.

"Naturally, my mindset is always the same, and I'm just disappointed. Since another opportunity will only come if I do better at my club, I want to keep showing good performances."

Manager Roberto Moreno, serving as interim head coach of the national team, spared his words when asked about the reason for leaving Lee out during the announcement of the international match squad.

뽑히지 않은 선수에 관해 이야기하면 끝이 없다.

"There would be no end if I talked about players who weren't selected."

Manager Moreno's Selection Criteria and Tactics

When announcing the national team roster, Manager Moreno presented current ability and attitude as the key criteria for player selection. His policy is to prioritize current performance over past reputation or name value.

He also stated a preference for balanced players who excel in both attack and defense, as well as those with a strong desire for the national team. As his primary tactic, he explained that he would utilize a 4-4-2 formation after analyzing K League and global trends.

Club Performances and Determination for a National Team Return

Lee has maintained strong form in his club matches, driving the team to victory with brilliant dribbling and scoring ability. Even when coming on as a substitute, he has given his all to contribute to his team's comeback wins.

Lee continuously expressed his passion for the national team and the World Cup stage, adding that he would do his best at his club to prove his worth.

평생의 꿈이고 평생 가고 싶은 곳이기 때문에 좋은 폼을 유지하고 있다면 또 기회가 오지 않을까 생각한다.

"It's a lifelong dream and a place I want to go all my life, so I think if I maintain good form, another opportunity will come."
