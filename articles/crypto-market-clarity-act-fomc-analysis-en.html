Crypto Market Pauses Ahead of Clarity Act Vote and FOMC Decision: What's Next for Bitcoin, XRP, and Ethereum?

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 비자 스테이블코인 결제 1년 새 15배…진짜 결제시장으로 들어왔다. Summarised from that video.

Crypto Market Enters Pause Ahead of Clarity Act Vote and FOMC Decision

The virtual asset market has entered a wait-and-see mode ahead of major policy votes and interest rate decisions. Amid rising international oil prices stemming from conflicts between the United States and Iran and an upward trend in long-term U.S. Treasury yields weighing on risk assets overall, a series of major events that could decide the direction of the crypto market are scheduled.

On domestic exchanges, the price of Bitcoin is trading around 105 million won, down about 6% from its peak, while Ripple (XRP) has also fallen by around 10%. The market's attention is focused on the vote to end debate on the Clarity Act submitted to the U.S. Senate and the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting.

Clarity Act and Rate Decision Heighten Market Uncertainty

The Clarity Act is a bill that classifies virtual assets into securities and commodities and sets rules for the overall market structure, including stablecoins. Expectations are emerging that securing regulatory clarity could lay the groundwork for institutional capital inflows, but political differences remain over securing the 60 votes in the Senate required for passage. Anti-money laundering, consumer protection, and conflict-of-interest provisions remain key points of contention.

Meanwhile, ahead of the FOMC meeting, concerns over monetary tightening are growing as the possibility of a 0.25 percentage point interest rate hike is being raised. This is affected by surging oil prices and an increase in the Producer Price Index (PPI) reigniting inflation pressures. The market expects the future direction of virtual asset prices to depend on whether regulatory uncertainty is resolved and the Federal Reserve's interest rate stance.

Discussions on Stablecoin Payments and Taxation Gain Momentum

The scope of stablecoin utilization is rapidly expanding beyond virtual asset exchanges into real-world payment methods. As stablecoin payment volumes linked to credit card company payment networks increase significantly, competition among major projects targeting the real-world asset (RWA) tokenization and corporate treasury management (CFO) markets is also heating up.

However, domestically, ahead of the implementation of virtual asset taxation next year, controversy over taxing gains generated from stablecoin payments is emerging. Critics point out that if gains resulting from Korean won exchange rate fluctuations or premiums are considered disposition gains, the administrative burden on users and companies who must calculate acquisition and disposition prices for every payment could increase.

Trends in Major Cryptocurrencies Including Bitcoin, XRP, and Ethereum

Bitcoin is undergoing a structural transformation from an individual-centered speculative asset to a mainstream institutional asset where Wall Street institutional investors, listed companies, and governments lead supply and demand. Spot ETF capital inflows, reduced new mining supply due to the halving, and falling exchange reserves are forming a long-term supply shortage structure.

Ripple (XRP) continues to face the monthly issue of 1 billion escrow releases, but as a significant portion of the released volume is re-locked, the actual volume circulating in the market is understood to be limited to 200 million to 300 million per month. In addition, Ethereum is projected to benefit from institutional capital inflows as a core network of the tokenization and stablecoin ecosystem.
