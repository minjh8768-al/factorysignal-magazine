Rep. Park Sun-won Accuses Prosecution of Targeted Investigation Against Democratic Party, Calls Informant Allegations Fabricated

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: MBC강원영동NEWS · [분석1] "조모 씨의 협박과 정치검찰의 조작 기소"… 박선원 최고위원이 밝힌 한동훈 의원과 제보자 조 모 씨 실체 의혹의 진실. Summarised from that video.

Doubts Raised Over Whistleblower Status and Purity of Intentions

Democratic Party Representative Park Sun-won claimed that a Mr. Cho, referred to by former Minister of Justice Han Dong-hoon and others as a whistleblower, was not a legitimate informant, but someone who reported out of malice after failing to recover money.

Rep. Park pointed out that Cho began recklessly filing reports with the Anti-Corruption and Civil Rights Commission, the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, and the Supreme Prosecutors' Office after his demands for money—using threats to expose alleged illegal lobbying to cover business losses—were rejected.

존경하는 국민 여러분, 더불어민주당 최고위원 박선원 의원입니다. 현재 김승원 법무부 장관 후보자에 대한 온갖 부적절한 호도가 난무하고 있습니다. 이것의 본질에 대해 말씀드리고자 합니다.

"Respected citizens, I am Representative Park Sun-won, a Supreme Council member of the Democratic Party. Right now, all kinds of inappropriate misrepresentations regarding Minister of Justice nominee Kim Seung-won are rampant. I would like to address the essence of this matter."

Criticism of Prosecution's Biased Investigation and Digital Forensic Circumstances

Rep. Park criticized the prosecution, stating that despite being aware of Cho's use of undisclosed information and circumstances involving extortion, they designated and protected him as a whistleblower and used him as a tool for an investigation targeting the Democratic Party.

He also emphasized that during the initial search and seizure, over 20 names of Democratic Party figures were entered as search keywords for PC forensics while People Power Party lawmakers appearing in the audio files were excluded, revealing the true nature of political prosecution.

오로지 검찰은 민주당만을 타겟으로 사실상 정치검찰의 본성을 보여준 것입니다. 이러한 것은 관련자, 이 사건 피의자들로부터 나온 공통적인 주장입니다.

"The prosecution targeted solely the Democratic Party, effectively showing the true nature of political prosecution. This is a common claim coming from related individuals and suspects in this case."

Demands for Immediate Inspection at the Ministry of Justice Level

Rep. Park urged Acting Minister of Justice Lee Jin-soo to conduct a full re-examination of relevant 2023 documents stored on the servers of the Ministry of Justice and the Supreme Prosecutors' Office, and to form a team to verify any criminality.

He added that an immediate inspection must be conducted to prevent acts of evidence destruction by prosecution insiders in advance, including the leak of indictment plans.
