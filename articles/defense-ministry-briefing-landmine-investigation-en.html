Defense Ministry and JCS Prioritize Security and Verification in Minefield On-Site Investigations

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · "장관이 현장에 왜 군복 아닌 양복을?" 기자 질문에 "문제 안 돼" 신경전 '폭발' / SBS / 바로이뉴스. Summarised from that video.

The Joint Chiefs of Staff and the Ministry of National Defense detailed their positions during a regular briefing regarding the standards for disclosing mine-related statements raised during recent on-site investigations, the definition of unconfirmed minefields, the deployment of unmanned equipment, and operational security issues.

Clarification on Mine Statements and Investigation Standards

Regarding criticism that the criteria for disclosing mine-related investigations and statements were being altered after the fact, an official from the Joint Chiefs of Staff responded that the investigation was conducted and the results were disclosed because preliminary requirements were met on the premise of an objective and transparent explanation.

Regarding statements that additional unexploded mines were discovered, the official emphasized that field detection and cross-checking procedures were essential rather than making conclusions based on individual statements.

현장에서 보고 그 부분을 다시 한번 탐지를 하면서 이것이 맞는 것인지 대조도 해봐야 하고 여러 가지 절차가 필요하다.

"We need to observe it on-site, detect that area once more, cross-check whether it is correct, and go through various procedures."

Definition of 'Unconfirmed Minefield' and Use of Unmanned Equipment

Regarding the term "unconfirmed minefield," military authorities explained that it refers to areas where South Korean forces have not continuously operated since 1953, and where data such as past burial records or terrain changes have not been clearly verified.

Regarding the reason why unmanned equipment such as drones or robots was not deployed first, the military cited the fact that an operation to clear a search route for the UN Command's on-site investigation had to come first. A military official added that the necessity was reviewed based on satellite and imagery data, and coordination and operations were carried out as needed.

Non-Disclosure of On-Site Investigation Schedules and Operational Security

Regarding keeping the schedule of on-site investigations undisclosed in advance, the military explained that it was an operational security measure to minimize the risk of location identification and heightened surveillance posture during operations.

Regarding why the Minister of National Defense wore a suit instead of a military uniform during an on-site visit, a Ministry spokesperson explained that the minister moved directly to conduct an urgent field inspection immediately after taking office.
