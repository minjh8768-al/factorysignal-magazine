"I Endured 13 Months Without Pay": Growth Strategy of Ajeongdang CEO Kim Min-ki, Who Built a 100 Billion Won Business

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: EO Korea · 6년만에 300배 매출을 만든 압도적 성장전략 | 아정당 김민기. Summarised from that video.

Kim Min-ki, CEO of 'Ajeongdang', an all-around lifestyle platform covering internet, home appliance rentals, and moving services, revealed the trials of his early days in business and the secrets behind his rapid growth. Just six years after its founding, Ajeongdang is recording steep growth, surpassing 110 billion won in annual sales.

Starting a Business to Help His Father, Followed by 13 Unpaid Months

CEO Kim Min-ki originally had no intention of starting a business, but entered entrepreneurship while conceiving a direct-deal platform to help his father, who worked delivering goods at a market. To solve the middleman commission issues of established service companies, he developed a service that directly connected customers with service workers.

With no income in the platform's early launch phase, he endured 13 months without pay, researching the essence of marketing and customer behavior. After expanding into internet subscription services, he laid the groundwork for a rebound, recording sales of 8 million won in the first month, 17 million won in the second month, and 25 million won in the third month.

13개월 동안 돈 한 푼 받지 않고 계속 어떻게 마케팅해야 좋은지, 사람들이 좋아하는 본질이 무엇인지 연구하다가 14개월 차 때 첫 매출이 났습니다.

"Without receiving a single penny for 13 months, I kept researching how to market effectively and what essential elements people like, and generated our first sales in the 14th month."

Marketing That Builds Trust and 3 Key Elements of Customer Persuasion

To overcome public distrust during early Naver marketing, CEO Kim conducted A/B testing. After introducing a strategy of revealing the author's face and real photos, the conversion rate increased by about 20 times compared to before.

He emphasized that three core elements must be defined to win customers' hearts and leap into a top 1% company.

  • Eliminate reasons not to buy: Remove resistance factors that make customers hesitate to purchase.
  • Explain reasons to buy: Provide a clear justification for why they should use the service.
  • Lower the hurdle to act: Reduce thresholds in the conversion process, such as consultation and registration procedures.

Driving Operational Efficiency Through AI Replaceability and Handover Capability

As the organization grew, Ajeongdang applied its own review criteria to boost task productivity. It conducts work adjustments with every employee every three months, asking two questions.

네가 지금 하고 있는 업무가 AI로 대체가 가능한지, 인수인계가 가능한지 이 두 가지 질문입니다.

"Those two questions are: whether the work you are doing right now can be replaced by AI, and whether it can be handed over to someone else."

Tasks that can be handed over have their processes standardized, while areas that can be replaced by AI or repetitive operations are assigned to the development team to build automated systems. Through such efficiency improvements, CEO Kim explained that the company achieves high performance while reducing marketing costs by 70 to 80 percent compared to competitors.
