Payment Methods in the AI Agent Era: Why Stablecoins Instead of Credit Cards?

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 티타임즈TV · 원화 스테이블코인 늦어지면 AI 커머스, 글로벌에 먹힌다 (김용진 서강대 경영학과 교수). Summarised from that video.

An era of "AI agent commerce" is arriving, where AI technology goes beyond simple searches and recommendations to make decisions and execute payments on behalf of consumers. Consequently, stablecoins are drawing attention as a new payment infrastructure optimized for AI agents, moving beyond the existing credit card-centered payment network.

The End of Search and Payment Labor... The Emergence of AI Agent Commerce

Traditional online shopping was structured so that users directly typed products into search boxes, added them to shopping carts, and made payments. In contrast, AI agent commerce is a system where an individual's personal AI agent learns consumer preferences and environments to handle everything in one batch, from schedule management to product search, reservations, and payment.

Kim Yong-jin (김용진), a business professor at Sogang University, explained that AI agents will remember individual consumer behavior patterns and environments to provide optimal customized services, thereby replacing the search and payment labor previously performed by humans.

AI 에이전트는 나를 대신하는 디지털 집사다. 검색과 선택, 결제에 들어가는 모든 노동이 사라지고 지능형 위임 시대로 전환된다.

"An AI agent is a digital butler that acts on my behalf. All the labor that goes into searching, selecting, and paying disappears, transitioning us into an era of intelligent delegation."

Stablecoins Overcoming Credit Card Limitations... Supporting 24-Hour Autonomous Payment

Analysis suggests that as transactions between AI agents become active, existing credit card payment networks will reveal their limitations. This is because credit cards require humans to directly enter card numbers or undergo identity verification, along with fixed per-transaction fees and complex settlement stages.

Professor Kim pointed out that even if an AI agent operating 24 hours a day finds a product meeting specified conditions during the early morning hours, executing autonomous payment without human intervention is difficult under the credit card payment method. On the other hand, stablecoins have extremely low fees, and transaction conditions can be programmatically set through smart contracts, making them well-suited for autonomous automatic payments, he explained.

신용카드는 사람을 위한 결제 수단이다. AI 에이전트가 쓸 수 있는 결제 수단이 법제화되고 제도화되지 않으면 에이전트 커머스가 지체될 수 있다.

"Credit cards are a payment method for humans. If payment methods that AI agents can use are not legalized and institutionalized, agent commerce could be delayed."

Concerns Over Global Standard Leadership and Data Dependence

Global Big Tech and payment companies, including those in the United States, are rapidly establishing digital identity authentication and stablecoin payment standards. Concerns are emerging that if global companies dominate platforms and standards, domestic companies could bear fee burdens and fall into data dependence.

Professor Kim emphasized that as a global Big Tech alliance is already forming payment and authentication standards, South Korea must also expedite policy decisions to establish a response framework.

Proposal for a Dual Structure of CBDCs and Private Stablecoins

Discussions regarding the roles of central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) and privately issued stablecoins are also continuing. While CBDCs offer high stability, concerns are raised that personal anonymity could be compromised due to the central bank's ability to trace transactions, and technical innovation in the private sector could be hindered.

Professor Kim proposed that a dual structure is desirable, utilizing CBDCs for wholesale transactions between the central bank and commercial banks while allowing private stablecoins backed by 100% reserves for general private transactions and the commerce domain.
