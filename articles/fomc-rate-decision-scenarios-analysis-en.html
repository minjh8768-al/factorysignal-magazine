Four Scenarios Ahead of FOMC Rate Decision: "Hawkish Pause and Dovish Hike Would Be Positive for Markets"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 경제 읽어주는 남자(김광석TV) · 금리 동결이 오히려 ‘최악’? FOMC 4가지 시나리오 총정리 | 클로즈업 | FOMC 예상 시나리오. Summarised from that video.

As tensions mount in financial markets ahead of the announcement of the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting results, an analysis has presented four market scenarios based on the rate decision and the Federal Reserve's stance.

Rising US Treasury Yields and Pressure on Asset Markets

The US 10-year Treasury yield surpassed 5% for the first time since the 2007 financial crisis, weighing on the market. Two main factors are cited for the surge in Treasury yields. First, a supply and demand imbalance occurred as selling by major countries such as China continued against the backdrop of the US government's large-scale Treasury issuance and fiscal spending. Second, increased defense spending in the aftermath of the war in the Middle East and a rebound in international oil prices stimulated inflation expectations.

Rising Treasury yields raise returns on risk-free assets, exerting downward pressure that drives capital out of risk asset markets such as stocks, cryptocurrencies, and gold. It also increases the interest rates on corporate bond issuance, adding to corporate investment burdens.

Four Scenarios Depending on the FOMC Outcome

Market attention is focused on whether the FOMC meeting outcome will bring a major market reversal or trigger further corrections. The four scenarios combining the rate decision (pause or hike) and the Fed's stance (hawkish or dovish) are as follows:

  • Quadrant 1 (Hawkish Pause): The benchmark interest rate is frozen, but a hawkish message is delivered through a statement or press conference indicating that further hikes could occur at any time if inflation becomes unstable. This is interpreted as the most positive scenario for capital markets, as it firmly demonstrates the Fed's commitment to price stability.
  • Quadrant 2 (Dovish Pause): Rates are held steady while signaling dovishly that there will be no further rate hikes in the future. Amid ongoing concerns over rising oil prices and inflation, this could convey the perception that the Fed has given up on proactive responses, eroding Fed credibility, stimulating higher Treasury yields, and acting negatively on the market.
  • Quadrant 3 (Hawkish Hike): The benchmark rate is raised while strongly hinting at the possibility of consecutive additional hikes. Even if the market had partially priced in a rate hike, the shock of successive hikes would cause Treasury yields to rise further and trigger a market correction.
  • Quadrant 4 (Dovish Hike): The benchmark rate is raised, but a dot plot or statement is released indicating that this hike is the last and there will be no additional hikes. Since the market has already accepted a rate hike as a given, this is received as a signal of the end of the rate-hiking cycle, having a positive impact on the market.

Monetary Policy Outlook and Investor Response

In terms of indicator trends such as the Consumer Price Index (CPI), inflation is showing a disinflationary pattern, passing its peak and falling gradually. From the perspective of monetary policy mechanisms, weight is placed on the possibility of a rate freeze, but it is difficult to conclude the outcome due to diverse opinions among internal committee members and political variables.

매파적 동결은 최상의 시나리오라고 생각합니다. 자본시장 입장에서 금리를 동결하지만 금리 인상 가능성을 내비치면서 물가 안정을 위해 노력하겠다는 매파적 금리 동결이 시장에 더 긍정적으로 해석될 것입니다.

"I think a hawkish pause is the best scenario. From the perspective of the capital market, a hawkish rate freeze—holding rates steady while signaling the potential for rate hikes and committing to price stability—will be interpreted more positively by the market."

In an environment where Treasury yields remain high, cryptocurrency markets such as Bitcoin and equity markets find it relatively difficult to gain momentum. Investors need to keep all four scenario possibilities in mind and formulate response strategies by understanding the impact each outcome will have on asset markets.
