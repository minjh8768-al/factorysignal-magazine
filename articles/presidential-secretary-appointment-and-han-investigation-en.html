Analysis of Chief of Staff Lim Kwang-hyun's Appointment Background and Han Dong-hoon Legal Risk Controversy

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: MBC 라디오 시사 · [뉴스하이킥] 헬마 완벽분석! 부메랑 된 한동훈 '조국 먼지털이 수사'&비서실장 된 임광현 - 임경빈, MBC 261016 방송. Summarised from that video.

Meaning Behind the Appointment of a Working-Level Economic Chief of Staff

In political circles, the appointment of former National Tax Service Commissioner Lim Kwang-hyun as Chief of Staff is evaluated as an unconventional choice. Writer Lim Gyeong-bin delivered an analysis on the background behind selecting an individual with relatively little experience in politics.

Lim pointed out that the decision appears to reflect a stance aimed at shifting away from the traditional politically oriented Chief of Staff system to focus on public livelihood outcomes and practical economic affairs. He explained that Lim's work performance shown in tax collection and institutional reform during his tenure as National Tax Service Commissioner may have been a key factor in this appointment.

Live Broadcast of Cabinet Meetings and Administrative Openness

Regarding criticisms raised by some over the policy of broadcasting Cabinet meetings live, the Presidential Office reaffirmed its position that administrative openness is the principle. Lim evaluated this move as an extension of the success formula used during the president's previous tenure as a local government head.

국무회의 생중계를 통해 국민들이 궁금해할 만한 지점을 대통령이 직접 주무 부처 장관에게 질의하고 점검하는 순작용을 높게 평가한 것으로 보인다.

"It appears they highly evaluated the positive effect of the president directly questioning and checking points of interest for the public with the relevant cabinet ministers through the live broadcast of Cabinet meetings."

Controversy Over Investigation into Former Minister Han Dong-hoon's Daughter and Legal Risks

In the subsequent discussion regarding the investigation controversy involving former Minister Han Dong-hoon, the debate over investigative agencies' willingness to verify and procedural appropriateness was highlighted. Points were raised during parliamentary audits and other forums that proactive investigative measures, such as search and seizures, were insufficient during the advanced state investigations and police inquiry processes.

Lim noted unreturned requests for cooperation from overseas agencies and differences in the process of securing school-related materials, pointing out that questions regarding the intensity and willingness of the investigation are bound to continue when compared with previous cases of entrance exam fraud investigations.

Future Appointments to Vacancies and Political Outlook

With key appointments remaining for heads of judicial and law enforcement agencies, such as the head of the Public Prosecution Office, the Minister of Justice, and the Commissioner General of the National Police Agency, related debates are expected to continue for the time being. Lim stated that confrontations over the investigation results and appointment process could persist depending on political interests.
