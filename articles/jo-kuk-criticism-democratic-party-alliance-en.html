Cho Kuk Warns Against Flatterers; DP's Kim Min-seok Stresses 'Competitiveness' as Alliance Principle

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 채널A News · 조국이 이 대통령 지지율 보고 한 말? [뉴스플레이리스트] / 채널A. Summarised from that video.

As Cho Kuk, director of the Innovation Policy Research Institute, continues his intense criticism of the Lee Jae-myung administration and the Democratic Party, remarks refuting his comments and addressing the direction of future alliances have emerged from within the Democratic Party.

"Pointing out president's shortcomings gets one branded anti-Lee"… Director Cho Kuk's candid advice

Cho Kuk, director of the Innovation Policy Research Institute, offered harsh criticism regarding the president's state administration and the mood within the party through broadcast interviews and other channels. Director Cho stressed that voices courageously offering advice to Yongsan and the ruling bloc should not be rejected.

저를 뭐 이재명 대통령 유죄 판결을 바라는 사람으로 몰고 공격하는 이상한 분들이 많더라고요. 의도적 난독이 아닌가 싶은데…

"There are many strange people framing me as someone who wants President Lee Jae-myung to be found guilty and attacking me. I wonder if it's intentional illiteracy..."

Director Cho then suggested that the president needs to take an approach of reaching out to the public by candidly sharing what went well and what was regrettable in the process of carrying out state affairs.

이재명 대통령이 이재명 정부의 가장 수장 아닙니까? 그렇다면 진솔하게 나오셔서 '제가 이러이러한 점은 이렇게 추진하려고 했는데, 이런 점은 잘했고 이런 점은 실수한 것 같아'라고 얘기하시면 사람들이 마음이 돌아서죠.

"Is not President Lee Jae-myung the head of the Lee Jae-myung administration? If so, if he comes forward candidly and says, 'I tried to promote this and that in this way; I did well on these points, but I think I made mistakes on those points,' people's minds would turn around."

Democratic Party asks "Is this not criticism for criticism's sake?"… Calls for self-reflection

Regarding these remarks by Director Cho, Kang Seong-pil, deputy spokesperson for the Democratic Party, pointed out that in reality, for any government or political party, there exist those who say pleasant things rather than party members or advisers who deliver harsh truths.

Deputy Spokesperson Kang noted that genuine criticism and advice must have persuasive power to bring about change in the other party, adding that Director Cho also needs to look back on his own political standing and the situation within the party.

Kim Min-seok says "Dividing shares is old politics… Competitiveness is the principle"

In this context, Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok set out clear standards for future alliance discussions with other parties, including the Rebuilding Korea Party, through a speech at a regional event.

조국혁신당과 여러 가지 연대 논의를 할 것입니다. 그런데 이 과정에 분명하게 지켜야 할 것이 있습니다. 그것은 경쟁력입니다.

"We will hold various alliance discussions with the Rebuilding Korea Party. However, there is something that must be strictly observed in this process. That is competitiveness."

Representative Kim indicated that artificial share allocations or division methods in party-to-party negotiations are a past form of politics, emphasizing that electoral competitiveness and viability will be the top criteria.

이미 어떤 지분을 놓고 나누는 시대는 지나갔습니다. 그것은 구정치입니다.

"The era of dividing up shares is already over. That is old politics."

Evaluations inside and outside ruling bloc and future prospects

Moon Jong-hyeong, media spokesperson for the People Power Party, analyzed that Representative Kim Min-seok's remarks on 'competitiveness' were a warning message clearly signaling to the Rebuilding Korea Party that there would be no artificial sharing of stakes.

Spokesperson Moon assessed that unless the Rebuilding Korea Party proves its self-reliant competitiveness in constituency elections, it will be difficult for it to gain the upper hand in election alliance or integration discussions with the Democratic Party, noting that political relations between the two parties have entered a new phase.
