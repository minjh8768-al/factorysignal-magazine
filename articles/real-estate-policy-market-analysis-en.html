"Expedite Housing Supply": Smart Tube Director Kim Hak-ryul Urges Deregulation and Faster Real Delivery

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: SBS Biz 뉴스 · [집중진단] 李 “집값 상승은 尹정부·오세훈 탓”?…부동산 시장 진단은 달랐다 (김학렬 빠숑). Summarised from that video.

President Lee Jae-myung expressed a strong commitment at a recent press conference to rapidly expand housing supply and firmly achieve real estate market stability. In response, Kim Hak-ryul, director of the Smart Tube Real Estate Research Institute, agreed with the government's policy direction while assessing that sophisticated measures considering field realities and market mechanisms are necessary.

"Not Suggesting the Demise of Jeonse": Gangnam Decline and Deepening Polarization

Regarding the president's remarks on the jeonse system during the press conference, Director Kim evaluated that "it was an explanation meaning that jeonse could naturally disappear according to market changes, not an intention to artificially eliminate it." He further analyzed the simultaneous occurrence of falling home prices in the Gangnam area and rising prices in the outer areas of Seoul, stating, "Unlike in the past, the price gap between Gangnam and Gangbuk has widened significantly, entering a state of hyper-polarization."

대한민국이 부동산 투기 공화국을 벗어나게 하는 것이 이 정부의 가장 중심적인 과제다. 다들 어렵다고 하지만 반드시 해내겠다.

"Making South Korea step away from being a real estate speculation republic is the most central task of this administration. Everyone says it is difficult, but we will certainly accomplish it."

Citing instances where high-income earners buy high-priced apartments in Gangnam using loan businesses or P2P lending despite loan regulations, Director Kim added, "It is difficult for partial price adjustments to lead to a downward stabilization of the entire market."

Has Apartment Auctions Increased? "Non-Apartments and Apartments Must Be Viewed Separately"

Regarding the view that increases in auctions and loan delinquency rates are signs of falling housing prices, Director Kim pointed out that factual distinctions need to be made. He explained that the sectors seeing increases in delinquencies and auction properties are non-apartment assets such as multi-family villas, commercial properties, and knowledge industry centers, while winning bid ratios for apartments still exceed 100% relative to appraised values.

Shortage of Move-in Volume and Limitations of New Land Supply

Regarding the cause of the supply shortage following the decrease in housing starts, Director Kim emphasized the time lag between permits and market conditions. He noted that housing starts are usually influenced by permitting measures and market conditions such as interest rates from three to four years ago, making it difficult to attribute the issue solely to short-term policies.

착공은 인허가와 다양한 사전 조치가 필요한 만큼 최소 3~4년 전의 흐름을 함께 살펴봐야 한다. 시장 환경에 따라 공급량이 변동하는 측면이 크다.

"Since housing starts require permits and various preparatory measures, we must look at trends from at least three to four years ago. Supply volume fluctuates significantly depending on the market environment."

Regarding announcements of new site candidates such as Yongsan or the Gwacheon Racetrack, he also stressed, "Practical procedures such as land return or remediation projects must be accompanied," adding that "accelerating the construction of the already announced third-phase new towns and expanding the Great Train eXpress (GTX) network early are more practical in terms of supply effects."

Tax and Loan Regulation Reform and Response Strategies for End-Users

Director Kim expressed concern that heavy taxation regulations targeting owners of multiple homes—such as the comprehensive real estate holding tax, capital gains tax, and acquisition tax—could shrink the market supply of jeonse and monthly rentals, suggesting that simplifying the tax system alongside relaxing loan regulations to vitalize transactions is necessary.

Finally, for end-users such as renters considering purchasing a home, he advised, "Given the loan restriction situation, rather than insisting solely on newly built apartments in Seoul, it is necessary to actively consider older apartments near subway stations in non-regulated areas such as northern Gyeonggi or Incheon."
