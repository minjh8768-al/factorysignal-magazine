KOSPI Rebounds for First Time in 8 Weeks: Tech Rally Could Target 7,200–7,500 Range

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 한국경제TV · 다음주 "코스피 7500 간다" 엔비디아 실적 전까지 즐겨라 | 박석현 우리은행 WM그룹 부부장｜위클리뷰. Summarised from that video.

Foreign Net Buying Returns on Validity of Big Tech AI Investment

The KOSPI successfully rebounded on a weekly basis for the first time in eight weeks. Park Seok-hyun, deputy director of the WM Group at Woori Bank, cited the confirmed continuity of AI investments by US big tech companies and the partial easing of profitability concerns as the primary drivers of the stock market rise.

In terms of supply and demand, foreign net buying strengthened significantly. This week, foreign investors recorded net purchases of around 6 trillion won in the KOSPI market, setting a new weekly record for the highest net buying volume this year. The buying trend was concentrated mainly in the semiconductor sector, driving the stock price rebound.

Easing Dollar Strength and Relief Over Fed Rate Concerns

Easing concerns over exchange rates and the U.S. Federal Reserve's monetary policy also contributed to the influx of foreign capital. July employment figures, the Consumer Price Index, and the Producer Price Index in the U.S. lowered the perceived need for an early rate hike by the Fed, easing dollar strength and fostering risk-on sentiment across emerging markets.

미국 빅테크 주식들의 강세 흐름이 이어지면서 국내 시장에서도 긍정적인 요인으로 작용했다. 외국인 순매수가 집중되고 있는 반도체를 중심으로 주가가 큰 폭으로 오르는 요인이 되었다.

"The continued bullish trend in US big tech stocks served as a positive factor in the domestic market as well. It became a key factor driving sharp price increases, particularly in semiconductors, where foreign net buying has been concentrated."

Key Schedule for Next Week and Release of FOMC Minutes

Major stock market schedules for next week include the domestic market closure on Monday and the release of China's July industrial production and retail sales data. On Wednesday, mandatory simulated trading for single-stock leverage will take effect, and on Thursday, the minutes of the US July FOMC meeting will be released.

Deputy Director Park expected that while the upcoming FOMC minutes might reveal a clash between hawkish and dovish views, recent inflation indicators show a slowing trend, which is unlikely to significantly suppress the market's upward momentum.

KOSPI Outlook and Key Checkpoints

Deputy Director Park set the technical resistance level for the KOSPI between 7,200 and 7,500, predicting that further rebound attempts will continue next week.

  • Monday: Domestic stock market closed for substitute holiday, China's July industrial production and retail sales
  • Tuesday: US July industrial production
  • Wednesday: Mandatory simulated trading for single-stock leverage
  • Thursday: US FOMC minutes release, outlook on China Unitree listing
  • Friday: Korea's Aug 1–20 import and export trends, US options expiration date

However, he added that upward momentum could slow somewhat ahead of major events scheduled for late August, including Nvidia's earnings report and the Jackson Hole meeting.
