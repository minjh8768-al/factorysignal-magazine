Bitcoin Surges Over 20%... "Prepare for Market Differentiation After Short-Term Rebound"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: JTBC News · '확' 달라진 코인 시장 분위기…지금 주목할 것은? [이슈PLAY] / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

While attention was recently focused on the stock market including Samsung Electronics, Bitcoin surged more than 20% in a week, bringing the virtual asset market back into the spotlight. The price of Bitcoin, which stood at around $64,000 per coin a week ago, soared to $81,000 before showing a slight correction.

Sparked by Treasury Buybacks and the 'CLARITY Act'

Multiple factors were cited as the background for this rally. As the U.S. Department of the Treasury announced an expansion of buybacks to directly purchase long-term Treasury bonds, Treasury yields fell, causing funds that had flocked to safe-haven assets to begin moving into risk assets such as stocks and Bitcoin.

In addition, former U.S. President Trump calling for the passage of the 'CLARITY Act,' a digital asset market structure bill, also contributed to improving investor sentiment. Furthermore, mid- to long-term funds totaling $2.6 billion net-flowed into Bitcoin and Ethereum spot ETFs over the past week, and short position liquidations continued, further expanding the gains.

Changed Status and the Significance of the CLARITY Act

Cryptocurrencies, which were previously evaluated as digital gold or safe assets, have recently transformed into representative risk assets as investment and speculative demand flooded in. Although they tended to be sold off first during stock market instability, their status has risen recently as stablecoins have drawn attention as a card to resolve U.S. Treasury yield issues and general challenges.

The CLARITY Act is legislation aimed at clearly defining the crypto market and bringing it into the institutional system. Legislative discussions had stalled due to jurisdictional debates over whether to view digital assets as financial securities (supervised by the SEC) or physical commodities (supervised by the CFTC), as well as pushback from the banking sector against moves to allow interest payments on stablecoins; however, expectations are rising again as policy necessity at the U.S. government level increases.

Exchange Rate Variables and the Need to Separate Wheat From Chaff in Altcoins

Experts analyze that this rally differs from the past in that it represents a supply-demand recovery driven by institutional funds beyond a simple short-term rebound. However, as the won-dollar exchange rate fell below 1,400 won, experts advised domestic investors investing in Korean won to definitely check changes in real returns due to foreign exchange losses.

Furthermore, it is difficult to expect a sector rotation where all altcoins rise together like in past bull markets, and differentiation is likely to proceed mainly around coins with clear uses and substance. Experts recommended approaching the market by managing risk through dollar-cost averaging rather than injecting large amounts of capital all at once.
