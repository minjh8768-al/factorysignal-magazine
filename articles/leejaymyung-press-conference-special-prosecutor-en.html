Lee Jae-myung Asks Special Counsel to Drop Charge Transfer Clause and Focus on Truth

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 안동MBC NEWS · #이재명 대통령 아..... 한참 망설이다 꺼낸 말'충격 고백' 회견장 술렁. Summarised from that video.

Speaking at a press conference, Lee Jae-myung stated his position on major state affairs, including state administration, approval ratings, personnel vetting issues, and the special counsel bill.

Reflection on Falling Approval Ratings and Will to Communicate

Regarding the recent drop in approval ratings, Lee said he reflected on himself, attributing it to his own shortcomings. Acknowledging a lack of consideration, delicacy, and communication, he expressed his intention to re-examine his respect for the public once again.

지지율이 왜 떨어졌을까 저도 많이 고심했지요. 결국은 그 모든 것들을 다 합쳐서 저의 부족 때문이었다라는 생각을 하게 됐습니다.

"I also agonized over why the approval ratings fell. In the end, putting all those things together, I came to think that it was due to my own shortcomings."

Re-examining Personnel Vetting System

In response to questions about the withdrawal of cabinet minister candidates and controversies over personnel vetting, Lee announced a plan to re-examine the personnel vetting system. Stating that while efforts and a system are in place, shortcomings always exist, he responded that future personnel decisions will be made carefully in line with the public's expectations.

Request to Delete Charge Withdrawal Clause from Special Counsel Bill

Regarding the scope of authority of the special counsel and cases suspected of fabricated prosecution, Lee stated that it is appropriate to uncover the truth through a special counsel for cases suspected of malicious investigation or fabrication. However, to reduce unnecessary political dispute and misunderstanding, he urged the National Assembly to remove clauses regarding the transfer or disposition of existing prosecution cases from the special counsel bill.

지금 논란이 되고 있는 특검의 권한 사항 중에 기소 사건들에 대한 이송 또는 처분에 관한 조항들은 삭제하시고 진상규명에만 집중해 주시기 바랍니다.

"Please delete the clauses regarding the transfer or disposition of prosecution cases among the special counsel's powers currently under debate, and focus solely on uncovering the truth."

Perspectives on Diplomacy and Naval Troop Deployment Issues

Asked about practical contributions and controversies over troop deployment regarding the Strait of Hormuz, Lee mentioned that defining terminology and making objective situational judgments should come first. He said he would respond to prevent misunderstandings, taking into account the status, purpose, and objective limits of each country's military resource deployments.
