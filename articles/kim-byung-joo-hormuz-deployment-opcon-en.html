Kim Byung-joo Urges Caution on Hormuz Deployment, Sees No Issue with Immediate OPCON Transfer

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: MBCNEWS · [외전인터뷰] "명분 없고 국제사회 인정 못 받는 전쟁‥파병 신중해야" (2026.09.14/뉴스외전/MBC). Summarised from that video.

Strait of Hormuz Situation and Troop Deployment Controversy

Representative Kim Byung-joo (김병주), the Democratic Party ranking member on the National Assembly National Defense Committee, assessed that the situation in the Strait of Hormuz is showing signs of prolongation and escalation, making securing energy security a crucial priority. Regarding the return of the Ministry of National Defense's local fact-finding team, Rep. Kim emphasized the government's official stance that nothing has been decided regarding troop deployment, stating that the survey was conducted to accurately evaluate the Middle East situation and gather detailed information.

호르무즈 상황이 점점 확전되는 국면으로 전환되고 있습니다. 국방부 실사단이 다녀온 것은 중동 정세를 정확히 평가하고 정보를 파악하기 위한 차원으로 보고 있습니다.

"The situation in Hormuz is shifting into an escalating phase. I view the Defense Ministry fact-finding team's visit as an effort to accurately evaluate the Middle East situation and gather information."

US Demands for Troop Deployment and National Interest-Centered Negotiations

Rep. Kim evaluated that although pressure from the U.S. to deploy troops is intensifying, directly intervening through troop deployment in an unjustified war could be risky in terms of the safety of South Korean service members and national interest. Accordingly, he stated that rather than joining a U.S.-led coalition, it is reasonable to discuss cooperation measures under the framework of a multinational force ensuring freedom of navigation after the war or within international coordination frameworks.

OPCON Transfer and Military Academy Integration Issues

Ahead of the confirmation hearing for Defense Minister nominee Kang Shin-chul (강신철), Rep. Kim offered his views on the transfer of wartime operational control (OPCON). Regarding nominee Kang's position that reclaiming OPCON immediately would pose no issues, Kim evaluated it positively, stating that as long as the structure of the ROK-U.S. Combined Forces Command is maintained, there will be no disruption to the combined defense posture.

연합사를 그대로 유지하면서 한국군 포스타가 사령관을 맡는 구조이기 때문에 당장 전작권을 전환하더라도 연합 방위 태세에는 문제가 없습니다.

"Because it is a structure where the Combined Forces Command is maintained as is while a South Korean four-star general serves as commander, transitioning OPCON immediately presents no problem for the combined defense posture."

Military Academy Consolidation and Personnel Hearing Verification

Regarding the proposal to integrate the Army, Navy, and Air Force academies, he explained that it is a demand of the times to train integrated military talent in line with modern warfare paradigms where land, sea, and air battlefields overlap. In addition, regarding personal allegations surrounding nominee Kang, such as false residency registration controversies, Kim added that he expects the nominee will provide a sufficient explanation directly during the personnel confirmation hearing process.
