Bill Proposed to Defer Crypto Tax as Major Infrastructure Improvements Move Forward

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 코인 과세 2029년으로 밀리나…여당 의원도 유예법 추진. Summarised from that video.

As controversies over tax fairness involving investors using overseas perpetual futures emerge ahead of the implementation of cryptocurrency taxation next year, a bill to defer taxation is being pursued in the National Assembly. At the same time, technical progress continues, including payment infrastructure partnerships and consensus mechanism upgrades by major cryptocurrency companies.

Controversy Over Overseas Perpetual Futures and Tax Fairness

As the implementation of cryptocurrency taxation approaches, discussions are spreading among investors about utilizing perpetual futures on overseas exchanges. This comes as investors note that, unlike spot trading, profits from crypto derivatives lack sufficient legal classification and tax basis.

Under current regulations, income generated from the transfer and lending of virtual assets is subject to a tax rate of 22%, including local income tax, on amounts exceeding an annual basic deduction of 2.5 million won. However, because the tax basis for overseas crypto futures is unclear, equity issues have been raised that tax burdens could differ even when investing in the same asset. Nevertheless, using overseas exchanges entails funding fee burdens, forced liquidation risks, and custody and withdrawal risks, making it difficult to determine investment advantages based solely on tax differences.

National Assembly Pushes Income Tax Act Amendment to Defer Taxation

Discussions on deferring cryptocurrency taxation are expanding in political circles. Representative Park Min-kyu of the Democratic Party of Korea plans to sponsor an amendment to the Income Tax Act that postpones the taxation start date to 2029.

The amendment stipulates that taxation will begin on January 1 of the year after the year following the first cross-border exchange of virtual asset transaction information. Under this structure, if the first exchange of information takes place in 2027, taxation would apply starting in 2029. Rep. Park Min-kyu's office pointed out that the level of preparation of tax infrastructure, including securing tax fairness between domestic and overseas investors and the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF), must be comprehensively considered.

Ripple and Solana Expand Financial Infrastructure and Technology

Ripple has partnered with DriveWealth, a US stock trading infrastructure company, to improve the efficiency of settlement and collateral management using its US dollar stablecoin "RLUSD." The two companies plan to first support XRP Ledger-based RLUSD settlement in Europe before expanding the scope of application to the US market.

Solana is preparing the "Alpenglow" upgrade to improve its consensus mechanism. Replacing the existing Tower BFT technology, it aims to reduce transaction finality time from approximately 12.8 seconds to around 0.15 seconds. By changing how messages are exchanged between validators through a new voting protocol called "Voter," the plan is to support rapid finality for payment and financial transaction services.

Outlook for Bank Bitcoin Adoption and Market Indicators

Phong Le, CEO of Strategy, predicted that bank adoption of Bitcoin-related services will increase significantly over the next 12 months. According to his analysis, gaining regulatory clarity and growing customer demand will accelerate banks' entry into custody, investment product trading, and collateralized loan services.

Meanwhile, immediately after the US September Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation rate was announced at 3.7% year-on-year, prices of major virtual assets such as Bitcoin and Ethereum showed a short-term decline. However, according to CryptoQuant data, 84% of 164 analysts maintain a bullish long-term outlook.
