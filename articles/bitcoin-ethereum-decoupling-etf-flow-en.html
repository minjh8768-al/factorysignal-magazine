Kang Seung-goo: "Bitcoin-Ethereum Decoupling Stemmed from Difference Between Scarcity and Network Utility"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: 딜사이트경제TV · 4분기 비트코인 시장 전략! 이 원칙 모르면 다 잃는다 개미들 포트폴리오 필수 원칙은?. Summarised from that video.

As Bitcoin maintains a high price level while Ethereum continues a relatively sluggish trend, the price gap between the two assets is widening. A crypto market expert analyzed that this is because the market has begun to evaluate the two assets based on different criteria.

Appearing on a broadcast, Kang Seung-goo, Vice President of Uproot Company, shared his views on the valuation criteria for Bitcoin and Ethereum as well as major market trends.

Scarcity Asset Bitcoin vs. Utility Asset Ethereum

Vice President Kang explained that the recent divergence between Bitcoin and Ethereum is the result of cryptocurrency market valuation criteria for each asset becoming distinctly divided.

비트코인은 희소성이 곧 사용성이 되는 자산이고, 이더리움은 네트워크가 실제로 얼마나 쓰이는지로 평가받는 자산이다.

"Bitcoin is an asset where scarcity itself becomes usability, while Ethereum is an asset evaluated by how much the network is actually used."

He pointed out that in the case of Bitcoin, an immutable monetary policy—including a supply cap limited to 21 million units and predictable halvings—is its core value. In contrast, he noted that Ethereum's value is determined by actual network demand for smart contract execution, stablecoin settlement, tokenization, and its role as a Layer 2 settlement layer.

As reasons for Ethereum's recent relative weakness, he cited the concentration of institutional capital on Bitcoin, reduced Layer 1 fee burning due to Layer 2 activation, intensifying competition with high-throughput blockchains such as Solana, and a high correlation with macroeconomic risk assets like the Nasdaq.

Bitcoin ETF Average Purchase Price Surpassed… Potential Shift to Additional Inflows

Regarding the phenomenon of Bitcoin exceeding the average purchase price of U.S. spot Bitcoin ETF investors, he assessed that it is more likely to serve as a catalyst for additional capital inflows rather than creating pressure from break-even selling.

According to the analysis, the average purchase price for U.S. spot Bitcoin ETF holders is estimated at approximately $81,722. As the price of Bitcoin passed this threshold, ETF investors overall entered a profitable zone.

Noting that the majority of ETF holders are institutions and asset managers aiming for long-term asset allocation, Vice President Kang projected that break-even selling sentiment typical of short-term retail investors would be limited. However, as current profit margins are not large, he expected this price level to serve as a key turning point dividing future buying and selling.

Corporate Bitcoin Purchases and Regulatory Guideline Trends

Corporate Bitcoin buying as a treasury asset was also analyzed as a factor supporting the market downside. He explained that major companies are employing strategies of using cash assets to make additional Bitcoin purchases while simultaneously managing their equity and capital structures.

Additionally, regarding the expanding trend of strategic partnerships between exchanges and stablecoin issuers—such as the tie-up between Binance and Circle—he evaluated it as a new industrial structure in which platforms with distribution networks and regulated issuers share revenue and risks.

Regarding U.S. cryptocurrency regulation, he pointed out that while administrative guidelines and exemptions by supervisory agencies allow for prompt market responses amidst legislative delays, there are limitations in durability, as guidelines can be reversed following changes in administration or the political environment.
