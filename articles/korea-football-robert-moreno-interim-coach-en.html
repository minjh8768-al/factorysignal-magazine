KFA Appoints Spain's Robert Moreno as Interim Coach, Citing Meticulous Preparation

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · "예상 밖인데" 스페인 현지 '갸웃'.."AI로 라인업" 축협 밝힌 '이유' / SBS / 모아보는 뉴스. Summarised from that video.

The Korea Football Association has appointed Spanish manager Robert Moreno as interim head coach of the men's national football team, filling the vacancy left by former manager Hong Myung-bo.

Appointed Interim Head Coach Following Open Recruitment

The Korea Football Association held a board meeting at Korea Football Park in Cheonan, South Chungcheong Province, and approved the motion to appoint Moreno as interim national team manager. This marks the first time a manager from Spain will take the helm of the South Korean national team. Moreno will manage six A-matches taking place between September and November.

The KFA stated that depending on evaluation results, his contract could be extended through the Asian Cup in January next year. Moreno is set to lead the national team alongside five Spanish coaching staff members, including a head assistant coach, and plans to enter the country to begin work as soon as his visa is issued.

Built Coaching Career Assisting Luis Enrique

Moreno embarked on a coaching career early without experience as an active professional player. Assisting manager Luis Enrique, he built coaching experience at AS Roma, FC Barcelona, and the Spain national team. In 2019, he led the Spanish national team for eight months in place of manager Enrique, recording an undefeated record of 7 wins and 2 draws.

He later took charge of professional clubs such as AS Monaco, Granada, and Russia's Sochi, but fell short of expectations as a club manager, facing early dismissals or failing to prevent his team from being relegated to the second division. Local Spanish media outlets also covered the news of Moreno's appointment to the South Korean national team, highlighting his past leadership and career.

"Thorough Analysis of Korean Football"... Praised for Tactical Design

A KFA official explained the background behind the appointment, noting that Moreno's thorough analysis of Korean football over an extended period was impressive. The official stated that Moreno received high evaluations for presenting a game model designed to maximize player strengths through more aggressive tactical changes.

Moreno had previously shown interest in moving to South Korea and made contact during the search for a successor to former manager Paulo Bento. The KFA stated that in this open re-recruitment process, Moreno earned high scores thanks to his meticulous preparation and was ultimately chosen.
