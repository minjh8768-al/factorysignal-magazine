U.S. June JOLTS Job Openings Slow as Oil Plunges and Crypto Remains Range-Bound

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: AL MAKKAH TECH & BUSINESS HUB · What Next ?- Latest Crypto Market Analysis & BTC News Updates Today. Summarised from that video.

As U.S. labor market job opening indicators came in lower than expected, the dollar's strength stalled while major currencies such as the Japanese yen staged a rebound. Meanwhile, a lull in Middle East tensions coupled with slowing macroeconomic indicators led to a sharp drop in international oil prices, while the cryptocurrency market continues a range-bound trend centered on Bitcoin with minor volatility.

U.S. June JOLTS Job Openings Decline, Signaling Labor Market Cooling

According to the June Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS) released by the U.S. Department of Labor, job openings in June stood at 7.359 million. This figure fell below both the market expectation of 7.4 million and the previous month's record of 7.537 million.

Following the release of the indicator, pressure on the U.S. dollar eased, allowing major currencies that had been weak, such as the Japanese yen (JPY), to take a breather. Market analysts pointed out that U.S. employment and labor-related data released consecutively over the past month have consistently sent cooling signals, advising that close attention should be paid to the upcoming Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) report.

Dollar Index Stagnates as Crude Oil Prices Plunge

The U.S. Dollar Index (DXY) stagnated around the 99.92 level, failing to recover the 100 mark. As geopolitical tensions in the Middle East entered a temporary lull and concerns regarding the Strait of Hormuz eased, the risk premium in the crude oil market was rapidly removed.

Consequently, West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude prices dropped over 5.5% to around $74.90 per barrel. Brent crude also fell sharply by more than 5.2% to around $79.40 per barrel, marking a steep decline in international oil prices.

Bitcoin and Ethereum Remain Range-Bound Alongside Altcoin Trends

The global cryptocurrency market capitalization maintained a level of approximately $2.27 trillion. Bitcoin (BTC) dominance dropped slightly to 56.6%, while an inflow of capital into certain altcoins was observed.

  • Bitcoin (BTC): After confirming support around the $63,500 level, it traded around $64,000, remaining flat after failing to break through resistance between $64,500 and $64,800.
  • Ethereum (ETH): Attempted a rebound based on support at $1,850, but faced resistance near $1,884 and underwent a slight correction to around $1,870.
  • Other Altcoins: Binance Coin (BNB) defended support at $588 to hold around $593, while Solana (SOL) tested short-term resistance between $73.29 and $74.29. XRP continued a stagnant trend around $1.07.

Gold (XAU) Market and Key Points to Watch

Prices for gold (XAU), a safe-haven asset, tested support around $4,090 per ounce before rebounding to $4,111, but upside potential remained limited again. Market participants expect upcoming major U.S. employment indicators and macroeconomic data to be key variables determining the overall direction of asset markets.
