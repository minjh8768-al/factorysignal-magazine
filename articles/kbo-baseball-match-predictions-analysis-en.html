KBO Pitching Analysis and Recommended Betting Lines for Key Matchups

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 토마 · 2026.08.26 KBO 한국 프로야구 경기 분석 한야 7연승가자!! 분석!!. Summarised from that video.

Sports analysis YouTuber Toma analyzed the starting pitchers' strength and recent performance for key KBO games, releasing moneyline and over/under recommendations for each matchup.

LG vs. NC: NC Forecasted to Win in a Pitchers' Duel

Regarding the matchup between LG's Im Chan-kyu and NC's Koo Chang-mo, Toma explained that the overall analysis was difficult because both starting pitchers have been in good form recently. Pointing out the likelihood of the game turning into a pitchers' duel early on, he suggested Under 4.5 for the first half, Under 8.5 for the game, and an NC win as his recommended lines.

SSG vs. Hanwha: Ryu Hyun-jin's Declining Form Highlighted

For the matchup between SSG's Choi Min-jun and Hanwha's Ryu Hyun-jin, he assessed that Ryu's recent form has dropped, leading to more runs allowed, and that Hanwha's bullpen and closer situation remains unstable. Although SSG's Choi Min-jun has not shown dominant pitching either, Toma recommended an SSG win and Over 10.5 taking overall conditions into account.

Lines for KIA vs. Lotte and KT vs. Doosan

In the game between KIA's Hwang Dong-ha and Lotte's Rodriguez, he viewed Lotte's Rodriguez as being in excellent condition after recently throwing over 6 innings with good stuff. Accordingly, he recommended a Lotte win, Under 9.5 for the game, and Under 5.5 for the first half.

In the game between KT's Daniel and Doosan's Jack Logue, he pointed out Jack Logue's inconsistency in hits and runs allowed, proposing a KT win and Under 9.5.

Analysis of Kiwoom vs. Samsung

In the matchup featuring Kiwoom's Park Jun-hyun and Samsung's Choi Won-tae, he noted Park's high OBP and batting average against, predicting that Samsung's lineup will score runs. Toma concluded his analysis by selecting a Samsung win and Over 8.5 as his recommended lines.
