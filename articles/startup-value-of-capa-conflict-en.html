The Reality Behind Fancy Jargon: Startup Partnership Dissolves Amid Boasts of '10 Billion Won in One Year'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 별놈들 · “직장인에서 스타트업 CEO까지” 20대 대표 마이클의 성공신화. Summarised from that video.

CEO Michael of startup Value of Capa revealed a consumer goods platform business concept utilizing convenience store inventory products, expressing his ambitions for business expansion. However, amidst a management style focused on flashy business jargon and social media exposure, internal conflict surfaced, eventually leading to the dissolution of the partnership.

Silicon Valley-Style Work Culture and Management Approach

CEO Michael stated that he emphasizes a horizontal organizational culture and autonomy, introducing the use of English names and flexible working hours. He explained that through lean and agile organizational management, he prioritizes marketing traffic exposure as the top task.

However, he remained tight-lipped regarding current concrete sales figures and acknowledged the reality of remaining at the outsourcing stage. Nevertheless, citing the example of Steve Jobs, he maintained the attitude that a business can succeed if there is value and a dream.

저희 회사는 워낙 수평적인 문화를 지향하기 때문에 커뮤니케이션을 활발하게 하기 위해 영문 이름을 사용하고 있습니다.

"Because our company aims for such a horizontal culture, we use English names to encourage active communication."

Internal Conflict Over Business Direction

CEO Michael's excessive use of social media and management focused on external promotion triggered a conflict with CMO Olise. CMO Olise expressed dissatisfaction with the CEO's behavior of focusing solely on filming content for social media such as Reels, without practical business execution or preparation.

Clashes of opinion intensified over matters far removed from practical work, such as choosing lunch menus or reviewing the next corporate vehicle, and the difference in perspective between the two gradually widened.

Departure of Key Personnel and Reduction to a One-Person Business

As the conflict continued, CMO Olise received an acceptance notice from another company and immediately announced his resignation, leaving the company with only the CEO in an instant. Even after the departure of key personnel, CEO Michael expressed his determination to rebuild the business by filming a video targeting annual sales of 10 billion won.

지금부터 저는 1년 안에 연 매출 100억 회사로 성장시켜 보겠습니다.

"Starting now, I will grow this into a company with 10 billion won in annual sales within one year."
