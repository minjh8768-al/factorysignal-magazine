8 Candidate Managers Ranked to Lead South Korea National Football Team Toward 2030 World Cup

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 숏매너 · 대한민국 축구 국가대표팀 차기 감독 후보 TOP 8. Summarised from that video.

A ranking of candidate managers to lead the South Korea national football team toward the 2030 World Cup has been presented. Various domestic and international managers have been named in the candidate pool.

Candidates Ranked 8th to 5th

In 8th place is Jesper Sørensen from Denmark. A young and promising manager, he is evaluated as utilizing a 4-3-3 tactic and high-intensity pressing football.

In 7th place is manager Yoon Jong-hwan, who currently leads Incheon United. He is noted for his skill in tailored tactics based on defensive organization.

In 6th place is manager Choi Yong-soo. Having previously led FC Seoul to a K League title and an Asian Champions League runner-up finish, he is mentioned as a candidate who could stabilize the national team in a short period through his characteristic charisma and leadership.

In 5th place is FC Seoul manager Kim Gi-dong. He is assessed as a manager who can efficiently utilize the national team squad with excellent tactical management skills and flexible tactical changes.

Top-Ranked Candidates

In 4th place is manager Lee Jung-hyo. He is a figure gaining support from young fans by implanting build-up play and high pressing.

In 3rd place is manager Hervé Renard from France. He has strengths in short tournament competitions, having won the Africa Cup of Nations and led Saudi Arabia to defeat Argentina at the 2022 Qatar World Cup.

In 2nd place is former Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors manager Gus Poyet. He achieved results while rebuilding teams and enjoys support from football fans as a foreign manager.

Paulo Bento Selected as No. 1

1st place was taken by former national team manager Paulo Bento. Serving the longest tenure in history from 2018 to 2022, he achieved a place in the round of 16.

His deep understanding of Korean football's system and characteristics is evaluated as his greatest strength.
