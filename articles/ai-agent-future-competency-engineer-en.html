In an AI Era Where Intelligence Becomes Commonplace, "Understanding People and Persuasive Power Are Key Capabilities for Engineers"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 티타임즈TV · “지능이 희소성을 잃은 시대, 가치있는 건 테이스트“ (안상현 메타 AX팀 E7엔지니어). Summarised from that video.

With the rapid advancement of AI technology, the era of AI agents that independently perform tasks beyond the stage of chatbots simply answering questions is entering full swing. In a conversation, former Minister Park Young-sun and engineer Ahn Sang-hyun examined the shift toward agent-centric organizations and fundamental changes in how work is done.

1. Shift Toward Agent-Centric Organizations and the UI Revolution

Engineer Ahn Sang-hyun presented "the shift from human-centric to agent-centric organizations" as a key keyword for the second act of AI development. According to the analysis, while AI was previously utilized as a calculator or auxiliary tool, we have now entered a phase where work leverage is maximized through agents.

Interfaces are also rapidly changing from traditional touchscreens and text input toward voice and wearable devices. Engineer Ahn predicted that wearable devices based on human natural voice, five senses, and surface electromyography (sEMG) sensor-based neural signal recognition will move away from button input methods and replace the role of smartphones.

2. Popularization of LLMs and the Rise of Token Economics

As large language models (LLMs) become popular like electricity or basic necessities, corporate AI utilization strategies are also changing. Engineer Ahn Sang-hyun explained that there is no need to use high-cost, top-performance models for all tasks, and that efficient model selection tailored to purpose and difficulty, as well as optimizing return on investment (ROI), have become important.

The perspective is that designing productive token economics—accurately measuring and managing the added value created relative to the AI costs incurred, rather than simply consuming many tokens—will become a major task for big tech and individual companies.

3. Three Key Capabilities Needed for Engineers in the AI Era

Discussions were also held on the future capabilities engineers must possess in an environment where AI handles coding, document writing, and analysis. Engineer Ahn Sang-hyun emphasized that human-centered goal setting and evaluation capabilities will be core rather than simple technical implementation, presenting three key capabilities.

  • Goal Design and Securing Empathy: The ability to clearly define goals to achieve and draw empathy from others.
  • Verification Capability: The evaluative ability to identify shortcomings in AI outputs and enhance completeness by reflecting human sensibility and taste.
  • Understanding People and Persuasive Power: While creating products with AI has become easier, the ability to deliver them to people and persuade them determines the product's success or failure.
진짜 좋은 엔지니어는 AI를 써서 잘 만들고, 사람을 이해하고 잘 설득할 수 있기 때문에 잘 팔 수 있고, 그러한 능력들이 되게 중요해지지 않나 생각을 합니다.

"I think a truly good engineer can build well using AI, and because they understand people and can persuade them well, they can sell well—and those abilities are becoming extremely important."

4. Decreasing Scarcity of Intelligence and the Importance of the Humanities

As intelligence and knowledge are no longer scarce resources, the analysis suggests that human charm and creativity will be recognized with high value over rote-memorization-based education or simple intelligence.

Engineer Ahn Sang-hyun repeatedly emphasized the need for studying the humanities, noting that as intelligence becomes common, sensibility and taste that move people's hearts and philosophical thinking that penetrates the essence of problems become even more important. Former Minister Park Young-sun also summarized that to utilize AI intelligence 100%, clear human will and goal setting must ultimately come first.
