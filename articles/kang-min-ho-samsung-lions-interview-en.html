Samsung's Kang Min-ho: "Adjusted Batting Point During Three-Day Break... Powered by My Family"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS Sports · "나이를 먹으니 공부를.." 이순철 칭찬에 빵 터진 강민호 | 인터뷰 | 2026 KBO리그 (26.09.29). Summarised from that video.

Samsung Lions catcher Kang Min-ho led his team to victory with a stellar performance. In a post-game interview, Kang revealed the secret behind his recent hitting surge, his chemistry with the pitcher, and his gratitude toward his family.

Hitting Surge Driven by Batting Point Adjustment

Kang expressed his delight over securing a victory in the game following his rest. He explained that after a period of struggling at the plate, adjusting his batting point through practice during the break paid off.

Recalling his home run, which registered an exit velocity of 170 km/h and a launch angle of 18 degrees, he said he watched the ball with anticipation to see if it would clear the fence.

Chemistry with Pitcher Pedack and Game Management

Kang also evaluated his chemistry with starting pitcher Pedack. He described Pedack as a pitcher with a heavy fastball and excellent command of breaking pitches. Referring to a dugout conversation with Pedack during the game, Kang mentioned a situation where Pedack, who was asking for curves via PitchCom, was advised to throw consecutive fastballs instead to get a strikeout.

앞 타석에서 커브를 두 번 잡았기 때문에 이번에는 타자도 생각할 수 있어 직구로 가는 게 맞았다고 이야기했다.

"I told him that since we got two curveballs called in the previous plate appearance, the batter might be anticipating it this time, so going with a fastball was the right choice."

Cheering for the Asian Games National Team and a Message to His Family

As a former national team player, Kang shared that he nervously watched the Asian Games baseball team's games. Understanding well the pressure and burden felt by the younger players, he said he rooted for them with an anxious heart.

He expressed his gratitude toward his family, saying that the time spent with them during the holiday break gave him great strength.

항상 가족 덕분에 지금까지 잘 버티고 있는 것 같다. 가족들 전부 건강했으면 좋겠고 저도 건강하게 올 시즌 잘 마무리하겠다.

"I feel like I've held up well so far thanks to my family. I hope my entire family stays healthy, and I will also finish this season in good health."
