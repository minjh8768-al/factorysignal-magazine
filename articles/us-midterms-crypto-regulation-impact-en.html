US Midterms: A Turning Point Shaping the Speed and Rules of Crypto Institutionalization Beyond Price Movements

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Behind the US Congress's hurry to process digital asset market structure legislation lies a major political event: the midterm elections. Once the August recess begins, lawmakers will return to their districts to focus on local activities before heading into full-fledged election season.

Shifts in Congressional Leadership and Regulatory Authority

Beyond simply replacing lawmakers, this midterm election will determine the leadership and legislative priorities of key standing committees, including the Senate Banking Committee, the Senate Agriculture Committee, and the House Financial Services Committee. Depending on the election results, it will also be decided whether the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) or the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) will hold greater authority over cryptocurrency market oversight.

The Republican Party takes the stance of enhancing the role of the CFTC and establishing clear regulations to promote rapid institutionalization, aiming to secure leadership in the digital asset industry and prevent capital outflow. Conversely, within the Democratic Party, moderate voices supporting innovation coexist with currents that emphasize consumer protection, anti-money laundering, and stronger oversight of major exchanges.

"This election is not about whether coins will rise or fall, but about how quickly and under what rules the US crypto market will enter the institutional framework."

Impact by Asset Class: Bitcoin vs. Altcoins

Bitcoin is a market that has already seen a large influx of institutional capital through spot ETFs and futures/options markets. Therefore, rather than being driven by election results alone, it is heavily influenced by macroeconomic variables such as the Federal Reserve's interest rate policy, the value of the US dollar, and ETF fund inflows and outflows.

On the other hand, altcoins including Ethereum and XRP react more sensitively to regulatory uncertainty. This is because the legal status of staking, DeFi, and tokenization projects, as well as exchange listing standards, are directly tied to regulatory guidelines.

Market Outlook by Scenario

Scenarios based on the election outcome are broadly analyzed into three possibilities.

  • Republicans retain both houses: Industry-friendly policies are likely to be pursued quickly, benefiting exchanges, custody, and tokenization-related companies.
  • Divided Congress: The pace of passing legislation may slow down, but a more stable regulatory framework could be established through bipartisan compromise.
  • Expanded Democratic influence: Stricter consumer protection and anti-money laundering regulations could pose short-term burdens on altcoin and DeFi markets, though the overall trend toward institutionalization will not halt.

Ultimately, investors need to move beyond a simple framing of party victory or defeat and comprehensively examine congressional leadership, changes in regulatory authority, and actual institutional capital flows.
