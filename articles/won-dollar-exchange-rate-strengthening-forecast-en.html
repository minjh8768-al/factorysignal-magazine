"Prepare for Sharp Won Strength Amid Exchange Rate Concerns": FX Market Distortion Expected to Unravel

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 매일경제TV · [정철진의 작전] 환율 상승? 급격한 원화 강세에 대비하라 Feat. 외화자금시장 넘치는 달러 vs 외환시장 부족한 달러, 닉스ADR·금리인상·외인 순매수 등 모멘텀 /매일경제TV. Summarised from that video.

Amid lingering market concerns over a rising USD/KRW exchange rate and a weak Korean won, some analysts suggest preparing for a sharp appreciation of the won as supply-demand imbalances resolve. Their analysis indicates that if the divergence between the foreign currency funding market and the foreign exchange market breaks through a specific trigger, the decline in the USD/KRW exchange rate could accelerate.

Divergence Between Foreign Currency Funding and Foreign Exchange Markets

Currently, the domestic financial market is experiencing distortion as the foreign currency funding market and the foreign exchange market display contrasting patterns. In the foreign currency funding market, US dollars are abundant—to the point where dollar borrowing costs have hit record historical lows due to trade surpluses and foreign capital inflows—yet a dollar shortage persists in the foreign exchange market.

Anchor Jung Chul-jin noted that this divergence must eventually be resolved. He explained that once an impetus is provided to clear the supply-demand imbalance accumulated in the foreign currency funding market, the Korean won will quickly pivot toward strength.

외화 자금 시장에서는 달러가 넘칩니다. 반면에 외환 시장에서는 달러가 또 부족하거든요. 이 괴리가 어떤 이벤트와 시기적으로 확 깨지게 되면 그때부터는 완벽하게 원화가 빠른 강세로, 그러니까 환율이 떨어진다는 얘기입니다.

"There is an overflow of dollars in the foreign currency funding market. On the other hand, there is still a dollar shortage in the foreign exchange market. If this divergence breaks wide open through a certain event at the right time, the won will shift completely into a rapid appreciation from that point—meaning the exchange rate will fall."

Key Triggers Driving Korean Won Strength

Major momentum factors expected to lead the won's strength include large-scale foreign currency inflows, the Bank of Korea's interest rate hike policy, and foreign net buying. SK Hynix plans to raise $26.5 billion (over approximately 40 trillion won) through ADR issuance, which is highly likely to flow domestically for domestic factory expansion and equipment procurement. Hanwha Ocean has also built a currency hedging position by selling $2 billion in forward exchange contracts.

In addition, the Bank of Korea Monetary Policy Board's stance on interest rate hikes and the intensity of its hawkish remarks could also serve as a key trigger to end the market's wait-and-see attitude.

만약에 요번에 하이닉스 자금 들어오는 것도 비롯해서 한국은행의 금리 인상 같은 거 하나만 터트리게 되면 이 눈치 게임이 깨지게 되는 것이고, 그렇게 되면 본격적으로 왜곡이 풀리면서 흔하디흔한 달러가 원래에 대한 몸값을 받게 된다는 겁니다.

"If just one thing breaks—such as the inflow of SK Hynix funds this time or an interest rate hike by the Bank of Korea—this waiting game will break. Then the distortion will unravel in earnest, and the abundant dollar will receive its proper value relative to the won."

Foreign Supply and Demand and Securing Downside Rigidity

The direction of foreign investor supply and demand also supports the possibility of won appreciation. Every time foreign ownership on the KOSPI drops to the 38% range, large-scale net buying by foreign investors tends to flow in. This acts as a factor that secures downside rigidity for the stock market while accelerating the speed of the USD/KRW exchange rate's decline.

Sectors Benefiting from a Strong Won and Response Strategies

Representative sectors that could benefit from a shift toward a strong won include utilities (such as Korea Electric Power Corporation and Korea Gas Corporation), domestic demand and food, finance, oil refining, and airlines. In particular, companies like SK Innovation, which operates both oil refining and secondary battery businesses and highlights undervalued appeal, could receive differentiated attention.

Based on USD/KRW chart analysis, as the 60-day moving average on a daily basis collapses and the possibility of entering the 200-day moving average and the 1,450 to 1,470 won range opens up, now is the time for a strategic response to prepare for a rapid won appreciation scenario rather than positioning heavily toward a rising exchange rate.
