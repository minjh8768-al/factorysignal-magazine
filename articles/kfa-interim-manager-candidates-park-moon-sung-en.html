KFA Open Recruitment for Interim Manager: Park Moon-sung Says Gus Poyet Dropped Due to Quantitative Assessment

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 시사김치전 · [대표팀 축구감독선임중]대표팀 축구감독 선임 어떻게되고있나. 박문성해설위원 설명. Summarised from that video.

As the Korea Football Association (KFA) conducts an open recruitment process to appoint an interim manager to lead the national team for three months, football commentator Park Moon-sung explained the applicants' backgrounds and the evaluation method.

Many Spanish Managers Apply… Stojkovic Says "Did Not Apply"

This open recruitment for an interim manager was launched to find a coach to take charge of six A-matches from September to November. According to Park, around five candidates passed the document screening stage.

It was learned that Spanish managers such as Roberto Moreno, Domènec Torrent, and Jesús Casas passed the document screening. Having served as head assistant coaches under managers like Pep Guardiola or Luis Enrique, they are evaluated as fitting the trends of modern football.

On the other hand, manager Dragan Stojkovic of Serbia, who was mentioned in some media as a candidate, turned out not to have applied at all. Park quoted manager Stojkovic's statement received through a media outlet.

나는 지원하지 않았고, 이런 보도가 나가고 있는 것에 대해 가짜 뉴스라고 분명히 이야기한다.

"I did not apply, and I clearly state that such reports being published are fake news."

Quantitative Assessment Introduced in First Open Recruitment… Background Behind Gus Poyet's Rejection

In this recruitment, manager Gus Poyet, who has winning experience in the K League, also applied but was confirmed to have failed the document screening. This is the first time the football association has selected a senior national team manager through an open recruitment process rather than for age-group national teams.

The football association applied quantitative evaluation criteria that scored all evaluation items based on the articles of association of the Korean Sport & Olympic Committee. In this process, along with the manager's own capabilities, career numerical metrics of the accompanying coaching staff were also strictly reflected, which was analyzed to have made manager Poyet's coaching staff score a deducting factor.

Park explained the evaluation system as follows.

그동안 협회가 정성 평가 위주로 감독을 선임하다 보니 청렴화하고 정량화하라는 요구가 있었다. 이를 그대로 매뉴얼대로 적용하다 보니 발생한 현상이다.

"Until now, as the association appointed managers focusing on qualitative assessment, there were demands to make the process clean and quantitative. This phenomenon occurred as they applied it strictly according to the manual."

Interim System Until November… New Executive Committee Expected to Appoint Official Manager

The football association plans to finalize the appointment of the interim manager within August following an interview process. The new interim manager will lead the national team starting from the September A-match period.

The reason the interim manager system will be maintained until November is linked to the election of the football association president. This is because the schedule was arranged so that the newly formed executive committee after the association presidential election scheduled at the end of the year will hold the authority to appoint the official manager. The official manager is expected to be appointed in December or during January next year to prepare in earnest for next year's Asian Cup.
