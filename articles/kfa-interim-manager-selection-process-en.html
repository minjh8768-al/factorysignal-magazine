KFA Closes Applications for Interim National Team Manager as Poyet and Koeman Apply

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 새벽의 축구 전문가 · "포옛은 지원 완료했다" 국대 임시감독, 괜찮은 감독들이 지원 했을까?. Summarised from that video.

As the Korea Football Association (KFA) closed document submissions for the open recruitment of an interim national team manager, attention is focused on the list of key applicants and the hiring process.

Gus Poyet's Application and the KFA's Response

Gus Poyet, who previously managed Jeonbuk Hyundai, announced that he has personally completed his application for the position of interim national team manager. Poyet showed enthusiasm by even installing a messaging program widely used in South Korea to facilitate smooth communication with local football officials.

While some foreign media reported that Poyet had been selected as the front-runner, the Korea Football Association immediately denied this.

포옛 감독 유력설에 대해서도 명확하게 사실과 다르다.

"The rumors that Manager Poyet is the front-runner are also clearly untrue."

A KFA official stated that only document submissions have been completed and that nothing has been finalized in the process.

Erwin Koeman and Other Candidates Enter the Race

In addition to Poyet, it was revealed that Erwin Koeman, a former assistant coach for the Netherlands national team, has also applied. Erwin Koeman is the older brother of Ronald Koeman, and it is reported that during the application process, he submitted a match analysis report on the South Korean national team alongside a performance review program for players based in Europe.

Additionally, Manager Davide Nicola, who has been active in Italy's Serie A specializing in escaping relegation zones, is among the candidates being mentioned.

Limitations of the Interim Role and Future Schedule

Uncertainties surrounding conditions such as contract duration and annual salary are pointed out as reasons why prominent foreign managers hesitate to step forward for the interim role. Consequently, analysis suggests that applicants are mostly managers seeking to take charge as interim manager first and then pursue an extension to a permanent position through performance.

The Korea Football Association plans to review the submitted documents and begin online interviews next week. To prepare for the September international match schedule without disruption, it plans to finalize the appointment process internally in August.
