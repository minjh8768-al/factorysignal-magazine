Won-Dollar Exchange Rate Projected to Fall to 1,200-Won Level in Mid-to-Long Term, While KOSPI Faces Potential Further Adjustment

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 김영익의 경제스쿨 · 5%를 넘어선 미 국채금리, 앞으로 시장에서 벌어질 일 | 김영익 교수. Summarised from that video.

As the won-dollar exchange rate, which surged in the first half of this year, recently shows a downward trend, forecasts for exchange rates and stock prices based on key economic indicator analyses have been presented.

Exchange Rate Determinants and Mid-to-Long-Term Outlook for Won Appreciation

Vector autoregression (VAR) model analysis showed that the biggest factors determining won-dollar exchange rate fluctuations were the US dollar index and the direction of the exchange rate itself. Professor Kim Young-ik (김영익) predicted that the weakening trend of the dollar will continue, citing the outlook for a shrinking share of the US economy in global GDP and the potential appreciation of the yuan and yen.

In addition, factors supporting the appreciation of the won were analyzed to include a rise in the domestic savings rate, a current account surplus trend, and the real interest rate differential between South Korea and the United States.

2008년 금융위기 때 원/달러 환율이 한때 1,590원까지 올라갔지만 5년 지나니까 1,030원까지 떨어졌습니다. 이번 사이클도 1,550원이 약간 넘었던 것을 정점으로 환율이 하락하는 과정으로 보고 있습니다.

"During the 2008 financial crisis, the won-dollar exchange rate once rose to 1,590 won, but five years later it fell to 1,030 won. I view this cycle as also being in the process of the exchange rate declining after peaking slightly above 1,550 won."

Risk of Short-Term Fourth-Quarter Rebound and Concerns Over Further KOSPI Adjustment

In the short term, the possibility was raised that the exchange rate could rebound during the fourth quarter due to US financial market instability and a flight to safety. However, the view is that this will be no more than a short-term rebound within an overall downward trend.

On the other hand, the KOSPI index was assessed to still be overvalued compared to fundamentals such as nominal GDP, M2 money supply, and average daily export value. It was pointed out that the stock price adjustment process is not over yet and that additional adjustments may follow.

환율은 단기적으로 반등할 수 있지만 장기적으로 하락할 것으로 봅니다. 반면 코스피는 명목 GDP와 통화량, 수출 금액 기준 여전히 과대평가 영역에 있어 주가 조정이 끝나지 않은 것으로 보고 있습니다.

"I believe the exchange rate may rebound in the short term, but will decline in the long term. On the other hand, I view the KOSPI as still being in overvalued territory based on nominal GDP, money supply, and export amounts, meaning the stock price adjustment is not over."

Key Risk Factors in Global Financial Markets

Risk factors facing global financial markets included risk asset price adjustments, concerns over an AI investment bubble, and high long-term interest rates in advanced economies. In particular, the rise in the US 10-year Treasury yield to around 5% was analyzed as a structural risk that could shrink household consumption and corporate investment, potentially plunging the US economy into recession.

In addition, geopolitical risks such as difficulties in making progress in US-Iran negotiations and the rise of the 'TACO index,' which reflects political uncertainty, were cited as factors that could increase future exchange rate and stock market volatility.
