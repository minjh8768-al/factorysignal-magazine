Justice Minister Nominee Kim Seung-won Arrives for First Day, Denying Allegations and Stating 'No Intention to Direct Indictment Withdrawal'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: OBS뉴스 · [뉴스'함'] 한동훈, 김승원 청문위원 가능? 패널 분석 들어봤더니... Summarised from that video.

Kim Seung-won, nominee for Minister of Justice, arrived for his first day of work at the office of the confirmation hearing preparation team and stated his position on various allegations surrounding him. Nominee Kim drew a line against the controversy over directing the withdrawal of indictments in the Lee Jae-myung case and also denied allegations of solicitation involving the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety.

Clarification on Lee Jae-myung Case Indictment Withdrawal Controversy and Food and Drug Safety Solicitation Allegations

Meeting with reporters on his way to work, nominee Kim Seung-won stated that as Minister of Justice, he not only lacks direct authority to withdraw indictments, but also has no intention of directing the Prosecutor General to do so. Regarding his past role as co-representative of a lawmaker group for withdrawing indictments, he explained that as a member of the National Assembly, he was merely conveying the public's position that fabricated indictments resulting from illegal investigations must be corrected.

He also refuted allegations of solicitation involving the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety related to clinical trials for a new drug, stating that there was no improper solicitation or granting of special favors. Nominee Kim explained that even the prosecution's notice of decision not to indict describes that there was no improper solicitation or provision of conveniences and that the approval process proceeded normally. He added that regarding the prosecution's suspension of indictment, he filed a constitutional complaint believing it was unfair.

법무부 장관으로서 공소취소할 직접적인 권한도 없을 뿐만 아니라 검찰총장을 통해서 지휘를 하는 것도, 그럴 생각은 지금 없다.

"As Minister of Justice, I not only lack direct authority to withdraw indictments, but I also currently have no intention of issuing directions through the Prosecutor General."

Strong Criticism from Representative Han Dong-hoon and Political Controversy

Independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon strongly criticized the lobbying allegations involving the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety surrounding nominee Kim Seung-won, questioning his qualifications to be minister. Rep. Han pointed out whether lobbying was conducted to have the head of the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety approve a dangerous drug and questioned the appropriateness of such lobbying, asserting that as the Ministry of Justice is an institution that upholds justice, the nominee must answer whether he is qualified to head it.

그렇게 저열한 로비를 했던 사람, 그리고 그게 명확하게 드러나고 거기에 부인도 못하는 사람이 대한민국의 공정, 법무부는 Ministry of Justice입니다. 정의부의 수장이 될 자격이 있는지 이것을 답해야 한다고 생각합니다.

"A person who conducted such disgraceful lobbying, a person for whom this has been clearly revealed and who cannot even deny it—fairness in the Republic of Korea, the Ministry of Legal Affairs is the Ministry of Justice. I believe he must answer whether he is qualified to be the head of the Ministry of Justice."

Outlook on Hearing Phase and Clarification of Past Record

Regarding his past record of defending sexual crime cases, nominee Kim Seung-won explained that he was not involved in some of the cases, but said he reflects on aspects that did not meet public expectations. The confirmation hearing preparation team also took action by releasing additional explanatory materials stating that the approval process was completed within the normal processing period.

Within political circles, alongside opinions that institutional improvements are needed—such as separating moral verification from competency verification during confirmation hearings—there are expectations that fierce battles between the ruling and opposition parties will continue during the hearing phase.
