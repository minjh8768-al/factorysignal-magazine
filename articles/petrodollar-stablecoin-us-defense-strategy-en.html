Combining Oil Supply Chains and Stablecoins: Kim Chang-ik Sees U.S. Defense Department at Center of 'Petrodollar 2.0'

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 달러 패권 2.0 선포 : 석유 공급망+스테이블코인+미 항공모함 결합한 미국의 빅픽처 [9월 9일 김창익 작가]. Summarised from that video.

An analysis suggests that the United States is building a new dollar hegemony order by combining oil supply chains, a strategic asset, with stablecoins, a form of digital finance. The explanation is that the U.S. is moving away from the "Petrodollar 1.0" system, which relied on past diplomatic promises, and transitioning to a "Petrodollar 2.0" system directly overseen by the Department of Defense and the Department of Justice.

Shift in Jurisdiction over Oil Assets: Department of Defense Secures Initiative

Author Kim Chang-ik (김창익), appearing on a broadcast, cited the shift in managing authority as a key element to note in the recent acquisition of a 35% stake in a Venezuelan oil company. He pointed out that while energy asset management was previously led by the Department of Commerce, the Department of the Treasury, or the Department of State responsible for diplomatic mediation, the U.S. Department of Defense (Pentagon) has emerged as the governing authority in this new structure.

Kim explained that assets such as oil, real estate, gold, and Bitcoin are being redefined beyond simple economic assets into national security assets.

석유 자산이 국가 안보 자산이기 때문에 국방부가 관리하게 되는 것입니다. 앞으로 석유 시장에서 가격을 교란하는 행위가 발생하면 외교적 해결이 아니라 국가 안보 패권을 위협한 것으로 간주되어 즉각적인 군사적 대응 논리로 이어질 수 있습니다.

"Because oil assets are national security assets, the Department of Defense manages them. If actions disrupting prices in the oil market occur in the future, they could be regarded not as subjects for diplomatic resolution, but as threats to national security hegemony, potentially leading to the logic of an immediate military response."

The Evolution from Petrodollar 1.0 to 2.0

If the existing Petrodollar 1.0, established through diplomatic negotiations with Saudi Arabia in the 1970s, was a diplomatic commitment centered on the Secretary of State, Petrodollar 2.0 takes on a security and judicial enforcement structure led by the Department of Defense and the Department of Justice. It is a method of directly controlling oil supply chains and settlement systems based on judicial power and military force.

On the digital finance front, stablecoins are used as a core settlement medium. This takes the form of combining oil transaction settlement networks, stablecoins, and the existing Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication (SWIFT) network. The U.S. Department of Defense is seeking to strengthen control by even discussing the application of a "kill switch" to shut off payment system hardware in the event of network security threats or settlement network disruptions by hostile forces such as China and North Korea.

Reshaping Global Oil Supply Chains and the Effect of Pressuring China

This strategy also aims to neutralize the oil pricing structure centered on Saudi Arabia and Russia, and reshape the market around supply chains centered on the U.S. administration and Wall Street. In particular, if oil export routes for Iran and Venezuela are blocked or controlled, China, which requires imports of over 10,000,000 barrels of oil per day, could face severe energy supply difficulties.

Kim projected that as the United States controls the oil supply chain itself in key confrontation frameworks, it will gain an overwhelming upper hand in foreign diplomatic strategy and in maintaining dollar hegemony.
