'Not the Responsibility of One or Two People': K-Football Innovation Committee Launches Amid Bento's Advice and Chung Mong-gyu's Resignation

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: JTBC News · [속보] 벤투 감독, 한국 대표팀 복귀 원해…축구협회에 의사 전달 [이슈PLAY] / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

Former Manager Bento's Advice Highlighted After World Cup Exit

Following the South Korean national football team's group stage elimination on the World Cup stage, the statements and philosophy of former national team manager Paulo Bento are drawing renewed attention. In a media interview, former manager Bento expressed that the national team's poor performance and confusion should not be attributed to the responsibility of just one or two people.

Former manager Bento stressed that the overall system needs to be re-examined from scratch and that each member must fulfill their role and responsibility. He added that, above all, maintaining mutual trust and a consistent tactical system in operating the national team is crucial.

Bond with National Team Players and Assessment of Son Heung-min

Even after leaving South Korea, former manager Bento reportedly maintained contact with national team players and continued to show interest in them. Hwang In-beom shared that whenever his playing time at his club decreased, he received supportive text messages from former manager Bento. Bento also confirmed that he remains in continuous contact with a number of Korean players.

Former manager Bento assessed national team captain Son Heung-min as having the highest ability among the players he has coached. He also expressed confidence that Son will continue to commit himself as a national team player and keep proving his ability going forward.

President Chung Mong-gyu's Resignation and Changes in Korea Football Association Leadership

Following criticism and strong demands for his resignation from football fans after the World Cup debacle, Korea Football Association (KFA) President Chung Mong-gyu submitted his resignation and stepped down. Concluding his term of 13 years and five months, President Chung stated that all shortcomings and mistakes that occurred during the operation of the association were entirely his responsibility.

Following President Chung's resignation, the Korea Football Association transitioned to an acting president system led by the vice president and entered procedures to hold the next presidential election within 60 days in accordance with its articles of association. Along with improvements to the election system of sports organizations, the necessity of governance reform is also being raised.

Launch of K-Football Innovation Committee Co-Chaired by Park Ji-sung

Coinciding with the announcement of President Chung's resignation, the K-Football Innovation Committee, aimed at fundamental reform within the football community, held its first meeting and officially began its activities. At the first meeting, Co-chairman Park Ji-sung expressed a grave awareness that South Korean football can no longer move forward using previous methods.

The Innovation Committee stated that it will focus on preventing arbitrary operation of the football association and establishing transparent procedures and systems. In particular, it plans to concentrate on restoring the lost trust of football fans by democratically and fairly improving the presidential election system, which has faced criticism for being opaque.
