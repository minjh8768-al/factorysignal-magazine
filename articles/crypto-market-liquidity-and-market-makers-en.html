Crypto Liquidity Shrinks: CoinHub Korea CEO Urges Institutionalizing Market Makers

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 매일경제TV · [크립토NOW] 미신고 해외거래소 차단, 크립토 시장 구조 변화의 시작? / 이준복 / 코인허브코리아 대표. Summarised from that video.

As structural changes in the virtual asset market become visible, the government has moved to strengthen regulations on unregistered overseas exchanges. At the same time, as the decline in liquidity in the domestic virtual asset market continues, calls to prepare measures for market stabilization are also growing.

Blocking Unregistered Overseas Virtual Asset Service Providers

Financial authorities implemented blocking measures against unregistered overseas virtual asset service providers operating targeting domestic users. Authorities issued orders to block website access to 27 out of 44 evaluated companies.

CoinHub Korea CEO Lee Jun-bok pointed out that overseas providers have attracted domestic users by offering Korean-language websites and pocketed high fees. While fee rates at major domestic exchanges stand at around 0.16%, unregistered overseas providers charged fees ranging from 1.5% up to 10%, gaining unfair profits, he explained. Lee analyzed that financial authorities strengthened regulations for the purpose of user protection and anti-money laundering.

Current State of Shrinking Liquidity in the Domestic Market

Along with the government's tightened regulations, liquidity in the domestic virtual asset market was shown to have decreased significantly. According to a survey by the Financial Services Commission, total evaluated virtual asset value in South Korea dropped by more than 30% from 87.2 trillion won in the second half of last year to 58.9 trillion won in the first half of this year.

Transaction volume and deposits also showed a downward trend. Average daily trading volume fell by around 44% from 5.4 trillion won to 3.1 trillion won, and Korean won deposits decreased by over 35% from 8.1 trillion won to 5.2 trillion won. Lee expressed concern that declining liquidity is leading to a vicious cycle that reduces the attractiveness of the Korean market and drives away users and overseas operators.

Role of Market Makers to Secure Liquidity

Lee emphasized that institutional adoption of market makers is necessary to secure market liquidity. Currently, an official market maker system is absent in domestic virtual asset exchanges.

현재 국내 거래소에는 마켓메이커라는 제도가 공식적으로 존재하지 않습니다.

"Currently, a system called market maker does not officially exist in domestic exchanges."

Market makers play the role of supplying liquidity by filling the gap between buy and sell orders so that even large-volume trades can be executed at reasonable prices. It is a concept similar to market specialists in the stock market.

Establishing Institutional Safeguards and Institutional Market Outlook

Since market making in the virtual asset market had side effects of being abused for price manipulation in the past, Lee suggested that institutional safeguards must be a prerequisite.

거래소와 시장 조성자 간에 이해상충을 어떻게 막을 것인지, 그리고 내부 정보를 어떻게 막을 건지, 특정 가격을 인위적으로 높여서 유지할 수 있는 행위 자체를 어떻게 구분할 건지에 대한 제도적인 장치가 필요합니다.

"Institutional safeguards are needed on how to prevent conflicts of interest between exchanges and market makers, how to block insider information, and how to distinguish acts that artificially inflate and maintain specific prices."

Mentioning a recent case where global liquidity provider B2C2 signed a contract with a domestic institutional platform, Lee projected that the participation of global players will serve as a stepping stone to make up for the domestic market's lack of liquidity and support large-scale trading by institutional investors.
