Assembly Urges Caution on Crypto Tax, Ministry Stands Firm on Next-Year Start

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 채널A News · 구윤철 부총리 "가상자산 과세 내년부터 예정대로 시행" [현장영상] / 채널A. Summarised from that video.

Discussions were held on the virtual asset taxation system and its implementation timing during a meeting of the Strategy and Finance Committee of the National Assembly.

Flaws Pointed Out in Virtual Asset Taxation System

A lawmaker who questioned during the session pointed out as a problem that the domestic virtual asset taxation system classifies crypto gains as other income, making it impossible to carry forward losses. The lawmaker explained that an irrational situation arises where taxes are levied even if an investor is in an overall loss position, such as suffering a loss in one year and making a profit the following year.

Furthermore, the lawmaker emphasized that while major advanced countries such as the United States, the United Kingdom, and Australia classify virtual asset gains as capital gains and allow loss carryforwards, South Korea is taking a regulation-centered policy without specific support measures, raising high concerns that domestic investors will flee to overseas exchanges.

가상자산 과세에 대해서는 굉장히 신중하게 판단을 하실 필요가 있다고 생각합니다.

"I think it is necessary to make a very careful judgment regarding virtual asset taxation."

Automated Crypto Information Exchange Framework and Timing of Taxation

Mentioning the international implementation timeline of the CARF system, an automated information exchange framework for virtual assets, the lawmaker suggested that the timing of taxation should be re-examined. Given that 29 countries including Australia plan to operate the CARF system starting in 2028 and the United States from 2029, the lawmaker argued that it is desirable to review taxation in line with when the system actually becomes operational.

Government Says "Implementation Scheduled for Next Year... Supplementary Measures if Needed"

In response, a government official stated that virtual asset taxation is deferred until the end of this year, and according to current regulations, taxation is scheduled to take effect starting next year.

금년 말까지 저희들이 과세하는 거에 대해서 유예 중이고, 내년부터는 과세가 되는 그런 상황입니다.

"Taxation is currently deferred until the end of this year, and taxation will begin starting next year."

The government official explained that transitioning to a capital gains tax system requires a comprehensive review of the overall capital market system, not just virtual assets. Accordingly, the official added that the government plans to proceed with taxation next year as scheduled while supplementing necessary systems along the way.
