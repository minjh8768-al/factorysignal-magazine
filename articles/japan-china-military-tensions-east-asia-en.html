Sino-Japanese Military Tensions Rise as Japan Rearms and China Expands Capabilities

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: KBS 세계는지금 · 중일 충돌, 동북아 긴장 고조 (KBS_2026.08.01.방송). Summarised from that video.

In Northeast Asia, Japan's accelerating rearmament and China's military expansion are colliding head-on, sharply heightening regional security tensions. Japan is speeding up the deployment of medium-range missiles and the acquisition of strike capabilities under the pretext of strengthening its defense capabilities, while China is countering with the expansion of nuclear and missile facilities and aggressive naval and air force exercises.

Japan's Accelerating Rearmament and Acquisition of Strike Capabilities

Japan's Ministry of Defense has operationally deployed domestically developed medium-range missiles in Kumamoto and Shizuoka. The missile deployed in Kumamoto has a range of 1,000 km, covering Shanghai, China, while the missile deployed in Shizuoka is planned to have its range extended up to 2,000 km in the future. The Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force has also completed modifications and operational training to equip the Aegis destroyer Chokai with Tomahawk missiles, moving forward with operational deployment a year ahead of the original plan.

Shinjiro Koizumi, head of the Japanese delegation attending the Asian Security Conference, expressed deep concern over China's increased military spending and military activities.

China continues to increase its defense spending at a high level. China's external approach and military activities are matters of serious concern for Japan and the international community.

"China continues to increase its defense spending at a high level. China's external approach and military activities are matters of serious concern for Japan and the international community."

China's Strengthening of Nuclear and Missile Capabilities and Stand-Off

As satellite imagery of nuclear weapons facilities in China's Xinjiang region was released, signs of China's nuclear force buildup were also revealed. About 80 nuclear missile launchers and mobile launcher hangars were detected in the area. In addition, China continued its display of force by conducting a test launch of a submarine-launched ballistic missile (SLBM).

In response, Japanese Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara stated as follows:

関連の動向を注視してまいります。

"We will closely monitor related developments while maintaining the highest level of vigilance and surveillance posture."

Diversification of the US-Japan Alliance and Joint Sino-Russian Military Actions

Japan has participated in joint exercises with the United States such as 'Resolute Dragon' and 'RIMPAC', while also officially sending armed Self-Defense Forces personnel to participate in 'Balikatan', a joint military operation with the Philippines. Taking the opportunity of Philippine President Marcos's visit to Japan, it is expanding its front to contain China, such as pursuing the signing of a military information protection agreement.

Meanwhile, China is consolidating military cooperation with Russia. Following joint naval exercises, the two countries conducted joint air force strategic patrols in the East China Sea and South China Sea areas. Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs spokesperson Lin Jian strongly criticized Japan's military moves.

实际上，已经自己摘下了所谓和平国家的面具。

"In fact, Japan has taken off its mask as a so-called 'peaceful nation' on its own."

Space Surveillance Network Competition and Deepening Northeast Asian Conflict

The fight for dominance in the space domain is also fierce. Japan successfully launched the H3 rocket to transport reconnaissance satellites, kicking into high gear the construction of its independent satellite surveillance system. China is also countering by securing rocket booster recovery technology to enhance its satellite surveillance and tracking capabilities. The military confrontation and power struggle between the two countries surrounding Northeast Asia are expected to intensify further for the time being.
