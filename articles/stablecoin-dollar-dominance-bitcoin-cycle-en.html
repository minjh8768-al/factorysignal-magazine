CEO Baek Hoon-jong: Stablecoins Will Bolster Dollar Dominance and Explosive Bitcoin Gains Remain Possible

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 미국 정부가 '스테이블 코인'을 국가 아젠다로 정한 진짜 이유. Summarised from that video.

CEO Baek Hoon-jong analyzed that the spread of stablecoins will serve to bolster dollar dominance rather than weaken it. He also assessed that, despite observations from some market participants that Bitcoin's price gains in its four-year cycle will gradually diminish, the possibility of explosive further upside remains open given Bitcoin's small proportion relative to traditional asset markets.

The Relationship Between Stablecoins and Dollar Liquidity Expansion

Baek explained that the U.S. government is in a position where it must actively seek sources of dollar demand overseas and in digital realms to maintain its reserve currency status and manage its debt. According to his analysis, as the digital asset market continues to grow, demand for dollar-pegged stablecoins has naturally arisen.

In particular, he noted that the amount of U.S. Treasury bonds held by stablecoin issuers has grown large enough to rival the holdings of major countries. Baek pointed out that the U.S. Treasury holdings of a single stablecoin issuing company, such as Tether, have surpassed the holdings of the South Korean or German governments.

스테이블코인 발행사 하나가 달러 기반 국채를 가지고 있는 나라의 규모와 거의 맞먹는 국채를 가지게 된 것이다. 테더라고 하는 단일 회사가 한국과 독일 정부의 규모를 넘어버렸다.

"A single stablecoin issuer has come to hold Treasuries nearly on par with the scale of countries holding dollar-based Treasuries. A single company called Tether has surpassed the scale of the South Korean and German governments."

Accordingly, he explained that U.S. authorities are seeking to foster stablecoin issuance within the institutional system through legislation such as the GENIUS Act and the CLARITY Act. The blueprint is to encourage banks and Big Tech companies to issue dollar-based stablecoins across all fronts to increase dollar demand and expand liquidity.

Velocity of Money and Value Appreciation of Scarce Assets

Baek emphasized that active circulation of dollar-based stablecoins not only supplies dollar liquidity quickly around the world but also accelerates the velocity of money. The combination of an increased money supply and rising velocity leads to value appreciation for scarce assets.

While capital previously gathered in scarce assets such as domestic real estate, liquidity could concentrate in the future on Bitcoin, the scarcest asset in the digital ecosystem.

Diminishing Gains in the 4-Year Cycle Premise Cannot Be Definitive

Regarding claims that price gains in Bitcoin's four-year cycle linked to halving events will be smaller than in the past, Baek presented a different perspective. This is because Bitcoin's size relative to the total pie of asset markets is still very small.

He pointed out that while global wealth totals approximately $990 trillion, and traditional asset markets such as equities, government bonds, and gold range from tens of trillions to hundreds of trillions of dollars, Bitcoin's market capitalization is only around $1.5 trillion.

전 세계적인 부를 총합산하면 한 990조 달러 정도 된다. 기성 자산들에서 계속 파이를 가져오면 신규로 공급되는 유동성도 비트코인으로 더 많이 들어오지 않겠나.

"If you total global wealth, it comes to about $990 trillion. If Bitcoin continues to take market share from traditional assets, wouldn't newly supplied liquidity also flow more into Bitcoin?"

Baek added that as the entire asset market pie grows due to money supply expansion, if capital shifts out of existing asset classes and flows into Bitcoin, explosive rally events surpassing the past could occur at any time within the four-year cycle.
