President Lee Jae-myung Returns from Overseas Trip Facing Heap of Pending Issues

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: JTBC News · 형사사법·인사·대법관까지…귀국하는 대통령 앞 '현안 산더미' / JTBC News. Summarised from that video.

As President Lee Jae-myung returned from his trip to the United States and Mexico, difficult problems across state affairs have piled up. Urgent pending issues have emerged as immediate tasks, including a reshuffle of Blue House aides, leadership vacancies in criminal justice agencies, conflict with the Supreme Court, and political confrontation between the ruling and opposition parties.

Leadership Vacancies in Criminal Justice System and Personnel Vetting Controversy

One of the most urgent tasks is resolving the leadership vacancy in the criminal justice system. Although the opening of the Major Crime Investigation Office and the Prosecution Office is imminent, the selection of agency heads to take the helm remains stalled. Due to backlash within the broader ruling coalition, including hardliners in the Democratic Party and the Rebuilding Korea Party, even the request for a confirmation hearing for Major Crime Investigation Office chief nominee Kim Ji-yong (김지용) has not been sent to the National Assembly.

Kim Jun-hyung (김준형), floor leader of the Rebuilding Korea Party, expressed concern over former prosecutors taking core positions in reform agencies.

간판만 바꾸고 사람과 조직문화는 그대로 남는 것 아니냐, 검찰개혁을 한다면서 결국 검찰 출신들이 핵심을 차지하는 것 아니냐.

"Aren't we just changing the signboards while leaving the people and organizational culture unchanged? While claiming to reform the prosecution, aren't former prosecutors ultimately taking over key positions?"

The position of Minister of Justice has remained vacant following the withdrawal of nominee Kim Seung-won (김승원). On top of this, as ministerial-level nominees including Gender Equality and Family Minister nominee Yong Hye-in (용혜인) continued to step down, calls holding the personnel vetting system overall accountable are intensifying across both ruling and opposition parties.

Resignations Among Blue House Aides and Conflict with Supreme Court

A reshuffle of Blue House aides is also a major point of focus. Along with whether to replace Chief of Staff Kang Hoon-sik (강훈식), who offered to resign taking responsibility for the personnel vetting controversy, selecting a successor for the Director of National Policy—vacant for about a month—is urgent. Regarding Chief of Staff Kang's offer to resign, President Lee praised his hard work instead of accepting it immediately, but observations suggest it will eventually be accepted after his return.

Conflict with Chief Justice Jo Hee-de (조희대) over the refusal to recommend Supreme Court justices is also intensifying. The Democratic Party is pressing Chief Justice Jo to resign, claiming he ignored the opinion of the National Court Administration, and Democratic Party spokesperson Lee Yong-woo (이용우) strongly criticized the Chief Justice's position.

제왕적 대법원장에 의한 인사농단이자 전례 없는 격노로 헌법해석의 변경을 요구한 직권남용에 해당할 수 있는 중대사안입니다.

"This is an act of personnel tyranny by an imperial Chief Justice and a grave matter that could constitute an abuse of authority, demanding a change in constitutional interpretation out of unprecedented rage."

New Deputy Prosecutor General Lee Jeong-hyun Reports to Work Amid Prosecution Withdrawal Controversy

New Deputy Prosecutor General Lee Jeong-hyun (이정현), who has assumed the role of acting Prosecutor General, pledged on his first day of work to establish the agency as a human rights protection institution ahead of the launch of the Prosecution Office. However, when reporters asked about the possibility of withdrawing indictments in cases involving President Lee Jae-myung, he responded that the results of the fact-finding investigation must be examined, sparking political dispute.

Representative Joo Jin-woo (주진우) of the People Power Party criticized this as an oath of loyalty to withdraw the indictments. Meanwhile, President Lee Jae-myung cautioned against unnecessary controversy over withdrawal of indictments, asking the National Assembly during a press conference to delete the provision granting prosecution withdrawal authority from the special counsel bill on manipulated prosecutions.

Inter-Party Clash over Delayed Investigation into DMZ Explosion

The positions of the ruling and opposition parties are also sharply opposed over the on-site investigation into a suspected landmine explosion in the Demilitarized Zone. The opposition, including People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyeok (장동혁), strongly attacked the government, claiming it intentionally delayed the investigation.

이재명 대통령이 고의로 현장 조사를 지연시켰다면 이는 명백한 이적행위입니다. 탄핵은 물론, 당장 구속되어 재판을 받아야 할 범죄입니다.

"If President Lee Jae-myung intentionally delayed the on-site investigation, this is a clear act of treason. It is a crime that warrants not only impeachment, but immediate arrest and trial."

On the other hand, Democratic Party spokesperson Jo Gye-won (조계원) countered that it was merely a procedure to ensure the safety of the investigation team, urging an end to political attacks. Democratic Party lawmakers on the National Assembly's National Defense Committee plan to visit the site to check on progress and encourage the troops.
