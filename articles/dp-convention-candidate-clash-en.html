Democratic Party Leadership Race Intensifies: "Stalker" vs. "I'm Closer to the President"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SBS 뉴스 · [성민종합정치] 송영길, 정청래 집중 공격 / 주영진 앵커 "왜 출마했는지 보여주는 중" / SBS / 주영진의 뉴스브리핑. Summarised from that video.

As the Democratic Party of Korea's leadership race heats up, sharp exchanges between candidates continue. In particular, candidates Song Young-gil and Jung Chung-rae engaged in a fierce debate over party-presidential relations and their understanding of state affairs.

"Claiming to Protect Lee Jae-myung Makes You a Stalker": Song Young-gil's Harsh Criticism

Candidate Song Young-gil appeared on "Kim Ou-joon's News Factory" broadcast and directly criticized Candidate Jung Chung-rae's leadership and party-government relations. Candidate Song expressed a negative stance regarding Candidate Jung attempting to run again while mentioning his relationship with President Lee Jae-myung.

"내가 이재명을 지키겠다"고 아무리 떠들어도, 본인이 싫다고 하는데 계속 떠들면 이게 스토커란 말이에요. 그렇지 않습니까?

"No matter how much you shout 'I will protect Lee Jae-myung,' if the person himself says he doesn't like it and you keep shouting, that's what a stalker is. Isn't that right?"

"Closer to the President": Jung Chung-rae's Immediate Rebuttal

Candidate Jung Chung-rae immediately pushed back against Candidate Song's remarks, emphasizing his close friendship with the president. Candidate Jung claimed to be the person who communicates most frequently with the president and shares deep conversations with him.

대통령과 저는 송영길 후보와 대통령이 친한 것보다는 더 친할 겁니다. 송영길 후보는 그런 얘기들은 좀 삼가해 주셨으면 좋겠습니다.

"The president and I are probably closer than Candidate Song Young-gil and the president are. I hope Candidate Song Young-gil refrains from making such remarks."

Fierce Clashes Over State Tasks and Verbal Slips in Honam Debate

The clash between the candidates continued at a TV debate in the Honam region. Candidate Song Young-gil moved to gain the upper hand by asking Candidate Jung Chung-rae about the number of state tasks under the Lee Jae-myung administration and what the No. 1 state task is. When Candidate Jung answered, "I think eradicating rebellion is the No. 1 state task," Candidate Song corrected him by stating that among the 123 official state tasks, No. 1 is "Inscribing the spirit of the May 18 Democratization Movement into the Constitution."

Candidate Song also criticized Candidate Jung for showing a lack of understanding of the Lee Jae-myung administration regarding a verbal slip Candidate Jung made during his speech, referring to the Gwangju semiconductor cluster by saying "within President Lee Myung-bak's term."

Current Cumulative Vote Rates and Impact of Preferential Voting System

The cumulative vote rates tallies for the Democratic Party of Korea's national convention so far are as follows:

  • Candidate Kim Min-seok: 46.01%
  • Candidate Jung Chung-rae: 44.53%
  • Candidate Song Young-gil: 9.47%

Seong Han-yong, senior reporter at The Hankyoreh, appeared on a broadcast and analyzed how Candidate Song Young-gil's candidacy and the introduction of the preferential voting system would affect the gap and voter sentiment between front-runner Kim Min-seok and second-place Jung Chung-rae, predicting that a fierce contest will be maintained until the final stages of the race.
