Bitcoin Could Face Wave C Correction After Wave B Rebound: Elliott Wave Analysis

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 서울경제TV · 역사상 최초, 비트코인 4년 주기 붕괴? 'B파동의 끝'을 절대 놓치면 안 되는 이유. Summarised from that video.

A technical analysis of Bitcoin's price movement and wave structure in the crypto asset market has been presented. Based on Elliott Wave theory, detailed explanations were given regarding the ongoing rebound pattern and the expected upcoming correction phase.

Analysis of Flat Pattern and Wave B Rebound Potential

The expert mentioned the possibility of an expanded flat pattern and an A-B-C wave structure unfolding on the Bitcoin chart. They explained a wave structure where, following the formation of Wave A, Wave B creates a rebound exceeding the starting point of Wave A before leading into a Wave C decline.

While leaning toward the possibility of surpassing previous peak levels such as the $82,814 mark in the short term, the analysis suggested maintaining a short-term bullish outlook until a clear five-subwave structure is completed.

C파 파동이 완성되기 전까지 단기적인 포커스는 더 높은 구간을 바라보지만, 최종적인 포커스는 결국 하방 조정을 염두에 두고 있다.

"Until the Wave C structure is completed, the short-term focus points toward higher levels, but the ultimate focus remains on a downward correction."

Wave C Correction Phase and Key Points to Watch

It was noted that after the Wave B rebound concludes, a strong Wave C decline could follow. The level of $57,717 was suggested as a key downside support line, with advice to stay mindful of market volatility until a complete correction wave structure is formed.

The analysis also pointed out that shifts in market participants' sentiment and bad news tend to align with the timing of price corrections.

Connection to Bitcoin's 4-Year Cycle Pattern

The four-year cycle pattern (three years of gains, one year of decline) historically observed in the Bitcoin market was also reviewed. The expert evaluated that ongoing observation is needed to see whether this cycle will maintain its standard timeframe or experience timing shifts due to wave extensions.

  • Keep an eye on key upside target levels during the Wave B rebound
  • Check whether $57,717 holds as support when transitioning to a Wave C decline
  • Confirm the completion of the lower five-wave structure according to the Elliott Wave fractal model
