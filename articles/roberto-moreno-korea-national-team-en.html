"Effort and Honesty Over Results": Manager Moreno Speaks Upon Taking Charge of Korean National Team

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: SPOMAN · 모레노 감독 한국 대표팀 부임하자마자 밝힌 진짜 각오. Summarised from that video.

Manager Moreno's Resolve to Prove Himself on the Pitch

Roberto Moreno, who was appointed as the new manager of the South Korea national football team, shared his determination regarding Korean football through his official personal channel. Manager Moreno stated that he had watched Korean football with deep respect for several years, and has now become a coach working directly for Korean football.

Manager Moreno did not make hasty promises, such as how many wins he would achieve right away or what results he would produce in specific tournaments. Instead, he emphasized that he would do his utmost at every moment, lead the team based on effort, honesty, and respect, and prove everything he can show on the pitch.

Tactical Analysis-Centered Coaching Philosophy and the National Team's Tasks

Having pursued a coaching path since his 20s, Manager Moreno built his own coaching philosophy while gaining experience in tactics and analysis. He is known as a coach who evaluates players based on what role they can perform within a tactical framework rather than selecting them simply by name value.

Standing at a new starting line after the World Cup, the Korean national team faces an upcoming schedule of international matches. Manager Moreno is in a situation where he must inspect players firsthand, organize tactics, and quickly instill his style of football. Based on his tactical analysis and coaching experience gained in Europe, his ability to combine players' strengths into a unified team is considered a key task.

From the Spain National Team Days to Club Career

In the past, Manager Moreno demonstrated his ability by taking charge of the Spain national team as interim manager while Manager Enrique was away briefly, smoothly navigating Euro 2020 qualifying. However, after Manager Enrique returned, he left the national team following a conflict and began his independent managerial career.

Afterward, he managed AS Monaco, Granada, and Sochi, but at club level, he failed to achieve results as impactful as his time with the Spanish national team. As a result, taking over the Korean national team is expected to serve as a new opportunity for Manager Moreno himself to prove his ability on the international stage once again.

Fan Reactions and Future Schedule

Korean football fans generally view positively that Manager Moreno mentioned effort and honesty over results first, interpreting it as an intent to stabilize the team's atmosphere and prove himself on the pitch. In addition, expectations are being expressed regarding the combination of existing national team players with Spanish-style football and fair opportunities for K League players.

The national team is set to announce its roster call-up on the upcoming 14th, and football fans' attention is focused on the initial vision of the team under Manager Moreno's leadership.
