Crypto Tax Deferral Excluded from Government Tax Reform Plan, Leaving Room for Assembly Revisions

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 4 min read

Source: 코인덕 차트아지 · 코인 세제개편안 정부 입장 한방 정리. Summarised from that video.

It has been confirmed that provisions regarding the deferral of virtual asset taxation and loss carryforward deductions were both omitted from the tax reform proposal announced by the government. As a result, taxation is set to proceed under current law, but attention is focused on upcoming National Assembly discussions as a final decision will be reached in December following a review process after being submitted to the National Assembly in September.

Taxation Deferral and Loss Carryforward Fall Through… Only NTS Inquiry Powers Newly Established

A review of the detailed tax reform plan revealed that language deferring the tax timing demanded by investors and items related to loss carryforward deductions were not included. On the other hand, a new clause was added allowing the National Tax Service (NTS) to directly query account balances at virtual asset service providers in bulk and exercise rights to question and investigate when conducting inheritance and gift tax audits.

An official from the Ministry of Economy and Finance reaffirmed the current stance in an interview.

"현행법대로 2027년 1월에 가상자산 과세를 할 것이다."

"We will tax virtual assets in January 2027 under current law."

Public Petitions and Proposed Legislation Ignored… Defeat at Initial Government Stage

A national consent petition demanding the abolition of crypto taxation, which gained 50,000 signatures and was referred to a National Assembly standing committee, has reportedly not even seen a meeting convened after two months. Similarly, an amendment to the Income Tax Act aimed at abolishing or delaying taxation failed to advance to the standing committee review stage.

Although the need for loss carryforward deductions was raised during National Assembly meetings, the government maintained its stance to implement first and make adjustments later. When questioned about the issue at the meeting, the Deputy Prime Minister replied as follows.

"일단 2027년에 시행하고 필요한 부분이 있으면 그때 보완하겠다."

"We will first implement it in 2027 and make necessary adjustments at that time if needed."

Case of ISA Reform Reconsideration… Possibility of Reversal in Assembly Review

The government's reform plan is not finalized legislation but a proposal at the legislative notice stage. Once it passes legislative notice and cabinet meeting review to be submitted to the regular session of the National Assembly in early September, a final opportunity opens up for tax laws to be amended through standing committee reviews.

In fact, regarding the Individual Savings Account (ISA) reform plan included in this tax reform proposal, intense backlash arose immediately after the announcement over limiting accounts to domestic assets and reducing benefits, prompting the President to order a review just four days later. At a situation assessment meeting, the President pointed out as follows.

"정확하게 준비도 하지 않고 왜 그렇게 한 것이냐?"

"Why was this done without proper preparation?"

Continued Attention Required Until Final Conclusion in December

In the past, the gathered voices of many investors during discussions on repealing the financial investment income tax and deferring crypto taxation led to system revisions. The final outcome of tax law amendments is decided every year in December during the National Assembly plenary session handling process.

While investor demands were not reflected in the initial government stage, the National Assembly review stage starting in September is expected to be the real turning point. Attention is on whether investors' continued interest and expression of views can be reflected in the Assembly's review process.
