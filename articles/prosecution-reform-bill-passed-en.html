National Assembly Passes Criminal Procedure Act Revision Abolishing Prosecution's Direct Investigative Powers After 70 Years

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 3 min read

Source: MBCNEWS · [속보] '보완수사권 폐지' 형사소송법 개정안..국회 본회의 與주도 통과 - [MBC 뉴스속보] MBC뉴스 2026년 07월 31일. Summarised from that video.

An amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act abolishing the prosecution's direct investigative authority and supplementary investigative authority has passed the plenary session of the National Assembly. As a result, the prosecution's direct investigative powers maintained for over 70 years have been eliminated, bringing the judicial system reform centered on separating investigation and prosecution into its final stage.

Revision to Criminal Procedure Act Abolishing Prosecution's Investigative Powers Passes National Assembly

The amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act passed at the plenary session focuses on completely prohibiting direct investigations by the prosecution and separating investigation from prosecution. Instead, the prosecution retains the right to demand supplementary investigations as a means of controlling police investigations, and the police must comply within a designated timeframe from the day the prosecutor's demand is received.

형사소송법 일부개정법률안 대안은 가결되었음을 선포합니다.

"I declare that the alternative bill for the partial amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act has been passed."

The amendment also includes victim protection procedures that grant complainants and victims the right to object to a police decision not to transfer a case, and require all cases of major crimes such as sex offenses and child abuse to be transferred.

Stark Differences in Perception and Political Confrontation Between Ruling and Opposition Parties

The Democratic Party evaluated the passage of the bill as a historical turning point that places power previously concentrated in the prosecution under the control of the Constitution and the public. Conversely, the People Power Party strongly opposed the bill's passage by conducting a filibuster and foreshadowed legal action, including an adjudication on jurisdiction disputes, citing issues such as provisions expanding the grounds for dismissal of prosecution.

검사가 수사와 기지도 하던 권력의 집중을 끝내고 수사와 기소를 분리해 검찰 권력을 헌법과 국민의 통제 아래 두는 역사적 전환점입니다.

"This is a historical turning point that ends the concentration of power where prosecutors conducted both investigations and prosecutions, separating investigation from prosecution to place prosecutorial power under the control of the Constitution and the public."

Prosecution Leadership Tenders Resignations and Future Challenges

As the bill cleared the National Assembly, Acting Prosecutor General Koo Ja-hyun (구자현) tendered his resignation, stating that he felt deep responsibility. Acting Prosecutor General Koo expressed his position hoping that the core judicial functions of victim protection and determining substantive truth would not be impaired during the institutional restructuring process. Confusion at investigation sites and challenges in adjusting powers are expected to continue until the new criminal justice system takes root.
