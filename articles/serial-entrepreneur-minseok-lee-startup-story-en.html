Born in 1999 with 15 Startups and 3 Exits, Lee Min-seok Says: "I'd Rather Build a Startup That Changes the World Than Settle for a Moderate Exit"

FSFactory Magazine 팀 · 5 min read

Source: EO Korea · 3번 엑싯한 99년생이 제로투원을 고집하는 이유 | 이민석. Summarised from that video.

15 Challenges and 3 Exits... Serial Entrepreneur Born in 1999

Born in 1999 and a computer science graduate from Boston University, CEO Lee Min-seok ( 이민석) has carried out a total of 15 startup projects to date, achieving three exits among them. Having moved forward boldly across diverse fields—from a dating app for international students to AI stock news curation and a defense risk management platform—he is recently continuing his pursuit with a new AI startup in San Francisco, U.S.

Influenced by his self-made father, Lee had harbored a desire to start a business since childhood and began studying hacking after watching the American television drama 'Mr. Robot'. Later, while serving as an information security soldier at the Naval Cyber Operations Center, he participated in a defense hackathon where he developed a defense risk management platform, earning recognition for his technical capabilities by winning the Minister of National Defense Award.

"빛이 있는 사람들은 다 빛이 없어지고, 돈 많은 사람들은 다 돈이 없어지고, 그런 걸 보고 모든 게 다 디지털화된 세상에서 가장 파워에 있는 건 해커 아닌가... 취미로 계속 공부를 했어요."

"Seeing how people with light lose their light, and people with money lose their money, I thought that in a world where everything has become digital, aren't hackers the ones with the ultimate power... So I kept studying it as a hobby."

From International Student Dating App to AI News... Success After Success

Focusing on the loneliness and dating needs experienced locally by Korean international students, Lee launched 'Oettanseom' (Isolated Island), a dating service exclusively for international students. Without any marketing expenses, relying solely on promotions using the Instagram stories of nearby international students, the service gathered 1,000 subscribers in just one week and soon achieved a successful exit.

Next, when he uploaded an AI stock news curation service that he had built in two weeks as a university class project to Product Hunt, he received an investment proposal worth $500,000 (approximately 600–700 million KRW) from MiraclePlus (formerly Y Combinator China). Although he declined the investment to focus on graduating from university at the time, he gained another successful experience by selling the service to a buyer who wished to acquire it.

"일단 목표가 제로투원이 되다 보면 직접 제로투원을 하는 것만큼 빠르게 성장하는 방법이 없는 것 같다고 느꼈어요."

"I felt that once your goal becomes Zero to One, there seems to be no faster way to grow than doing Zero to One yourself."

"I Will Build a Product That Changes the World Rather Than Settle for a Moderate Exit"

After moving to San Francisco, he received an acquisition offer worth $300,000 (approximately 500 million KRW) for a character chat AI service he developed in just one month. However, he declined the offer with the goal of creating a product that can make a major impact on the world rather than exiting for a moderate amount.

This was because he came to realize, as famed investor Chamath Palihapitiya remarked, that in one's youth, one should focus on products that will change the world rather than businesses that simply make money. Moving away from the iterative method of rapidly testing MVPs and pivoting, he chose the direct approach of patiently building a product in which users feel true value.

Aiming to Earn 100 Billion KRW by Age 30 to Establish a VC Assisting Young Entrepreneurs

Lee expresses a goal of earning 100 billion KRW by age 30, five years from now, to establish his own venture capital (VC) firm. His vision is to indirectly change the world for the better by investing in promising young entrepreneurs.

He emphasizes that youth, when one has fewer family responsibilities or burdens, is precisely the right time to challenge grand goals and pursue dreams.

"30살까지 1천억 원을 벌고, 그 1천억으로 하고 싶은 거는 제 벤처투자회사를 만들고 싶어요. 제 VC를 만들어서 정말로 가능성이 있는 다른 젊은 창업가들한테 투자를 해주고 싶고..."

"I want to earn 100 billion KRW by age 30, and what I want to do with that 100 billion is create my own venture capital firm. I want to build my VC and invest in other young entrepreneurs who really show promise..."
